Backlog Age Report

How old is your backlog, in buckets and in one number.

Bucket a project's open work by age, see each bucket's share and oldest item, and export the distribution as CSV.

What it does

A backlog grows quietly. Nothing in Jira answers the question a grooming session actually needs - how much of this is more than a year old - and the issue navigator shows dates one row at a time, sorted by whatever you asked for.

Backlog Age Report is a Jira page that measures one project's open work. It reads the project's backlog, calculates the age of each item in days, and sorts them into six buckets: 0-7 days, 8-30, 31-90, 91-180, 181-365 and over a year. Each bucket shows its count, its share of the total, and its oldest issue with a link. Under the buckets is a table of the twenty oldest items with key, summary, age, status and type - the list a clean-up meeting works from.

The source is honest about itself: if the project has a board, the report reads that board's backlog; if it does not, it reads unresolved issues in the To Do status category, oldest first. The report says which source it used, because a board backlog and a JQL can differ.

A scan reads at most 2000 items and marks the report when it stopped at that cap. It works in batches - four pages of 50 for a board, two pages of 100 for a JQL - and only one scan runs at a time. Reports are stored per project so the page opens on the last result, and the CSV export gives the buckets for a slide or a spreadsheet.

The page reads issues and stores its own report; nothing in Jira is changed. Stored reports and scan state are removed on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Backlog Age Report.

  • Six age buckets from under a week to over a year
  • Count, share and oldest issue for every bucket
  • The twenty oldest items with links into Jira
  • Board backlog when the project has a board, otherwise an unresolved To Do query
  • The source used is named on the report
  • Up to 2000 items per scan, in batches, with a truncation notice
  • CSV export of the buckets; stored reports removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
