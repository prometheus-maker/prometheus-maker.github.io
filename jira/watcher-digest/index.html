Watcher Digest for Jira

Everything that changed on the issues you watch, in one page.

See the issues you watch that changed since your last visit, grouped by project and status, with counts of new comments and status changes.

What it does

Watching issues is passive: the changes arrive as email, mixed with everything else, and the issues you actually care about get lost in it.

Watcher Digest adds a page to Jira that lists what changed on the issues you watch since you last marked the digest seen. The result is grouped by project and then by status, each issue showing how many comments and status changes happened in the window, with no email or notification involved - you open the page when you want it.

The window starts at your last visit (seven days on first use) and is never earlier than 30 days. A digest checks at most 60 of your most recently updated watched issues, a few at a time, so a large watch list stays quick. Marking the digest seen resets it, and everything the app stores is kept per user and removed on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Watcher Digest for Jira.

  • One page listing watched issues that changed since your last visit
  • Grouped by project and status, with comment and status-change counts
  • No email and no notifications: you open the page when you want it
  • Window from your last visit, clamped to 30 days
  • Up to 60 most recently updated watched issues per digest
  • Per-user storage, removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
