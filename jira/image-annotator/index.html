Image Annotator for Jira

Draw on screenshots and attach the marked copy in one step.

Arrows, boxes, labels and redaction on any screenshot before it becomes a Jira attachment, from the issue panel.

What it does

A bug report with a screenshot and no markings still needs a paragraph explaining where to look, and redacting a customer name usually means opening a separate image editor and re-uploading by hand.

Image Annotator lives in the issue panel. It lists the images already attached to the issue - PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP, up to 50, newest first - and opens any of them on a full-size canvas at its native resolution. Six tools are available: arrow, box, ellipse, freehand pen, a text label you type before clicking the image, and a solid black redaction block. There are five colours, two stroke widths (2 px and 6 px), undo for the last shape and clear for all of them.

One click renders the canvas to a flat PNG and uploads it to the same issue as <original-name>-annotated.png, next to the original; the panel then shows it in the list. The file is checked against the size limit before editing starts - the site's own upload limit or your configured limit, whichever is lower - and an oversized file is reported instead of opened.

The drawing happens entirely in your browser and the upload goes straight from the browser to Jira, so image bytes never pass through the app's backend. The app stores two small records: the site settings and a counter of annotations made. Admins set the default colour, stroke width and size limit (10 MB by default, up to 20 MB) on a settings page that requires the Administer Jira permission; everyone else can still annotate.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the panel on the right side of any Jira issue.

  • Arrow, box, ellipse, pen, text label and solid redaction block
  • Five colours, two widths (2 px and 6 px), undo and clear
  • Works on up to 50 PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP attachments per issue, newest first
  • Saves as a new PNG named <original>-annotated.png on the same issue
  • Size limit checked before editing: 10 MB default, configurable up to 20 MB
  • Admin settings page for colour, stroke width and size limit
  • Drawing and upload happen in the browser; image bytes never reach the backend

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
