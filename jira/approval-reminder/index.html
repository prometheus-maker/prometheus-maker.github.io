Page Approval Reminder

Set review dates on Confluence pages and remind owners on a schedule.

Give a Confluence page a review date and owner, see what is overdue by space, and post a mention comment on the page when it is due.

What it does

Policies and runbooks go stale because no page knows when it was last checked or who owns it. A spreadsheet of review dates is out of date the moment it is written, and it lives nowhere near the page it describes.

Page Approval Reminder stores a due date and an owner for each page you add. Add a page by its content id, enter the due date and the owner's account id, and the app reads the page title and space itself. The overview lists every review grouped by space, with overdue and due-soon counts at the top, and a lead time in days (14 by default, 0 to 90) decides what counts as due soon.

A daily scheduled job posts a comment on each overdue page that mentions its owner, so the reminder sits in the page's own activity. At most 20 pages are commented on per run, and the same page is not commented on again within six days. Nothing is sent by email. Mark a page reviewed and it leaves the overdue list; save a new due date to put it back in rotation.

The app stores up to 5,000 review records, reads page titles and spaces, and writes only comments. An uninstall removes every stored review.

Install

Where to install it: Confluence Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Confluence settings → Apps → Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install. After that the macro is available in the macro browser when you edit a page.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Page Approval Reminder.

  • A due date and owner per page, with title and space read from Confluence
  • Overdue and due-soon counts, grouped by space and owner
  • Daily comment mentioning the owner, at most 20 pages per run, with no email sent
  • No repeat comment on the same page within six days
  • Lead time from 0 to 90 days, 14 by default, setting what counts as due soon
  • Mark reviewed to clear a page, or reschedule with a new date
  • Up to 5,000 reviews; stored data removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
