Jira Roadmap for Confluence

The epic timeline of a Jira project, inside a Confluence page.

A Confluence macro drawing each epic of a Jira project as a bar between two date fields, over a window of 3 to 12 months.

What it does

Plans live in Confluence and the work lives in Jira. The roadmap page is usually a screenshot of the Jira timeline, pasted and stale within a week, or a hand-drawn table that only one person updates.

Jira Roadmap for Confluence is a macro that reads a project's epics and draws them on a month timeline. Start and target come from two date fields you choose - Jira dates or custom date fields - so the roadmap follows whichever dates your teams actually maintain. Each bar carries the epic's key, summary and status colour.

Each Confluence space has its own settings: a project, the two date fields and a window of 3 to 12 months starting with the current month, six by default. Settings are changed on a Confluence global page by someone with access to both products, and every page in that space with the macro picks them up.

Epics with both dates draw a full bar. An epic with only a target date draws a short bar ending there, and one with only a start date a short bar starting there, so missing dates are visible as approximations rather than hidden. Epics with neither date are flagged in a single column instead of being dropped.

The macro reads at most 100 epics, ordered by target date, and says when the project has more. Results are cached for five minutes per space, with a refresh control on the macro. It is read-only, and the configuration it stores is all it owns.

Install

Where to install it: Confluence Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Confluence settings → Apps → Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install. After that the macro is available in the macro browser when you edit a page.

What you can do with it

You find it under the macro browser while editing a Confluence page.

  • Epics of one Jira project as bars on a monthly timeline
  • Any two Jira date fields as start and target
  • A window from 3 to 12 months, starting with the current month
  • Status colours per epic, with key and summary on the bar
  • Epics with a missing date drawn short and labelled, not dropped
  • Configuration per Confluence space, set once on a global page
  • Up to 100 epics per view, five-minute cache, refresh control

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
