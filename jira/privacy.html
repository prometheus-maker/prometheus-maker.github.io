Privacy policy: Jira and Confluence apps

Effective date: 2026-11-17

Who runs the apps

These apps are published by RiverHub d.o.o. on the Atlassian Marketplace. Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me.

What the apps store

Everything an app keeps is stored with Atlassian Forge storage, attached to the site where it is installed: settings you enter, queued job state, and cached results. The apps send no data to any server of ours, and we have none to send it to.

What they read

Each app reads only what it needs for its feature, with the permissions of the person using it: issues, comments, changelogs, attachments, users, or Confluence pages and their bodies, depending on the app. The Marketplace listing and the documentation page for each app say which.

What is not done

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no profiling, no sale or sharing of data, and no transfer to third countries.

Retention

Cached results expire or are removed when the app is uninstalled. Apps that keep job state delete it when the app is removed from the site.

Your rights

Write to the address above to ask what is stored for your site, to have it deleted, or to raise any other privacy question. Uninstalling the app also removes its stored data.
