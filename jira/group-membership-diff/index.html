Group Membership Diff

Compare two Jira groups and copy or remove the difference.

See exactly who is in one Jira group but not another, then copy or remove members in batches after a dry-run count.

What it does

Two Jira groups drift apart over months: the leads group gains three people, the team group loses one, and nobody notices until access breaks. Comparing them by hand means exporting both member lists and matching rows in a spreadsheet.

Group Membership Diff puts two groups in one table with three sections: only in A, only in B, and in both, sorted by name and paged 50 rows at a time. Groups are read up to 5,000 members each, with a warning when one is larger.

When the difference is clear, choose to copy A's extra members into B or remove them from A. The app states how many members will change before anything is written, and the write then runs in batches of 50: the first batch happens when you press Apply, and a five-minute trigger continues the rest of the job. A job covers at most 500 members, one job runs at a time, and Cancel stops it after the current batch. Members already in the target group or already gone from the source are listed as skipped rather than treated as failures.

The diff copies out as CSV with account id, display name, email, active flag and side. Only Jira administrators can open the page; stored diff and job data are removed on uninstall, and membership changes are never reverted by the app.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under Jira settings → Apps → Group Membership Diff.

  • Three sections: only in A, only in B, in both; groups read up to 5,000 members each
  • Dry-run count before any change is written
  • Copy A's extra members to B, or remove them from A
  • Batches of 50, continued by a five-minute trigger
  • One job at a time, 500 members per job, cancellable
  • CSV of the diff with account id, name, email and active flag
  • Administrator permission required; uninstall clears stored data

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
