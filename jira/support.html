Support: Jira and Confluence apps

Effective date: 2026-11-18

How to get help

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me. Every message reaches a person; there is no ticket bot that answers first. If you would rather write from Jira, use the app's support link on its Marketplace listing.

What to include

Please send the app name, your Atlassian site address (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net), what you expected and what happened, and the exact steps that led to it. A screenshot helps when the problem is in what you see.

How quickly a reply comes

Requests are answered within one business day, usually the same day. A confirmed bug in one of the apps is fixed as quickly as its impact requires, and you are told when the new version is live. If a request needs a change in your site's configuration, the reply says exactly what to change.

Free and paid plans

Support is the same on both. Installing the app from the Marketplace starts a 30-day trial on the paid plan; nothing is charged until the trial ends, and uninstalling before then costs nothing. On a free plan, the limit of each app is written on its documentation page.

Documentation

Each app has its own page with setup steps, what the free plan includes, and what Premium adds. See the full list of apps.

Security reports

A suspected vulnerability goes to the same address; the security policy says what happens next.
