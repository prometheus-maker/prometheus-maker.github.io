Issue Age Heatmap for Jira

How old your open work actually is, as a dashboard heatmap.

A dashboard gadget that buckets open issues by age, status category and assignee, from a project or any JQL.

What it does

A backlog looks calm until you count the shelf life of its issues. Five buckets - under a week, a week to a month, one to three months, three to six months, and older - tell a different story than a list of issue keys.

Issue Age Heatmap is a dashboard gadget. Point it at a project or paste any JQL, and it counts the open issues into those age buckets, split by status category and assignee, using creation or last-update age as the gadget is configured. Each gadget keeps its own configuration, so a dashboard can show one project next to another and compare them.

Rendering reads up to 1000 issues, so it stays quick on a normal dashboard. It is read-only: no field is changed, nothing is stored about the issues themselves, and the gadget's own configuration is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

  • A dashboard gadget with five age buckets
  • Split by status category and by assignee
  • Age measured from creation or last update, per gadget
  • Scope a project or use any JQL
  • Per-gadget configuration, so several projects sit side by side
  • Reads up to 1000 open issues per render
  • Read-only; only the gadget's own configuration is stored

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
