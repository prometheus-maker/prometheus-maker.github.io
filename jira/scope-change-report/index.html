Sprint Scope Change Report for Jira

How much a sprint's scope changed after it started, per sprint and per assignee.

Read a board's closed sprints and report issues added after the start and removed before the close, with a trend over recent sprints.

What it does

Sprint scope creep is usually invisible after the fact: the board is closed, the issues look settled, and nobody can say whether the team finished what it planned or what was added mid-sprint.

Sprint Scope Change Report reads the closed sprints of one scrum board and counts, per sprint and per assignee, the issues added after the sprint started and the issues removed before it closed. The result includes a trend across the last closed sprints - six by default, up to twelve - so a team can compare one sprint with the ones before it.

Everything is derived from issue changelogs and sprint names. The page is read-only, requests read access to Jira work only, and stores just its own report cache and job state, which are removed on uninstall. Very large projects are handled by reading at most 300 changelog entries per issue and 600 issues per build, with anything cut flagged rather than hidden.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Sprint Scope Change Report for Jira.

  • Issues added after a sprint started, per sprint and per assignee
  • Issues removed before the sprint closed
  • A trend over the last six to twelve closed sprints
  • Board picker and sprint count on one page
  • Changelog and issue caps that are flagged, not silently applied
  • Read-only, with stored cache removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
