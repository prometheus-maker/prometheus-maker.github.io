Issue Link Matrix for Jira

See how issues in a set are linked to each other, type by type.

Read the links among a JQL, epic or version scope and show them as a matrix, a per-issue grid and a CSV export.

What it does

Links are the relationship layer of a Jira project, and Jira shows them one issue at a time. Understanding how a release hangs together, or which epics depend on each other, means opening dozens of issues.

Issue Link Matrix takes a scope - a JQL query, a version, or an epic - reads the links among those issues and lays them out three ways: a matrix counting each link type (blocks, duplicates, relates to and the rest), a grid of each issue with its links, and a CSV export you can paste anywhere. One-way links are marked so a dependency that nothing points back to is visible.

The page is read-only: it requests read access to Jira work and nothing else, changes nothing, and stores no copy of your data. A scope can hold up to 200 issues, and issues outside the scope that appear in the links are counted and labelled so the totals add up.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Issue Link Matrix for Jira.

  • Three scopes: JQL, version, or epic
  • A matrix of link types with counts
  • One-way links marked, so missing back-links stand out
  • A per-issue grid of links, with out-of-scope issues labelled
  • CSV export of the whole result
  • Read-only with a single read scope; nothing is written or stored

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
