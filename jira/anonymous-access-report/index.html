Anonymous Access Report

Every Jira permission scheme and Confluence space open to visitors.

Two report pages that find where anonymous or "Anyone" holders have been granted access, with a severity per finding.

What it does

Anonymous access is granted once, for a public help centre or a marketing page, and then quietly stays in place for years. Finding it later means walking permission schemes one grant at a time in Jira, and spaces one at a time in Confluence, hoping to spot the one row that says Anyone.

Anonymous Access Report is a pair of pages, one in Jira and one in Confluence. In Jira it reads every permission scheme and reports each one that grants anything to an anonymous holder - the anyone holder, the Anyone group, or the anonymous user - listing which of eight tracked permissions are exposed. In Confluence it reads the space list and flags every space whose read permission allows anonymous access, including whether unlicensed access is allowed.

Findings carry a severity so the urgent ones come first: schemes that grant browse, edit or delete issues are high, create, comment, assign or manage watchers are medium, and anything else low; a Confluence space readable by anonymous visitors is always high. The list is filterable by severity, and each row links to the scheme administration page or the space.

A Jira scan needs the Administer Jira permission and a Confluence scan needs Confluence administrator rights; without them the page says so rather than showing an empty report. Confluence scans read up to 1000 spaces. The last result per product is kept so the page can be reopened without rescanning. Both pages are read-only - no permission or space setting is changed.

Install

Where to install it: Confluence Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Confluence settings → Apps → Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install. After that the macro is available in the macro browser when you edit a page.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Anonymous Access Report.

  • A Jira page scanning every permission scheme for anonymous grants
  • Eight tracked Jira permissions, with high, medium and low severity
  • A Confluence page flagging every space readable by anonymous visitors
  • Unlicensed anonymous access called out separately
  • Filter the findings by severity
  • Rows link to the scheme administration page or the space
  • Last report kept per product; nothing is changed in either product

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
