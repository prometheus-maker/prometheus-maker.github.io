Sandbox Copy Planner

Measure a project before you copy it into a sandbox.

Scan one Jira project and get its issue count, attachment volume, fields and people, with a rough copy time, before you start the copy.

What it does

Copying a project into a sandbox is a one-way decision: once it starts, you find out how long it takes. A project with two years of screenshots, forty custom fields and a hundred thousand issues behaves nothing like the small one you tested with, and Jira's own copy dialog tells you neither before you commit.

Sandbox Copy Planner lives on a project page. One scan walks the project's issues, reading all fields so it can see which are actually populated, and totals what a copy has to carry: issues by type, attachment count and total size, versions, components, custom fields in use with their names, and the number of people referenced as assignee or reporter.

From those totals it produces a plan table and an estimated copy time, using a stated model (Model v1: a fixed start-up cost, one minute per 400 issues, three minutes per gigabyte of attachments). It also flags the two cases that usually hurt - more than 100,000 issues or more than 50 GB of attachments, and attachments averaging over 5 MB - and warns when a copy should be scheduled outside working hours.

It also says what a project copy does not bring, in plain words: dashboards, filters, automation rules, board configuration and apps are not carried.

The scan reads 50 issues per page, three pages per batch, and stores its progress, so a large project is measured across several presses rather than one long request. One scan runs at a time. The plan exports as CSV. Scans are read-only, and the plan and scan state are removed on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

  • One scan per project, across every issue it can read
  • Issue count by type, attachment count and total size
  • Versions, components and custom fields in use, by name
  • People referenced as assignee or reporter
  • An estimated copy time with its model stated
  • Flags for very large projects and heavy attachments
  • CSV export of the plan; plan and scan state removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
