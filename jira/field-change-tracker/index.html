Field Change Tracker

See how often a field changed and where it moved.

A dashboard gadget that counts one field's changes over 7, 30 or 90 days, per target value and per day or week.

What it does

Jira's history tab tells you what happened to one issue. It never answers the question a team asks in a retro - how often did priority change this month, and where did it move to - because that answer is spread across hundreds of issues' changelogs.

Field Change Tracker is a dashboard gadget. It tracks one field, either a standard one (Status, Assignee, Priority, Reporter, Due date) or a custom option or text field, across a project or a JQL query, over the last 7, 30 or 90 days. It reads each issue's changelog, counts the changes to that field, and draws a bar per target value with the day or week each change landed in.

The scan works through the issues in chunks of 40, reading six at a time, and shows progress - so the first load on a large project is a visible count, not a spinner. The result is cached for five minutes per gadget and viewer and a Refresh button forces a rebuild. Five consecutive permission errors stop the scan rather than hammering Jira.

The gadget reads at most 400 issues per scan and says when the scope was cut off at that cap, so it is a sample of a large project rather than a full count. It is read-only: no issue is touched. Its configuration and cached results are removed on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

  • One tracked field per gadget: five standard fields or any custom option or text field
  • Scope by project or by JQL, with the period appended automatically
  • Periods of 7, 30 or 90 days, bucketed by day or by week
  • A bar per target value, with the smallest values grouped as Other
  • Chunked scan of 40 issues with live progress and a five-minute cache
  • Up to 400 issues per scan, with a notice when the scope was truncated
  • Read-only; gadget settings and cached results removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
