Attachment Gallery for Jira

See an issue's images as a gallery, not a file list.

An issue panel that shows image attachments as a thumbnail grid with a lightbox, and lists other attachments with size and type.

What it does

Screenshots are the most common evidence on a Jira issue and the hardest thing to browse. The attachment list shows file names, so finding the one screenshot that matters means opening them one by one.

Attachment Gallery adds a panel to the issue that shows image attachments as a thumbnail grid - twelve to a page - with a lightbox that steps through them with Previous and Next. Non-image attachments are listed underneath with their size and type, so nothing is hidden.

The panel is read-only and requests read access to Jira work only; it stores nothing. The first 100 attachments of an issue are covered, and a thumbnail too large to display becomes a file tile labelled Preview unavailable rather than a broken image.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the panel on the right side of any Jira issue.

  • Thumbnail grid of an issue's image attachments
  • Lightbox with Previous and Next
  • Non-image attachments listed with size and type
  • Paged twelve at a time, first 100 attachments per issue
  • A clear tile when a thumbnail cannot be displayed
  • Read-only; nothing is written or stored

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
