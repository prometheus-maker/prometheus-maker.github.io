Retro Board for Confluence

A retro board on a Confluence page, connected to your Jira sprint.

Run the retrospective on a Confluence page: sprint issues, notes, votes, and one-click action items that become Jira issues in the sprint.

What it does

Retro notes end up in a page nobody reopens, and the action items are retyped into Jira the next morning, when half of them are already forgotten.

Retro Board is a Confluence macro. Insert it on a page, connect it once to a Jira board and an active or future sprint, and the page shows that sprint's issues split into two groups by their real status: issues in a Done status category sit under Went well, everything else under To improve. Notes go into three columns - Went well, To improve and Actions - and the team votes on them during the meeting; a vote is a toggle per person.

Any note can become an action with one click. The macro creates a Task in the project that owns the board, adds it to the same sprint, and replaces the note with a card linking to the new issue. Existing issues are never edited; creating the Task and adding it to the sprint are the only writes the app makes, and it asks for just the Jira scopes it uses.

The sprint's issues are read from the Jira Agile API and cached for 300 seconds, up to 500 issues per sprint; a refresh button forces a re-read. Notes and page settings live in Forge storage keyed by the Confluence page, so each page keeps its own board. A board holds up to 200 notes of 1 to 500 characters, and a person can delete only their own note.

Install

Where to install it: Confluence Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Confluence settings → Apps → Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install. After that the macro is available in the macro browser when you edit a page.

What you can do with it

You find it under the macro browser while editing a Confluence page.

  • Sprint issues grouped by real status: Done under Went well, rest under To improve
  • Three note columns with per-person voting on every note
  • One-click action: a note becomes a Task in the same sprint, with a link back
  • Board and sprint picked in the macro; closed sprints are refused
  • Issue list cached for 300 seconds, up to 500 issues, with force refresh
  • Up to 200 notes per page, 500 characters each, own notes only for deletion
  • Settings and notes stored per Confluence page

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
