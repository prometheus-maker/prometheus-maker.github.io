Page Diff Notifier

See what changed on a page, line by line, where the page lives.

Watch Confluence pages and get a readable added and removed line diff in a footer comment when one changes.

What it does

A policy page, a spec or a runbook changes, and the people who rely on it find out by reading the whole thing again or by noticing too late that a number moved.

Page Diff Notifier watches the pages you name - paste a page URL or its ID, up to 50 pages. An hourly job checks the five least recently checked pages, and a Check now button runs the same code path for one page on demand. When a new version appears, it reads both versions in storage format, converts them to text lines, and compares them line by line.

The result lands as a footer comment on the page itself: a heading with the old and new version numbers, how many lines were added and removed, and the changed lines marked with + and - with one line of context. Up to 80 lines are shown, followed by a count of any further changed lines, so the comment stays readable on a long page. The last three diffs per page are kept and can be opened again from the app's page.

Two modes: mention, the default, mentions every subscriber in the comment, so Confluence's own mention notifications email them; or comment, a plain comment with no mentions. Each person subscribes and unsubscribes themselves on the app's page. The app never edits page content, sends no mail itself, and removes its watch and diff records on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Confluence Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Confluence settings → Apps → Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install. After that the macro is available in the macro browser when you edit a page.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Page Diff Notifier.

  • Watch up to 50 pages by URL or page ID
  • Hourly check of the five least recently checked pages, plus Check now per page
  • Line diff with added and removed counts and one line of context
  • Comment shows up to 80 changed lines and counts the rest
  • Mention mode notifies subscribers through Confluence, or a plain comment mode
  • Last three diffs per page kept and reviewable
  • Never edits page content; all records removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
