Assignee Load Gadget for Jira

Who is carrying too much, as one bar per person.

A dashboard gadget drawing each assignee's open issues and story points against the capacity you set.

What it does

Sprint planning starts with a question nobody can answer quickly: who is already full? Issue counts alone mislead - ten small fixes are not ten large features - and story points alone hide the person drowning in unestimated work.

Assignee Load Gadget draws one bar per person, showing their open issues and story points side by side against a capacity you set per person or as a default for everyone. A project key selects the work, or a JQL replaces it when the work crosses projects. Colour makes a person over capacity visible at a glance.

Settings live with the gadget, so a team dashboard and a manager dashboard can hold different capacities. The gadget reads at most 1000 issues and is read-only; its configuration is removed on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

  • One bar per assignee with open issues and story points
  • Capacity per person, plus a default capacity for everyone
  • Project key scope or any JQL
  • Over-capacity visible without reading numbers
  • Reads up to 1000 issues, story points field chosen in settings
  • Read-only; gadget settings removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
