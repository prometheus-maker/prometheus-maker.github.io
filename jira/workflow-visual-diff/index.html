Workflow Visual Diff

Compare two Jira workflows and see exactly what changed.

Put two Jira workflows side by side and mark every transition added, removed or changed before you publish the edit.

What it does

Reviewing a workflow change in Jira means switching between two editors and remembering what the old one looked like. The one transition whose condition changed is easy to miss, and publishing a workflow affects every project that uses it.

Workflow Visual Diff loads two workflows at once. Statuses are listed in each column, with a count of the transitions added or removed at that status, and every transition is listed underneath with a tag: ADDED, REMOVED or CHANGED. For a changed transition the app compares its rule signature - conditions, validators, post functions and screen - so a change that only adds a validator or a screen is tagged rather than hidden. A summary line counts added, removed, changed and unaffected transitions, and unchanged transitions are hidden unless Show unaffected transitions is switched on.

The app reads workflows through Jira's REST API and makes only GET requests. Both workflows are picked by name from the site's workflow list, and the page needs the Administer Jira permission. It keeps the last 10 compared pairs so you can return to a comparison, and removes that list on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under Jira settings → Apps → Workflow Visual Diff.

  • Two workflows side by side, statuses listed in each column
  • Every transition tagged ADDED, REMOVED or CHANGED
  • Conditions, validators, post functions and screen compared per transition
  • Added and removed counts shown against each status
  • Summary line of added, removed, changed and unaffected transitions
  • The last 10 compared pairs kept for later
  • Read-only; recent list removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
