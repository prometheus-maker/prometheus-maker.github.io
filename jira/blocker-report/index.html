Blocker Report for Jira

Every flagged or linked blocker, with its blocker and age in days.

Find issues blocked by the Flagged field or an "is blocked by" link, dated from the changelog, sorted oldest first, exportable as CSV.

What it does

Blockers hide in two places: the Flagged field and the "is blocked by" issue link. No Jira screen combines them, so a weekly blocker review starts with a manual hunt across filters and issue links, and the oldest blocker is the one most likely to be missed.

Blocker Report scans a JQL selection, keeps the issues carrying either the flag or an inward "is blocked by" link, and dates each block from the changelog. The table shows the issue, its type and status, assignee, why it counts as blocked, the blocking issues and how many days it has been blocked. Results are sorted oldest first, and a minimum age in days hides the blockers raised this morning.

The scan is a resumable job: 50 issues per search page and at most 25 changelog reads per run, continued by a five-minute trigger until it finishes, so a large project does not have to complete in one request. The report copies out as CSV under a file name with today's date.

The app only reads issues, fields, link types and changelogs. It creates no flags or links, and its stored scan is removed on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Blocker Report for Jira.

  • Any JQL selection as the scope, with a sensible default
  • Catches both the Flagged field and inward "is blocked by" links, naming the blocking issues
  • Each blocker dated from the changelog with age in days, sorted oldest first and filterable by minimum age
  • The blocking issues listed on each row, with the reason it counts as blocked
  • Resumable scan: 50 issues per page, 25 changelog reads per run
  • CSV of the report with today's date in the file name
  • Read-only; stored scan removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
