Page Owner Rotator

Rotate Confluence page reviews across your team, on a schedule.

Assign pages to reviewers in turn, create a Jira review issue for each, and show the current owner and due date on the page.

What it does

Documentation rots because nobody owns it. A policy or runbook is written once, and the question "who keeps this true?" has no answer, so the answer becomes "nobody, until someone notices".

Page Owner Rotator takes a space and a label - the pages that should be reviewed - and a list of reviewers. It rotates ownership so nobody carries all of it: each page goes to the reviewer with the fewest open reviews, and the count rotates through the list on ties, so the load stays even over time rather than random.

When a page comes due, the daily job assigns it, writes the owner and due date onto the page as a content property, and creates a Jira issue in the project you set - summary "Review page: <title>", assigned to the reviewer, due on the review date, labelled doc-review, with a link back to the page. A Page Owner macro on the page shows who owns it now and how many days are left.

Review intervals run from 1 to 365 days, so the same app covers a monthly check and an annual one. A daily run processes at most 40 pages across at most 2 rotations; Run now works through up to 20 pages of one rotation on demand. A rotation's page list comes from a CQL search of its space and label, read in pages of 50 up to 10 search requests.

If Jira rejects an issue, the failure is recorded and the assignment still happens, so the rotation does not stall. Stored rotations, assignments and run records are removed on uninstall; the owner property already written on a page stays there.

Install

Where to install it: Confluence Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Confluence settings → Apps → Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install. After that the macro is available in the macro browser when you edit a page.

What you can do with it

You find it under the macro browser while editing a Confluence page.

  • Rotations per space and label, with any number of reviewers
  • Least-loaded assignment with a rotating tie-break
  • Review interval from 1 to 365 days
  • A Jira review issue per assignment, with due date, assignee and a back-link
  • Owner and due date on the page as a content property
  • A Page Owner macro showing owner and days left
  • Daily run of up to 40 pages, Run now for 20 pages, and an overdue filter in the list

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
