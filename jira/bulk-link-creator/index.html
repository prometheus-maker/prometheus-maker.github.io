Bulk Link Builder

Link two JQL selections in bulk, without opening each issue.

Preview every link between two JQL sets, skip the ones that already exist, then create the rest in batches with a failure list.

What it does

Linking a test plan to its stories, or a dependency set to a release, means opening every issue and adding the link pair by pair. With forty issues on each side that is an afternoon of clicking.

Bulk Link Builder takes two JQL queries and one link type. It reads both result sets, builds every pair, and previews the plan before anything is written: how many links are new, how many already exist, and how many pairs it dropped because an issue would link to itself. Two modes control the pairing - every issue on one side to every issue on the other, or one to one by position when both sides return the same count.

Nothing is written until you press Create links. Then the plan is saved in shards of 500 pairs and worked through 40 links at a time. The first batch runs immediately; a scheduled job continues every five minutes, so a large plan finishes without holding a request open. Progress, the created and skipped counts and every failure are on the page, with Cancel and a CSV export of the outcome.

The app caps each JQL side at 500 issues and the plan at 2000 pairs, and tells you to narrow the queries when a plan is larger. Links are created with a link type that already exists on your site; existing links of that type and direction are skipped, not duplicated. The plan, the job record and the CSV rows are deleted on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Bulk Link Builder.

  • Two JQL selections, one link type, inward or outward
  • Cross-product or one-to-one pairing by position
  • Preview of new, existing and self-referencing pairs before writing anything
  • Links created 40 per run, continued by a scheduled job every five minutes
  • Live progress with created, skipped and per-pair failure detail
  • Cancel at any point, and CSV export of what was created and what failed
  • Caps of 500 issues per side and 2000 pairs, stated before you start

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
