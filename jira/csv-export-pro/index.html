CSV Export Pro for Jira

Export Jira issues with comments, worklogs and status history.

Build one CSV from any JQL query with the fields you choose, plus comments, worklogs and status history per issue.

What it does

Jira's own CSV export drops comments, worklogs and status history, so a weekly report means a second export and a manual join in a spreadsheet.

CSV Export Pro for Jira starts from a JQL query. Choose the fields you want, including custom fields, then switch on up to three optional columns that flatten the history of each issue onto its row: comments, worklogs and status changes. The result is one row per issue with a fixed set of base columns - key, summary, type, status, priority, assignee, reporter and the main dates - followed by your fields and the optional history.

The export runs in pages of 100 issues with a progress line, and stops at 5,000 issues or 8 MB of CSV text, whichever comes first, with a notice when it does. Comments are included as the first 20 per issue, each trimmed to 500 characters; worklogs and status changes are listed in full for the issues fetched.

UI Kit cannot save a file to your machine, so the finished CSV appears in a read-only box with a Copy CSV button. The last export and your field selection are kept in storage and removed on uninstall; the app only reads issues.

Install

Where to install it: Jira Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Apps → Manage your apps in Jira, choose Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → CSV Export Pro for Jira.

  • Any JQL query as the source
  • Standard and custom fields, chosen per export
  • Optional comments, worklogs and status history columns, joined onto one row per issue
  • Pages of 100 issues with a progress line
  • Stops at 5,000 issues or 8 MB of CSV text, with a notice
  • Copy CSV from a read-only box
  • Read-only; stored export and settings removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
