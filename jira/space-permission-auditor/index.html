Space Permissions Auditor

Who can do what in every Confluence space, as one CSV.

Audit all Confluence space permissions at once, flag anonymous access and administer grants, then export who can do what per space as CSV.

What it does

Space permissions live behind one screen per space. Nobody can answer "which spaces are open to anonymous users" or "who can administer space ENG" without opening every space in turn and reading the same long list again.

Space Permissions Auditor scans the site's global spaces and builds one table of space, permission, holder and risk. Each row says whether the holder is a user, a group or anonymous access, so a group grant is not read as one person. Anonymous access and administer grants are sorted to the top, and a banner states how many spaces allow anonymous access and how many grant administer to a group, so the answer is visible before you read a row.

The scan runs five spaces per call with progress on the page, covering the first 500 global spaces, and keeps its rows in storage so the result survives a reload. The CSV has one row per space, permission and holder, ready for a review spreadsheet or an audit file. A very large audit stops the CSV at 9 MB.

The app asks only for read scopes, never changes a permission, and removes its stored audit on uninstall.

Install

Where to install it: Confluence Cloud, from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Open Confluence settings → Apps → Find new apps, search for the app name and click Install. After that the macro is available in the macro browser when you edit a page.

What you can do with it

You find it under the Apps menu in the top bar → Space Permissions Auditor.

  • One table of space, permission, holder and risk
  • A banner counting spaces with anonymous access and group administer grants
  • Risk-first sorting: anonymous, administer, space administer, normal
  • Five spaces per batch with progress, up to the first 500 global spaces
  • Results kept in storage and reloaded with the page
  • CSV with one row per space, permission and holder, capped at 9 MB
  • Read-only scopes; stored audit removed on uninstall

Your data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own site. Settings and any cached results are stored in Forge storage, which belongs to your site; nothing is copied to another service, and nothing is read beyond what the app needs to do its job. Removing the app removes what it stored. Details: privacy policy.
