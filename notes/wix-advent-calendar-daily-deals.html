Wix advent calendar: a deal behind every door

23 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the app mentioned below.

Every November, Wix store owners try the same thing: a small discount for each day of December, opening like an advent calendar. The calendar stalls: 24 doors need a code that appears on its day, a door that stays shut before it, and a deal tied to one product.

What Wix gives you on its own

The coupons are built for this. Wix's help page on coupons puts it plainly: "You can schedule a 1-day sale that starts and ends at midnight without having to be by your computer at midnight to start and end it."

Dates are part of the coupon itself. The guide to creating a price discount coupon says: "You can choose the discount amount and set dates when the coupon is valid." The steps are "Set when the coupon is valid: Enter the start date. Enter the end date",

So the discounts can be ready in advance. What is missing is the calendar: a grid of doors, each locked until its date, with only the day's code on show. You can build that by hand with 24 coupons, 24 images and a text box per code, but that means editing the page for 24 days, and every code sits on it for anyone who looks closely.

A calendar that does the switching

Advent Deals is a widget plus a small dashboard page. You pick a product and a deal for each door. The app creates an ordinary Wix Stores coupon for that door, scoped to the product and valid from midnight to midnight of its day. On the page, today's door opens with the product photo, the deal, the new price beside the old one, the code with a Copy button, and Shop now.

Past doors say Ended. Future doors stay locked and show only their date, with a star when a deal waits behind them. Empty doors show one of your store's products, so the calendar is never blank; sold-out products are left out.

The free plan gives you the Advent calendar, December 1 to 24, with deals behind up to six doors and a small credit link. Premium is $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly. It puts a deal behind every door and adds three more calendars: the 12 Days of Christmas, the 8 Nights of Hanukkah (4 to 11 December in 2026) and a countdown to New Year. It also adds themes, a door colour, a today-only layout and removes the credit link.

The app needs Wix Stores, since doors lead to products. Its coupons are normal store coupons, so they work at checkout and appear in Coupons in your dashboard next to any you made yourself.

Which route to take

For a short run, say the last few days before Christmas, make the coupons yourself with their dates and add the codes to the page. For the full 1 to 24 December calendar, the app is the smaller job: set the deals once and the page keeps the schedule.
