How to run a timed exam on a Wix site

28 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the app mentioned below.

A certification exam needs a clock. Wix Online Programs has none. Its quiz help article covers the quiz name, where the quiz sits, and an optional passing grade. There is no field for minutes, no setting for how many questions to draw, and no control over going back.

Whatever Wix stores about an attempt is thin too. The Managing a Quiz article says: "Each participant is limited to a single submission. If they retake the quiz, their previous answers will be overwritten by their new results, as we only track the most recent attempt to avoid storing unnecessary data." For an exam, that means the sitting you would want to review is gone the moment the learner tries again.

Set up the quiz in Wix first

The questions still live in Online Programs. Create the quiz there with the questions and the passing grade, then paste its quiz ID into the app. Choose how long the sitting runs and how many questions to draw from the quiz. Those two settings are the exam.

What the learner sees

The player runs one question at a time and does not let anyone go back to an answered question. A countdown stays in view for the whole sitting, and when it reaches zero the paper is handed in without waiting for a click.

The score is worked out on the app's server from the questions that were actually drawn, not from anything the browser sends. Each sitting lands in the dashboard attempt log with its date, its length and pass or fail, and the attempt is also written back to Wix as a quiz submission, so the program keeps its own record even though Wix only keeps the latest one.

A different paper each time

The free plan runs one exam, in the order the questions already have, with ten attempts a month. Pro is $18 a month, or $180 a year at $15 a month. It adds random draws from a question bank (set a pool of 30, draw 10, and a learner who repeats the exam does not get the same paper), a practice mode, unlimited exams and attempts, and an attempt history export.

Where it stops

The app needs Online Programs and a quiz in it; it does not replace the quiz editor. The clock belongs to the player, not to the Wix quiz, so if the same quiz is also reachable from the program page, a learner can answer it there without a timer. Keep the exam in a program where the player is the way in. The free plan's ten attempts a month is a real limit for a class of any size.
