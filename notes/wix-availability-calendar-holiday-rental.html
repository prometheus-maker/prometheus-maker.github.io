Wix availability calendar for a holiday rental: show booked and free dates

23 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the app mentioned below.

A guest asks if 12 to 19 August is still free. You open Airbnb, then Vrbo, then Booking.com, because no page on your own site knows the answer. Three tabs later you reply, and the next guest asks the same thing. A calendar that shades the taken dates would have answered both in one look.

What Wix gives you on its own

Wix Hotels is built for this. Its overview says it suits "a B&B, apartment, boutique hotel or vacation rental", and you can add its calendar to any page: "Add a calendar to any page of your site to show your hotel availability." It also reads iCal files. In the import article you paste an Airbnb, HomeAway or VRBO link under Reservations, iCal, and Wix notes that "The sync is one way only, from iCal to the Wix Calendar." You add a separate URL for each room type.

Two catches. Wix Hotels is a full booking engine with reservations and payments, and Wix's newer version of it is Wix Hotels by HotelRunner. In that app's Airbnb iCal article, connecting Airbnb "requires a Premium plan", and the direction is the reverse of what most owners want: "The iCal integration will only transmit your Wix Hotels by HotelRunner calendar to Airbnb iCal. No data will be received from Airbnb iCal."

Wix Bookings is the other native option, and its availability is about time slots, not nights. Wix's help page says it "uses your settings and schedules, including existing bookings, to continuously calculate all the available time slots." For a cottage let by the week, that is not the shape you need.

A calendar for a normal page

I built Availability Calendar: iCal for the site that doesn't run on Wix Hotels. A widget you place on any page, with no Bookings or Hotels app behind it. In the dashboard you click a date to take it, click again to free it, or shift-click a whole stay. Check-in and check-out days show as half days, so a Saturday changeover reads correctly. Visitors see the month ahead with taken dates shaded.

Premium reads the calendar links from Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com. Paste the links once and the app fetches them every two hours, so their bookings appear without typing. That sync is one way: it never writes back to those calendars. Premium also gives you several calendars, twelve months ahead, an export link for the dates you marked, and a request form that saves the guest's dates, name, email and message in your dashboard and as a contact labelled "Date request".

Pro turns requests into bookings with the guest's name and a downloadable register, and sets a nightly rate and minimum stay for each season so visitors see a live price quote. It also checks up to 20 iCal links every 20 minutes and names any link that fails.

What it doesn't do

Because the sync is one way, a date you mark in the app does not block Airbnb; keep taking bookings on the platforms. The minimum stay is shown to the guest as a note, not enforced by the date picker. And an iCal file carries dates only, so guest names never reach your site.

Which route to take

If Wix takes your reservations and payments, Wix Hotels is the native path. If your bookings already live on the platforms and you want one calendar on your site that shows them together, this is the lighter route: free for one calendar, this month and next, with a small credit link; Premium is $6 a month or $5 a month billed yearly; Pro is $9 a month or $7.20 a month billed yearly.
