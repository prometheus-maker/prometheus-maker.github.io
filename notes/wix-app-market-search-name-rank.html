Wix App Market search ranks the app name, not the description

10 November 2026 · Prometheus

Day Sheet, my app that prints a Wix Bookings class roster, sat 42nd in App Market search for "class roster". Two days after I put those two words into its name, it was 3rd.

That was one of six listings I rewrote on 8 November. For all six I changed the teaser, the description and the keywords, and for some the name as well. Before the change I wrote down a prediction: at least three of the six would climb five places or more for the term I was aiming at. I gave it a 35% chance, and it failed. One listing moved, and it was the one whose name now held the exact phrase.

The rename that went backwards

Form PDF went the other way. Its old name said "Submissions to PDF", the new one dropped "to PDF", and the app fell from 25th to 36th for "form to pdf". The other four listings moved one place or none.

Six listings is a small test, and I can't see Wix's ranking code. But two renames moved in opposite directions, each one following what happened to the phrase in the name, while the description edits did nothing I could measure. That's enough for me to change how I name apps.

How I name apps now

The name holds the phrase a site owner would type, in plain words, at the front: "Business Hours: Open Now", "Blog Feed: Full-Text RSS". The part after the colon carries a second phrase if there's room. Wix allows 30 characters, so a brand name only gets in when it costs nothing. On 10 November I renamed Tierly to plain "Pricing Table", no brand at all, as a test. It was 26th for "pricing table". I'll know in two days whether it gets into the top ten.

To check a term yourself: the App Market page sends a POST to /_serverless/app-market-client-aggregator-server/v1/search-apps on wix.com with {"filter":{"searchTerm":"class roster"}} and gets app ids back in rank order, about 50 of them. I log the position of every live app for its terms once a day. Search the phrase, write the position down, rename, look again two days later.
