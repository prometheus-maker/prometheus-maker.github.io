Wix digital membership card with a QR code: what Wix has, and how to add one

23 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the app mentioned below.

Saturday morning at the studio desk. A member says she's on the monthly plan. Staff opens Subscriptions in the dashboard, types her name, scrolls to the right order, and checks the end date while a queue builds. A card on her phone with a code to scan would have settled it in two seconds. Wix doesn't make one.

What Pricing Plans gives members

When you add Pricing Plans, Wix creates a My Subscriptions page inside your members area. Wix's page on customizing it says: "Clients can log in to track their plan's status, see when it's set to expire, or cancel a plan (if cancellation is enabled)." That's the member's plan on a page. It isn't a card, it doesn't look like one, and there is nothing for staff to scan.

Wix knows this. Its own feature request page, Creating Digital Membership Cards, is still collecting votes and reads: "Currently, it's not possible to create digital membership cards. This feature would let you send a digital card to each client with their subscription details."

The card I built

Membership Card: Member Pass is a widget that shows the logged-in member their own Pricing Plans membership as a wallet card. It has the plan name, a status chip (Active, Paused, Starts soon or Ended), the month they joined, when the plan renews or ends, and a short card number. You set the title, colour, style, corners and font in the editor, and every word on the card can be changed.

Nothing is typed in. The card reads that member's own orders in Pricing Plans and follows a renewal, a pause or an end. Each member sees only their own card, because the app works out who is asking from the Wix login token, not from anything in the page. Visitors who aren't logged in get a short prompt and a link to your plans page.

Premium adds the QR code. It holds a link to the staff check page in your dashboard, with that order's id in it. Staff scan it with a phone that is logged in to your Wix account, or type the 8-character card number by hand, and the page answers at once: Active today, or Ended, with the member's name and plan. Premium also shows member photos and your logo, and removes the small credit link.

Pro keeps a check-in log of the last 500 staff checks, ready to list or download as a CSV. It lists members who run out in the next 14 days with a renewal message you can send, and it lets each Pricing Plan have its own card design.

What to know before you install it

The app requires Pricing Plans; without it there are no memberships to show. The QR opens a page inside your dashboard, so the phone scanning it has to be signed in to the site owner's account. Treat it as a staff tool, not a public page. The app stores no data of its own; the card is built from the orders Wix already has.

The free plan gives the card with plan, status and dates, your colours, and a small credit link. Premium is $5 a month or $4 a month billed yearly. Pro is $9 a month or $7.20 a month billed yearly.

If nobody checks membership at the door, you don't need any of this. The My Subscriptions page already tells each member where their plan stands.
