How to issue a credit note for a Wix invoice

28 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the app mentioned below.

A customer returns two of the five items you invoiced, or a job gets cancelled after the invoice went out. Your books now need a document that reverses part of that invoice and the tax on it. In Wix Invoices there is no button for that, and the gap is old enough to have its own feature request.

What Wix says about it

The request page is blunt: "Currently, it's not possible to create a negative balance invoice when crediting a customer account to correct a payment error or for a product/service that was refunded or returned (e.g. refunded store orders, items returned by your customer)." It is still collecting votes.

The same page points to the one thing Wix does handle: "While you can't issue an invoice reflecting a refund, you can issue a refund through Wix Payments." That returns the money. It does not give your accountant a document that names the invoice, the net amount and the tax being reversed.

Creating an invoice in Wix covers the forward direction well: items, tax, discounts, a due date, and an email the customer can pay from. The reverse direction is where you end up in a word processor at 11pm.

A numbered credit note that points at the invoice

I built Credit Notes for Invoices for this. Open the app, pick the invoice, and choose what you are crediting: one line, part of a line, a typed amount such as 50 of 200, or several lines together. The app reads the invoice from your account and issues the credit note as a PDF.

The note carries its own number from your CN series, names the invoice it reverses, and shows the net amount and the tax you are taking back off that invoice. It never credits more than the line still has left, so a second note on the same invoice cannot take the tax down twice. A note that turns out wrong is voided and keeps its number, which is what an accountant expects; numbers are not reused. Nothing on the original invoice is edited.

The free plan issues two credit notes a month. Premium is $7 a month, or $70 a year billed yearly, and adds unlimited notes, your logo on the PDF, a CSV export for the accountant and voiding. Your invoice data stays in your site: the app reads your invoices and keeps its notes there.

Where it stops

The app needs Wix Invoices and an invoice to point at, so it cannot credit a sale that was never invoiced. It makes the document; it does not move money. Send the refund through Wix Payments or your bank as you do now, and give the customer the credit note PDF alongside it. The CN numbers come from the app's own series, not from Wix's invoice numbering, so tell your accountant where the series starts.
