Wix vCard and QR code: a Save contact button on a Wix site

11 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the Save Contact app mentioned below.

There are two different jobs hiding behind "Wix vCard". One is a QR code on a flyer or a shop window that a phone scans and offers to save as a contact. The other is a button on your website that does the same when someone taps it. Wix covers the first one for free. The second is where people get stuck.

A vCard QR code for print: Wix's free generator

Wix runs a free QR code generator with a vCard type. You type your name, phone, email and the rest, and it makes a code that phones read as a contact. The details are baked into the image, so if your number changes you make a new code and reprint. For a business card that's normal. For a sticker on a door it's worth knowing.

A Save contact button on the site

A button needs a .vcf file behind it, and Wix doesn't give you a place to put one. In a Velo forum thread on exactly this, the asker couldn't store the file in Wix, and the answers point outside: host the .vcf somewhere else (a Dropbox or Google Drive link, or your own server) and link a button to it. I haven't found a newer Wix feature that changes that. The weak spots are the same every time. The file is a frozen copy of your details, a shared-drive link may show a preview page instead of opening Contacts, and nobody remembers to update it when the phone number changes.

A card that writes the vCard from Business Info

I built Save Contact so there's no file to keep in sync. It reads the Business Info you keep in your dashboard settings (name, logo, phone, email, address) and shows it as a contact card with a Save contact button. The button serves a vCard 3.0 file generated from those details at the moment someone taps, so on a phone it opens straight into Contacts. Change your phone number in Business Info and the card and every new download follow within about ten minutes. The card also has tap to call, email and a Directions link.

The free plan has the card, the button and a small credit link. Premium is $4 a month ($3.20 a month billed yearly) and adds a QR code beside the card, downloadable as PNG or SVG for print, a card for each of your locations, social links, a footer strip and a button-only layout, your own button text, and no credit. The QR code there points to the live vCard, so it doesn't go stale when your details change.

Which one to pick

Printed business cards only: Wix's free QR generator. A button on the site and details that rarely change: a hosted .vcf, but test it on an iPhone and an Android phone before you trust it. A button that stays right on its own, or one QR code for print that never needs reprinting: an app that builds the vCard from Business Info.
