Recurring invoices on Wix for clients who pay by bank transfer

28 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the app mentioned below.

A retainer client gets the same invoice every month: one line, one amount, due in 14 days. They pay it by bank transfer, as their accountant prefers. In Wix Invoices you make that invoice by hand each month, or you set a reminder in your own calendar and hope you keep it.

What Wix's own recurring invoices do

Wix Invoices has a Recurring Invoices screen, and it is real: the help article says "Recurring invoices enable you to automatically issue invoices to customers on a regular basis. Choose to invoice customers weekly, bi-weekly, monthly or yearly." You pick the billing cycle, when the series ends, the due date and the line items once.

The catch is how the money comes in. The same article says: "For recurring invoices, you need to use suitable payment providers such as Paypal, Stripe, Wix Payments or Isracard." The invoice is charged to the card or account on file. That suits a subscription. It does not suit the client who wants a PDF each month and pays it from their business account.

Wix has an open feature request for exactly that case: "Currently, you can issue invoices for recurring payments, but it is not possible to create recurring invoices which are paid manually." It is still collecting votes.

A schedule that issues the invoice and stops there

I built Recurring Invoices: Auto Send for the manual-payment case. You choose the client, the line item, the amount and the cycle: monthly, quarterly or yearly. The app creates the invoice on the issue day and leaves the payment to your client, the same as one you made by hand.

Each schedule shows its next three issue dates. A schedule that starts on 31 January stays on the last day: 28 February, then back to 31 March. Each period produces exactly one invoice, because the app records the period before it creates anything, so a restart or a catch-up run after downtime cannot bill the same month twice. Pausing a schedule stops it without touching the invoices it already made, and deleting it leaves them in place.

On the free plan the app makes the invoice as a draft, so you check it and send it yourself. Premium is $12 a month, or $120 a year billed yearly, and adds automatic sending on the issue day, a reminder before the due date, unlimited schedules and a CSV of every run. The free plan runs one schedule at a time.

Where it stops

The app needs Wix Invoices on the site, and it bills from the details you type into the schedule, not from a product catalogue. Each schedule carries one client, so a client with two separate retainers needs two schedules. If your clients pay by card and you want the money taken automatically, Wix's own Recurring Invoices does that already and you don't need this.
