Wix store locator: what the Google Maps element does, and when you need a list beside it

11 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the Store Locator app mentioned below.

If you have two or three shops, you may not need an app at all. Wix's own Google Maps element takes several pins. The Help Center article Wix Editor: Adding and Setting Up Google Maps walks through it: click Manage Locations, choose Multiple, edit the template location into your first shop, then use Add Location for each of the others. Each pin can carry a title, a description and a link. The article gives no limit on the number of pins.

Where the map element stops

That article says nothing about a list of locations next to the map, a search box or sorting by distance, and I found none of those in it. So a visitor sees pins and has to click them one by one to read an address. That's fine for three pins in one town. With twenty dealers across a country, people want to type their postcode and see the nearest one first, with its phone number and opening hours, without zooming around a map.

The other catch is that the pins are a second copy of your addresses. Wix already keeps your locations in Settings, then Business Info, then Locations. Move a shop and you have to fix it there and again in the map element. Forget one and the map sends people to the old address.

A locator that reads Business Info

I built Store Locator for the second case. It reads the locations you already keep in Business Info and puts each one on a map with a numbered pin, next to a list with the address, phone number and a Directions link that opens the route in Google Maps. Click a card and its pin grows; click a pin and the list jumps to that card. Change a phone number in the dashboard and the page follows. Layouts are list beside map, map on top, or list only.

The free plan shows up to 3 locations with a small credit link, which is the same job as the map element but with the list. Premium is $5 a month ($4 a month billed yearly) and shows every location, adds a search box for town, postcode or street, a Near me button that sorts by distance, open now and closing time from each location's own hours, and your pin colour, and drops the credit. Near me sorts in the visitor's browser; their position isn't sent anywhere.

It only knows what Business Info knows. A location with no coordinates shows in the list without a pin, and a dealer you haven't added as a location won't appear at all. If your dealers live in a spreadsheet or a CMS collection rather than Business Info, this isn't the right tool; Wix documents showing CMS collection addresses on Google Maps for that.

Which one to pick

A handful of shops in one area: the Google Maps element with Multiple locations, free. More than three, or customers who need the nearest one fast: a locator with a list and search. Addresses in a CMS collection: Wix's CMS-to-map route.
