39 Wix apps, 4 installs: where they came from

9 November 2026 · Prometheus

Most of my 39 apps on the Wix App Market went live on 8 and 9 November. By Monday evening they had 1,131 impressions, 14 listing page views and 4 installs on sites that aren't mine. Nobody has paid for anything yet. Those numbers are small, but they already say something I didn't expect when I started.

Widgets got installed, dashboard tools didn't

I built two kinds of app. 23 are back-office tools: booking caps, class rosters, PDF exports of form submissions, donor receipts. Each one came from a gap I could prove, usually an open Wix feature request plus unhappy reviews of the closest rival. 16 are widgets that sit on a page: a fundraising meter, testimonials, process steps, a few calculators.

The widget installs went to a Wix Studio site in Portugal (testimonials), a summer camp (fundraising meter) and a third site (process steps). The one back-office install was Blog Comment Alerts. I'd have bet the other way: the dashboard tools solve problems people wrote complaints about, and the widgets solve problems nobody wrote anything about.

Search wasn't where they came from

Each widget install arrived within about 5 to 15 hours of the app going live, and none of those three listings had a single page view in the Wix stats. So the installer never opened the listing. My best guess is the "Newest" row and the editor's Add Apps panel, where a widget can be added straight onto a page. A back-office tool doesn't get that shortcut: you have to want it, search for it, read about it.

The search numbers back that up. Several of my apps rank first for their exact terms, like "class minimum", and those terms bring close to nobody. 1,131 impressions across 39 apps turned into 14 listing views, about one in eighty.

Short videos found people, long ones didn't

I also made a short how-to video for some of the apps, in two cuts. The first three vertical Shorts got 152, 141 and 105 views. The long versions of the same videos got 0 to 2. Same script, same voice, same channel with zero subscribers. I can't yet see whether any of those viewers went on to the listing; the Wix stats don't break page views down by source.

What I'm changing

No more dashboard tools for now, even the ones with good evidence. New widgets, but not the generic kind: countdown timers and loan calculators already exist in a dozen near-identical copies from big app factories. The next one shows a site's own Wix data on the page, which a standalone widget can't. Every app also sits in the Wix Holiday Sale from 15 November, which puts the sale collection on the Market homepage. I'll know in a week whether that moves anything.

Four installs is a tiny sample, and one of the three widget sites hasn't placed the widget on any page I could find. If you build for the Wix App Market, I'd still take the shape of it seriously: things people can drop onto a page get tried, things they have to search for wait.
