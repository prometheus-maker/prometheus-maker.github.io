Wix hit counter after Wix retired its own: the options left

11 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the Visitor Counter app mentioned below.

If you search the editor for Wix's Hit Counter and land on its help page, the first line is the news: "As of May 24th, 2025 the Hit Counter app will no longer be supported." The article, Adding and Setting Up the Wix Hit Counter App, goes on: "we recommend switching to the Hit Counter app by POWR." There's no built-in counter element I could find in the Help Center, so a counter on a Wix site means an app.

The one Wix points to

Hit Counter by POWR had 256 reviews averaging 4.3 when I looked today. Its free plan lists one counter per site, your own design and a starting number. The first paid plan reads "All of Free + remove POWR logo", so the free counter carries POWR's logo. If you want a plain number on one page and don't mind the logo, it does that job.

A counter that agrees with your dashboard

The question I kept hitting was which number a counter shows. Your Wix dashboard already counts visitors in Analytics. A counter that counts on its own can drift from that, and then the number on your page and the one in your dashboard disagree. I built Visitor Counter to read the Analytics totals instead. It adds nothing to your visitors' browsers: no tracking script, no cookie, nothing new for a consent banner. The backend reads a few site-wide totals about once an hour.

It also starts with history. On install day it already knows up to 61 days of visits, so a busy site doesn't start at zero, and after that it keeps one total per month so the all-time figure grows. The trade-off is freshness: an hourly refresh is fine for "4,812 visitors this month" and wrong for a live "someone is here now" ticker.

The free plan shows one counter (unique visitors or visits, for the last 30 days or this month) as a card or a line of text, with a small credit link, and can stay hidden until it reaches a number you're happy with. Premium is $4 a month, 20% less billed yearly, and adds the all-time total with a starting number for the years before, today, 7 days and this year, up to three numbers side by side, a goal bar, badge and odometer styles, and no credit link.

Which one to pick

A classic hit counter with your own start number: POWR's, which Wix itself recommends. A number that matches your Wix Analytics and needs no tracking script: one that reads Analytics. A live "people online now" figure: neither of these, look for a live-visitors app.
