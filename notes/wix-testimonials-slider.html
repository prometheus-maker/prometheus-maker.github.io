Wix testimonials slider: the built-in ways, and when an app is worth it

11 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the Kudoline app mentioned below.

Wix's Help Center has a page for this, Wix Editor: Adding Customer Testimonials to Your Site, with two routes: a testimonial strip, and a list built from a repeater (Add Elements, then List, then Repeaters). You type each quote into the text fields yourself. Both are free and both stay inside the editor, which is the best reason to try them first.

What the built-in routes leave to you

A repeater lets you edit what's in each item, but its layout is fixed. A slider that moves on by itself, shows three cards on a computer and one swipeable card on a phone, and stops while someone reads takes real fiddling with strips and slideshows. Star ratings are something you draw with icons. And if you want Google to see your reviews as review markup, that's code. None of this is hard on its own; it just adds up when all you wanted was five nice quotes.

The other route people suggest is pasting a third-party widget into an Embed Code box. Check your plan first: guides I read say embedded external code doesn't run on a free Wix plan's live site.

A slider that looks done when you add it

I built Kudoline for people who want the finished look without the fiddling. It shows quotes as a slider or a grid of cards: the quote, a name, a role or town, and one to five stars. Three cards side by side on a computer; on a phone one card fills the width and the next one peeks in. You type or paste each testimonial in its settings and pick colours, corners and font, and the editor shows each change at once. Every quote is plain text in the page, including the ones off screen, so search engines and screen readers get all of them.

The free plan gives you 5 testimonials with no branding. Premium is $5 a month ($4 a month billed yearly) and allows up to 40, with customer photos, a masonry wall, a moving row, a rating summary above the quotes, and review schema markup.

Which one to pick

Three quotes on one page, no motion: Wix's strip or repeater. A slider that behaves on phones, or stars without drawing them: an app. If what you need is to collect testimonials from customers through a form, neither of these does that; they display what you already have.
