Wix Events waitlist for ticketed events: what Wix allows, and a workaround

11 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the Ticket Waitlist app mentioned below.

Wix Events has a proper waitlist, but only for RSVP events. The Help Center article Limiting Registration and Creating Waitlists for RSVP Events covers it, and Wix keeps a separate feature request, a feature-request page for waitlists on ticketed events, which tells you where those stand. Once a ticketed event sells out, the page just says sold out.

Switching to RSVP doesn't help

The obvious idea is to make the event RSVP so you get the waitlist. Two problems: RSVP events don't sell tickets, and Wix's developer docs say an event's type can't be converted after it's created. So for a paid event you're left with what most people do today, a note saying "email us if you want a ticket", and an inbox full of messages you have to keep in order by hand.

A waitlist that appears when the event sells out

I built Ticket Waitlist for that inbox. You put the widget on the event page. While tickets are on sale it takes no room. When the event sells out, visitors see "Sold out. Join the waitlist", leave a name, an email and how many tickets they need, and get their place straight away ("You are number 12 in line"). Signing up twice keeps the first place. A dashboard page lists everyone per event in order and tells you when a refund or a cancelled order puts tickets back on sale. Click Email next to the first person and your mail app opens with a ready message and the event link. It doesn't sell the ticket for them or hold one; you decide who gets it.

The free plan covers any number of events, with up to 30 people per event and a small credit link. Premium is $5 a month ($4 a month billed yearly) and removes the limit, gives each ticket type its own line, and adds a CSV download, one email to everyone waiting, and no credit link.

Which one to pick

A free event: make it RSVP from the start and use Wix's own waitlist. A paid event that might sell out: a sold-out waitlist on the event page, so people queue in order instead of in your inbox.
