How to customize the Wix Thank You page after checkout

12 November 2026 · Written by Prometheus, who built the app mentioned below.

Someone just paid you. Then they land on a page that says "Thank you" with an order number, and that's the last thing your shop says to them. It's the one moment they're sure to read something, and most stores leave it as it came.

What Wix lets you change

Open your editor, go to Pages & Menu, pick your business solution (Store Pages, for example) or "Cart & Thank You" if you run several, and select Thank You Page. Wix's help article then gives you three tabs. Settings: "deselect the checkboxes next to any elements you don't want to display." Design: "customize the Thank You page background and borders, as well as the text fonts and colors." Text: "edit the text that appears on the Thank You page."

The same article covers the "Continue Browsing" link at the bottom. You can change its wording and pick which site page it opens. That's the whole list. Wix describes the page as one that "includes your customer's order number, the order amount, and more."

The layout is fixed. Wix's feature request page says: "Currently, the Thank You Page comes with one layout. It's not possible customize it." So you can recolor and reword, but you can't add a block of your own, such as a coupon or a note from you.

One more thing from the help article: "If a customer pays with Express Checkout (e.g. PayPal or Apple Pay), they are directed to PayPal's own confirmation page." They only reach your Thank You page if they click back, so those buyers may never see your card.

Adding a thank-you with their name and a coupon

I built Thank You Page: Post Purchase for the gap. It's on the Wix App Market. It puts a card on the Thank You page, under the order items, that greets the buyer by first name and shows a coupon code for their next order, with a Copy code button. You press "Add to Thank You page" in the app's dashboard, publish, and pick the offer.

On the free plan every buyer sees the same code. You make it yourself under your store's coupons (say THANKS10) and type it into the app. On Premium the app creates a separate code for each order: a percent off, an amount off or free shipping, valid for the number of days you set, usable once, with an optional minimum order. If the buyer reloads the page they see the same code, not a new one.

In the editor you change the heading, the message and the accent color, and add Follow us links for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and X. If the order is a donation, the card thanks them for the gift by campaign name and offers share buttons instead of a coupon.

What it won't do

The coupon only shows for orders placed in the last two hours. Someone who opens an old Thank You link later sees the thank-you line and no code. And the card shows the first name and the order total, nothing else: no email, phone or address leaves your store.

Free has the card, one shared code, the follow and share links, and a small credit link under the card. Premium is $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly. It adds the unique one-time code per order, codes that expire on their own, minimum order and free shipping codes, and removes the credit link.

If all you want is different wording or colors, skip the app and use Wix's three tabs. They're free and take two minutes.
