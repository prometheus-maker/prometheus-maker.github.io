Support: Waiver: Consent & Signatures

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plans.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what the form or the desk showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Getting the form onto a page

Add the Waiver widget to the page you want and publish the site. Then open the Waivers tab in the dashboard, add or edit your text, and save. If you have several waivers, choose which one the widget shows in the widget's settings.

Why does the form say the waiver is not set up?

The page has the widget but no active waiver with text yet. Open the dashboard, press Add waiver, paste your text and save. The form shows it on the next visit.

Someone is under 18 (or under the age I set)

The guardian block appears as soon as the date of birth is entered. The parent or guardian's name, email and signature are required before the form will send. The guardian age is set per waiver in the dashboard; the free plan has no guardian block.

I changed the text. Who has to sign again?

Everyone who signed an older version. The app bumps the waiver's version when the text changes and puts those people on the "Needs signing again" tab, with a message you can copy for each person. Put the address of your form page in Settings so the message links straight there.

Searching for someone at the desk

Open Signed forms and type part of a name or email. The status tells you whether the signature matches the current text and whether it has expired. Print opens a printable page of one signed form in a new window; use your browser's print dialogue to save it as a PDF.

What is the PRO plan for?

PRO keeps the exact text each person signed with their record, seals every record so you can check in one click that it has not been altered, and imports paper waivers you already collect so the whole list lives in one desk.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The desk goes back to one waiver, 30 days of records, no guardian block, no search or CSV, and the small credit link comes back. Signed forms stay in your site.

Data and deletion

No IP address is stored. You set the retention in Settings; records past it are deleted automatically, and everything is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can delete a single record in your CMS under the app's collections.
