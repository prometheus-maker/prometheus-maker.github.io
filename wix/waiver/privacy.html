This policy describes what the Waiver: Consent & Signatures app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

You publish waiver texts in the app's dashboard. A form on a page of your site shows the text, takes a name, email, date of birth and a drawn signature, and stores the signed form in your site so the front desk can find it. For someone under the age you set, the form also asks for a parent or guardian's name, email and signature.

Data the app handles

  • Waivers: the title, the full text, how many months a signature stays valid, the guardian age, whether it is active, its version number and when it was last changed. Stored in a data collection on your own site.
  • Signed forms: the name, email address, date of birth, the signature image (a PNG), the guardian's name, email and signature image when the signer is a minor, the waiver title and version, the date and time signed, the expiry date, and a short browser description. Stored in a second collection on your own site.
  • On the PRO plan only: the exact waiver text the person agreed to, and a SHA-256 seal over the record so the desk can check later that it has not been changed. Paper forms you import yourself add the same fields with the source "paper".
  • Settings: how many days signed forms are kept, the address of the page that holds the form, and a signed copy of your plan status with its expiry time. Stored in a third collection on your own site.

What is not collected

No IP address is stored, and the app sets no cookies of its own and does not track anyone across sites. To stop repeated submissions, the backend keeps a salted, one-way hash of the visitor's address for as long as the signed form exists; the address itself cannot be recovered from it. No analytics, advertising or profiling of any kind.

Site visitors

The form is filled in by the person signing. What they type, and the signature they draw, go to you, the site owner, and are stored on your site. The line under the form says what is stored and for how long. A visitor can ask you, the owner, to show or delete their record.

Where the data goes

Everything stays inside your site. The app's backend reads and writes your site's collections under its own app identity. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and nothing leaves the site except what you export yourself.

How long it is kept

You choose the retention in the dashboard settings (30 days to 10 years). On the free plan records are kept 30 days. Older records are deleted automatically on the next visit to the form or the dashboard, and all records are deleted when the app is removed from the site. You can delete any single record in your CMS.
