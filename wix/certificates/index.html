What it does

Certificates for the people who finished your course or came to your event, made from the attendance you already track. The app reads your course and class bookings with the attendance you marked, and your event guest lists with check-ins. Each person shows up with their record. "6 of 8 sessions." "Checked in."

You decide who qualifies: everyone who booked once the course has ended, people who attended at least a set number of sessions, or a share of them. Event certificates can go to checked-in guests only. People from outside your bookings can be pasted in as a list of names.

Every certificate is a wide-format PDF in A4 or US Letter with its own number. Numbers are never given twice, so a certificate can be looked up later, and making one again keeps its number. You get a ZIP with one PDF per person, ready to print or send.

The wording is yours, with fields for the name, course, date, sessions, hours and number. Pick a template and a colour, add up to two signers, and check the live preview first.

Premium adds unlimited certificates, three more templates, your logo and signature, a CSV list, and filing each PDF on the person's contact card.
