This policy describes what the Certificates: Course & Event app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app is a page in your Wix dashboard. It reads who booked your courses and classes and who attended, or who registered for your events and checked in, and turns the people you pick into certificate PDF files. On the paid plan it can also save each certificate in the Files of the person's contact.

Data the app handles

  • Bookings: for the course or class you open, the confirmed bookings with each person's name, email and contact ID, and the attendance marked for each session. They are read to show the list and to write the certificates.
  • Events: for the event you open, the guest list with names, emails, contact IDs and check-in status.
  • Pasted lists: names and emails you paste into the app. They are used only to make the PDFs and are not stored unless a certificate is made.
  • Contacts: when you file certificates on contact cards, the PDF is uploaded to that contact's attachments. The app does not read other contact details.
  • Your design: template, colour, wording, signer names, logo, signature and numbering, stored in a collection on your own site.
  • A certificate log: for each certificate made, its number, the person's name, email and contact ID, the course or event name and ID, the month, and the filing result, stored in a collection on your own site. It keeps numbers from being given twice.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro and review boxes, kept in your browser's local storage.

Where the data goes

PDFs, ZIP and CSV files are made in your browser and saved on your computer. The app talks only to Wix's own APIs. The developer runs no server of its own, never receives, stores or sees your bookings, guests or certificates, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app stops all reading and filing. The design and the certificate log live in your site's collections, and filed certificates stay on the contact cards until you delete them.
