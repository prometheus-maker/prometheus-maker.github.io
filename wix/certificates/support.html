Support: Certificates: Course & Event

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the course or event name and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I make certificates for a course?

Open the app, keep "Course or class" selected and pick the course. Everyone with a confirmed booking appears with their attendance. Choose who qualifies, untick anyone you want to leave out, and press Make certificates. A ZIP with one PDF per person downloads to your computer.

Where does the attendance come from?

From the attendance you mark in your Bookings calendar. For a course, the app counts the sessions where you took attendance, so "6 of 8 sessions" means 8 sessions were marked and this person attended 6. If you never mark attendance, use "Everyone booked, once the course ends".

Can I make certificates for an event?

Yes. Choose Event and pick it. With "Checked-in guests only" ticked, only guests checked in at the door get one. Untick it to include everyone registered.

What about people who are not in my bookings?

Use "Paste a list": one name per line, with an email after a comma if you like.

How do certificate numbers work?

Each certificate gets the next number the first time it is made, and keeps it. Making it again later gives the same number. Numbers are never handed out twice, even if you change the first number. Set the prefix and first number under Design.

What can I change in the design?

The title, the line above and below the name, signer names and roles, colour, paper size (A4 or US Letter) and date format. Fields like {name}, {course}, {date}, {sessions}, {hours} and {number} are filled in for each person. Premium adds three more templates, your logo and a signature image.

How many certificates does the free plan make?

10 new certificates per calendar month. Certificates you already made can be downloaded again at any time and do not count again.

How does contact card filing work? (Premium)

Tick "Also file each PDF on the person's contact card" before you press Make certificates. Each PDF is added to the person's contact Files. People without a contact (such as extra guests on one RSVP) are skipped, and the Issued tab shows the result for each one.

Some letters look different

The PDF fonts cover Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters. Right-to-left scripts and Chinese, Japanese or Korean text are not supported yet.
