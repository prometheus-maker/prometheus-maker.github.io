Common questions

My products did not hide on the closing date

The app applies a pause the next time it is read: open the dashboard and press Refresh, or open a page of your site that has the Vacation Notice element. A pause is applied by the first of those visits after the closing date.

The notice does not appear on my site

Add the Vacation Notice element to a page in the editor. The notice shows only while a pause is running, so an empty editor preview means no pause is active.

The store did not reopen

Open your site once after the reopen date, or press Refresh in the dashboard. The app has no background scheduler; the reopen happens on the first read after the date.

Some products are still hidden

Open the dashboard and check the pause's status. If a pause was cancelled while it was running, press Refresh to restore the products. Write to the address above if a product stays hidden.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
