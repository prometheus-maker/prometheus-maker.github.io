This policy describes what the Vacation: Pause Store & Reopen app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you schedule a store pause. On the closing date it hides your products, shows your notice on the site through the Vacation Notice element, and on the reopen date it shows the products again.

Data the app handles

  • Pauses: the pause name, the closing and reopen dates, the notice wording and the hide-products choice you enter are stored in the app's own data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Hidden products: for each pause the app stores the ids of the products it hid, so it can show exactly those again on the reopen date.
  • Visitors: the site element asks the app's backend whether a pause is running, in order to draw the notice. The backend keeps no record of the visitor, sets no profiling cookie and stores no personal data.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app sends no e-mail, keeps no logs of visitors and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page of its own.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a pause removes its stored row. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them; products it hid are shown again when the app is removed.
