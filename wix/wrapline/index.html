What it does

Holiday buyers want to send a present straight to someone else, and a store without gift wrap loses that order or answers emails about it. Wrapline adds a small gift wrap box to your Side Cart and to the order summary at checkout. The buyer ticks "Gift wrap this order", types a gift message, and that's it.

The wrap goes into the cart as its own line with the price you set, so it shows in the total and on the order. The gift message sits right under it, so whoever packs the parcel reads the note in the order instead of hunting through emails. Nothing is added to your product list.

You set the wrap name, the price and the longest message in the app dashboard. In the editor you can change the box text and the colour so it fits your cart. A short note on your product pages can tell buyers that gift wrapping is waiting in the cart.

The free plan has one wrap at your price and a small credit link under the box. Premium adds up to four wrap styles with their own prices (paper, kraft, a gift box), free gift wrap above an order amount you choose, a gift receipt box so buyers can ask you to leave prices out of the parcel, and no credit link.
