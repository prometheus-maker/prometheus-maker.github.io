Support: Wrapline: Gift Wrap & Message

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, where you saw the problem (Side Cart, checkout or product page) and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where does the box show up?

Wix adds it to the Side Cart when you install the app. In the app dashboard (Apps, Wrapline) you can also add it to checkout and add a short note to your product pages. Publish your site once after adding them.

How do I set the price?

In the app dashboard, under "2. Your gift wrap", type the wrap name and price, then Save. Prices are in your store currency.

What do I see on the order?

A line named like your wrap (for example "Gift wrap") with your price. The buyer's gift message is under it, and on Premium a "Gift receipt" line when the buyer asked you to leave prices out.

Does it add a product to my store?

No. The wrap is added straight to the buyer's cart, so your product list and shop pages stay as they are.

Can a buyer change the price?

No. The price comes from your settings and is added by the app's backend; buyers cannot add or edit that line themselves.

How do I change the box text or colour?

In the editor, open the Side Cart or checkout, click the box, then Settings.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

New gift wraps use your first wrap at its normal price, the gift receipt box is hidden and the small credit link comes back. Orders already placed do not change.
