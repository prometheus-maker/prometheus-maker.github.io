This policy describes what the Wrapline: Gift Wrap & Message app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a gift wrap box to your store's Side Cart and checkout and an optional note to your product pages. When a buyer ticks the box, the app adds your gift wrap to their cart as its own line, with the gift message they typed.

Data the app handles

  • The buyer's cart: the box sends the ID of the buyer's current cart (or checkout) to the app's backend. The backend asks Wix for that cart, removes the app's own gift wrap line if there is one and adds a new one. It only reads the cart's items, subtotal and currency. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses and payment details are not read, stored or logged.
  • The gift message and the gift receipt choice: they are written into the gift wrap line of the buyer's own cart, so they appear on the order like any other item detail. The app does not keep a copy.
  • Your gift wrap settings: wrap names, prices, the longest message and the Premium options are saved as one item in a data collection the app keeps on your site. Box texts and colour are element settings that Wix stores with your site.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the box and its settings panel whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID. This answer is kept in the server's memory for up to ten minutes.

Site visitors

The box does not set cookies and does not track anyone. It acts only when the buyer ticks it, changes the wrap or saves a message.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your orders, carts, buyers or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
