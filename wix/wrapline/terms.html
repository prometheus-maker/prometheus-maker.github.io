1. What you get

Wrapline: Gift Wrap & Message is a Wix app with a gift wrap box for your store's Side Cart and checkout, a note for product pages, and a dashboard page where you set the wrap name and price. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in your Wix dashboard and editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan offers one gift wrap at your price with a gift message and shows a small credit link under the box. The paid plan adds up to four wrap styles with their own prices, free gift wrap above an order amount you choose, a gift receipt box, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the box keeps working with the free plan's options.
