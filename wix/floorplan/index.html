What it does

Visitors ask "where is the coffee stand?" and nobody has an answer they can point at. This app turns a picture of your venue — a mall, a market hall, an office, a museum floor — into a map people can click.

Upload a JPG or PNG of the plan and click the spot where each unit sits. Give the unit a name, a short description, a photo and a link to its own page, menu or booking form. On your site the map shows a labelled pin on every unit; a visitor clicks a pin to read about it, and clicks a floor tab to look at another level of the building.

The widget has a search box, so a visitor types a shop name and jumps straight to it instead of hunting across the plan.

Premium adds unlimited units and floors, so every level of the building gets its own tab, plus the search directory.

The free plan covers one floor and 10 units. Premium makes units and floors unlimited, for $6 per month or $5 per month billed yearly.

The map loads from your own site's backend, so the pins appear the moment you save them in the dashboard. Nothing is sent to visitors by e-mail and nothing is sold through the app.
