Support: Floor Plan: Venue Map

Need help with the app? Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me and describe what you see; a screenshot helps.

Common questions

  • The map on my site is empty. Upload a plan image in the dashboard first, then click the plan to place a unit and press Save unit. Give the site a moment, then reload it.
  • I cannot add another unit. The free plan covers 10 units on one floor. Premium removes that limit.
  • My plan image will not upload. Use a JPG or PNG. A PDF export can be saved as a picture first.
  • A pin sits in the wrong place. Open the unit, use the arrow buttons to nudge it, and save.
