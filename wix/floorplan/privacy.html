This policy describes what the Floor Plan: Venue Map app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you upload a picture of your venue plan and mark where each unit sits, and a map on your site where visitors click those units.

Data the app handles

  • Plan and units: the plan image you upload, and for each unit its name, description, photo, link and position on the image, are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Plan status: the dashboard asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Visitors: the map on your site is public. The app keeps no logs of visitors, sets no cookies, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it in the dashboard or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
