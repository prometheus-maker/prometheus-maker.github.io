This policy describes what the ROI Calculator app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a calculator widget to pages of your Wix site. Visitors type numbers into it and see the return on investment, payback time and net gain.

Data the app handles

  • Widget settings: the mode, title, intro, starting numbers, labels, currency, colours, font and display options you choose. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Numbers visitors type: the calculation runs in the visitor's browser. The numbers are not sent anywhere, not stored and not seen by you or the developer. They are gone when the visitor leaves the page.
  • Plan status: the widget and its settings panel ask the app's backend on Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID. It stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro box on the app's dashboard page, kept in your browser's local storage.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they type or do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your settings, your visitors' numbers or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
