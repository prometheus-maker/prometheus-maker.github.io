Quick answers

Where is the calculator?

Installing the app adds a calculator with sample numbers to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then ROI Calculator. Each one has its own settings.

How do I set my own numbers?

Click the calculator in the editor, then Settings. Under Starting numbers, type what visitors should see first. They can change every number on the live page.

What is the difference between the two modes?

Payback mode is for something that costs money up front and saves or earns money every month: it shows the payback time and the ROI over the months you choose. Return mode is for an amount invested and an amount got back: it shows the ROI and the yearly ROI.

How is ROI worked out?

ROI is net gain divided by total cost. In payback mode the total cost is the up-front cost plus the monthly running cost for every month, and the gain is the monthly saving or revenue for every month. The payback time is the up-front cost divided by the monthly gain after running costs. Yearly ROI in return mode is the compound yearly rate that turns the amount invested into the amount got back over the years held.

Can I use euros, pounds or another currency?

Yes. Type any symbol in Currency and choose whether it goes before or after the number.

Can it use my site's font?

Yes, that's the default. Use Change font in Settings to pick a different one, and "Use site font" to go back.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The calculator keeps working. The compare view, the chart and the button switch off and the credit line comes back.
