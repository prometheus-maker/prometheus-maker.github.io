Common questions

The footer shows the wrong business name

Open the page in the editor, select the footer widget and change "Business or owner name" in its settings panel. Each placed widget has its own settings.

The imprint fields do not show on my site

The imprint block is part of the paid plan. In the panel the fields carry a Premium badge, and on a free site they stay off on the live page.

The year is wrong

The year comes from each visitor's own clock when the page loads, so it changes by itself on 1 January. Clear the browser cache if an old copy still shows the previous year.

A legal link opens nothing

Check that the address starts with http:// or https://. Addresses in any other form are ignored on purpose.
