This policy describes what the Imprint & Copyright Footer app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to the footer of your site. It shows a copyright line with the current year, and on the paid plan a legal notice (imprint) and a bar of links to your legal pages.

Data the app handles

  • Widget settings: the business name, optional first year, company details, legal page addresses, colours and label language you type into the widget's settings panel. These are stored by your site as settings of the placed widget, the same way any element's settings are stored.
  • Plan status: the app asks whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and that one answer decides which parts of the footer are shown.
  • Rendered text: the copyright year is read from the visitor's own browser clock when the page loads. Neither the year nor the visitor is stored anywhere.

The app sends no e-mail, keeps no visitor logs, uses no cookies of its own and shares no data with third parties. Nothing is sent to a server outside your site.

Keeping and removing data

The settings live with the widget on your site. Remove the widget from the page, or uninstall the app, and the settings are no longer used. Your site's own backup rules apply to the stored settings.

Your rights

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me for any question about this policy or about data the app handles, including a request to access or delete it. You can also reach the app's support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date.
