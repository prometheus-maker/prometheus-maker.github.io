What it does

Many countries in Europe require a legal notice, an imprint, on every business site. Building one by hand means a text box, a long list of company details, and a date you have to remember to change.

Drop this widget into your footer, type your business name, and the copyright line is done: "© 2019–2026 Harbour Bakery". The year is worked out when the page loads, so on 1 January it already says the new one. No edit, no rebuild.

Premium adds the imprint block: company name, address, register court and number, VAT ID, managing director, supervisory authority, e-mail and phone, laid out as readable rows. Turn on the legal links bar and point it at your privacy, terms, withdrawal and imprint pages; only http and https addresses are accepted.

Labels come in English, German, French, Spanish and Italian, so the block reads "Impressum" or "Mentions légales" on the sites that need it. Colours, alignment and font size follow your footer, and several widgets can sit on one site when you run more than one brand.

The free plan is the copyright line with the automatic year. Premium is $6 per month, or $5 per month billed yearly. Nothing is stored outside your site.
