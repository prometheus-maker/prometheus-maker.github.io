Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds a "How it works" section with three sample steps to the page you were on. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Stepwell. Each widget has its own steps.

How do I change the steps?

Click the widget in the editor, then Settings. Pick a step in the list and edit its title and text. The buttons under the list add a step, move it up or down, or remove it. The widget changes as you type.

Why do my side-by-side steps stack on phones?

Four columns of text don't fit on a phone screen, so in narrow boxes the steps always sit one below the other, with the line running down the side.

Can I remove the heading or the line under it?

Yes. Clear the field in Settings and it disappears.

What is an "or" step?

A choice inside one step, for example "visit us" or "a video call". Both share one number, with a small "or" between them. It is part of Premium.

Is it accessible?

The steps are a numbered list of ordinary page text with a heading for each step, so screen readers announce them in order. The scroll animation switches off for visitors who ask their system for reduced motion.

Why can I add only 4 steps?

The free plan shows up to 4 steps per widget. Premium allows 20.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working. The first 4 steps stay; icons, links, "or" steps, the extra line styles and the scroll animation switch off.
