Privacy Policy: Utility: Meter Readings

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Utility: Meter Readings app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you keep a log of your utility meters (gas, water, electricity) per property, record the numbers you read and see the usage and the cost between readings.

Data the app handles

  • Meters: the property name, the meter label, the kind, the unit, the tariff per unit and the currency you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Readings: the reading date, the number on the meter and the optional note you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Plan status: the dashboard asks the platform whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and receives that one fact. The app has no backend of its own and keeps no separate copy of anything.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail, reads no other part of your site and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a meter removes its readings too. Removing the app removes its collections and everything in them.
