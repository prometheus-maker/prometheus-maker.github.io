What the app does

The app keeps a log of your utility meters and readings in your site's dashboard, works out the usage between two readings of the same meter, and, on the paid plan, the cost of that usage at the tariff you entered.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the data you enter. The app stores it on your site. You are responsible for the numbers you enter and for the tariffs you set; the app does the arithmetic with exactly those values.

Plans

The free plan covers one property and three meters, with unlimited readings on them. Premium adds unlimited properties and meters, the cost of each interval at the meter's tariff with the total cost, and a CSV export of every reading. Premium is billed by the platform at $4 a month, or $3 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.

Availability

The app is provided as it is. A reading that was typed wrong gives a wrong usage, so check the numbers against the meter before you rely on a cost. The developer is not liable for billing decisions, refunds or other losses that follow from the data entered.
