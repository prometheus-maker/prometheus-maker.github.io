Common questions

A reading is refused

The app refuses a number lower than the previous reading of the same meter, because usage would turn negative. If the meter really was replaced or reset, add the new meter instead, or enter the reading after correcting the older one.

The cost shows "— Premium"

The cost of an interval is a Premium feature. The tariff you type is stored on the free plan and used as soon as the app is on Premium.

The CSV export is greyed out

Export is part of Premium. The free plan shows the meters and the usage on screen.

I added a meter and nothing appears

Check the kind and unit on the meter, and look at the filter boxes above the table: a search text or a kind filter left in place can hide a row.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
