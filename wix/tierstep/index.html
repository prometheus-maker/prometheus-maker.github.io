What it does

A buyer looking at a mug for twelve colleagues wants to know one thing: what does it cost if I take more? This app puts the answer in a small table right under the product price. Buy 1 at $16.00, buy 2 to 4 at $14.40 each, buy 10 or more at $12.80 each, with the row for the quantity they picked marked as they change it.

The numbers come from your store's own automatic discounts, the ones with a quantity condition. Checkout applies only the best automatic discount per item, so the table works out the price the same way and shows what the buyer will really pay. A store-wide sale that already beats a tier is taken into account, and a product without a quantity discount shows nothing at all.

You can set tiers in the app's dashboard: pick a product, type "buy 5, 20% off" and so on, and the app saves them as normal automatic discounts. Discounts you make yourself under Marketing show up as well.

In the editor you can change the heading, hide the savings labels and pick the accent colour. Premium adds a line such as "Add 2 more to pay $13.60 each", a card layout, and tiers on every product instead of one.

The free plan shows a small credit link under the table. Premium removes it.
