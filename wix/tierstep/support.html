Support: Tierstep: Volume Discounts

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the product you tested with and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I add the table?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Tierstep, and click Add to product page. Then publish your site once. You can also add it in the editor: open a product page and add the Tier Table plugin.

How do I set tiers?

In the same dashboard page, pick a product, type the quantities and discounts (for example 2 items 10% off, 5 items 20% off) and click Save tiers. They are saved as automatic discounts, so checkout applies them.

Why does a product show no table?

The table appears only when a product has a quantity discount that lowers its price. Products without one show nothing.

Why does the table show a smaller saving than my tier?

Checkout applies only the best automatic discount per item. If a store-wide sale already gives more, the table shows that price, the same as checkout.

Do discounts I made myself show up?

Yes. Automatic discounts with a quantity condition that you made under Marketing show in the table too. Discounts with a minimum order total, members-only or custom conditions are left out, since the table cannot promise them.

How do I change the heading or colour?

In the editor, open a product page, click the table, then Settings.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The table keeps showing, with the free plan's options and the small credit link. Tiers already saved stay in your store and keep working.
