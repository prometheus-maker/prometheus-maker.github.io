1. What you get

Tierstep: Volume Discounts is a Wix app with a quantity price table for your product pages and a dashboard page where you set quantity tiers. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in your Wix dashboard and editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows the table for every product with a quantity discount, lets you set tiers on one product in the dashboard, and shows a small credit link under the table. The paid plan lets you set tiers on every product, adds the "add more to save" line and the card layout, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the table keeps working with the free plan's options and the discounts already saved stay in your store.

3. Your responsibilities

The tiers are normal automatic discounts in your store and checkout applies them. Choose prices you are happy to give, keep your discounts in order, and make sure your offers follow the pricing and promotion rules that apply to you.
