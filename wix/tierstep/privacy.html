This policy describes what the Tierstep: Volume Discounts app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a small table under the price on your store's product pages. It shows the price per item at each quantity, worked out from your store's automatic discounts. A dashboard page lets you set quantity tiers, which the app saves as normal automatic discounts in your store.

Data the app handles

  • Products: the product page gives the table the product's ID, the chosen variant and the quantity. The app's backend asks Wix for that product's prices and passes on only the prices and the store currency.
  • Automatic discounts: the backend reads your store's running automatic discounts and passes on only the ones that apply to the product (the quantity range and the discount). Discount names and usage counts are not passed on.
  • Tiers you save: the dashboard writes them as automatic discounts in your own store. You can see, change or delete them under Marketing at any time, and the app's Remove tiers button deletes the ones it made.
  • Table settings: your heading, colour and display choices. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the table and its settings panel whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID. This answer is kept in the server's memory for up to ten minutes.

The app reads no orders, customers or contact details. The backend keeps no logs of visitors and writes nothing to a database.

Site visitors

The table does not set cookies and does not track anyone. The quantity a visitor picks is used in their browser only, to mark the matching row.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your products, discounts or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
