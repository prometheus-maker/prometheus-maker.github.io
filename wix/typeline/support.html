Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds it to the page you were on. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Typeline. You can use as many as you like, each with its own phrases.

How do I change the words?

Click the widget in the editor, then Settings. Write the text before and after, and the typed phrases one per line.

The text is too big on phones

The widget shrinks the text to fit narrow screens. If it still wraps more than you like, lower Text size or shorten the phrases.

Can it use my site's font?

Yes, that's the default. Use Change font in Settings to pick a different one, and "Use site font" to go back.

Why does it show only 3 phrases?

The free plan types the first 3 lines. Premium types all of them.

Visitors who prefer less motion

If a visitor's device asks for reduced motion, the widget shows the first phrase without animation.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working. Premium options switch off and it types the first 3 phrases.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget shows your text and collects nothing.
