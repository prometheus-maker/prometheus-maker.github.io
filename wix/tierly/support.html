How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the table and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the pricing table?

Installing the app adds a sample table with three plans to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Tierly. Each table has its own plans.

How do I change the plans?

Click the table in the editor, then Settings. Pick the plan under "Plan to edit", then change its name, price, button text and features. Add plan adds one more; Delete removes the one you picked; the arrows move it left or right.

How do I write features?

One feature per line. Start a line with a dash (-Priority support) for something the plan doesn't include: it shows with a cross. Write "Storage: 50 GB" to show the value in bold. On Premium, add | and a short text (SSO | Sign in with your company account) to show an info tip.

Where do the buttons go?

Use Choose link under Button link to pick a page of your site, a section, an email or a web address. You can also paste a web address. Turn on "Open link in a new tab" if you want that.

How does the monthly/yearly switch work?

It's a Premium option. Give each plan a yearly price per month, and visitors can flip between the two prices. The "Save 17%" label is worked out from your prices; you can write your own.

Why only 3 plans?

The free plan shows up to 3 plans per table. Premium allows 6.

Can it use my site's font?

Yes, that's the default. Use Change font in Settings to pick a different one, and "Use site font" to go back.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The table keeps working. The switch, the comparison table, tips and number formats turn off, and only the first 3 plans show.
