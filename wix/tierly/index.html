What it does

Visitors who reach your pricing page want three answers fast: what each plan costs, what it includes, and which one most people pick. Tierly puts that on any page as clean plan cards: name, price, a short line, a button and a list of features with ticks and crosses.

Click the table in the editor and open Settings. Type each plan's name, price, button text and features, one per line. Start a line with a dash for something the plan doesn't include, or write "Storage: 50 GB" to show the value in bold. Link each button to a page, a booking form or a checkout. Mark one plan as the highlighted one and it gets a coloured border and a "Most popular" label. Pick your colours, corners and font; the editor shows each change at once.

A new table starts with three sample plans, so the page never shows an empty box. On phones the cards stack into one column. The script is small and loads after your page.

The free plan gives you up to 3 plans with no branding. Premium adds a monthly/yearly switch with a "Save 17%" label worked out from your prices, up to 6 plans, a comparison table built from your features, small info tips on features, and number formats like 1,299.00 or 1.299,00 €.
