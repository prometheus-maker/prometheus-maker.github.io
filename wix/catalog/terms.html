1. What you get

Catalog: Digital Product List is a Wix app with a site widget that shows a browsable, printable product list on your site: sections, item rows with name, product code, price, unit and description, a search box, section filters, an Enquire button and a print view. You install it from the Wix App Market and add the widget in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one catalog of up to 25 items, with prices, search, sections, the Enquire button, the print view and a small credit link under the list. The paid plan adds unlimited catalogs and items, hiding prices on a catalog, dated lists that go live and expire on their own, the store price watch, imports, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the list keeps working with the free plan's options.
