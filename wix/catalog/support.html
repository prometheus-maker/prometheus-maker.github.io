Support: Catalog: Digital Product List

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the list and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do I add my products?

Open your site's dashboard, then Apps, then Catalog. The first time, it creates a sample catalog of nine items so the widget shows something at once; remove them with one click and add your own, or paste a list from a spreadsheet.

How do I paste a list from a spreadsheet?

Select the cells in Excel or Numbers, copy, and paste them into "Paste a list from Excel". The first row can name the columns (name, SKU, price, unit, section, image); without it, the columns are read as name, code and price.

Can I import the products from my store?

Yes, on Premium: "Import from Wix Stores" copies each product's name, code, price and description into the catalog. It never changes your store products. You can edit the catalog rows freely after that.

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds it to the page you were on. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets, then Catalog.

How does printing work?

Click Print on the widget (or your browser's print command on that page). The app builds A4 pages with the catalog title and date and real page numbers, and opens the print dialog. Choose "Save as PDF" there. Nothing is uploaded to a PDF service.

The prices in the printed list are different from my store

The catalog keeps its own prices, so a special trade price is never overwritten by a store price. Premium adds a price watch: when a store price you imported has moved, the dashboard tells you, and one click updates the catalog's copy.

A catalog's date passed. What happens?

On Premium, a catalog with an end date shows as ended after that day, and a catalog with a start date shows as coming on that day; the widget says so under the title. Dates are a Premium option.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The list keeps working with one catalog of up to 25 items, prices always shown, no dated lists, no price watch, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: your catalog, prices and products stay in your site; the app never reads your store products except to import them or to check a price, and never stores anything about visitors.
