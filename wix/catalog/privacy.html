This policy describes what the Catalog: Digital Product List app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a product list to pages of your Wix site: sections, item rows with name, product code, price, unit and a short description, a search box, an Enquire button and a print view that builds A4 pages in the visitor's browser.

Data the app handles

  • Your catalog data: the catalogs you create (title, intro, currency, valid from and to dates, whether prices are shown, the enquiry email or contact page), the items you add (name, product code, price, unit, description, section, image address) and your logo address. The app keeps these in its own two data collections on your site. They are your data, and they stay in your site.
  • Store products: when you import your store products, or when the Premium price watch checks whether a store price has changed, the app's backend reads your products from Wix Stores (name, price, product ID, currency). It only reads them. It never changes a store product.
  • Plan status: the widget, the settings panel and the dashboard page ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID. The answer is kept for a few hours in your site's data, signed so it cannot be changed.
  • Widget settings: the catalogue you picked and your display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors and no copy of your data outside your site.

Site visitors

Visitors see the items you published. The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. The Enquire button opens the visitor's own email program, or the contact page you named; what the visitor writes there goes to you, not to the developer.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your catalog, your prices or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
