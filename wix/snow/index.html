What it does

Snow puts gently falling snow over every page of your site, so it feels like the season the moment someone arrives. There is nothing to drag onto pages and nothing to code. Open the app, press Turn on snow, and your published site has it.

You choose how much snow falls, how big the flakes are, how fast they drift and what colour they are. A live preview in the app shows each change before you save. White snow disappears on a white page, so pick a soft blue or grey there.

The snow sits on its own transparent layer above the page. Clicks, taps and scrolling go straight through to your site, so buttons, menus and forms work as usual. It starts only after the rest of the page has loaded, pauses while the browser tab is hidden, and visitors who ask their device for less motion see still flakes instead of moving ones. It stores nothing about visitors.

The free plan gives you classic snow on every page, up to 60 flakes at once, in any colour. Premium adds snowflakes, stars, hearts, confetti and autumn leaves for other seasons, up to 200 at once, wind, start and end dates so it switches itself on and off, a home page only option and a way to hide it on phones.
