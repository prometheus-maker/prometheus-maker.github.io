Common questions

How do I turn the snow on?

Open the app from your site's dashboard and press Turn on snow. It appears on every page of your published site. The same switch turns it off.

I can't see the snow on my site.

Check three things: the switch at the top of the app says Snow is on, your site is published, and the colour stands out from your page (white snow on a white page is invisible). On the paid plan, also check the dates, the home page only option and the phone option. Visitors who set their device to reduce motion see still flakes.

Does it slow my site down?

The script is small and starts only after the rest of the page has loaded. It pauses while the browser tab is hidden.

Can visitors still click my buttons?

Yes. The snow is on a transparent layer that lets every click, tap and scroll through.

What happens if my paid plan ends?

The snow keeps working with the free plan's settings: classic snow, up to 60 flakes.
