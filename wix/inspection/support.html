Common questions

My check-out does not show a comparison

The app pairs a check-out with a check-in that has the same property, tenant and reference, dated on or before the check-out. If any of those three differs, there is no match. Open both reports and make the reference and tenant name agree.

A line is missing from the comparison

Only lines that got worse are listed: a line that stayed good, or went from fair wear to good, is not damage. A damaged line that is new at check-out also appears.

The damage total is not what I expected

Each damaged line carries its own amount, rounded to the currency cent. Check the lines in the check-out report; the total is the sum of those amounts.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.

Can I attach photos?

Not in this version. The report records the condition in words, with a note per line. Photo attachment is planned for the next version.
