Privacy Policy: Form Backup: Submission Export

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Form Backup: Submission Export app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you save dated copies of your forms' submissions, compare each copy with the one before it, and download a copy as a CSV file.

Data the app handles

  • Submissions: the app's backend reads your site's form submissions and their form names through the site's own services, and stores a copy of each answer in the app's data collections on your site. The app keeps no separate copy on any other server.
  • Files: an uploaded-file answer is stored as the file's name only. The file's bytes are never copied, downloaded or read.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail, and shares no data with third parties. Data read by the app is sent nowhere except your own site's storage.

Keeping and removing data

Backups stay on your site until you delete them in the dashboard or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and every backup in them. A CSV you download is yours and stays wherever you save it.
