These terms cover the Form Backup: Submission Export app ("the app") on your site.

What the app does

The app reads the submissions your site's forms collect, stores dated copies in the app's own data collections on your site, compares each copy with the previous one, and downloads a kept copy as a CSV file. It does not restore deleted submissions; the Forms trash already restores them for 180 days.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the data the app copies. The app stores its copies on your site. You are responsible for your site's forms, the answers they collect and who may see them.

Plans

The free plan backs up the 50 newest submissions and keeps the 2 most recent copies. Premium backs up every submission from every form, keeps 12 months of backup history and takes a weekly automatic copy. Premium is billed by the platform at $6 a month, or $5 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.

Availability

The app is provided as it is. It reads what the site's services return at that moment, so check a backup and its CSV before you rely on them. The developer is not liable for lost answers, for a backup that could not be taken, or for losses that follow from either.
