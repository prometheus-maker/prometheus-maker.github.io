Support: Booking Policy: Cancellations

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on. To add it somewhere else in the editor: Add Elements, then App Widgets, then Booking Policy.

Where do the rules come from?

From your Bookings settings, under Booking Policies: cancellation and rescheduling rules, cancellation fees, how early and how late clients can book, and whether a card is saved. Change them there; the widget follows within about 5 minutes.

I see a sample policy

Wix Bookings is not on your site yet. Only you see the sample, in the editor. Your visitors see nothing until you add Bookings.

Can I change the wording?

You can change the heading, and choose which parts to show (your policy description, rescheduling, booking window, card). On the paid plan you can add your own note under the rules and pick the language.

I have several policies

The free plan shows your default policy. On the paid plan, choose "All policies in use" to show each policy with the services that use it, or pick one policy for a page.
