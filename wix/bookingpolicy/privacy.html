This policy describes what the Booking Policy: Cancellations app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that writes your booking rules out in plain words, for example "Free cancellation up to 48 hours before. 48–24 hours before: 50% fee."

Data the app handles

  • Booking policies: the app's backend reads your site's booking policies from Wix Bookings (cancellation and rescheduling rules, fee windows, how early and how late clients can book, whether a card is saved, and the policy description you wrote).
  • Services: it reads the names and page links of your services to show which policy each one uses. It does not read bookings, clients, prices, staff or schedules.
  • Plan: the backend reads whether your site has the free or the paid plan.

What the app stores

  • Nothing in your site and nothing in a database of ours. The backend keeps the rules and service names in memory for about 5 minutes so pages load quickly.
  • In your visitors' browsers the widget remembers the last rules it showed, so it appears at once on the next page. It sets no cookie and adds no tracking script.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read or store any data about your clients or site visitors.
  • It does not sell, share or use any data for advertising, and keeps no logs of visitors.

Retention and deletion

The in-memory copy expires within 5 minutes. Removing the app stops all reads. The copy in a visitor's browser storage is replaced on the next visit and can be cleared with the browser's site data.
