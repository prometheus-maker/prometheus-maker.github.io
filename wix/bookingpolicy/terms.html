1. What you get

Booking Policy: Cancellations is a Wix app with a site widget that writes your Wix Bookings booking policies out in plain words. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows your default booking policy in a card layout in English, with a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan shows every policy with the services that use it, adds timeline, question and compact layouts, writes the rules in English, German, Spanish, French or Portuguese, shows a "last updated" date and your own note, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

You set your booking policies in Wix Bookings and decide where the widget appears. The widget repeats your settings; check that they say what you mean, and that your policy follows the law where you do business. The app does not give legal advice.

4. Accuracy

The text is built from your booking policies as Wix returns them, refreshed about every 5 minutes. A change can take that long to show. Fees are shown as set in your policy; what is charged in the end is decided by Wix Bookings and your payment settings.
