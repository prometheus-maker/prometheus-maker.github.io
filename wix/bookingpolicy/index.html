What it does

Clients read your cancellation rules before they book. On most sites they are typed by hand, and sooner or later the page says something different from what the booking settings charge. Booking Policy writes the rules out from the booking policies you already set up, so page and checkout always agree.

The widget reads your cancellation windows and fees and turns them into short sentences: free cancellation up to 48 hours before, 48 to 24 hours before a 50% fee, less than 24 hours before the full price. It adds when clients can reschedule, how far ahead and how late they can book, whether a card is saved at booking, and the description you wrote for the policy. Change a window or a fee in your settings and the page follows within minutes.

Made for salons, lash studios, spas, therapists and tutors. Put it on a policies page, under your services or in an FAQ.

The free plan shows your default policy in a clean card in your colours and font, with a small credit link. Premium shows every policy with the services that use it, adds a coloured timeline bar and a question layout, writes the rules in English, German, Spanish, French or Portuguese, and removes the credit link.
