Quick answers

How do I add the count?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Soldmark, and click Add to product page. Then publish your site once. You can also add it in the editor: open a product page and add the Sold Count plugin.

Why does a product show no count?

The line appears only once a product has sold at least your minimum (5 by default) in the time span. Change the minimum in the editor: click the line on a product page, then Settings.

What counts as sold?

Units of the product in paid orders, including partly refunded ones. Canceled and unpaid orders are left out. Every variant of a product counts toward the same number.

Does it show who bought?

No. Visitors only see the number. No names, cities or order details are shown anywhere on your site.

How fast does it update?

Each page view counts afresh, so a new paid order shows up the next time the page loads.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The line keeps showing the last 30 days with the default wording, plus the small credit link.
