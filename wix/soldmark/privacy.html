This policy describes what the Soldmark: Social Proof Count app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a small line such as "37 sold in the last 30 days" to your store's product pages. A dashboard page lists your best-selling products and lets you add the line to your product page.

Data the app handles

  • Orders: to count a product, the app's backend asks Wix for your store's paid orders that contain that product and adds up the quantities. Only the resulting numbers (units in the last 7, 30 and 90 days or all time) leave the backend. Buyer names, email addresses, postal addresses, order numbers and prices are never passed on or shown.
  • Dashboard: the dashboard page reads your paid store orders of the last 30 days inside your own Wix dashboard to list product names and unit counts. Only you see it.
  • Line settings: your minimum, wording, icon and colour choices. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the line and its settings panel whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID. This answer is kept in the server's memory for up to ten minutes.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors, keeps no copy of your orders and writes nothing to a database. Each count is worked out when the page asks for it.

Site visitors

The line does not set cookies and does not track anyone. Visitors see a number only, never who bought.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your orders, customers or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
