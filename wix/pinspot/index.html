What it does

A photo says more when people can ask it questions. Pinspot puts small dots on any picture, and each dot opens a card with a title, a short text and a link. Use it for a room where every piece is for sale, a dish and what is in it, the parts of a product, a team photo or a map of your venue.

It looks finished the moment you add it: a sample living room with four pulsing dots and white cards. Swap in your own picture and the dots stay where you put them on every screen size.

Placing a dot is a click. Open Settings, click the spot on the picture in the panel or drag a dot, then type its title and text and add a link to a page or another site. Pick the dot, card and text colours, the corners and your font. The editor shows each change at once.

On a computer the card opens beside its dot. On a phone it slides up over the bottom of the picture, so nothing is cut off. Dots are real buttons that work with a keyboard and screen readers, and the card text is in your page for search engines.

The free plan gives you 5 hotspots per picture with no branding. Premium adds up to 40, plus-sign and numbered markers, cards that open on hover, titles next to the dots and a photo in each card.
