What it does

Put your store products on a photo. Each pin on the picture opens a card with the product's photo, name and current price, and a button to its product page. When a product is on sale, the old price shows struck through. When it sells out, the card says so. You never retype a price: the cards read your store on every page load.

It looks finished the moment you add it, with a sample living room and three priced pins. Open Settings, choose your own lookbook photo, click where a pin should go, and pick the product from a search box that lists your store. Drag a pin to move it. The editor shows each change at once.

No store on the site? A pin can also hold your own title, price, text and a link, so it also works for a menu photo or a venue map.

On a computer the card opens beside its pin. On a phone it slides up over the bottom of the picture. Pins are real buttons that work with a keyboard and screen readers.

The free plan gives you one picture with up to 4 pins and a small credit link under it. Premium adds more pictures that visitors flip through with arrows, up to 40 pins on each, sale badges, plus and number pins, cards that open on hover, and removes the credit link.
