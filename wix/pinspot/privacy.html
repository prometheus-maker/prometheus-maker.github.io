This policy describes what the Shop the Look app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a shoppable image widget to pages of your Wix site. It shows the picture, pins, text, links and colours you enter in its settings panel, and for pins linked to a product of your Wix Stores store, that product's name, price, sale price, main image, product page link and stock state.

Data the app handles

  • Widget settings: the picture links, their descriptions, each pin's position, linked product ID, title, price text, text, link and photo link, colours, font and pin options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Pictures: the widget loads your picture from your site's Media (or the link you entered). The sample picture comes from the app's own files on Wix.
  • Store products: the widget and its settings panel ask the app's backend on Wix for the products linked to pins (or, in the settings panel, for products matching a search). The backend reads them from your store with the app's read-only product permission and returns only what your product pages already show visitors: name, price, sale price, main image, page link and whether it is in stock. Hidden products are never returned. Nothing is stored. The visitor's browser may keep the last product details it saw in local storage so prices appear at once on the next visit.
  • Plan status: the widget and its settings panel ask the app's backend on Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID. It stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro box on the app's dashboard page, kept in your browser's local storage.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It only shows your picture and its cards. The free plan shows a small "Made with Shop the Look" link to the app's page on the Wix App Market; it is a plain link and tracks nothing.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix, except that a picture link you paste from another website is loaded from that website by the visitor's browser. The developer never receives your settings or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
