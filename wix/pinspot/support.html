Support: Shop the Look

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the picture and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds a sample picture with three pins to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another one, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Shop the Look. Each widget has its own pictures and pins.

How do I link a pin to a product?

Click the widget, then Settings. Pick the pin, press Choose a product and pick it from the list (type to search your store). The card then shows the product's photo, name and price, and a View product button that opens its product page. Products need Wix Stores on the site; hidden products are not listed.

Do prices update by themselves?

Yes. The widget reads the product from your store on every page load, so a new price, a sale price or a sold-out product shows without touching the widget.

How do I use my own picture?

Click the widget, then Settings, then Choose from Media. If the media picker does not open, upload the picture to your site's Media, open its menu (three dots), choose Copy URL and paste the link into Picture link.

How do I place a pin?

Pick the pin in the list, then click the spot on the picture at the top of the panel. You can also drag any pin. Positions are stored as a share of the picture's width and height, so pins stay on the same spot on phones and large screens.

Can I use it without a store?

Yes. Leave the product empty and type your own title, price, text and link for the pin.

Why only 4 pins and one picture?

That is the free plan. Premium allows several pictures (visitors flip through them with arrows) and up to 40 pins on each.

What is the small link under the picture?

On the free plan a small "Made with Shop the Look" link shows under the widget. Premium removes it.

What do visitors see on a phone?

The pins shrink a little and the card slides up over the bottom of the picture, so it is never cut off at the edge of the screen.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working with the first picture and its first 4 pins; extra pictures, sale badges, plus and number pins, hover cards and labels switch off and the credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget shows your picture, text and the products you linked, and collects nothing from visitors.
