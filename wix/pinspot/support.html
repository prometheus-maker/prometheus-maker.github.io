Support: Pinspot

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the picture and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds a sample picture with four hotspots to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another one, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Pinspot. Each widget has its own picture and hotspots.

How do I use my own picture?

Click the widget, then Settings, then Choose from Media. If the media picker does not open, upload the picture to your site's Media, open its menu (three dots), choose Copy URL and paste the link into Picture link. Any image link that starts with https:// works.

How do I place a hotspot?

Pick the hotspot in the list, then click the spot on the picture at the top of the panel. You can also drag any dot. Positions are stored as a share of the picture's width and height, so the dots stay on the same spot on phones and large screens.

How do I add a link to a hotspot?

Use Add link to pick a page of your site, or paste any web address. Links to other sites open in a new tab.

Why only 5 hotspots?

The free plan shows up to 5 per picture. Premium allows 40.

What do visitors see on a phone?

The dots shrink a little and the card slides up over the bottom of the picture, so it is never cut off at the edge of the screen.

Can it use my site's font?

Yes, that is the default. Use Change font in Settings to pick a different one, and "Use site font" to go back.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working. The first 5 hotspots show as dots that open on click; numbered and plus markers, hover cards, titles next to dots and card photos switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget shows your picture and text and collects nothing from visitors.
