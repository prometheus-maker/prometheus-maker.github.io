Common questions

The invoice list is empty

Press Refresh. The app lists published invoices of your site's invoice system at the moment you open it. Drafts do not appear.

A contact has no email address

The app can only send to a contact that has an email address on file. Add the address to the contact first, then press Refresh.

The send is blocked on my test site

On a test site the app only sends to reserved test addresses (example.com, example.invalid and similar), so nobody real receives test invoices. The same send works on a live site.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
