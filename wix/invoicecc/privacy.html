Effective date: 2026-11-26

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Invoice CC: Send to Many app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you list your invoices and contacts and send one invoice to several contacts.

Data the app handles

  • Invoices: the app reads your site's invoices (number, title, total, currency, status, customer name) with the app's own identity when you open the page or press Refresh. It keeps no copy.
  • Contacts: the app reads your site's contacts (name and email) for the picker. It keeps no copy.
  • Sends: when you send, the app asks the platform to email the invoice to the contacts you ticked. The app keeps a log of the sends in the page for the current session only, and drops it when the page closes.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no email of its own and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.
