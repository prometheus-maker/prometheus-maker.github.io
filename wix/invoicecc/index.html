What it does

An invoice has one customer on it. When several people should receive it — a couple splitting a bill, two contacts at the same company, a bookkeeper next to the client — the usual workaround is to download the PDF and email it by hand, one address at a time.

This app puts the send on one screen. It lists the invoices on your site and the contacts in your contact list, newest first. Pick an invoice, tick the people who should receive it, add a short note if you like, and press Send. Every ticked contact gets the invoice email with its payment link, and a send log shows what went out in this session.

A search box narrows the contact list by name or email, so a long list is quick to work through. The free plan sends to up to 3 contacts per invoice with the standard invoice email. Premium lifts the cap to 50 contacts and adds your own message above the invoice.

Premium costs $6 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly ($48 a year), with a 14-day free trial.
