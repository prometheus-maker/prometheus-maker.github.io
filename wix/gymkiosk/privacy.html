This policy describes what the Gym Kiosk: Self Check-In app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app turns a page of your site into a self check-in screen for a tablet at your door. A member types their email or phone number (or, on the paid plan, scans a membership pass) and sees their first name and plan status. Each check-in is written to a log on your own site, which you see in your dashboard.

Data the app handles

  • Contacts and members: to find the person who checks in, the app's backend searches your site's contacts for that exact email or phone number and reads the matching member. It never lists or returns other people.
  • Plan orders: the backend reads that member's Pricing Plans orders (plan name, status, start, end and renewal dates, payment status) to decide what the screen shows.
  • Bookings (paid plan): the backend reads that member's bookings for today, marks the next one attended, and reads plan balances (sessions left).
  • Check-in log: each check-in (time, full name, plan, result, class, contact ID) is stored in a data collection on your own site. Only you and the app can read it; you can delete rows in your CMS at any time.
  • Kiosk settings: the kiosk code you make in the dashboard, your site's time zone and a cached copy of your plan status are stored in a second data collection on your site.
  • On the tablet: the kiosk code is kept in that browser's local storage so the screen stays unlocked.

Site visitors

The check-in screen shows only the first name and plan status of the person who just checked in, and clears itself after a few seconds. It sets no cookies and does not track anyone.

Where the data goes

Everything stays inside your Wix site. The developer never receives your members, contacts, orders or log, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
