Support: Gym Kiosk: Self Check-In

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, what you typed on the tablet and what the screen showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I set up the tablet?

In the editor, add a page (for example "Check-in"), hide it from the menu and move the Gym Kiosk widget onto it, then publish. In your dashboard open Apps, then Gym Kiosk, and click Make a kiosk code. Open the check-in page on the tablet, tap "Staff: set up this tablet" and type the code.

A member says "We couldn't find you".

The app looks for a contact whose email or phone number matches exactly what was typed. Check the spelling, or try the other one. People who never bought a plan or signed up as members show "No plan".

Why does it say "Please see the front desk"?

The member's plan is paused, ended, not started yet or not paid. The line under the name says which.

What does the free plan's daily limit count?

Each check-in that found a person counts. After 30 in one day (your site's time zone) the screen asks people to see the front desk until midnight.

Which tablets can scan passes?

On the paid plan, "Scan my pass" appears in browsers that can read QR codes from the camera (Chrome on Android tablets). A USB or Bluetooth QR scanner works on any tablet: it types the pass into the box.

How do I lock a lost tablet out?

Click Make a new code in the dashboard. Every tablet with the old code asks for the new one.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

Check-ins go back to 30 a day, pass scanning and class attendance stop, and the small credit link comes back.
