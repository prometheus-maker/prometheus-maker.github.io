What it does

A member walks in at 6:40, types her email on the tablet by the door, and the screen says "Welcome, Maya: Monthly Unlimited, renews 7 Dec". If her plan ended last week, it says that in red and asks her to see the front desk. Nobody at the desk had to look anything up.

Gym Kiosk reads the plan from your Pricing Plans orders at the moment of check-in, so paused, unpaid and ended plans show up at the door instead of at the end of the month. It shows the first name only and clears the screen after a few seconds, so the next person never sees who came before.

The tablet stays locked until you type the kiosk code from your dashboard, and it only answers for the one person who matches exactly. Every check-in lands in a log you can filter by day.

The free plan takes 30 check-ins a day and shows a small credit link. Premium removes the limit and the link, scans the QR pass from our Membership Card app, marks the class the member booked today as attended in Bookings, shows sessions left on class packs, and downloads the log as CSV.

If your staff check people in by hand at the desk, our Roll Call app does the same plan check from the dashboard.
