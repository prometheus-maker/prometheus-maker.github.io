3. Plans and payment

The free plan accepts 30 check-ins a day and shows a small credit link. The paid plan removes the daily limit and the credit link, scans Membership Card QR passes, marks the member's booked class for today attended, shows sessions left and downloads the log as CSV. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the app keeps working with the free plan's limits.
