What it does

"Do you deliver to 10001?" is a question delivery businesses answer by phone and DM every day. This app puts the answer on your page before anyone orders.

Visitors type their zip or postal code and click Check. They see "Yes, we deliver to 10001" with your delivery fee, "Pickup only" when you don't drive there, or "Sorry, we don't deliver to 10001 yet".

Restaurants with online ordering type nothing: the answer comes live from the delivery areas already set up there, radius or zip code, with fee, minimum order and delivery time. Florists, bakeries, cleaners and everyone else type their own zones: single codes, ranges like 10001-10050, wildcards like 100*, or UK districts like SW1A.

It fits a header bar, a hero or its own page, takes your colours and font, and works on phones. The last code a visitor checked stays filled in while they browse.

The free plan answers yes or no with the fee for one zone, with a small credit link. Premium gives you up to 8 zones with their own fee, minimum, free delivery threshold and time, an order button, your own wording in 7 languages, and an email box for visitors outside your area. They land in your contacts, labelled by zip code.
