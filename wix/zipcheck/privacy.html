Effective date: 2026-11-11

This policy describes what the Zip Code Check: Delivery Area app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to your Wix site where visitors type a zip or postal code and see whether you deliver there.

Data the app handles

  • Postal codes typed by visitors: when your site uses restaurant online ordering, the code (and your site's country) is sent once to Wix's own ordering service, which answers which of your delivery and pickup options serve that code. When you typed your own zones, the code is compared in the visitor's browser and is not sent anywhere. The app does not store postal codes, except as described under "Email requests".
  • Your restaurant setup: the app's backend on Wix reads your fulfillment methods (delivery areas, fees, minimum order, delivery time, pickup) to answer visitors. It does not read orders, customers or payments.
  • Email requests (paid plan, only if you turn the email box on): a visitor outside your area can type their email address and ask to be told when you deliver there. The app then creates a contact in your site's Contacts with that email and the postal code, labelled "Wants delivery" and "Wants delivery: <code>". If the contact already exists, only the labels are added. You, as the site owner, are the controller of this data.
  • Widget settings: zones, texts, colours and font. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last code they checked for the current visit (session storage), so it stays filled in while they browse your pages.

Site visitors

The widget sets no cookies and does not track visitors. The email box says what the address is used for. Visitors who want their details removed can ask you; you can delete the contact in Contacts.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives postal codes, emails, your settings or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The backend keeps no logs of visitors.

Retention

The app keeps nothing itself. Contacts created from email requests stay in your site until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
