Support: Zip Code Check: Delivery Area

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget, the code you tried and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do I put the widget?

Anywhere: your header, the home page hero, your menu or order page. In the editor click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Zip Code Check. Pick Bar in its Settings for a slim strip that fits a header.

I run a restaurant with online ordering. What do I set up?

Nothing in the widget. It asks your restaurant ordering setup which delivery areas cover the code, radius or zip code areas, and shows the fee (Premium: minimum order, free delivery threshold and delivery time). If your areas are zip code lists, check the Country in the widget's Settings matches yours.

I don't use restaurant ordering. How do I add my area?

Open the widget's Settings and type your codes in Zone 1, separated by commas or new lines. 10001-10050 covers a range, 100* covers every code starting with 100, and SW1A covers a whole UK district. Add your delivery fee (0 = free delivery).

My live site doesn't show the widget.

On a site with no zones and no restaurant delivery areas there is nothing to answer, so visitors see nothing. In the editor you see a sample with a note until you add your codes.

Can I use it for a service area instead of delivery?

Yes. Set Wording to Service area: the answers then read "Yes, we serve 10001".

Where do the emails from the email box go?

To your site's Contacts (Premium). Each person gets the labels "Wants delivery" and "Wants delivery: 10001", so you can filter by label and see which areas ask most.

What happens if my paid plan ends?

The widget keeps working on the free plan: the first zone, yes or no with the fee, and the small credit link comes back. Your other settings are kept for when you upgrade again.
