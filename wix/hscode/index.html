What it does

Cross-border parcels need an HS code, a country of origin and a net weight on the customs form. Most shops keep those in a spreadsheet, and every order turns into a copy-paste job.

Open your dashboard, tick a product and save its HS code (6 to 10 digits, written 851762.00), the country of origin, the net weight of one unit and the customs description. Each product shows Ready or Needs data, so gaps show early.

Then pick an order and the app builds the customs invoice: seller and buyer blocks, invoice number and date, one row per line with description, HS code, origin, quantity, unit value, net weight and amount, then totals, currency, reason for export, incoterm, destination and a signature line. Download it as a PDF or open a print view.

A line whose product has no customs data still prints, with a blank HS code and a warning.

The free plan covers 25 products with customs data and 5 invoices per calendar month. Premium removes both limits and adds country rules, proforma invoices and bulk fill by category: $6 per month, or $5 per month billed yearly.

Nothing is written back to the store: the app reads your products and orders and saves its customs data inside your site.
