Support: HS Code: Customs Invoice

The HS Code: Customs Invoice app keeps the customs data of your Wix shop's products in one dashboard page and builds a print-ready commercial invoice from any of your orders.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "HS Code: Customs Invoice" in your Wix dashboard and go to Products & customs data. Tick a product and fill in its HS code, country of origin, net weight and customs description, then press Save customs data. The badge changes from Needs data to Ready when every field is valid.

Open the Invoices tab, pick an order and check the preview. Download PDF saves the invoice; Print view opens it in its own window for your printer. The Invoice settings tab holds the seller block that prints at the top, the reason for export and the incoterm.

Questions people ask

  • Where does the invoice get the buyer address? From the order's shipping address.
  • What if a product has no customs data? Its line still prints, with a blank HS code and a warning, so you see what to fix.
  • Does the app change my products? No. It only reads them and keeps its own customs data in the app's collections.
  • How many do I get for free? 25 products with customs data and 5 invoices in each calendar month, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? Unlimited products and invoices, country rules, proforma invoices and bulk fill by category.
  • Where is the data? In the app's collections on your Wix site; removing the app removes it.
