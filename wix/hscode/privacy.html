Privacy Policy: HS Code: Customs Invoice

Effective date: 2026-11-20

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the HS Code: Customs Invoice app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard where you save customs data (HS code, country of origin, net weight, customs description) for the products in your store, and where you build a customs invoice for one of your orders as a PDF or a print view.

Data the app handles

  • Products and orders: the app reads your store's products and orders through the Wix APIs, with your permission, at the moment the page needs them. It keeps no copy of them.
  • Customs data: the HS code, origin, weight and description you save for a product are stored in the app's data collections on your Wix site.
  • Invoice settings: the seller block, reason for export, incoterm, template and country rules you save are stored in the app's collections on your site.
  • Usage counter: the app keeps a count of invoices produced per calendar month, so the free plan's limit can be applied.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail, builds the invoice in your own browser, shares no data with third parties and writes nothing back to your store.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your Wix site until you delete it in the dashboard or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
