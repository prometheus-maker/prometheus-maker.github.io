These terms cover the HS Code: Customs Invoice app ("the app") for Wix sites.

The service

The app gives a shop a dashboard page where customs data is saved per product and a customs invoice is produced for an order as a PDF or a print view. It stores the customs data on your Wix site; the invoice is built in your browser.

Plans

The free plan covers 25 products with customs data and 5 invoices in a calendar month. Premium removes both limits and adds country rules, proforma invoices and bulk fill by category. Plans are billed by Wix through the App Market; the price shown on the app's page at the time of purchase applies. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the free plan's limit applies again from then.

Your part

You use the app only on sites you are allowed to use it on, and you check the customs data and every invoice before you ship. The app does not classify goods for you, does not decide a tariff and does not file anything with a customs authority. A wrong HS code, origin or weight is your responsibility to correct.
