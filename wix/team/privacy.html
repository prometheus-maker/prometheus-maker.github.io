This policy describes what the Team app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a section to pages of your Wix site that shows your Bookings staff as cards: photo, name, description, the services each person gives and a link to book them.

Data the app handles

  • Staff and services: the app's backend on Wix reads your Bookings staff members (name, description, photo) and your services (name, booking page link, which staff give them). This is the same public information your booking pages already show. The app never reads or shows staff e-mail addresses or phone numbers.
  • Widget settings: your heading, button text, colours, font, layout and display options, and on Premium the people you hide and the order you set. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last staff list it received from the backend, so the cards show at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. Photos load from the Wix image servers, like any other image on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your staff list, your settings or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
