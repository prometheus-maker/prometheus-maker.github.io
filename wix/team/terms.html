1. What you get

Team is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your Wix Bookings staff as cards with a link to book each person. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows up to four people in a grid, with your heading, button text, colours and font, and a small credit link under the widget. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
