What it does

Team shows the people who work at your business as cards on any page: photo, name, a short description, the services each person gives, and a button that says "Book with Hannah". The people come straight from your Bookings staff, so there is nothing to type twice. Add someone in Bookings or change a photo there, and the cards follow.

The old built-in staff members widget was retired and has no replacement. Team fills that gap: the cards are real headings, links and lists that screen readers can follow, the photos have alt text, and every button can be reached by keyboard.

A new widget looks finished the moment it is added, with no setup. People without a photo get a soft tile with their initials instead of a broken image. On phones the cards turn into compact rows. You can change the heading, button text, photo shape (portrait, square or circle), colours and font, or drop the card background.

The free plan shows up to four people in a grid, with a small credit link under it. Premium shows your whole team, adds list and carousel layouts, can show only the people who give one service, lets you hide people or set your own order, and removes the credit link.
