Support: Team

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the team section and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the team section?

Installing the app adds it to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Team.

Where do the people come from?

From your Bookings staff (dashboard: Bookings, then Staff). Add a photo and a description to each person there; the widget shows them and follows every change. Staff without a photo get a tile with their initials.

Why does someone have no "Book with" button?

The button links to the booking page of a service the person gives. Assign them to an appointment service in Bookings and the button appears. Staff of classes and courses show once they have recurring sessions.

The widget shows a sample team

That only happens inside the editor while your site has no Bookings staff. Visitors never see sample people.

Can I hide the site owner or change the order?

On Premium: click the widget, then Settings, then "Show, hide and order". On the free plan the order is the order you added people in Bookings.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The grid keeps working with the first four people and the credit link comes back. List and carousel layouts, the service filter, hidden people and your own order switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget shows the staff your booking pages already show and never tracks visitors.
