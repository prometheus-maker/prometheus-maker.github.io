What it does

EU shops that advertise a reduction have to show the lowest price of the previous 30 days next to it. That number is easy to promise and hard to produce: your site only knows today's price, and the older ones are gone.

This app keeps its own daily price log for every product and adds the line to your product page: "Lowest price in the last 30 days: €X". Your dashboard lists each product with its current price, that 30-day low and the day it was seen, and a button to copy the line.

The log fills from real traffic. A visit to a product page records that day's price once, and opening the dashboard records every product. Nothing runs on a schedule, so a quiet shop collects less: the dashboard shows how many days are covered.

A sale that started before you installed the app has no history. Type the lowest price you actually charged into the product's field; the app uses it when it is lower than anything recorded.

Premium lifts the 10-product limit, keeps the full log in a table you can export as CSV, and adds a sale badge and the same line on category pages.

Prices are the ones your store shows; the line reports them, it is not tax advice. Check the wording with your accountant.
