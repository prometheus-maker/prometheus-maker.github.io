Common questions

  • The line does not appear on a product page: open the app in your dashboard, check that the product is in the list, then add the element to the page from the app's settings panel. A product with no recorded price shows no line; open the dashboard once to record today's price for every product.
  • The history is short: samples are written by real visits and by dashboard opens. A new install has no history yet; a manual lowest price covers a sale that started earlier.
  • The lowest price looks wrong: the line uses the smallest price recorded in the last 30 days. A manual entry can only make it lower, never higher.
  • Upgrade or cancel: plans are managed in your account's app subscriptions page.
  • Remove the app: remove it from your site; its collections and their data go with it.
