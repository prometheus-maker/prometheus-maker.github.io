This policy describes what the 30-Day Lowest Price: Omnibus app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard and a line on your product pages that shows each product's lowest price over the last 30 days.

Data the app handles

  • Price samples: once a day, for each product, the app stores the product id, the day and the price. A sample is written when a shopper views the product and when you open the dashboard. The samples are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Settings: the manual lowest price you type for a product, and whether the line is hidden on a page, are stored in the app's settings collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the dashboard asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app stores no shopper identity, no e-mail address and no order data. It keeps no visitor logs and shares no data with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
