Support: Raisemeter

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the meter and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the meter?

Installing the app adds a sample meter to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Raisemeter.

How do I set my goal?

Click the meter in the editor, then Settings. Type the goal and the amount raised so far, pick your currency, and publish.

Does it count donations by itself?

No. It shows the total you type, so it works for money from anywhere: PayPal, a crowdfunding page, cash, cheques or pledges. When new money comes in, change "Raised so far" in Settings and publish again.

How do I add a Donate button?

In Settings, under Donate button, paste the link to your donation page (PayPal, GoFundMe, your own form). The button shows once it has a link.

Can I count something other than money?

Yes. Pick "No currency (count items)" and type what you count, for example meals, books or volunteer hours.

My numbers disappear when I publish

They shouldn't, on any plan. Click Publish in the editor after your changes and reload the live page. If they still don't show, email the site address and we'll look.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The meter keeps working. The circle style changes to a progress bar, and milestones, the countdown, the donor count and confetti are hidden.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the meter shows your numbers and collects nothing.
