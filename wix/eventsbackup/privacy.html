Privacy Policy: Events Backup: Guests & CSV

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Events Backup: Guests & CSV app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you copy your events and their guests into a dated backup, download the backup as a ZIP of two CSV files, and see what changed since the previous backup.

Data the app handles

  • Events: the title, dates, time zone, status, registration type and status, location, event page, questions, guest counts and check-in counts of every event the backup covers are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Guests: the name, e-mail, phone, guest type, attendance, RSVP answer, order number and status, tickets and ticket types, check-in time, contact id and the guest's own form answers are stored in the app's data collections on your site, as part of the backup you take.
  • Settings: the plan status of the site is asked of the app's backend each time the page opens.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app reads events and guests through the platform's own APIs, using the signed-in owner's permissions. It sends nothing to any other service, keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Backups stay on your site until you delete them in the dashboard or remove the app. On the free plan the app keeps the 2 most recent backups; on Premium it keeps 12 months and deletes older ones. Removing the app removes its collections and everything in them.

Your responsibility for guest data

A backup contains personal data of your guests. You decide what you download and where you keep the ZIP; keep it as carefully as you keep the guest list itself.
