What the app does

The app reads your site's events and their guests, stores a dated backup of them on your site and lets you download the backup as a ZIP of two CSV files. It also lists what changed between two backups.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to your data. The app stores the backups on your site and sends nothing elsewhere. You are responsible for what you download and for handling guest data as the law requires of you.

Plans

The free plan covers every upcoming event and your 10 most recent past events, with all their guests, and keeps the 2 most recent backups. Premium covers every event ever and every guest, keeps 12 months of backups and takes a weekly automatic backup the first time the page is opened after 7 days. Premium is billed by the platform at $6 a month, or $5 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.

The app is provided as it is. It reads your events when you press Take backup or when a Premium site opens the page after 7 days; it does not run in the background otherwise. The developer is not liable for losses that follow from a missing or incomplete backup.
