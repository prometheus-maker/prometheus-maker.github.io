Common questions

The backup misses older events

The free plan keeps every upcoming event and your 10 most recent past events. The page names how many older events and guests are left out; Premium covers every event ever and every guest.

A guest that cancelled is gone from the list

A backup copies the guest list as it is when you press Take backup. A guest removed from the event before a backup was taken is not in that backup; earlier backups keep what they copied, and the change list shows what disappeared.

How do I open the ZIP

The ZIP holds two plain CSV files, events.csv and guests.csv, which any spreadsheet program opens. Download it from the app page; it is not e-mailed.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.

When does the weekly backup run

On Premium, the first time you open the app page after 7 days have passed since the last backup, the app takes a new one. The app has no outside server, so it cannot run while nobody opens the page.
