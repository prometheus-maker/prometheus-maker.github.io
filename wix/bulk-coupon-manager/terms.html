1. What you get

Coupon Forge is a Wix app that creates, lists, exports and deletes coupon codes on your Wix site through Wix's Coupons API, and can email one code to each of your site's contacts through Wix's email service. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan allows sets of up to 200 codes and 10 code emails a month. The paid plan allows sets of up to 10,000 codes and emails to all your contacts, within your site's own email limit. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.
