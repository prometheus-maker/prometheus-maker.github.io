This policy describes what the License Keys: Auto Delivery app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you keep a pool of license keys per product. When an order is paid, the app assigns an unused key to each unit bought and shows it to the buyer on the Thank You page.

Data the app handles

  • License keys: the keys you paste, the product id they belong to, any expiry date you set, and once delivered, the order id, the order number and the buyer's e-mail address. They are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Orders: the app reads the paid order that triggers a delivery, so it can match the product and the number of units and place the key. It stores only the order id, number and buyer e-mail next to the key.
  • Plan status: the app asks the site whether it is on the free or the paid plan, and that one fact decides the monthly cap and the Premium tools.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app sends no e-mail, keeps no visitor logs and shares no data with third parties. A buyer sees a key only on the Thank You page of their own order, for about two hours after the order; after that the app shows nothing there.

Keeping and removing data

Keys and their delivery records stay on your site until you delete them or remove the app. Removing the app deletes its collections and everything in them.
