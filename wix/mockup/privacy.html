This policy describes what the Mockup Studio: Product Shots app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your dashboard. There you pick one of your store products, a scene and a background colour, and the app draws the product photo into the scene on a canvas and lets you download the result as an image.

Data the app handles

  • Product data: the app reads your products from your store (name and photo address) so you can pick one. It reads nothing else about them and keeps no copy.
  • Product photos: your photo is fetched by the browser from the address your store already serves it from, drawn on the canvas and never sent to the app's servers.
  • Usage counter: the number of exports in the current month and the month itself are stored in the app's data collection on your site, so the free limit can be counted.
  • Plan status: the app asks whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and gets that one fact back.
  • Account: the app runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

Generated images are never uploaded or stored by the app; they are created in the browser and saved by you. The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

The usage counter stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collection and everything in it. Images you downloaded stay wherever you saved them.
