Support: Mockup Studio: Product Shots

The Mockup Studio: Product Shots app turns your product photos into scene shots (framed print, canvas, t-shirt, mug, tote bag, poster, phone case, sticker) inside your dashboard, and lets you download them as images.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Mockup Studio: Product Shots" in your dashboard. Choose a product under "1. Choose a product", pick a scene under "2. Pick a scene", choose a background colour and an output size, and press "Download image". The picture appears above the button as soon as it is drawn.

Premium sites also see "Export all products (ZIP)", which renders every product in your store in the chosen scene and downloads the set as one ZIP.

Questions people ask

  • Where do my photos go? Nowhere. The app reads the photo from your store and draws it in the browser; it is never uploaded and never stored.
  • How many exports do I get? 50 in each calendar month on the free plan; Premium has no monthly limit.
  • What is different on Premium? All 8 scenes, a custom background colour, output up to 2000 px, and batch export of your whole catalogue.
  • Which image size should I use? 2000 px for product pages and ads, 1000 px is fine for small thumbnails.
  • The product image does not load. Check that the product has a photo in your store, then pick it again.
  • Can I use the images anywhere? Yes, they are your product photos in our scenes; you can use the result as you like.
  • Where is my data? Only a monthly export counter is stored, in the app's collection on your site; removing the app removes it.
