What it does

Your product photos are on a plain white background, and a plain white background sells nothing. This app puts the same photo into a room: a print in an oak frame on a wall, a canvas, a tee, a mug, a tote, a phone case.

Pick a product from your store, pick a scene, pick a background colour and an output size, and the picture is drawn in your dashboard. Download it as a PNG or a JPG and use it on your product page, your social posts or your ads.

The image is never uploaded and never stored. The app reads your product photo straight from your store and draws it in the browser, so nothing leaves your site.

Free gives you 3 scenes (framed print, canvas, t-shirt), preset background colours, 1000 px output and 50 exports a month. Premium opens all 8 scenes, a custom background colour, 2000 px output and batch export: every product in your store in one ZIP, each one in the scene you picked. Premium is $6 per month, or $5 per month billed yearly.

The scenes are drawn from scratch by the app, so no stock photos and no licences are involved. Your product image stays yours.
