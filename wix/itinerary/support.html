Support: Itinerary: Tour Day Plan

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the service you tested and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I add the plan to my service pages?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Itinerary, and click Add to service page. Then publish your site once. You can also add it in the editor: open the Bookings service page and add the Day by Day Plan plugin.

Where do I write a plan?

In the same dashboard page: click a service, add days, and click Save. Saved plans show on the live page right away; you don't need to publish again.

Why does a service show nothing?

A service without a saved plan shows nothing, on purpose. Write its plan in the dashboard.

Can I change the colours or the heading?

Yes. In the editor, click the plan, then Settings. By default it uses your page's own font and text colour.

How does the print button work?

On the paid plan, visitors can click Print itinerary to open a clean printable page; the browser's print window can also save it as a PDF.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

Your plans stay saved. Service pages show the first 3 days of each plan with text and photos, without the lists, map links and print button, and the small credit link comes back.
