This policy describes what the Itinerary: Tour Day Plan app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app shows a day-by-day plan on each Wix Bookings service page. You write the plans in a dashboard page of your site.

Data the app handles

  • Bookings services: the dashboard and the backend read your services' names, IDs and page addresses so you can pick a service and so the page shows the right plan. Bookings, customers and payments are never read.
  • Your plans: the titles, text, photo links, stops and lists you write are saved in a collection inside your own site (Wix Data). They are shown to anyone who opens the service page, like the rest of the page.
  • Plan status: the backend saves whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, with a signature, in the same collection, so pages load without asking Wix each time. It is refreshed every few hours.
  • Plugin settings: heading, layout and colours. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors and stores nothing outside your site.

Site visitors

The plan does not set cookies and does not track anyone. Map links open Google Maps in a new tab only when a visitor clicks one.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. The developer never receives your plans, customers or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
