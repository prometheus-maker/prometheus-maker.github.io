Support: Blog Feed

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the feed address and what the feed reader showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is my feed address?

Open the Blog Feed page in your site's dashboard (Apps > Blog Feed). The address is at the top, with a Copy button.

Why not the blog's own feed?

The built-in feed carries a one-line summary of each post and the 20 newest posts only. Blog Feed carries the whole post with its images and links, and on Premium up to 100 posts and a feed per category or tag.

How fast do new posts show up?

Within about five minutes of publishing. Feed readers and newsletter tools check feeds on their own schedule, often every 15 to 60 minutes.

Do drafts or private posts show?

No. Only published posts are in the feed.

The feed says it needs Premium

Category, tag, Atom, JSON, excerpt and UTM feeds are on the paid plan. The main feed of the latest 20 posts is free.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The main feed keeps working with the latest 20 posts. Category, tag, Atom and JSON addresses stop answering.

What happens if I remove the app?

The feed address stops working.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the feed holds what your published posts already show; the app stores nothing.
