This policy describes what the Blog Feed app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app publishes RSS, Atom and JSON feeds of the published posts on your Wix site's blog, at a feed address shown in the app's dashboard page.

Data the app handles

  • Published blog posts: when a feed reader or newsletter tool opens your feed address, the app's backend on Wix reads your published posts, categories and tags from the Wix Blog service and turns them into a feed. Drafts are never read. The feed contains what your published posts already show to every visitor: titles, text, images, links, dates, categories and tags.
  • Site name and address: used as the feed's title and to build absolute links to your posts.
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, to decide which feeds and options to serve.
  • Feed address: it holds your app instance ID and a signature, so the backend knows which site's blog to read. Anyone with the address can read the feed, the same way anyone can read your published blog.
  • Browser storage: the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The backend keeps a copy of each feed in memory for about five minutes so readers that poll often do not slow down your blog. It stores nothing on disk and keeps no logs of who reads the feed.

Site visitors and feed readers

The app adds nothing to your site's pages, sets no cookies and does not track visitors. Feed readers that fetch your feed send the usual web request (address, user agent); the app does not record it.

Where the data goes

Your feed goes to whoever opens the feed address, which is the point of a feed. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
