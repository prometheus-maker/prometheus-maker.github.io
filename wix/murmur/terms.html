1. What you get

Murmur is a Wix app with a site widget that lists your blog's newest reader comments, each linking to its post. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows your 3 newest comments in a list, with your title, colours and font. The paid plan adds up to 10 comments, a category filter, a word filter, a choice of comment length, and speech bubble and compact styles. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
