Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the strip and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the strip?

Installing the app adds the strip to the page you were on, in its own section. To put it somewhere else, like next to your blog posts, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Murmur.

How fast do new comments show up?

Within about 5 minutes. A visitor who comes back first sees the strip from their last visit, then the fresh one.

Which comments are shown?

Published comments on your blog posts, newest first. Comments you hide or delete, and comments waiting for approval, are not shown. Your own replies are left out unless you switch "Leave out my own replies" off.

Why does a comment say "A reader"?

The writer is not a site member, or their member profile is private. Murmur shows a first name only from a public profile.

Can I keep spam or certain words out?

On Premium, list words in "Hide comments with these words". A comment that contains any of them stays on the post but is left out of the strip.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The strip keeps working with your 3 newest comments. More comments, the category and word filters, the length setting and the bubble and compact styles switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the strip shows a short excerpt, a first name, the time and the post title, and does not track visitors.
