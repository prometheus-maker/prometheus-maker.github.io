This policy describes what the Murmur app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a strip of your blog's newest reader comments to pages of your Wix site. Each comment links to the blog post it was written on.

Data the app handles

  • Blog comments: the app's backend on Wix reads the newest published comments on your blog posts. It sends visitors only a short excerpt of each comment, the date, and the writer's first name (see below). Hidden, deleted or pending comments are never sent.
  • Commenters' names: for comments written by site members, the backend reads the member's public profile and shows only the first word of the public nickname. Members with a private profile and visitors who are not members show as "A reader". Emails, photos, last names and member IDs are never sent to visitors. You can switch names off.
  • Blog posts: the backend reads the title and link of the posts the comments belong to.
  • The comments, post titles and first names are kept in the backend's memory for up to 5 minutes so your blog is not asked again on every page view; they are never written to storage.
  • Blog categories: the settings panel lists your categories by name so you can pick one. This list is only answered for the site's owners and collaborators, not for visitors.
  • Widget settings: your title, the number of comments, category, word filter, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last strip it saw so the widget shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.
  • The site assistant: if you ask the Wix site assistant about your blog comments, the app answers it with counts, excerpts, first names and post titles from the same data.

The app keeps no database and no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies and does not track visitors. Comments are already public on your blog posts; the strip shows less about each one than the post itself does.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your comments, your members' data or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
