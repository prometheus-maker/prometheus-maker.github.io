1. What you get

Comment Alerts is a Wix app that emails you when someone comments on your site. You install it from the Wix App Market and manage it inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan sends up to 50 alerts per month to one address. The paid plan sends unlimited alerts (within your site's email allowance) to up to 5 addresses and adds the keyword filter. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. Your responsibilities

Only add email addresses of people who agreed to receive these alerts. Use the app only on sites you are allowed to manage and in line with Wix's terms.

4. Your data
