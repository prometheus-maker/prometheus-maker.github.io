Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

This policy describes what the Arrival Guide: Check-In Info app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a guide page to your site for each property you rent, and gives you a private link to send to a guest so they can read their check-in details.

Data the app handles

  • Guides: the property name, address, check-in and check-out times, door code, wifi name and password, map link, parking notes, house rules and an optional phone number that you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Guest links: each guide has a random token, and the guest page reads only the guide that matches its token. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The public site shows only the text you choose to show on the page you place.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a guide removes it at once, and its link stops working. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
