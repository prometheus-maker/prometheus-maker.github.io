Common questions

The guest page says the link is no longer valid

The link works only for a guide that still exists in your dashboard. If you deleted and re-created the guide, copy the new guest link and send that one.

The door code is missing on the guest page

Door code, wifi and the map link are Premium fields. They can be typed on the free plan and are kept, and they appear on the guest page once the site is on Premium.

Where does the page sit?

Place the Arrival Guide element on any page of your site, for example a page called Arrival, and publish. The page address plus the private token is the link you send.
