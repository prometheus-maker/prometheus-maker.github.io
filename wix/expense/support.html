Common questions

The New claim button does nothing

On the free plan you can have 10 claims in a month. When the month's count is used up, the app shows the upgrade card instead; wait for the next month or upgrade to Premium.

A mileage claim has the wrong amount

The amount is kilometres multiplied by the rate per kilometre you enter. Edit the claim and check both numbers. Amounts are stored with the claim and do not change when the rate is edited later.

I approved a claim by mistake

Open the claim and use Reject or Reimburse as needed, with a note saying why. The record keeps the latest decision together with the approver and the date.

Export CSV is greyed out

CSV export is part of Premium. The button stays visible on the free plan so you know what the upgrade adds.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
