This policy describes what the Expense: Staff & Volunteer app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where your team records expense claims, approves or rejects them with a name and a note, and marks them reimbursed.

Data the app handles

  • Claims: the date, the person who paid, the category, the purpose, the amount, and any receipt or ticket number you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Approvals: the approver's name, the decision, the note and the date are stored on your site, together with the date a claim was marked reimbursed.
  • Mileage: the kilometres and the rate per kilometre you enter are stored with the claim; the amount is worked out from them.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page. CSV export is produced in your browser from the claims already on your site.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
