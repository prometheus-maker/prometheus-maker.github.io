Support: Size Chart: Fit Guide

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the table and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do I add the chart to a page?

Open the product page in the Wix editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Size Chart: Fit Guide. It picks the chart from the product in the page address, so you add it once to a product page layout.

What do I do first?

Open Apps > Size Chart: Fit Guide in your site's dashboard, click "New chart from the sample", change the sample's numbers to your own, and save. That chart is the default and shows on every product until you assign others.

How do I give one product its own chart?

In the dashboard, edit a chart and choose the products (or a whole category) it belongs to. The product page shows the chart assigned to that product, or its category, or the default one.

Inches or centimetres?

Type the numbers in the unit you already keep. On Premium, shoppers can switch the table between centimetres and inches with one tap; the app converts the values.

I sell shoes. Can the sizes be UK 6, 6.5 and so on?

Yes. The size column is free text, so UK half sizes, EU numbers or "One size" all work.

How many sizes and columns can a chart have?

Up to 40 sizes and four measurement columns (for example chest, waist, length and sleeve). The free plan shows the first eight sizes of one chart.

Can I paste my measurements instead of typing them?

On Premium, use "Import from a spreadsheet": paste rows from a spreadsheet (size, then the measurements), or load a CSV file.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

Your first chart keeps working with up to eight sizes on every product, and the small credit link comes back. The charts you made are kept in your dashboard.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: your charts stay on your site, and visitors are not tracked.
