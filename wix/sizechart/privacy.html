Privacy Policy: Size Chart: Fit Guide

Effective date: 2026-11-16

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

This policy describes what the Size Chart: Fit Guide app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app shows a measurement table on clothing or shoe product pages of your site. You write the tables in the app's dashboard and choose which products or categories they belong to.

Data the app handles

  • Your charts: the name, the unit (centimetres or inches), the measurement column names, the size rows, the note under the table and the how-to-measure picture you added. They are stored in the app's own data collections on your site.
  • Assignments: the product and category ids a chart is assigned to, and which chart is your default. They are stored with the chart.
  • Store catalogue: to offer product names in the dashboard and to tell which product page the widget is on, the app's backend reads the products in your store (names, ids, slugs and their categories) with the app's own token. It does not store them; the widget cache in the visitor's browser holds no catalogue data.
  • Plan status: the widget and the dashboard ask the app's backend whether the site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact, the app instance id and a signed copy of it kept in the app's settings collection.
  • Browser storage: the dashboard remembers whether you closed its short introduction and its review prompt. The widget stores nothing in the browser.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors and stores nothing about them.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. A visitor's browser asks the app's backend for the chart of the product on the page; the request carries the product id or slug from the page address and the site's own visitor token, which Wix issues, and nothing else.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your charts, your catalogue or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
