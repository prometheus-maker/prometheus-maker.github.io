1. What you get

Size Chart: Fit Guide is a Wix app with a site widget that shows a measurement table on the clothing or shoe product pages of your store, and a dashboard where you write the tables. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one chart of up to eight sizes on every product, with a small credit link under the table. The paid plan adds many charts, a chart for each product or category, the centimetre and inch switch, the how-to-measure picture, spreadsheet import, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
