1. What you get

PDF Viewer: Document Library is a Wix app with a site widget that lists the documents in a folder of your Media Manager and lets visitors read or download them. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one folder as a list, with a reader for PDFs, your heading, colours and font, and a small credit link under the list. The paid plan adds several folders as tabs, a search box, a card layout, sort order, a "New" label, the option to hide the download button, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
