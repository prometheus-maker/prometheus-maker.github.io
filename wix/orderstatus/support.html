Support: Order Status: Pickup Times

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Order Status.

I paused ordering. Do I need to publish again?

No. The status is read when a visitor loads the page, and again every few minutes while the page stays open. A pause or a new closure shows within about a minute.

Which location does it show?

Your main ordering location until you choose. Click the widget in the editor, then Settings, and pick the location. Premium shows up to 6 locations at once.

Where does the Order now button go?

To /online-ordering on your site. If your ordering page has another address, paste it under Button link in Settings. The button hides while ordering is paused or closed.

Which times does it show?

The first pickup time your ordering setup offers, rounded up to the next 5 minutes, in your restaurant's time zone, and the delivery time when one is set.

What happens if the paid plan ends?

The widget keeps working with one location in the card layout. The closures list, extra locations, bar and badge layouts, your own wording and other languages switch off, and the small credit link comes back.
