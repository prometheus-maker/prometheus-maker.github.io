1. What you get

Order Status: Pickup Times is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your restaurant's live online ordering status, read from your Wix Restaurants ordering setup. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows the live status of one location in the card layout, with an Order button and a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan lists upcoming closures, shows up to 6 locations, adds the bar and badge layouts, your own wording and 7 languages, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
