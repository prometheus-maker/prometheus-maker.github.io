This policy describes what the Order Status: Pickup Times app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows whether your restaurant's online ordering is open right now, the first pickup and delivery times, a pause ("paused until 5:00 PM") and your upcoming closures.

Data the app handles

  • Ordering setup: the app's backend on Wix reads your restaurant's own ordering setup: the ordering locations (name and time zone), whether online ordering is on, off or paused and until when, ASAP or pre-order scheduling with the preparation time, the first available pickup and delivery time, and your closures (name, start and end). It does not read orders, customers, menus or prices.
  • Widget settings: the locations you picked, layout, wording, button text and link, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last status it saw for up to five minutes, so it appears at once on the next page.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your ordering data or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
