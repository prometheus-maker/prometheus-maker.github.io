This policy describes what the Contacts & Members Backup app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard where you take dated snapshots of your contact list, your members and your form submissions, compare them with the previous snapshot, download them as a CSV, and on the paid plan put deleted contacts back into your contact list.

Data the app handles

  • Snapshot data: the contact details you already have on your site (names, e-mails, phone numbers, company, job title, labels, created and updated dates), member profiles (login e-mail, status, activity dates, nickname), and form submissions (the answers as text, plus the name and link of any uploaded file). Copies are stored in the app's data collections on your Wix site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy, and uploaded files themselves are never copied.
  • Settings: the automatic snapshot timestamp is stored in the app's settings collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and that one fact decides what the page offers.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app sends no e-mail, keeps no visitor logs and shares no data with third parties. Snapshots are readable only through the app's dashboard page on your site.

Keeping and removing data

Snapshots stay on your Wix site until they are removed by the retention rule (the two most recent on the free plan, twelve months on Premium) or by you. Removing the app deletes its collections and everything in them.
