These terms cover the Contacts & Members Backup app ("the app") for Wix sites.

The service

The app gives you a dashboard page that copies your contacts, members and form submissions into dated snapshots on your own Wix site, shows what changed between snapshots, exports a snapshot as a CSV, and on the paid plan re-creates deleted contacts in your contact list.

Plans

The free plan keeps the two most recent snapshots, with the diff and the CSV download. Premium keeps twelve months of snapshots, takes an automatic snapshot once a week, and adds the restore of deleted contacts. Plans are billed by Wix through the App Market; the price shown on the app's page at the time of purchase applies. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the free plan's limits apply again from then.

Your part

You use the app only on sites you are allowed to use it on. You decide what to restore; the app re-creates the contacts you select and does not merge or overwrite anything by itself.

Limits

A snapshot can hold up to 25,000 records in total, and the app says so when a snapshot is capped. Automatic snapshots run when the dashboard page is opened; Wix apps cannot run on a timer, so a week without opening the page means the automatic copy waits until you next open it. The app is provided as it is. The developer is not liable for lost profits or indirect damages, and the total liability is limited to what you paid for the app in the twelve months before a claim.

Ending it

You can remove the app at any time from your Wix dashboard; the snapshots in its collections are removed with it. Contacts that were restored into your contact list stay there.
