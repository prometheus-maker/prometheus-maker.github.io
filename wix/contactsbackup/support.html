Support: Contacts & Members Backup

The Contacts & Members Backup app keeps dated copies of your Wix contacts, members and form submissions inside your dashboard, so a deletion or a bad cleanup has somewhere to come back from.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Contacts & Members Backup" in your Wix dashboard. Press "Take a snapshot now". The page reads your contacts, members and form submissions, stores the copy in the app's own collections, and lists it under Snapshot history with how many contacts, members and form answers it holds.

Take another snapshot after you change something and the newest row shows what was added, removed and changed since the previous one. The CSV button downloads that snapshot.

Questions people ask

  • Where is the copy stored? In the app's own data collections on your Wix site. Nothing is sent to another service.
  • Does it back up members too? Yes, member profiles are included and exported. Restore re-creates contacts only: a member's login cannot be restored by an app.
  • What does Free keep? The two most recent snapshots. Take one before a big cleanup and one after, and you still have both.
  • What does Premium add? Twelve months of snapshots, an automatic snapshot once a week and the restore of deleted contacts.
  • When does the automatic snapshot run? The first time you open the dashboard page after seven days. Wix apps cannot run on a timer, so a quiet month waits until you next open the page.
  • A snapshot says "Capped at 25,000". The app stores at most 25,000 records in one snapshot. Split very large sites by taking snapshots after archiving old submissions.
  • I deleted a contact and have no Premium. Download the older snapshot's CSV and import it back with Wix's contact import.
