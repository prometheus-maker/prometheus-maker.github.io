What it does

A gift membership is an easy holiday sale for a yoga studio, gym, dance school or tutor, but plans can't be sold as gift cards today. This app adds the missing piece.

Make a gift code for any of your plans in the dashboard: a 3-month pass, a 10 class pass, a year of membership. Add who it's for, who it's from and a short message. Then copy the gift text, open it in your own email, or (Premium) download a printable gift card with the code on it.

The person who gets the gift opens your redeem page, types the code and logs in or signs up. The plan goes on their account straight away as a paid order, with its normal length and benefits. Each code works once. Codes can expire after 6 months, 1 year, 2 years or never, and you can void a code you made by mistake.

With Premium you can sell gift memberships in your store. Link a store product to a plan, and every paid order makes its codes for you, with the recipient's name, email and message taken from the product's custom text fields. Premium also lifts the 3-code limit and adds a CSV export.

Plans with a fixed length work best as gifts. The person redeeming needs a member account, because a plan always belongs to a member.
