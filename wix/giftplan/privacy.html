This policy describes what the Gift Membership app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app lets a site owner make gift codes for the site's Pricing Plans, and adds a widget where the person who received a gift redeems the code. Redeeming gives that person the plan as a paid order in Pricing Plans.

Data the app handles

  • Gift codes: for each code the owner makes, the app keeps the plan, the code, its state (open, redeemed, void), its expiry, and the details the owner types: recipient name and email, sender name and a message. For codes made from paid store orders (paid plan), it also keeps the order number and the buyer's name and email from that order, and the recipient details the buyer typed into the product's text fields. All of this is stored in the app's own collections inside the owner's Wix site.
  • Redeeming: when a logged-in member redeems a code, the app's backend on Wix learns who is redeeming from the member's Wix session (never from anything the browser sends), creates the plan order for that member, and stores the member ID, the member's name and the order ID with the gift, so the owner can see who redeemed it.
  • Wrong codes: to stop guessing, the backend counts wrong codes per member for 10 minutes in memory. Nothing about it is stored.
  • Widget settings: texts, colour, corners, font and words. Wix stores them with your site.
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan of this app.

The app sends no emails. The "Email the code" button opens the site owner's own email program. The widget sets no cookies and stores nothing in the browser.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives the gift details, your members or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else. Owners can delete gift data at any time in the site's CMS collections.
