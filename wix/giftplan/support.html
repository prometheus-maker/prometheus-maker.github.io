Support: Gift Membership

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the gift code (or its first 4 characters) and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I sell a gift membership?

Take the payment the way you like (at the desk, by invoice, or in your store). Then open the app in your dashboard, press New gift code, pick the plan, and send the code: copy the gift text, use Email the code, or on the paid plan download a printable gift card.

Where does the person redeem it?

On the page with the Redeem a Gift widget. Installing the app adds it to your site; move it to the page you want in the editor (Add Elements, then App Widgets). Put that page's link in the app's "Gift text & card" tab so it appears in every gift text.

Does the person need an account?

Yes. A plan always belongs to a site member, so the widget asks them to log in or sign up before the plan is added. Their code stays in the field while they do.

Which plans work best?

Plans with a fixed length, such as 3 months or a 10 class pass that ends after 6 months. A plan that renews every month stays active until you cancel it, and Pricing Plans may show its later payments as due.

Can a code be used twice?

No. Each code works once. You can also void an open code, for example after a refund, and set codes to expire.

How do store sales work? (paid plan)

Add a store product such as "Gift: 3 months" with text fields for the recipient's name, email and a message, then link it to a plan in the "Sell in your store" tab. When an order with that product is paid, the app makes one code per item and lists it under Gift codes with the buyer and recipient, ready to send.

What happens if I cancel the paid plan?

Codes you already made keep working. You can have up to 3 open codes at a time again, store orders no longer make codes, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: gift details stay in your own site, and the app sends no emails.
