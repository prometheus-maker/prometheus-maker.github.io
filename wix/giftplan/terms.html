1. What you get

Gift Membership is a Wix app for selling Pricing Plans as gifts: a dashboard page where you make gift codes for your plans, and a site widget where the person who received a gift redeems the code and gets the plan as a paid order. You install it from the Wix App Market and place the widget in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan allows up to 3 open (not yet redeemed) codes at a time, the redeem widget, copying or emailing each code from your own email program, and shows a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan removes the code limit, makes codes from paid store orders, adds printable gift card images and a CSV export, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, existing codes keep working and you can make new ones within the free plan's limit.

Orders the app creates are marked paid because the gift was paid for when you sold it.
