  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the viewer and what you saw. A screenshot helps.

Quick answers

Where is the viewer?

Installing the app adds a viewer with a sample hotel room to the page you were on, in its own section. To add more, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Panora. Each viewer has its own photo.

How do I show my own photo?

Upload it to your site's Media first. Then click the viewer, open Settings and press Choose from Media. If that button is missing, open Media in the editor, go to Site Files, select the photo, open More Actions and choose Copy URL, then paste the link into "360° photo link".

What photos work?

Photos from a 360 camera or from a phone's photo-sphere mode. They are twice as wide as tall. Wide phone panoramas work too: set "Photo type" to how far the panorama reaches (270°, 180° or 120°).

The photo looks stretched or the floor bends

The photo is probably not a full 360° photo. Pick the matching "Photo type" in Settings.

The mouse wheel scrolls the page instead of zooming

That is on purpose, so visitors can scroll past the viewer. Click into the photo once and the wheel zooms. The + and - buttons and pinching always zoom.

Can visitors on phones look around?

Yes, they swipe sideways. In full screen they can swipe in every direction, and the phone button lets them turn the phone itself to look around.

How do I make a virtual tour?

On Premium, press Add scene, pick a photo for each scene, then aim the preview in Settings at a door and press Scene arrow. Visitors click the arrow to move to the next room.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The viewer keeps working with the first scene. Arrows, info points, the start view and the intro switch off, and the small Panora link comes back.
