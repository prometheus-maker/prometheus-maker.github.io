Quick answers

Where is the calculator?

Installing the app adds the calculator to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Calorie Calculator.

How do I set my own starting values?

Click the calculator in the editor, then Settings. Under Units and starting values, choose metric or US units, calories or kilojoules, and the sex, age, height, weight and activity level visitors start from.

How are the calories worked out?

Resting burn comes from the Mifflin-St Jeor formula (weight, height, age and sex). It is multiplied by the activity factor, from 1.2 for a desk job to 1.9 for very hard training, which gives the calories burned in a day (often called TDEE). On Premium, the lose goal takes 500 kcal a day off (about 0.5 kg or 1 lb a week) and never goes below 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men; the gain goal adds 300 kcal.

Can visitors switch between kilograms and pounds?

Yes, on every plan. The Metric / US switch on the calculator changes the fields to feet, inches and pounds and back; the result stays the same.

How do I add a button such as "Book a consultation"?

On Premium, type the button text in Settings and choose a page or paste a link. The button shows only when it has a link.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The calculator keeps working. The goal switch, the protein / carbs / fat line and the button are hidden, and the small credit line comes back.
