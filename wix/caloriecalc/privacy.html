This policy describes what the Calorie Calculator app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a calorie calculator widget to pages of your Wix site. Visitors enter their sex, age, height, weight and activity level, and the widget works out the calories they burn in a day and, on the paid plan, a target for their goal with protein, carbs and fat.

Data the app handles

  • Widget settings: the units, starting values, the title and text, number format, colours, font, button text and link you choose. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its settings panel ask the app's backend on Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID. It stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro box on the app's dashboard page, kept in your browser's local storage.

Site visitors

The numbers a visitor enters (including age, height and weight) are calculated inside their own browser. They are never sent to the developer, to Wix or to anyone else, and they are not stored. The widget does not set cookies and does not track visitors.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your settings, your visitors' numbers or anything else about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Changes
