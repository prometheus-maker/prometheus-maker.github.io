Common questions

The app says a dish is not on the menu

A row is matched to a dish by its ID, or by its exact name when there is no ID. Check the spelling, or download the menu again and edit that file so the IDs match.

Nothing was written

Only the fields you changed are written. If a row is identical to the dish, it is skipped. Check the results table for the reason on each row.

How do I change every price by 10 percent

Enter 10 in the percentage box and press Recalculate prices. The preview shows each new price before you press Update.

The free plan stopped at 25 dishes

One free edit writes up to 25 dishes. Premium writes every row in one pass.
