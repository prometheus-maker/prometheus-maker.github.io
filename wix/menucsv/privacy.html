This policy describes what the Menu CSV: Bulk Edit Dishes app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you pick one of your restaurant menus, download its dishes as a CSV file, paste or upload the edited rows back, and write the changes to the dishes.

Data the app handles

  • Menu and dishes: the app reads your restaurant menus, sections and dishes from your site each time you open the page, and writes back only the fields you changed.
  • Pasted CSV: the rows you paste or upload are processed in the page and in a short request to the app's backend, and are not stored after the edit finishes.
  • Results: the last result list is kept in the page so you can download it as a CSV; nothing is kept on a server.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way. No other account data is read or stored.

The app stores no menu data of its own, keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
