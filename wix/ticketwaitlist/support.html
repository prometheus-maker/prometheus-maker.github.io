Support: Ticket Waitlist: Sold Out

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do I put the widget?

On your event page. In the editor, open the event details page (Events), click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Ticket Waitlist. It finds the event of that page by itself. You can also pick one event in the widget's Settings and place it on any page.

I added it but I don't see it on my live site.

That is on purpose: while tickets are on sale the widget hides and takes no room. It appears when the event is sold out. In the editor you always see a preview with a note.

Which events does it work with?

Ticketed events in Wix Events. RSVP events already have a waitlist built into Wix Events, so the widget stays hidden there.

How do people on the list get their ticket?

When tickets come back (a refund or a cancelled order), the dashboard page says "Tickets are on sale again". Click Email next to the first person: your mail app opens a ready message with the event link. The person is marked as emailed, so you can move down the list.

Can I have a separate line for each ticket type?

Yes, with Premium. Turn on "One waitlist per ticket type" in the widget's Settings. Visitors then choose the sold-out ticket type they want, and each type keeps its own order.

What happens if my paid plan ends?

Everyone already on a waitlist stays. New sign-ups stop at 30 people per event, ticket-type lines become one line per event, and the small credit link comes back.

How do I delete someone's details?

Click Remove next to them on the dashboard page. Removing the app also removes its collection with all sign-ups.
