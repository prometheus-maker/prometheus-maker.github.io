1. What you get

Ticket Waitlist: Sold Out is a Wix app with a site widget for ticketed Wix Events. When an event (or, on the paid plan, a ticket type) is sold out, visitors can join a waitlist and see their place in line. The sign-ups appear on the app's dashboard page, where you can email the next person when tickets come back. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan works for any number of events with up to 30 people on each event's waitlist, and shows a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan removes that limit, adds one waitlist per ticket type, a CSV download and one email to everyone waiting, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, everyone already on a waitlist stays there; new sign-ups follow the free plan's limit.

3. Emails and tickets

The app does not send emails itself and does not hold or sell tickets. When you click Email, your own mail app opens a message to the person; you decide whether and when to send it. Whether a person on the waitlist gets a ticket is up to you and your event's ticket sales.

4. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for how you use the names and email addresses visitors leave on your waitlist, including any privacy notice your site needs. Use them only to contact people about the event they waited for, and delete them when you no longer need them (you can remove anyone on the dashboard page).
