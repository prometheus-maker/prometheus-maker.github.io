This policy describes what the Ticket Waitlist: Sold Out app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to your Wix site that lets visitors join a waitlist when one of your ticketed events is sold out, and a dashboard page where you see the waitlist and email the next person when tickets come back.

Data the app handles

  • Events: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's events: title, start time, time zone, event page address, registration type and whether tickets are sold out; on the paid plan also the names of the ticket types and whether each is sold out. This is what your event pages already show visitors. The app does not read your guest lists, orders or payments.
  • Sign-ups from your visitors: name, email address, the event, the ticket type they want (paid plan), how many tickets they need, and the time they joined. They are stored in an app collection in your own site's CMS. You, as the site owner, are the controller of this data; the app processes it only to show you the waitlist and each visitor's place in line.
  • Your actions: the time you clicked Email for a person, stored with their sign-up.
  • Widget settings: the event, texts, colours, font and the most tickets per person. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last event status it got so the widget shows at once on the next visit, and your browser remembers whether you closed the intro box on the dashboard page.

Site visitors

The widget sets no cookies and does not track visitors. It only sends what a visitor types into the form after they click the join button. The form says the details are used only to contact them about the event. Visitors who want their details removed can ask you; you can remove anyone on the dashboard page.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. Sign-ups stay in your site's collection. When you click Email, your own mail app opens a message to that person; the app sends no emails. The developer never receives the sign-ups, your settings or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The backend keeps no logs of visitors.

Retention

Sign-ups stay in your site until you remove them, or until you remove the app and its collection.
