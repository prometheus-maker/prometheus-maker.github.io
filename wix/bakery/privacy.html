Effective date: 2026-11-15

This policy describes what the Bakery: Today's Bakes app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

You list your bakery's bakes in the app's dashboard page: a name, a note, a price, a photo, and the days each one is baked. A widget on your site shows today's list to your customers, with a sold-out mark for anything that has run out.

Data the app handles

  • Your bakes and shop settings: names, notes, prices, the photos you choose, the days each bake is made, sold-out marks, your pickup note and the link to your ordering page. All of it is stored in two data collections on your own site, together with a cached copy of your plan status.
  • The app does not ask for or store names, phone numbers, email addresses, payment details or any other personal details of your customers. Orders happen on your own checkout, not in this app.

Site visitors

The widget reads today's board from the app's backend when the page opens. It sets no cookies, keeps nothing in the visitor's browser, and does not track anyone.

Where the data goes

Your bakes and settings stay inside your site. The developer never receives them, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The app sends no emails or text messages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
