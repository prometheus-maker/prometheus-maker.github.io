How to set up the board, and what to do when something looks wrong.

Add the board to your site

1. Add the app, then open its dashboard page "Today's bakes".

2. Add your bakes: a name, a price, an optional note and photo, and the days each one is baked. Leave the days empty for a bake you make every day.

3. Press Save, then place the widget on a page in the editor.

Mark something sold out

In the dashboard, switch the bake to sold out. On Premium and Pro the mark clears by itself at midnight in your shop's time zone. On the free plan it stays until you clear it.

The board is empty

Check the time zone in the dashboard: the board shows the day it is where your shop is, not where your visitor is. A bake with no days set every day; a bake with one day shows only on that day.

Change the colours or heading

The widget takes the colours of your site. Set the heading and one accent colour in the widget's own settings in the editor.

Plans

Premium and Pro are billed through Wix and can be cancelled in your Wix account at any time. If you paid for a plan and the app does not show it, press Save in the dashboard once and reload the page.

Contact

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with your site address and what you saw. Replies within two working days.
