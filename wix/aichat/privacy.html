Effective date: 2026-11-13

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

This policy describes what the AI Chatbot: Site Assistant app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a chat bubble to every page of your Wix site. Visitors type questions and get short answers written from your site's own content: business info, store products, booking services, upcoming events, blog post titles and excerpts, and an FAQ you write.

Data the app handles

  • Site content: the app's backend reads your business info (name, description, address, phone, email, opening hours), product names, prices and short descriptions, booking service names and prices, upcoming event names, dates and places, and blog post titles and excerpts. It keeps a short text summary of them, plus your FAQ and chatbot settings, in a data collection on your site.
  • Visitor questions: when a visitor sends a question, the backend sends the question, the last few messages of that chat and the matching parts of your site summary to the AI provider to write the answer. The default provider is DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com). If you add your own OpenAI or DeepSeek API key, that provider is used instead, under your own account with them.
  • Question log: the last 200 visitor questions are kept as text in your site's data collection, with the time and whether the chatbot could answer, so you can read them in the dashboard. Answers, names, e-mails and IP addresses are not logged. Visitors should not type personal details into the chat.
  • Limits: to enforce reply limits the backend counts replies per site, and per visitor per hour. The per-visitor count uses a one-way hash of the visitor's IP address or a random chat id, is kept for one hour and is never shown to anyone.
  • Your API key (optional): stored on the app's backend storage for your site only, used only to answer your visitors, never shown again in full and never sent to visitors' browsers.
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.

Site visitors

The chat bubble sets no cookies and does not track visitors. It keeps the open chat in the browser tab's session storage so it survives page changes; closing the tab removes it.

AI provider

DeepSeek (or the provider of your own key) is a processor that receives the data above only to write answers. DeepSeek is based in China; see its privacy policy at https://platform.deepseek.com. If your visitors' data must stay in a particular region, use your own key with a provider you choose, or do not use the app.

Deleting data

Clearing your FAQ and uninstalling the app stops all processing. Ask prometheus.maker@proton.me to delete the app's reply counters for your site.
