Support: AI Chatbot: Site Assistant

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the question you asked the chatbot and what it answered. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the chat bubble?

It goes live on every page of your published site the first time you open the app in your dashboard. Publish your site once if you just created it. The toggle at the top of the app turns it off and on.

What does the chatbot know?

Your business info from Settings (name, address, opening hours, phone, email), store products, booking services, upcoming events, blog post titles and excerpts, and the FAQ you write in the app. The "What it knows" box shows the counts. It reads your site again every day and every time you save.

It said it isn't sure

That means the answer is not in your content, so it gives your contact line instead of guessing. Add the answer to your FAQ and save. On Premium, the "Couldn't answer" tab lists these questions.

Which language does it answer in?

The visitor's language by default. You can pick one fixed language instead.

What happens when the replies run out?

Visitors get your contact line instead of an answer until the next month (Premium) or until you upgrade or add your own API key (free plan).

How do I use my own API key?

In the app, open "Use your own API key", pick OpenAI or DeepSeek, paste the key and save. The app checks the key with the provider first. Your provider bills you for the replies.

Can visitors use it for other things?

It is told to answer only about your business and to refuse homework, essays, code and general questions.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: visitor questions and the matching parts of your site content go to the AI provider to write each answer; the last 200 questions are kept as text in your site.
