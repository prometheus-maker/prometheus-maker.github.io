What it does

AI Chatbot puts a chat bubble on every page of your site and answers visitors from your own content: your products and prices, booking services, upcoming events, blog posts, opening hours, address and phone. An FAQ you write in the dashboard covers anything else people ask, like delivery, parking or returns.

It answers in the visitor's own language and keeps to your business. If the answer isn't in your content, it says it isn't sure and gives your email or phone instead of guessing a price or an opening time. It politely refuses homework and essays.

The bubble goes live the first time you open the app, with a clean default look. You can change its name, greeting, colour and corner in the dashboard and try it there before your visitors do. The dashboard keeps the last 200 visitor questions, text only.

The free plan gives you 20 replies to try it, or unlimited replies with your own OpenAI or DeepSeek API key, billed by that provider. Premium includes 2,000 replies a month, lists the questions it could not answer so you can fill the gaps, and removes the small credit link.

Visitor questions and the matching parts of your site content are sent to DeepSeek to write each answer.
