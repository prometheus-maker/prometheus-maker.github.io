Privacy Policy: Maintenance: Property Requests

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Maintenance: Property Requests app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you record tenant repair requests, their property, priority, status, contractor and due date, and read them back as one list.

Data the app handles

  • Requests: the title, description, category, priority, status, property, unit, tenant name, contact details, contractor and due dates you type are stored in the app's data collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks the platform whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and shows the answer in the dashboard.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail, calls no outside service and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a request removes it from the collection. Removing the app removes its collection and everything in it.

Questions

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me.
