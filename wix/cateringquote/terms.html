1. What you get

Catering Quote from Menu is a Wix app with a site widget for Wix Restaurants menus. It shows your menu with prices and a live catering estimate for a number of guests. On the paid plan visitors can send the estimate to you as a quote request, which you see on the app's dashboard page and receive by email. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows the estimate, a button to your own link and a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds quote requests with an email to you, minimum guests, notice and deposit rules and a CSV export, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, requests you already have stay on your site; the form is no longer shown.

3. Estimates and orders

The estimate uses the prices on your menu when the visitor looks at it. It is not an order, a booking or a payment, and the app takes no money. Whether and at what price you cater an event is between you and your customer.

4. Your responsibilities

Keep your menu prices correct. You are responsible for how you use the names, email addresses and phone numbers visitors send, including any privacy notice your site needs. Use them only to answer the request, and delete them when you no longer need them (you can delete any request on the dashboard page).
