Privacy Policy: Catering Quote from Menu

Effective date: 2026-11-10

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Catering Quote from Menu app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to your Wix site that shows your Wix Restaurants menu with prices and a live catering estimate for a number of guests. On the paid plan visitors can send that estimate to you as a quote request, and a dashboard page lists the requests.

Data the app handles

  • Your menu: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's restaurant menus: menu, section and dish names, descriptions, photos, prices, price variant names (such as "12 servings") and dietary labels. This is what your menu pages already show visitors. The app does not read your orders, customers or payments, and it never changes your menu.
  • Quote requests (paid plan): the name, email address, phone number (optional) and notes a visitor types, the date and time of their event, the number of guests, the dishes they chose and the estimate. They are stored in an app collection in your own site's CMS. You, as the site owner, are the controller of this data; the app processes it only to show you the request and to send you one email about it.
  • Email to you: when a request arrives, the app sends one email with the request to the site owner's email address, through Wix.
  • Widget settings: the menu, texts, rules, link, colours and font. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last menu it got so the widget shows at once on the next visit, and your browser remembers whether you closed the intro box on the dashboard page.

Site visitors

The widget sets no cookies and does not track visitors. Choosing dishes and guests happens in the visitor's browser. Only when a visitor presses Send on the quote form are their details sent, and the form says they are used only to answer the request. Visitors who want their details removed can ask you; you can delete any request on the dashboard page.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. Requests stay in your site's collection and the email goes to you. The developer never receives the requests, your settings or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The backend keeps no logs of visitors.

Retention

Requests stay in your site until you delete them, or until you remove the app and its collection.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
