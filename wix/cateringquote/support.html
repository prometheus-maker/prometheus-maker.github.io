Support: Catering Quote from Menu

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Which menu does the widget show?

The menu named "Catering" (or a name with buffet, party or platter in it). If there is none, your first menu. You can pick any menu in the widget's Settings.

How do I give a tray two sizes?

In your Restaurants menu, price the dish with price variants and name the variants with the servings, for example "12 servings" and "24 servings". The widget shows the sizes, and the first tap adds enough trays for the guest count.

How do I price a buffet per guest?

Put "per person" in the dish name or description, for example "Lunch buffet, per person". The widget multiplies it by the number of guests. You can turn this off in Settings.

A dish is missing.

The widget leaves out hidden dishes, hidden sections and dishes marked out of stock. Changes to your menu show within a minute.

Where do quote requests go? (Premium)

They are saved on your site and listed on the app's dashboard page, and you get an email for each one at your account email. Click Reply to answer in your own mail app with the order filled in.

Can I set a minimum number of guests or a notice period? (Premium)

Yes. In Settings, set Minimum guests and Notice needed (in hours, for example 48). Visitors then can't pick an earlier date or send a smaller request. A deposit percentage adds a deposit line under the estimate.

What happens if my paid plan ends?

Requests you already have stay. The quote form is no longer shown, the rules switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

How do I delete a request?

Click Delete next to it on the dashboard page. Removing the app also removes its collection with all requests.
