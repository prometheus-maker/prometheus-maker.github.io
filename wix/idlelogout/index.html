What it does

A member signs in on a shared shop computer, a library PC or a phone left on a counter, and walks away. The session stays open. This app closes it.

Set the idle time in one dashboard page. From the moment a signed-in member's last click, scroll or keypress passes, the clock runs. Touch the page again and it resets. Leave it alone and the member is signed out, and the page reloads logged out.

Free covers one page of your site at a fixed 30 minutes. Point it at the account page, where the member data sits, and that page gets the protection.

Premium covers every page, lets you pick any timeout from 1 to 240 minutes, and warns the member first: a popup counts down from 60 seconds with a "Stay logged in" button, so nobody in the middle of a form is thrown out. Premium also sends the member to a page you choose after the logout, and excludes pages such as checkout or support where a timer would be in the way.

Every open tab shares one deadline, so a member reading in two tabs is not logged out of one while active in the other.

Visitors who are not signed in are never touched, and the app sends nothing anywhere. The timer is a script on your site, and the settings live in the app.
