These terms cover the Idle Logout: Member Security app ("the app") for sites built on Wix.

The service

The app signs a signed-in member out of your site after a period without activity, and optionally warns the member before it happens. The timeout, the warning, the redirect page and the pages it skips are set on a dashboard page and applied by a script on your site.

Plans

The free plan covers one page of your site at a fixed 30-minute timeout. Premium covers every page and adds a timeout from 1 to 240 minutes, a warning popup, a redirect page and a list of excluded pages. Plans are billed through the App Market; the price shown on the app's page at the time of purchase applies. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the free plan applies again from then.

The app runs in the member's browser and depends on the browser staying open; it is not a server-side session limit, and a member who blocks scripts or clears storage can get around it.
