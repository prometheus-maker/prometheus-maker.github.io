Privacy Policy: Idle Logout: Member Security

Effective date: 2026-11-19

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Idle Logout: Member Security app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app signs a member out of your site after a set time without activity. You choose the timeout on a dashboard page, and the app applies it to the site.

Data the app handles

  • Settings: the timeout, the warning, the redirect page and the pages list you save are stored as settings of the app's embedded script on your site. They are not sent to the developer.
  • Activity: the app's script in the page listens for clicks, scrolls and keypresses, and keeps the time of the last one in the browser's own storage so the open tabs agree. It reads nothing else, and the timestamp stays in the browser.
  • Member session: when the timer runs out, the app asks the site's own sign-in service to end the member's session. No personal data is sent to the developer.
  • Plan status: the dashboard page asks the site's app service whether the site is on the free or the paid plan and receives that one fact.

The app keeps no logs of members or visitors, stores no personal data and shares nothing with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

The settings stay on your site until you change them or remove the app. Removing the app removes its embedded script and the settings with it. The timestamps in a visitor's browser are cleared when the browser clears its storage.
