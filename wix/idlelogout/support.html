Support: Idle Logout: Member Security

The Idle Logout: Member Security app signs a member out of your site after a set time without activity.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Idle Logout: Member Security" in your site's dashboard. Leave the logout switch on, set the page that holds member content (the account page is usual), and press Save. The settings apply from the next page load of your site.

To try it, sign in to your site as a member and leave the page alone. After the timeout the member is signed out and the page reloads logged out. On the free plan the wait is 30 minutes; Premium lets you shorten it to as little as 1 minute while you test.

Questions people ask

  • Can I test it quickly? Yes. Premium takes any timeout from 1 to 240 minutes. On the free plan the logout is fixed at 30 minutes.
  • What counts as activity? A click, a scroll, a keypress or a touch. Moving the mouse pointer alone does not.
  • What happens with several tabs open? They share one deadline, so a member working in one tab is not logged out of another.
  • Does it log out visitors who are not signed in? No. Only members with a session are touched.
  • Can I skip a page? On Premium, add it to the excluded pages, for example /checkout or /support.
  • Where does the member land after the logout? On the page you set, or on the same page reloaded without a session.
  • Does the app send e-mails? No. Nothing is sent anywhere.
