Support: Church Bulletin: Weekly PDFs

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the bulletin and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the bulletin?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Church Bulletin.

Which folder does it show?

The first time you open Settings, it picks a folder named like "Bulletins" (or the one with the most documents). Click the widget in the editor, then Settings, and choose another folder if you like.

I uploaded this week's PDF. Do I need to publish again?

No. The folder is read when a visitor loads the page, so the new bulletin takes the top spot the next time the page is opened.

How does it know the date of a bulletin?

From the file name: "Bulletin 2026-11-08.pdf", "Nov 8 2026.pdf", "8th November 2026.pdf" and "11.8.26.pdf" all work. For dates like 11.8.26, choose in Settings whether the month or the day comes first. A file without a date in its name uses the day it was uploaded.

The wrong bulletin is on top

The newest date wins. Check the file name of the one on top: a typo in its date (for example 2027 instead of 2026) puts it first. Rename it in the Media Manager and reload the page.

A file in the folder does not show

Only public documents are listed. Private files, images and videos are left out.

Why does a PDF open in a new tab on my phone?

Most phones can't show a PDF inside a page. The phone's own viewer reads it better, with zoom and sharing.

Can I use it for minutes or a lunch menu?

Yes. In Settings, set "The folder holds" to Meeting minutes, Lunch menu or Newsletter, and the wording changes to match. You can also type your own.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working with this week's bulletin and the last 8. The full archive, month groups, search, tabs and hiding the download buttons switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app lists the public files you picked and never reads private files.
