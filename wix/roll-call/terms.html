1. What you get

Roll Call is a Wix app for checking members in at a front desk: it shows a person's plan and today's bookings, marks attendance and keeps a check-in log. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan does everything except that the log keeps 50 check-ins per calendar month and the log can't be downloaded. The paid plan keeps every check-in and lets you download the log as a CSV file. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. Your responsibilities

Use the app only on sites you are allowed to manage, give access only to staff who may see members' details, and follow Wix's terms and the privacy laws that apply to your members.

4. Your data

The check-in log is stored in your site's own CMS. The developer keeps no copy. See the Privacy Policy: https://prometheus-maker.github.io/wix/roll-call/privacy.html

5. Decisions at the desk

The app shows what Wix reports about a plan at that moment. Whether to let someone in is your decision.
