Support: Tierstep Pro: Quantity Tiers

The Tierstep Pro: Quantity Tiers app prices by quantity: a tier table on the product page, and cart lines that keep the unit price of the quantity in them.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Tierstep Pro: Quantity Tiers" in your dashboard. Under Products, use Find a product and pick one. Type a starting quantity under From (units) and a Discount % for that step; press Add tier for more steps and Save tiers. Up to twelve steps are allowed.

Open the product page in your store: the tier table shows each step with its unit price. The button reads "Add 10 to cart — $X each" for the quantity a shopper picks, and the cart shows that unit price.

Pack sizes

If you sell a product as a 6-pack, a 12-pack and a 24-pack, open Pack-variant import preview: the app reads those options and proposes tiers. Nothing is saved until you press Apply, and your products and variants are never changed.

Questions people ask

**Do my tiers create discount rules?** No. The app keeps its own tiers and applies the price in the cart; no discount rule is created in your store.

**What happens when a shopper lowers the quantity?** The line goes back to the price of the step it now belongs to, and under the first step it returns to the base price.

**Is there a free plan?** No. Pro is $19 per month, or $15 per month billed yearly, with a 14-day free trial.

**Where are my tiers stored?** In the app's own storage on your site. Removing the app removes them.
