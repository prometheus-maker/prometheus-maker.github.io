What it does

Price by quantity without writing a single discount rule: set the steps once, and every product line in the cart charges the price that matches the quantity in it.

Most shops sell at one price and hope for bigger orders. Tierstep Pro adds a tier table to a product page: from 10 pieces each costs less, from 25 it costs less again, and the shopper sees the per-unit price before adding anything. The button adds the line already at that price.

When a shopper changes the quantity in the cart, the line is re-priced to the tier it now belongs to; when it drops back under the first step, the base price returns. Checkout carries the same price, so what the cart shows is what the order says.

The dashboard lists your products; open one and type the quantities and unit prices, up to twelve steps. A pack importer reads your size options (a 6-pack, a 12-pack, a 24-pack) and turns them into tiers with one preview and one click. Nothing is written until you confirm.

Your tiers live in the app's own storage inside your shop. No discount rules are created, so nothing else in your store is touched.
