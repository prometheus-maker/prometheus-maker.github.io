Privacy Policy: Tierstep Pro: Quantity Tiers

Effective date: 2026-11-21

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Tierstep Pro: Quantity Tiers app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app sets quantity tiers for products in your shop and shows a tier table on the product page. It also keeps cart lines at the unit price that matches their quantity.

Data the app handles

  • Products: the app reads your product names, prices, variants and options to show them in its dashboard page and to build the tier table.
  • Tiers: the quantity steps you enter, and the unit price of each step, are stored in one collection in the app's data storage on your site.
  • Carts and orders: when a cart changes, the app reads the cart's line items (product, quantity and price) to decide whether a line needs its tier price, and it reads orders to confirm the price that was charged. It keeps no separate copy of carts or orders.
  • Plan status: the app asks whether your site is on the paid plan and answers with that one fact.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. What you save stays on your site.

Keeping and removing data

Your tiers stay on your site until you change them or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its collection and everything in it.
