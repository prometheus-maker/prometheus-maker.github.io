1. What you get

Sold Works: Collected Art is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your sold-out store products in their own gallery, with a red dot and a count. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows up to 12 sold works with the count, in a grid or masonry layout, with your own heading and texts, and a small credit link under the gallery. The paid plan shows up to 60 works and adds a category filter, a row of works still for sale with their prices, a commission button, other marker wording and styles, more colour themes and your own dot colour, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

The gallery shows the products, photos and stock status from your store. Keep them correct, and only show works you have the right to show.
