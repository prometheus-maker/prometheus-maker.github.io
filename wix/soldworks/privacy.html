This policy describes what the Sold Works: Collected Art app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a gallery to pages of your Wix site. It shows your store products that are sold out (stock 0), and on the paid plan a row of works still for sale.

Data the app handles

  • Your store products: the app's backend asks Wix for the site's visible products and passes on only what the gallery shows: each product's name, price, main image and its size, product page link, the IDs of its categories, whether it is out of stock, and when it was last updated (to put the newest sold works first). Hidden products are left out. Orders, customers, buyers and inventory numbers are not read.
  • Your store categories: their names, for the category filter.
  • Widget settings: your heading, texts, layout, theme, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last answer (plan and the products above) so the gallery shows at once on the next page.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows artworks. Clicking one opens its product page on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your products, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
