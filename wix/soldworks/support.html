Support: Sold Works: Collected Art

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the gallery and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the gallery?

Installing the app adds the gallery to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Sold Works.

Which works show?

Every visible product in your store whose stock is 0, newest first. Give each original a stock of 1: when it sells, it shows up in the gallery by itself. Products that are out of stock but open for pre-order are still for sale, so they stay out. Sold works never show a price.

The editor shows paintings I didn't make

Those are samples so you can see the look before anything has sold. Your live site shows only your own sold products, and hides the gallery while there are none.

Prints and cards show up too

On Premium you can keep the gallery to chosen store categories, such as Originals. Tick them in Settings under Only these categories.

Can I write "Collected" instead of "Sold"?

Yes, on Premium: Settings, Sold marker, Wording. You can also write your own words. The count line ("48 works collected") can be reworded on every plan: write {n} where the number goes.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The gallery keeps working with up to 12 sold works, the red dot and the white look. The category filter, the Available now row, the commission button and the other themes switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the gallery reads your products' names, prices, photos, links and stock status, and sends nothing to the developer.
