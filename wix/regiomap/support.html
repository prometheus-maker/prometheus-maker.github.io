Support: Regiomap

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the map and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the map?

Installing the app adds a sample world map to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Regiomap. Each map has its own settings.

How do I color my regions?

Click the map in the editor, then Settings. Under Regions, type a country or state name and pick it from the list, then choose its group. "Add hover text or link" opens two more boxes for that region. You can also paste a list, one region per line: name | group | hover text | link.

A region I typed does not get colored

The panel lists names it can't find on the current map ("Not on this map"). Use the name from the list, a common short name (USA, UK) or a code (TX, DE). Switching from the world map to the US map keeps your list, so countries won't match states.

My changes disappear when I publish

They shouldn't, on any plan. Click Publish in the editor after your changes and reload the live page. If they still don't show, email the site address and we'll look.

Why only 10 regions?

The free plan colors up to 10 regions in up to 3 groups, on the world and US maps. Premium removes the limit and adds the Europe, Canada, UK and Ireland, and Australia maps, color by value, pins and zoom.

How do pins work?

On Premium, add one pin per line under Pins: name | latitude, longitude | text | link. You can copy latitude and longitude from any map service by right-clicking a place.

Can it use my site's font?

Yes, that's the default. Use Change font in Settings to pick a different one, and "Use site font" to go back.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The map keeps working. Premium maps fall back to the world map, and only the first 10 regions, the first 3 groups and no pins show.
