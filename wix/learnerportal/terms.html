1. What you get

Learner Portal: Progress is a Wix app for Online Programs. It gives each learner a widget that shows their progress in every program they joined — percent done, steps finished and the next step — and gives the site owner a Wix dashboard page listing every participant with their progress and last activity, with learners who have gone quiet for 7 or more days marked (paid plan). You install it from the Wix App Market and add the widget to a member page.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan gives the progress widget and the participant list. The paid plan adds the quiet-learner list and priority support. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. There is no free trial.

3. Your responsibilities

You decide which programs and members are on your site and where the widget appears. The progress the app shows comes from the data Wix holds for your programs; if a participant's steps are recorded incorrectly in Wix, the app shows that same data.
