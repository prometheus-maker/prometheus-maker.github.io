Support: Learner Portal: Progress

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan. Paid sites get priority replies.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the program involved, and what you saw. A screenshot of the dashboard page helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days; priority support aims for one business day.

Quick answers

How do I start?

Install the app from the Wix App Market, then add the Learner Portal widget to a members-only page in the Wix editor and publish. Learners who are signed in see their progress; signed-out visitors see a prompt to sign in.

Where does the progress come from?

From the Online Programs your site already keeps: each participant's completed steps and last activity. There is nothing to type in or import.

What does the dashboard show?

Every participant in your programs, sorted by how much they finished, with percent, steps done and the date they last opened a step. Learners quiet for 7 or more days are marked on the paid plan.

A learner's percentage looks wrong

The percent is completed steps out of the program's total steps, as Wix holds them. Check the program's steps in the Wix dashboard; the app follows that data.

Nothing shows on the page

The widget is for signed-in members. Check that the page is members-only and that the member has joined a program.

How do I cancel or change plan?

Manage the subscription from your Wix dashboard under Manage Apps; Wix handles billing and refunds.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then Learner Portal: Progress.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app runs on Wix, and the developer receives nothing.
