This policy describes what the Learner Portal: Progress app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app shows each learner their progress in the Wix Online Programs they joined, and shows the site owner a dashboard list of every participant with their percent done, steps completed and last activity date. A custom widget on the owner's site shows a progress card for each program; the dashboard is opened from the Wix dashboard.

Data the app handles

  • Online programs and participants: program IDs, titles and step definitions, and each participant's completed steps and last activity date, read through Wix's Online Programs APIs to compute progress.
  • Member identity: the signed-in member's Wix member ID and, for display in the owner's dashboard, the participant's name where Wix provides one.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether the site is on the free or the paid plan.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro box, kept in your browser's local storage.

The app does not collect payments, card details, passwords or email addresses. It stores nothing on a server of the developer's.

Where the data goes

Everything is read through Wix's own APIs by the app's code running on Wix's platform. The developer has no other server and never receives your programs, participants or progress data. Nothing is shared with anyone else. Removing the app stops all processing.
