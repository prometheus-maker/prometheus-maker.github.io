This policy describes what the Sale Countdown app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a bar to pages of your Wix site that counts down to the end (or the start) of your store's automatic discounts.

Data the app handles

  • Your automatic discounts: the app's backend asks Wix for the site's automatic discounts and passes on only what the bar shows: each discount's name, start and end time, the deal (for example 25% off) and its minimum order amount or item count. Discounts limited to chosen customers are left out. Coupons, orders and customer data are not read.
  • Widget settings: your texts, button link, theme, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status and currency: the backend also tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan and which currency your store uses, together with the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last answer (plan, currency and the discounts above) so the bar shows at once on the next page.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows a discount name, a deal and a clock. If you add a button, clicking it opens the page you chose.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your discounts, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
