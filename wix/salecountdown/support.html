Support: Sale Countdown

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the bar and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do I set the end date?

On the discount itself: in your dashboard open Catalog, then Discounts, then Automatic Discounts. Create or edit the discount and set its end date and time. The bar counts down to it. The discount's name is what shoppers see, so name it the way you want it shown, for example "Black Friday".

Where is the bar?

Installing the app adds the bar to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Sale Countdown.

The editor shows "Black Friday 25% off" but I have no such discount

That is a sample so you can see the look before your first discount exists. Your live site shows only your own discounts, and hides the bar when none is running.

I have several discounts

The bar shows the running discount that ends first. With none running it shows "Starts in" for the next discount that starts within 14 days. On Premium you can pick one discount, or let all running discounts take turns.

Some discounts never show

Discounts limited to chosen customers are left out, because every visitor would see the bar. Paused discounts and ones that have ended are left out too.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The bar keeps counting down in the Black Friday theme with your texts and button. The other themes, your own colours, the chosen discount and rotation switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the bar reads your discounts' names, deals and dates, and sends nothing to the developer.
