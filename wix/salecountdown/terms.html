1. What you get

Sale Countdown is a Wix app with a site widget that counts down to the end of your store's automatic discounts. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan counts down to your next sale in the Black Friday theme, with your own texts and button, and a small credit link under the bar. The paid plan adds the Cyber Monday, Christmas and light themes, a theme matched to the discount name, your own colours, a chosen discount, several sales taking turns, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

The bar shows the discounts you create in your dashboard. The discount itself is given by your store's checkout, not by the app: keep the discount's name, deal and dates correct, and make sure the button points to a page where the offer applies.
