1. What you get

Pet Adoption is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your adoptable pets (cards, ribbons and a page per pet) and a dashboard page where you manage them. You install it from the Wix App Market.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows up to 12 pets with Available, Pending and Adopted ribbons, a page per pet, an Apply button that opens your own page, and a small credit link under the widget. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
