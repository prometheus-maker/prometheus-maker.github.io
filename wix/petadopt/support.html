Support: Pet Adoption

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what you saw. A screenshot helps. Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget? Installing the app adds it to the page you were on. An Adopt page is a good home for it. To add it elsewhere, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets, then Adoptable Pets.

Where do I add my pets? In your site's dashboard: Apps, then Pet Adoption. The first visit shows a few sample pets so the widget looks right from the start; edit or delete them. The order in the dashboard is the order on your site.

What happens when a pet is adopted? Set its status to Adopted. It leaves the grid at once. On the paid plan it moves to the Happy tails strip under the grid.

Where does Apply to adopt go? On the free plan, to the page you set in the widget's Settings (Application page link): a page on your site such as /adoption-form, any web address, or mailto:you@yourshelter.org. On the paid plan the built-in form opens under the pet instead.

Where do applications land? (paid plan) In the dashboard's Applications tab, with a CSV download, and in your Contacts with the label "Adoption applicant". To get an email for each one, create a Wix Automation that starts when that label is added.

How does the sponsor bar work? (paid plan) Install Wix Donations and create a campaign with a goal. Edit a pet in the dashboard and pick the campaign. In the widget's Settings set the Donation page link to the page with your donation form.

Can I import my pets? (paid plan) Yes: Import CSV takes a spreadsheet saved as CSV, or rows pasted from Google Sheets, with columns name, species, breed, age, sex, size, status, photo url and story.
