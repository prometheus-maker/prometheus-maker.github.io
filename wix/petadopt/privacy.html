This policy describes what the Pet Adoption app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds an adoptable pets widget to your Wix site and a dashboard page where you add and edit your pets. On the paid plan visitors can send an adoption application from the widget.

Data the app handles

  • Pets: the names, photos, facts, stories and health notes you enter. They are stored in your site's own collection (Pet Adoption pets) and shown to your visitors in the widget. A new site gets a few sample pets once; you can edit or delete them.
  • Adoption applications (paid plan): the name, email, phone, home type, other pets and message a visitor types, the pet they applied for and the time. They are stored in your site's collection (Pet Adoption applications). The applicant is also added to your site's Contacts with the label "Adoption applicant" (an existing contact with the same email only gets the label). The visitor ticks a consent box before sending.
  • Spam protection: to limit each visitor to 5 applications an hour, the app stores a one-way salted hash of the visitor's IP address next to the application. The IP address itself is never stored.
  • Donations (paid plan): if you link a pet to a Wix Donations campaign, the app reads that campaign's goal and total to draw the sponsor bar. It does not read donors.
  • Widget settings: heading, links, colours and words. Wix stores them with your site.
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan of this app, and keeps a signed copy of the answer for up to 6 hours in your site's collection (Pet Adoption settings).

The widget sets no cookies and stores nothing in the browser. The app never sends email.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. Everything stays in your Wix site, where you can see, export and delete it in your CMS and Contacts. The developer never receives your pets, your applicants or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
