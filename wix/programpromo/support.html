Support: Program Promo: Sale Pricing

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the promo you made, and what you saw. A screenshot of the dashboard's promo list or change log helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I start?

Open the app from your dashboard (Manage Apps, then Program Promo), tick the programs the sale covers, choose a percent off or type the exact sale price, and set the start and end. The preview shows each price as it will be during the sale. Nothing is touched until the start time.

When does the price change?

When the promo starts, the app writes the sale price to each program. When it ends, the app puts the list price back. It checks on its own every few minutes, and you can press Run now or End now in the dashboard.

Which price is restored when the sale ends?

The list price saved when you created the promo. An edit made on the site during a sale does not lose the original, because the restore reads the saved price.

Can I change a promo after creating it?

Start it early with Run now, end it early with End now, or delete it. To change the discount or the dates, make a new promo.

Where does the countdown ribbon go? (Premium)

Add the Program Promo ribbon from the Wix editor's Add panel, choose it in the panel's settings, and publish the site. It shows the struck list price, the sale price in your site's currency, and the time left.

The price did not change on time

Wix can delay or refuse a price write. Open the dashboard's change log: every write and its result is listed there. Press Run now to try again.

How many promos can I run?

The free plan runs one active promo at a time. Premium runs any number.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then Program Promo: Sale Pricing.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app runs on Wix, and the developer receives nothing.
