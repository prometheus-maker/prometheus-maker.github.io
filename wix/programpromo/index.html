What it does

Program Promo runs a sale on your Online Programs and puts the price back when the sale ends. Pick the programs, choose a percent off or type the exact sale price, set the start and end, and the app changes each program's price at the start and restores the list price at the end.

The dashboard lists every program with its list price. Tick the ones a promo covers and the preview shows each price as it will be during the sale, with the saving in money and as a whole percent. Nothing is touched until the promo's start time.

The list price is saved when you create the promo, so an edit made on the site during a sale does not lose the original. Every change the app writes gets a row in the change log, with the program, the price before and after.

A promo can be started early with Run now, ended early with End now, or deleted. The app applies due changes on its own, and the same run is one button away in the dashboard.

The ribbon widget counts down to the end of the sale. It shows the struck list price and the sale price in your site's currency, and it disappears once the sale ends.

The free plan runs one active promo. Premium runs unlimited promos and adds the countdown ribbon.
