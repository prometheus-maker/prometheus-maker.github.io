Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds a sample text with three explained words to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Hintly. Each widget has its own text and terms.

How do I add a tooltip to a word?

Click the widget in the editor, then Settings. Type your text, then click Add term, type the word exactly as it appears in the text and write its definition. The word gets an underline at once. If the panel says "not in text", check the spelling.

Can I add a tooltip to a button or an image elsewhere on my page?

No. Hintly explains words inside its own text widget. It doesn't attach tooltips to other elements on the page.

How do visitors on phones open a tooltip?

They tap the underlined word. A tap anywhere else closes it.

Is it accessible?

Each word can be reached with the Tab key and opened with Enter; Esc closes the tooltip. Screen readers read the definition with the word.

Why are only 3 words underlined?

The free plan explains up to 3 terms per widget. Premium allows 60.

Only the first match of a word is underlined. Why?

That keeps a long text calm. Premium has a switch to underline every match.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working. The first 3 terms keep their tooltips; links, the glossary, the tooltip side and every-match underlines switch off.
