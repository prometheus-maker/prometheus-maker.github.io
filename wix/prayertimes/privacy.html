This policy describes what the Prayer Times app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows the day's prayer times for your mosque: begin times calculated for the place you choose, your iqamah times and your Jumu'ah time.

Data the app handles

  • Widget settings: the place name, coordinates, time zone, calculation method, iqamah and Jumu'ah times, colours, font and options you enter in the settings panel. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its settings panel ask the app's backend on Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID. It stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.
  • Browser settings: how many times you opened the settings panel (for one optional review link), kept in your browser's local storage.
  • Prayer times are calculated inside the visitor's browser from the saved coordinates. No location of the visitor is read or sent anywhere.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not record what they click. It does not ask for the visitor's location. It only shows your timetable.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your settings, your text or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
