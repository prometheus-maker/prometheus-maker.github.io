Support: Prayer Times

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget, your city and calculation method, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds today's prayer times to the page you were on. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Prayer Times.

Why does it say "Sample times for London"?

No place is saved yet. Click the widget, then Settings, and pick your city or type your mosque's latitude, longitude and time zone.

The times are a minute or two off from our printed timetable

Timetables differ in method, rounding and local adjustments. First pick the method your mosque follows and Standard or Hanafi Asr. If one prayer is still off, use "Fine-tune begin times" to shift it by whole minutes.

How do I set iqamah (jamaat) times?

For each prayer type either a fixed time, like 13:30 or 1:30 pm, or +15 for 15 minutes after the adhan. Leave a box empty to hide that iqamah. "Round up to 5 minutes" turns 13:12 into 13:15.

Which time zone do visitors see?

Always your mosque's, whatever the visitor's own clock says.

Our Hijri date is a day off

Choose -1 or +1 day under Calculation > Hijri date to follow your local moon sighting.

What does Premium add?

The whole month as a table visitors can print, Ramadan mode (Suhoor ends, Iftar, the Ramadan day and a countdown to Iftar), up to four Jumu'ah times, iqamah changes from a date (for example winter and summer times), and no credit link.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps showing today's times, the countdown and your first Jumu'ah time. The month table, Ramadan mode, extra Jumu'ah times and date changes switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: times are calculated in the visitor's browser and nothing is collected from visitors.
