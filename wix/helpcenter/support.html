Support: Help Center: Knowledge Base

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the help centre and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do the articles come from?

From your blog. Each published blog post is an article, and each blog category you pick is a topic. Write a new post, give it a category, publish it, and it appears in the help centre within 10 minutes.

How do I pick the topics?

In the editor, click the help centre, then Settings, and tick the categories under Topics. With none ticked, it uses the categories with the most posts.

Why doesn't a search find my article?

Search looks at the title, the excerpt and the hashtags of the posts in your topics. Posts in other categories are not part of the help centre. Add a hashtag with the word people use ("refund", "delivery") to help them find it.

How do I add "Was this helpful?" under my posts?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Help Center, and click Add to blog post page. Then publish your site. It is a Premium feature: on the free plan visitors don't see it.

Can I keep help articles out of my main blog feed?

Your blog's own settings decide what the blog page shows. The help centre only reads your posts; it doesn't change your blog.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The help centre shows 3 topics and 5 popular articles in the tile layout, the vote under posts is hidden, and the small credit link comes back. Saved votes and searches stay in your site's collections.
