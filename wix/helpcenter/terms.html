1. What you get

Help Center: Knowledge Base is a Wix app that turns your published blog posts into a help centre on your site: a search box, topic tiles from your blog categories and your most-read posts, plus a "Was this article helpful?" question under blog posts and a dashboard page. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in your Wix dashboard and editor.

2. Your content

The articles are your own blog posts. You are responsible for what they say. The app only shows what you have published.

3. Plans and payment

The free plan shows the search box, up to 3 topics, 5 most-read articles and a small credit link. The paid plan allows up to 12 topics and 3 layouts, your own labels, the "Was this article helpful?" question, the report of least helpful articles and of searches that found nothing, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the app keeps working with the free plan's options; votes and searches already saved stay in your site's collections.
