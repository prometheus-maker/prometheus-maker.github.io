This policy describes what the Help Center: Knowledge Base app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app shows a help centre on your site, built from your own published blog posts: a search box, a tile per blog category you pick, and your most-read posts. On the paid plan it also adds a "Was this article helpful? Yes / No" question under each blog post and a dashboard report.

Data the app handles

  • Your published blog posts and categories: the app's backend reads the title, excerpt, link, categories, hashtags, publish date and view count of your newest 300 published posts, and your category names and descriptions. These are already public on your site. The list is kept in the server's memory and in a server cache for up to ten minutes, then read again.
  • Searches (paid plan only): when a visitor finishes a search in the help centre, the backend saves the search words (in lower case, at most 80 characters) and how many articles matched, in a data collection on your own site ("Help Center searches"). It counts how often each search was made and when it was last made. Nothing about the visitor is saved with it.
  • Votes (paid plan only): when a visitor answers "Was this article helpful?", the backend adds one to that post's Yes or No total in a data collection on your own site ("Help Center votes"), together with the post's title and link. Nothing about the visitor is saved. The visitor's browser remembers its own answer so the question is not asked twice.
  • Settings: your topics, texts and colours. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend tells the widget, its settings panels and the dashboard whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID. This answer is kept in the server's memory for up to ten minutes.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors. Searches and votes stay in your site's collections, where you can see and delete them in your CMS, and they are removed with the collections if you uninstall the app. Visitors should not type personal details into a help search; if one does, the words appear in your searches collection and you can delete that row.

Site visitors

The app sets no cookies and does not track anyone. It stores the last article list and the visitor's own vote in the visitor's browser (local storage) so the help centre shows at once on the next visit.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your posts, searches, votes or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
