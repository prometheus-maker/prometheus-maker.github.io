1. What you get

Ski Report: Snow & Trails is a Wix app that lets you post a daily snow report in your dashboard and shows it on your site: open or closed, snow depths, lifts, runs and trails open, when it was updated, and a 3-day weather forecast. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in your Wix dashboard and editor.

2. Your responsibility

You type the report, and you are responsible for keeping it correct and current; the app does not measure snow or check lifts. The forecast comes from MET Norway and is a forecast, not a promise. Don't rely on the app for safety decisions such as avalanche or closure warnings.

3. Plans and payment

The free plan covers one area with the status card and the forecast, and shows a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds lift, run and trail lists with grooming, a roads and parking line, an alert bar, a TV screen layout, a snowfall history chart and several areas, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the app keeps working with the free plan.
