Support: Ski Report: Snow & Trails

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what the widget or the dashboard showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the report on my site?

It was added to your home page when you installed the app. In the editor, drag it where people look first (your home or conditions page), click it and open Settings to change the layout and the colours.

How do I post today's report?

Open Apps, then Ski Report, in your dashboard (your phone works). Tap Open, Closed or Opening soon, type the base depth and new snow, set each lift, run or trail to Open, On hold or Closed, and press Save report. The widget shows it within a few minutes, with "Updated … ago".

Where do the lifts and runs come from?

You add them once under Setup in the dashboard: a name, and for runs and trails a difficulty mark (North American or European). Then you only flip their status each day.

Why is the "Updated" line amber?

The report was saved more than 24 hours ago. Save a fresh report and it turns grey again.

Where does the forecast come from?

From MET Norway, for the latitude and longitude you pasted under Setup (in Google Maps, right-click your base lodge and click the numbers to copy them). "Expected snow" is the precipitation forecast while the air is below 0.5 °C, counted as about 1 cm of fresh snow per mm of water.

Is anything measured automatically?

No. The report shows exactly what you typed. The app has no lift sensors or snow stakes.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget goes back to the status card for your first area, and the small credit link comes back. Your lists and other areas stay saved in the dashboard.
