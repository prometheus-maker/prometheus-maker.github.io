This policy describes what the Ski Report: Snow & Trails app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

You post a daily snow report from the app's dashboard page: open or closed, snow depths, surface, a note, and the status of your lifts, runs and trails. A widget on your site shows that report with a 3-day weather forecast for the spot you chose.

Data the app handles

  • The current report: area names, the latitude and longitude you typed for the forecast, depths, notes, and your lift, run and trail names with their status. It is stored in a data collection on your own site, together with a cached copy of your plan status.
  • Report history: each time you press Save, one row with the time, status, depths and counts is added to a second data collection on your site (used for the snowfall chart). You can delete rows in your CMS at any time.
  • The app does not ask for or store names, phone numbers, email addresses or other personal details of anyone.

Site visitors

The widget reads the report from the app's backend when the page opens and every 5 minutes while it stays open. It sets no cookies and does not track anyone.

Weather data

To get the forecast, the app's backend sends the latitude and longitude of your area (rounded to 4 decimals) to MET Norway (api.met.no). Nothing else is sent, and nothing about your visitors.

Where the data goes

Your report stays inside your Wix site. The developer never receives it, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The app sends no emails or text messages.

Changes
