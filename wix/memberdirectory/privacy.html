This policy describes what the Member Directory for Clubs app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a member directory to your site. You enter each member in your site's dashboard and add the directory element to a page in the editor, where visitors see cards for the members you have listed.

Data the app handles

  • Members: the name, role, company, section, short description, e-mail, phone, website, photo link, the two display switches and the display order you enter are stored in the app's data collection on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Public display: a member's name, role, company, section, description and photo appear on the page where you added the directory, whenever that member is switched to listed. E-mail, phone and website appear only when the member is also switched to show contact details.
  • Photos: a photo is shown from the image address you enter, so a visitor's browser loads that image from the host you chose. That host sees the visitor's request in the usual way.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sets no cookies of its own, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The CSV download happens in your browser and stays on your computer.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collection and everything in it. You decide which members are listed and which show contact details, and you are responsible for having each member's agreement to publish their details.
