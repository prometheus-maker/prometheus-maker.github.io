Common questions

The directory does not show on my site

The element is added in the editor, not built into every page. Open the editor, choose Add Apps, pick Member Directory for Clubs and place the element on the page. Publish the site after placing it.

A member is missing from the page

Open the app in your dashboard and check the member's Listed switch. The directory shows listed members only. On the free plan it shows the first 10 listed members and says more are there.

A member's e-mail or phone is not on the page

The member's Show contact details switch is off, or the field is empty. Details appear only when the member is listed and the switch is on.

A photo does not load

A photo must be a full https:// address that points straight at an image file. A page address instead of an image file will not show.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
