What it does

Most clubs keep their roster in a spreadsheet, and a visitor who wants to reach the treasurer has to send a message to the club and wait. This app puts that roster on your site as a member directory that visitors can read.

Add each member in your site's dashboard: name, role, company or club, a section, one or two lines about them, and their email, phone, website and a photo link. Two switches decide what the outside sees. One lists the member or keeps them hidden, and the other shows their contact details or keeps them private. Contacts never appear unless that member has opted in, so the directory stays in the club's hands.

On your site the directory is a card grid with initials, name, role and section. On the free plan it shows the first 10 listed members, with a line that says more are there. Premium lists every member and adds a search box and a section filter, so someone looking for the coach or the membership secretary finds the right person at once.

The roster stays with the app on your site, and a CSV download gives you a copy for your own records. Free covers 10 listed members. Premium is $6 a month, or $5 a month billed yearly, with a 14-day free trial.
