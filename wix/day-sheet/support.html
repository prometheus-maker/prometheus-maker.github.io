Support: Day Sheet for Bookings

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

A booking is missing from the sheet.

The sheet shows confirmed and pending bookings. Canceled and declined bookings, and checkouts that were never finished, are left out. If a booking was made after you opened the app, pick the day again to reload.

The times are off by some hours.

Times are shown in your business time zone, set in your site's settings under Language & Region. The time zone in use is printed at the bottom of the sheet.

Print does nothing.

Some browsers block printing from inside the dashboard. Press "Open in new tab" and print from the new tab. If the new tab is blocked too, allow pop-ups for the Wix dashboard.

The sheet is too wide for the paper.

Untick columns you don't need, or pick Letter or A4 to match your printer. In the print dialog you can also choose landscape.

My booking form question isn't listed.

Questions appear once at least one booking on the chosen days has an answer to them. Name, email and phone have their own columns.

Class participants are in a strange order.

Rosters and class lists are sorted by last name, then first name. People who gave no last name go at the end.

How do I print a different day or a whole week?

The free plan prints today and tomorrow. The paid plan adds "Next 7 days" and "Pick dates" for any range up to seven days.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Apps, then Day Sheet for Bookings, or search for "Day Sheet" in the dashboard search.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app reads your bookings, keeps nothing, and the developer receives nothing.
