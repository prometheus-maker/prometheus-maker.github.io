What it does

Your booking calendar shows the day on a screen. The front desk and your staff often want it on paper, and Day Sheet prints it.

Pick today or tomorrow and the sheet is ready: every appointment and class in time order, with the client's name, phone and payment status, and a blank column for notes. Class bookings are grouped under the session and sorted by last name, so a long class is easy to scan. The preview on screen is exactly what comes out of the printer.

With Premium, each staff member can get their own page, and every class gets a roster with tick boxes, a head count and spare lines for walk-ins. You can print any dates up to a week at a time and save layouts for the front desk and for each room.

Times are always in your business time zone, even if you open the app from somewhere else. A4 or Letter, 12 or 24-hour clock. The same rows download as a CSV file on both plans.

Day Sheet only reads your bookings. It stores nothing, writes nothing to your site, and sends nothing anywhere.
