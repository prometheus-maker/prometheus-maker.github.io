1. What you get

Day Sheet for Bookings is a Wix app that turns your bookings into a printable daily or weekly sheet and class rosters, and can download the same rows as a CSV file. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan prints today and tomorrow, with everyone on one sheet. The paid plan prints any dates up to seven days at a time and adds one page per staff member, class rosters and saved layouts. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

4. Your data
