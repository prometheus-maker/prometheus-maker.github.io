What it does

Sponsor Wall puts your sponsors' logos on any page, grouped by tier: Platinum at the top with the biggest logos, then Gold, Silver and Bronze. Each logo links to the sponsor's website in a new tab. A "Become a sponsor" button can send the next business to your sponsorship page or a form.

You keep the sponsor list in one place, the app's dashboard: add a name, pick the tier, choose the logo from your media and paste the link. Every page that shows the wall reads that list, so a new sponsor appears everywhere at once. Rename the tiers or use up to six of them. You can also edit the list in your content manager.

The wall looks finished with no setup. In the editor it shows sample sponsors until you add your own, and the live site shows nothing until you do. You can change the heading, logo size, colours and font.

It suits schools and parent groups, sports clubs, charity events and conferences. The free plan shows two tiers and eight sponsors, with a small credit link. Premium shows every tier and sponsor, counts the clicks each logo gets with a report and CSV you can send to sponsors at renewal, adds start and end dates, grey logos and a carousel, and removes the credit link.
