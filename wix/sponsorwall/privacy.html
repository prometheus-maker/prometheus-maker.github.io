This policy describes what the Sponsor Wall app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a section to pages of your Wix site that shows your sponsors' logos grouped by tier, each linking to the sponsor's website, and gives you a dashboard page to keep the sponsor list.

Data the app handles

  • Sponsor list: the names, tiers, logo images, links and (on Premium) start and end dates you enter. They are stored in a data collection on your own site, which you can also open in your content manager. Logos stay in your site's media.
  • Tier names: stored in the same place, in a small settings collection.
  • Click counts: when a visitor clicks a logo, the app adds 1 to that sponsor's count for the month, in a collection on your site. Only the sponsor and the month are stored. Nothing about the visitor is stored. To stop one visitor from inflating the counts, the backend keeps a short-lived, hashed form of the visitor's network address in memory for about ten minutes; it is never written anywhere.
  • Widget settings: your heading, button text and link, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget, its panel and the backend ask Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last sponsor list it received, so the wall shows at once on the next visit, and remembers which logos it already counted during the visit; the dashboard remembers whether you closed its intro box.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect personal data. Logos load from the Wix image servers, like any other image on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your sponsor list, the click counts, your settings or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Deleting data

Remove a sponsor in the dashboard and it is deleted from the collection. Uninstalling the app removes its collections from your site.
