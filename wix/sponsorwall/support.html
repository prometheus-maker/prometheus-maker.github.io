Support: Sponsor Wall

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the sponsor wall and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do I add sponsors?

In your site's dashboard, open Apps, then Sponsor Wall, and click Add sponsor: name, tier, logo (from your media) and link. You can also edit the "Sponsors" collection in your content manager.

Where is the wall on my site?

It is added to the page you were on when you installed the app. To place it elsewhere, open the editor, then Add Elements, App Widgets, Sponsor Wall.

The widget shows sample sponsors

That only happens inside the editor while your list is empty. Visitors never see sample sponsors; the live site shows nothing until you add your first sponsor.

Which logo files work best?

PNG or SVG with a transparent background. The wall fits each logo into its box without cropping, and the top tier gets the biggest boxes.

How do I rename or add tiers?

In the dashboard, under Tiers. Up to six tiers; the free plan shows the first two that have sponsors.

How are clicks counted?

Each visitor's first click on a logo during a visit adds 1 to that sponsor's count for the month. The report and CSV are on Premium; counting happens on every plan.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The wall goes back to two tiers and eight sponsors, the credit link comes back, and dates, grey logos and the carousel switch off. Your sponsors and click counts stay.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: your sponsor list and click counts stay on your own site, and the widget never tracks visitors.
