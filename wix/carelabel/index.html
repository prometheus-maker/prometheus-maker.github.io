What it does

Clothing and accessory sellers have to show what a garment is made of and how to care for it. This app keeps both next to your products and turns them into a label sheet you can print.

Pick a product from your store, add its fibres as percentages that must add up to 100, and choose the care lines: wash temperature, bleach, drying, ironing, dry cleaning. The preview is exactly what the label says. Press Generate PDF sheet and you get an A4 page of labels, ready to print and cut.

The same text appears on your product page, under the product details, in the language the page is shown in. A customer sees "Material & care" only when the product has a label saved.

The free plan covers 10 products and one label size. Premium makes the number of products unlimited, adds three label sizes (S 70 × 35 mm, M 90 × 50 mm, L 105 × 74 mm) and prints the sheet in English, German, French, Spanish or Portuguese, for $6 per month or $5 per month billed yearly.

The app reads your product names and nothing else from your store; it never changes a product. Labels live in the app's own collections on your site, and removing the app removes them.
