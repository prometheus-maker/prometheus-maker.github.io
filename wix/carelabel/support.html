Support: Care Label: Fabric & Fibre

The Care Label: Fabric & Fibre app keeps each product's fibre composition and care instructions in one dashboard page, shows them on your product pages and prints them as an A4 label sheet.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Care Label: Fabric & Fibre" in your dashboard. Pick a product from the list. Add the fibres with their percentages (for example 80 cotton, 20 polyester); they must add up to 100. Choose the care lines: wash temperature, bleach, drying, ironing and dry cleaning. The preview shows the label as it will print. Press Save.

To print, set the sheet size and the number of copies, then press Generate PDF sheet: an A4 page opens in a new tab and downloads as a PDF, ready to print and cut.

The "Material & care" block appears on the product page of every product that has a saved label, in the language the page is shown in.

Questions people ask

  • Which label size do I need? M (90 × 50 mm) is the free size and fits most garments. Premium adds S (70 × 35 mm) and L (105 × 74 mm).
  • Can I print a sheet in German, French, Spanish or Portuguese? Yes, on Premium: the care lines are translated, and the sheet follows the language you choose.
  • How many products can I label? Ten on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What does the product page show? The saved composition and the care lines only. It stays hidden for products without a label.
  • Does the app change my products? No. It reads product names to show the picker and writes nothing to your store.
  • Where is the data? In the app's collections on your site; removing the app removes it.
