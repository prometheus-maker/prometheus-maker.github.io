This policy describes what the Care Label: Fabric & Fibre app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your dashboard where you describe each product's fibres and care instructions, and it shows that text on your store's product pages. It also builds a printable label sheet in your browser.

Data the app handles

  • Labels: the fibre rows (material and percentage), the care lines, the brand and the origin you enter for a product are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Settings: the sheet size, sheet language and number of copies you save are stored in the app's settings collection on your site.
  • Product names: the dashboard reads the names and ids of the products in your store to show the picker, and the product page passes the id of the product being viewed. Nothing else is read, and no product is ever changed.
  • Plan status: the app's backend asks whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties.

Printing

The label sheet is built in your browser from the labels already stored on your site; the file is handed to your browser to open or download, and the app keeps no copy of it.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete a label in the dashboard or remove the app. Removing the app removes its collections and everything in them.
