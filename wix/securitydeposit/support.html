Common questions

How do I record a deposit?

Add the rental first, with its deposit amount and the refund window in days. Then record a booking: the guest, the dates, the amount held and the day you took it. The record appears in the ledger with the status Held.

Where does the money go?

Nowhere in the app. The app is the record of a deposit you already took and will return; the money itself moves in your own payment account. Record the refund here on the day you send it back.

A deduction is refused

Deductions can never add up to more than the amount you hold. Lower a deduction or raise the amount held, and save again.

The refund shows as past due

Each rental has a refund window in days after check-out. A record that is still held after that date is flagged as past due. If you returned the money, open the record and press Record refund.

The guest terms do not show on my site

Open the rental in the dashboard and switch it to listed on the site. The element shows only the rentals you marked as listed. On the free plan, one rental is shown; Premium shows every rental.
