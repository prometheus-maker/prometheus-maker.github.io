This policy describes what the Rental Deposit: Hold & Refund app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you record your rentals and the deposit for each booking, and an element on your site where guests read the deposit terms.

Data the app handles

  • Rentals: the rental name, its kind, the deposit amount, the currency, the refund window in days and the house-rules line you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Deposit records: the guest name, the optional contact detail, the booking reference, the check-in and check-out dates, the amount held, the day it was taken, the deductions with their reasons and amounts, the damage notes and the refund date you enter are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.
  • Public element: the deposit terms element shows the rental name, the deposit amount, the refund window and the house-rules line that you marked as listed. It shows no guest data and no deposit record.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a rental removes its deposit records. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
