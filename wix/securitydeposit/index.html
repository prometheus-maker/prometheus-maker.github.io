What it does

Short-let and equipment rentals take a deposit at booking and give it back some days after the guest leaves. Most owners track it in a notebook, and damage can end in a dispute with no record. This app keeps that ledger.

Add each rental with its deposit amount, currency and how many days after check-out the money goes back. Then record a booking: the guest, the reference, the dates, the amount held and the day you took it. Every record shows held, part refunded, refunded or kept, and the amount still refundable.

When something is damaged, open the record and add a deduction with a reason and an amount; deductions can never pass the amount you hold. Add notes about the damage, then press Record refund on the day you return the money, which stamps the date.

The ledger shows open holds, what is still held, what was kept and how many refunds are past due: a deposit should go back check-out plus the window you set for each rental. The ledger exports to CSV in one press.
