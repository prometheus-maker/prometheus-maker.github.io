1. What you get from the app

Equipment Rental: Gear Booking is an app for a site that rents out gear by the day. You add your items in the dashboard; a widget on your site shows a visitor how many units are free for their dates and the total, and takes a booking request that you approve or decline. Requests carry the visitor's name, email and message, and you answer them from the dashboard. You install it from the app market and set it up in your dashboard and editor.

2. Your responsibility

You decide which requests to approve, and you confirm every booking with the visitor by email. You also choose how many units of each item you own. If you decline a request, its units stay free for other visitors. The app does not take payment and does not charge a deposit, so the prices and the units shown depend on the items and requests you enter; check them before you promise gear to a customer.

3. Plans and payment

The free plan holds up to 3 items and includes the site widget, the request form and the month calendar. The paid plan raises the cap to 200 items. The marketplace sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under its own terms. You can cancel the paid plan at any time, and it runs until the end of the period you paid for. Before you pay, the marketplace shows the price and the billing period. If the paid plan ends, the app keeps working with the free plan's limit of 3 items; your items and approved bookings stay.
