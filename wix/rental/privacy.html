This policy describes what the Equipment Rental: Gear Booking app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

You list the gear you rent out in the app's dashboard, and a widget on your site shows a visitor how many units are free for the dates they pick, the total price for the range, and a form to send you a booking request.

Data the app handles

  • Items: each item is stored in a data collection on your own site with its name, quantity, day price, optional week price, optional photo link, public note and sort order. Only you and the app can read it.
  • Requests: when a visitor sends a request, the item, the dates, the quantity, the total, the name, email address and message they typed are stored in a data collection on your site, together with the time and the status (new, approved or declined). You can decline or delete requests in the dashboard at any time.
  • A cached copy of your plan status is stored in a third data collection on your site.

Site visitors

The widget on your site receives the item list and the date ranges of approved bookings only: never visitor names, email addresses or messages. It sets no cookies and does not track anyone. A visitor who sends a request shares the details they type with you, the site owner, and with no one else.

Where the data goes

Everything stays inside your site. The developer never receives your items, your bookings or your requests, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The app sends no emails and no text messages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
