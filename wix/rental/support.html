Support: Equipment Rental: Gear Booking

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what the widget or the dashboard showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Finding the widget

It was added to your site when you installed the app. In the editor, drag it onto your rental page, click it and open Settings to change the heading and the accent, text and card colours.

Adding your gear

Open Apps, then Equipment Rental, in your dashboard. Under Items, type the name, how many units you own, the day price and, if you like, a week price, a photo link and a note. The free plan holds 3 items; Premium raises the cap to 200. Press "Add three sample items" to see the shape of it, then edit them.

When is an item taken out of the calendar?

Only when you approve a request. A new request sits under Requests and blocks nothing, so two visitors can ask for the same dates and you choose between them. When you decline a request, the units stay free.

How is the price worked out?

From the start day up to the day before the end day. If you set a week price, whole weeks use it and the remaining days use the day price. The visitor sees the same total before sending.

Can the visitor pay online?

No. The request carries a total, but nothing is charged. The visitor sends name, email and message; you confirm by email and take payment however you already do.

Why does the widget say availability could not load?

The widget reads the app's backend when the page opens. A temporary failure shows that line; refresh the page. If it keeps happening, email us with your site address.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The dashboard keeps working with the free limit of 3 items. Your existing items and their approved bookings stay; you just cannot add more until you are under 3.
