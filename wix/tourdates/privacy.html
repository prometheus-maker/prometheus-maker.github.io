This policy describes what the Tour Dates: Shows & Tickets app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that lists your shows from Wix Events as a tour table: date, city, venue and a ticket or RSVP button, with past shows in an archive on the paid plan.

Data the app handles

  • Events: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's published events: title, date and time zone, venue name and address, status, registration type and status (including whether tickets are sold out), the lowest and highest ticket price, an outside ticket link if you set one, the event page address, calendar links and categories. All of this is already shown on your site's event pages.
  • Event categories: their names, so you can pick which ones the widget lists.
  • Guests: the app does not read your guest lists, orders or registration forms.
  • Widget settings: the categories you picked, layout, wording, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last list of shows it saw for up to one day, so it appears at once on the next visit.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. Ticket and calendar buttons are plain links: a visitor who clicks one goes to the page you or Wix set for it. The developer never receives anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
