Support: Tour Dates: Shows & Tickets

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the tour dates and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the list?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Tour Dates.

Where do the shows come from?

From Events in your dashboard. Each event is one row. Give it a date, a venue name and an address: the city and state come from the address.

My tickets are sold on Ticketmaster, Eventbrite or the venue's site.

In the event's registration settings, choose registration on an external site and paste the link. The row's Tickets button then opens that link in a new tab.

How do I show a show as sold out?

Ticketed events show Sold out by themselves when every ticket is gone. You can also close registration for the event: the row then says Sold out too.

I added a show. Do I need to publish again?

No. The shows are read when a visitor loads the page, so a new or changed show appears the next time the page is opened.

How do I keep workshops out of the tour list?

Give your shows a category in Events (for example Tour), then tick it under Only these categories in the widget's Settings. The category filter is part of Premium.

What happens to shows that have passed?

They leave the list. With Premium they move to the Past shows archive, with a tab per year.

What happens if the paid plan ends?

The widget keeps working with your next 10 shows. The archive, next show line, calendar buttons, category filter and compact layout switch off, and the small credit link comes back.
