1. What you get

Tour Dates: Shows & Tickets is a Wix app with a site widget that lists your shows from Wix Events as a tour table. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan lists your next 10 upcoming shows with their ticket, RSVP or outside ticket links and their sold out, canceled and on sale soon labels, with a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds an archive of past shows by year, a next show line, add to calendar buttons, a category filter, a compact layout, up to 100 shows, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

You decide which events are shown and keep their dates, venues and ticket links correct in Wix Events. The widget shows what your events say.
