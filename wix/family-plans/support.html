Support: Family Plans

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the plan and the buyer, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I make a family plan?

Open the app, go to Family plans, pick one of your plans, set how many people one purchase covers and whether the buyer counts as one of them, then press Make it a family plan.

What is the "(family member)" plan?

A free, hidden plan the app creates for family members. Open it in Pricing Plans and tick the same benefits as the family plan (services, pages, programs), so members get the same access. Don't sell it: if someone buys it directly, the app cancels that order.

How do I add someone to a family?

In Families, press Add member on the buyer's row, then pick a site member or type their email. They get the family-member plan for free.

Someone isn't found by email

A plan always belongs to a site member. Ask the person to sign up on your site with that email, then add them.

What happens when the buyer cancels?

When the buyer's plan is canceled or ends, every family member's plan is canceled too. If the buyer cancels at the end of a billing period, members keep access until that date, like the buyer.

Can I swap a member?

Yes. Press Manage, then Swap next to the member, and pick the new person. The seat count stays the same.

How do buyers add family members themselves? (Premium)

Put a form with an email field on a members-only page. In Member form, pick that form and the email field. When a logged-in buyer sends it, the member with that email joins their family. Refused answers (family full, not a member yet) are listed in Activity.

A member shows "Waiting"

Pricing Plans sometimes asks an app to slow down when it gives many plans in a short time. The member keeps the seat, and Family Plans gives the plan automatically the next time you open the app or a plan order changes, usually within the hour. Nothing is lost.

What does the free plan cover?

One family plan, with up to 3 people per purchase. Premium covers every plan and up to 50 people.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Apps, then Family Plans.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: your records stay in your site's collections, the app uses only Wix's own APIs, and the developer receives nothing.
