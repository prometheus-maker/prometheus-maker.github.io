This policy describes what the Family Plans app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app is a page in your Wix dashboard plus app code hosted by Wix. You mark a pricing plan as a family plan; the app creates a free, private plan for family members, gives it to the members you add to a buyer's family, and cancels it when the buyer's plan is canceled or ends. On the paid plan it can also read the answers to one form you choose, so buyers can add family members themselves.

Data the app handles

  • Pricing plans: names, prices, perks and visibility, to list your plans and create the family-member plan.
  • Pricing plan orders: plan, buyer's member ID, status and dates. Used to show each family and to give or cancel family members' plans.
  • Members: name and login email, to show who is in each family and to find a member by email.
  • Form answers (paid plan, the form you choose only): the email typed into the field you pick, and who sent the form.
  • Your records, stored in collections on your own site: the app's settings, one record per family seat (order IDs, member ID, name, email, status, dates, note) and an activity log.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro and review boxes, kept in your browser's local storage.

Where the data goes

Everything stays in your site. The app talks only to Wix's own APIs, and its automatic steps run as app code hosted by Wix. CSV files you download are made in your browser. The developer runs no server of its own, never receives, stores or sees your members or orders, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The app sends no email.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app stops all reading and changes. The settings, seats and log live in your site's collections until you delete them. Plans and orders the app created stay in your pricing plans until you archive or cancel them.
