1. What you get

Family Plans is a Wix app that lets one pricing plan purchase cover several people: it gives family members a free, private plan and cancels it when the buyer's plan ends. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan covers one family plan with up to 3 people per purchase, adding, removing and swapping members, and automatic removal when the buyer's plan ends. The paid plan covers every plan with up to 50 people per purchase and adds a member form, full seat history and a CSV export. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.
