This policy describes what the Sale Page app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a block to pages of your Wix site that lists the store products that are on sale right now: products with a sale price, or products an automatic discount lowers.

Data the app handles

  • Your store products: the app's backend asks Wix for the site's visible products and passes on only the ones on sale, with what the block shows: each product's name, old price, new price, percent off, the names of the discounts that lower it, main image, product page link, creation date and the IDs of its categories. Hidden and out-of-stock products are left out. Orders, customers and inventory numbers are not read.
  • Your automatic discounts: their names and end dates, for the "Ends Sunday" lines.
  • Your store categories: their names, for the category filter.
  • Widget settings: your heading, texts, layout, sort order, filters, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status and currency: the backend also tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan and which currency your store uses, together with the app instance ID.
  • Short-lived copies: the backend keeps the product list above in memory for up to five minutes so pages load faster, and the visitor's browser keeps the last answer so the block shows at once on the next page. Nothing is written to a database.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows products and their prices. Clicking a product opens its product page on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your products, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
