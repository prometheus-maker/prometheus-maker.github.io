What it does

Sale Page puts every product you have on sale on one page, and keeps it current. Drop it on a page called Sale or Black Friday and it fills itself from your store.

A product counts as on sale when it costs less today: it has a sale price, or one of your automatic discounts lowers it. Each card shows the photo, the old price struck through, the new price, the percent off and the name of the discount, with a button to the product page. Biggest savings come first. Sold-out products stay off the page. When a discount ends, its products leave by themselves, so nobody finds a sale that's already over. With nothing on sale, the block hides (or shows your own message).

You can change the heading, the line under it, the button text and the font. On phones the cards sit two across.

The free plan shows up to 12 products in a grid, with a small credit link under the page. Premium shows up to 200 products and adds a list layout and a carousel strip for your home page, sorting by price, newest or ending soonest, a filter for one category, a minimum discount, "Ends Sunday" lines taken from your discounts' end dates, your own ribbon and sale colour, and removes the credit link.
