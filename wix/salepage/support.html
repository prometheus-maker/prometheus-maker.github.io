Support: Sale Page

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the block and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the block?

Installing the app adds it to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Sale Page. A page called Sale or Black Friday in your menu is the usual home for it.

Which products show?

Every visible product that costs less today than before and is in stock. That means a product with a sale price (a "compare at" price above its price), or a product that one of your automatic discounts lowers right now. Coupon codes don't count: they lower the price only at checkout. A product with options shows its lowest price as "From $20".

A product left the page

Its sale price was removed, its discount ended, or it sold out. That is on purpose: shoppers never land on a sale that is already over.

The editor shows a wool throw and a copper kettle I don't sell

That is a sample so you can see the look while nothing in your store is on sale. Your live site shows only your own products, and hides the block (or shows your message) when nothing is on sale.

Can it show only one category, or only big discounts?

Yes, on Premium: pick a category under "Only one category", and a minimum such as "20% off or more".

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The block keeps working as a grid of up to 12 products, biggest discount first. The list and carousel layouts, sorting, filters, end dates, ribbon and own colour switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the block reads your products' names, prices, photos and links and your discounts' names and end dates, and sends nothing to the developer.
