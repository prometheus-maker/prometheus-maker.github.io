1. What you get

Calendar Subscribe: Events is a Wix app with a site widget that gives your visitors a live calendar feed of your published Wix Events, with buttons for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and other calendar apps. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan gives one calendar of all your upcoming events with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Copy link buttons and a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds one calendar per event category, an Outlook button, the next 3 events under the buttons, the last 30 days of events in the feed, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options, and category calendars that people already subscribed to switch to all upcoming events.

3. How updates reach subscribers

The feed changes within about 15 minutes of a change to your events. Each calendar app decides when it downloads the feed again (Google Calendar usually within a day), so the developer cannot promise when a change appears in a subscriber's calendar.

4. Your responsibilities

You decide which events you publish. Everything in a published event (title, time, place, short description) goes into the feed.
