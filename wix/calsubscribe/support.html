Support: Calendar Subscribe: Events

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the subscribe box?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Calendar Subscribe.

Which events are in the calendar?

Every published event that has not ended yet, soonest first. Cancelled events stay in the feed marked as cancelled, so subscribers' calendars drop them. Events without a date yet are left out.

I changed an event. When do subscribers see it?

The feed changes within about 15 minutes. Each calendar app then picks it up on its own schedule: Apple Calendar and Outlook within hours, Google Calendar usually within a day.

Can I give each group or city its own calendar?

Yes, with Premium. Put the events in an event category (Events, then the event, then Category), add one widget per category and pick the category in each widget's Settings.

Can I put the calendar link in my newsletter?

Yes. The widget's Settings show your calendar link, and the Copy link button gives the same address.

What happens if the paid plan ends?

The widget keeps working with Google, Apple and Copy link. Category calendars that people already subscribed to switch to all upcoming events, the Outlook button and the event list go away, and the small credit link comes back.
