Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

This policy describes what the Calendar Subscribe: Events app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site with buttons that let visitors subscribe to your events in their own calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook or any app that reads an iCalendar link).

Data the app handles

  • Events: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's published events: title, start and end time, time zone, location name and address, short description, event page address, category names and status (for example cancelled). It turns them into a calendar feed. This is the same information your event pages already show to anyone.
  • Guests: the app does not read your guest lists, registrations, tickets or orders.
  • The calendar feed: its address contains your app instance ID and a signature, so only the app can make it. Calendar apps that subscribe (Google, Apple, Microsoft and others) download the feed from the app's backend from time to time. What those services do with the calendar is covered by their own privacy policies and the subscriber's account with them.
  • Widget settings: category, buttons, wording, colours, font and layout. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last answer it got (the feed address, the calendar name and, on Premium, the next 3 events) so the buttons work at once on the next visit.

The backend keeps a copy of each feed in memory for up to 15 minutes, stores nothing else and keeps no logs of visitors or subscribers.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. Copy link uses the browser's clipboard only when the visitor presses it.

Where the data goes

Your public event data goes to the calendar apps of the people who subscribe. Nothing else leaves Wix. The developer never receives anything about your visitors or subscribers, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
