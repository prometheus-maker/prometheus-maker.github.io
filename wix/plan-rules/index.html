Cap plan sales and set early-bird plan prices

Add to your site from the Wix App Market

  • One subscription per member, or once ever
  • Early-bird and returning-member prices
  • Waive the joining fee for people you pick

What it does

You want to sell 50 founding memberships and then stop. Pricing Plans has no setting for that, so the 51st person can still buy one. Plan Rules adds the missing limits and lets you change who pays what.

Each plan gets four limits: how many can ever be sold, how many can be active at once, how many one member can hold at the same time, and how many one member can ever buy. Set "one per member" and nobody ends up with two copies of the same membership. The plan list shows sold and active counts next to each limit, so full plans stand out.

Premium adds price rules. Give everyone 20% off until 1 March, let returning members pay less, or set a price for people you pick. You can also waive a joining fee or change it for the same groups, and copy a plan's rules to your other plans. A table shows what a first-time buyer, a returning member and each picked member would pay on any date you choose.

Every change is shown as before and after, with warnings, until you press Save. If someone changed the plan in the meantime, nothing is saved and you're asked to reload.

Limits apply to purchases on your site. Plans you add for someone by hand in the dashboard are not limited.
