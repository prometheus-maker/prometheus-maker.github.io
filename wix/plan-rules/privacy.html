This policy describes what the Plan Rules app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app is a page in your Wix dashboard. It lists the plans you sell with Pricing Plans, shows how many of each were sold, and, when you press Save, changes a plan's sales limits, price rules and fee rules.

Data the app handles

  • Plans: name, price, fees, sales limits and price rules, read to show them and changed only when you press Save.
  • Plan orders: plan, status and the member each order belongs to, read only to count sold and active subscriptions.
  • Members: names and login emails, read when you search for members to give them a special price, and to show the names of members already in a rule.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan of this app.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro and review boxes, kept in your browser's local storage.

Where the data goes

Everything is read and written through Wix's own APIs from your browser while the page is open. The app has no server and no database: the developer never receives, stores or sees your plans or your members' data, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
