Support: Plan Rules

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, what you tried and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

What is the difference between the four limits?

"Total sold, ever" counts every subscription ever sold, including ended and cancelled ones. "Active at the same time" counts only current ones, so a place opens up when someone's plan ends. "Active per member" set to 1 means a member can't hold two of the plan at once. "Per member, ever" set to 1 means each member can buy the plan only once.

I set a limit below the number already sold. What happens?

Nobody loses a plan. New sales on your site stop until the count is below the limit (for active limits) or for good (for total sold).

Can I still add a plan for a member myself?

Yes. Limits apply to purchases on your site. Plans you add for someone in the dashboard are not limited.

Two price rules match the same buyer. Which price do they pay?

The lower one. Once any rule matches, the plan's normal price no longer counts, even if the rule's price is higher.

Does a discount apply only to the first payment?

No. The price a buyer gets applies to every payment of that subscription.

Who counts as a returning member?

A member who has had any plan on your site before, on any plan, including ended and cancelled ones.

It says the plan changed since I opened it

Someone saved the plan in the meantime, in the dashboard or in another tab. Nothing was saved. Reload, check the rules and save again.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then Plan Rules: Membership Limits.
