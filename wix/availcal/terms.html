1. What you get

Availability Calendar: iCal is a Wix app that shows which dates are free and which are booked on your site. You mark dates in your Wix dashboard; on the paid plan the app can also read calendar links (iCal) from booking sites, export the dates you mark, and let visitors request dates. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in your Wix dashboard and editor.

2. Your responsibility

You decide which dates are shown as booked. The app shows availability; it does not take bookings or payments and does not prevent double bookings. Dates read from iCal links are only as current as the last successful sync (about every two hours), so check your other calendars before you confirm a stay or an event.

3. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one calendar for this month and next, with a small credit link under the calendar. The paid plan adds iCal sync and export, several calendars, 12 months ahead, a date request form and a note under the calendar, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the app keeps working with the free plan's limits.
