Support: Availability Calendar: iCal

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what the calendar or the dashboard showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the calendar?

It was added to your site when you installed the app. In the editor, drag it to your availability or booking page, click it and open Settings to change the heading, the words for free and booked days, the week start and the colours.

How do I mark booked dates?

Open Apps, then Availability Calendar, in your dashboard. Click a date to mark it booked and click again to free it. Shift-click marks every date between your last click and this one. For stays, mark the nights: from the arrival day up to the day before departure.

What are the half-shaded days?

In the default "Nights" mode, an arrival day is shaded in its lower half (the afternoon is taken) and a departure day in its upper half (free again from the afternoon). Venues and photographers can switch the widget to "Whole days".

Where do I find my Airbnb, Vrbo or Booking.com link? (paid plan)

Airbnb: Calendar, Availability, Connect calendars, Export calendar. Vrbo: Calendar, Import/Export, Export. Booking.com: Rates & Availability, Sync calendars, Export. Paste the link in the dashboard and press Sync now. The app refreshes it about every two hours.

Does it stop double bookings?

No. It shows dates; it does not take bookings. The export link lets booking sites import the dates you mark here, but they read it on their own schedule, so confirm stays in one place.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

iCal dates, extra calendars and the request form stop showing, the calendar goes back to this month and next, and the small credit link comes back. Your marked dates stay.
