This policy describes what the Availability Calendar: iCal app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

You mark booked dates in the app's dashboard page, and a calendar on your site shows which dates are free and which are taken. On the paid plan the app can also read calendar links (iCal) you paste from booking sites, and visitors can send you a request for dates.

Data the app handles

  • Calendars: each calendar is stored in a data collection on your own site with its name, the date ranges you marked, the date ranges read from your iCal links, those links, the time and result of the last sync, an optional note and minimum stay, and your time zone. Only you and the app can read it.
  • iCal links: the app's backend downloads each link you add (about every two hours while your calendar is viewed, and when you press Sync now) and keeps only the start and end dates of each entry. It does not read or store guest names, descriptions or any other text from those files.
  • Date requests (paid plan): when a visitor sends a request, the dates, the name, email address, number of guests and message they typed are stored in a data collection on your site, and a contact with the label "Date request" is created or updated in your site's contacts. You can delete requests in the dashboard or your CMS at any time.
  • A cached copy of your plan status is stored in a third data collection on your site.

Site visitors

The calendar on your site receives date ranges only: never names, notes about guests or any other personal data. It sets no cookies and does not track anyone. Visitors who send a date request share the details they type with you, the site owner.

Where the data goes

Everything stays inside your Wix site. The developer never receives your calendars or requests, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The export link (paid plan) shows only the dates you marked, to whoever has the link; you choose where to paste it. The app sends no emails or text messages.
