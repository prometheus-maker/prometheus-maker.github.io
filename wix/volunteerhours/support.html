Support: Volunteer Hours: Hour Tracker

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I set it up?

Add a form to your site (Add Elements, then Contact & Forms) with a Number field labelled Hours, plus name and email. Then add the widget: installing the app adds it to the page you were on; to add another, click Add Elements, then App Widgets, then Volunteer Hours. It picks your newest form with an hours field by itself.

The widget shows 0 or the wrong form

Click the widget, then Settings, and pick the form under "What to add up". If your form has several number fields, choose the one with the hours under "Number to add up". New entries show up within a minute.

Can it count minutes, laps or pages instead?

Yes. Pick that number field and change the text after the number, for example "minutes read" for a read-a-thon.

Who can see names?

Only you and your site's collaborators, on the app's dashboard page. Site visitors see the total and the number of volunteers.

How do I start again for a new school year?

On Premium, set "Count from (date)" in the widget settings to the first day of the year. Older entries stay in your Forms submissions but no longer count.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working with one form as a number or a goal bar. Extra forms, paper hours, the start date and the circle style switch off, the dashboard shows the top five people again, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: visitors get two numbers, names stay in your dashboard, and nothing goes to the developer.
