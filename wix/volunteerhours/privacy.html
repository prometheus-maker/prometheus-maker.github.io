This policy describes what the Volunteer Hours app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds up a number field (such as Hours) across the submissions of a form on your Wix site, shows the total on your site and lists in your dashboard who logged how many hours.

Data the app handles

  • Form submissions: the app's backend asks Wix for the submissions of the form you chose. For the site widget it passes on only two numbers: the total of the number field and how many different people sent the form. For your dashboard, which only the site's owners and collaborators can open, it also passes on each person's name and email from the form, their hours, number of entries and the date of their last entry.
  • Form list: the names of your forms and of their number fields, so you can pick one in the widget settings.
  • Widget settings: your texts, goal, button link, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last two numbers so the total shows at once on the next page. Your dashboard browser keeps the required hours you typed.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors or submissions.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and shows no names or emails. Visitors see the total, the number of volunteers and your texts.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix, except the CSV file you choose to download to your own computer. The developer never receives your submissions, your contacts, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
