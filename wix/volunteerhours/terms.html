1. What you get

Volunteer Hours is a Wix app with a site widget that shows the total of a number field (such as volunteer hours) across your form's submissions, and a dashboard page listing who logged how many hours. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan adds up one form, as a big number or a goal bar with the number of volunteers and a button, shows the top five people in the dashboard, and has a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds up several forms, adds hours from paper sheets, counts from a start date you pick, adds the circle style, shows everyone in the dashboard with the hours each person still needs, downloads the list as CSV, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

The total is only as right as the hours people send. Check entries that look wrong in your Forms submissions; the app leaves out single entries above 1,000 and anything that is not a positive number. Tell the people who use your form how their hours are shown.
