Privacy Policy: Bookings Setup: Backup

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Bookings Setup: Backup app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where it reads your booking setup and stores dated copies, which you download as a ZIP file and a JSON snapshot.

Data the app handles

  • Services: the service id, name, type, description, duration, capacity, price and currency, online booking setting, booking policy, staff members, locations, add-ons, form field count and the dates the service was created and updated are written to a snapshot in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Staff: the staff member id, name, e-mail, phone, role and description, as configured on your site.
  • Booking policies and resources: their names and plain settings.
  • Availability: the bookable windows for each service over the next eight weeks, on the paid plan.
  • Settings: the time of the last backup and the automatic-copy state are stored on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The setup is read when you press Back up now, or when the app checks whether an automatic copy is due. The copies stay inside the app on your site; the app sends them nowhere, keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Media files

Service photos and other media files are not read or stored. The app keeps the text and settings of your booking setup only.

Your choices

You can delete a backup from the app at any time, and removing the app removes its stored copies from your site. You can write to prometheus.maker@proton.me to ask what the app holds for your site.

Changes

This policy may be updated with the app; the effective date above shows the current version.
