Common questions

The page says it could not read my setup

The app reads the booking setup with your signed-in session. Reload the dashboard page and try again. If the message stays, write to the address above with your site name and the time you tried.

How much does the free plan back up

The free plan covers your first three services and keeps the two most recent copies. The page shows how many services, staff members, booking policies and resources your site has before you take a copy.

What is in the ZIP

A services spreadsheet with one row per service (names, durations, prices, add-ons, staff), and on Premium the staff, policy and resource spreadsheets plus eight weeks of availability. Every ZIP also holds the versioned JSON snapshot, the file to keep for a move to another site.

Are service photos included

No. The app keeps the text and settings of your booking setup; photos and other media files are not included, and the page says so.

The automatic copy did not run

Apps cannot run a timer. The automatic copy is taken the first time you open the page after seven days, on Premium, and the page shows the time of the last copy.
