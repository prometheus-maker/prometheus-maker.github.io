What the app does

The app reads the booking setup of your site — services, staff members, booking policies, resources and availability windows — and stores dated copies in your site's dashboard, which you download as a ZIP file and a JSON snapshot.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the data the app backs up. The app stores the copies on your site. You are responsible for keeping the downloaded files safe and for having the right to read the booking setup on your site.

Plans

The free plan backs up your first three services, keeps two copies and includes the spreadsheet and the snapshot file. Premium backs up every service, all staff members, booking policies, resources and eight weeks of availability per service, keeps 12 months of history and takes an automatic copy on the first page open after a week. Premium is billed by the platform at $6 a month, or $5 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.
