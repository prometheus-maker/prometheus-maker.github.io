Common questions

The board is empty after I add a property

Press Refresh calendars. The app fetches the calendar link when you refresh, not in the background. A calendar with bookings in the past or more than 14 days ahead shows no jobs for this board; jobs appear on the day the clean has to happen.

My calendar link is refused

The link must be a plain calendar address that returns an iCalendar file, usually ending in .ics, from a public host. Password-protected or private links cannot be read.

A job does not appear

A job is made from a checkout that is followed by another booking in the same property. If there is no next booking, there is no turnover to clean for.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
