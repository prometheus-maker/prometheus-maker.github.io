This policy describes what the Turnover: Cleaner Schedule app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you record your properties and cleaners, fetch each property's booking calendar and see the cleaning jobs that follow from the bookings.

Data the app handles

  • Properties: the property name, the calendar link you paste and the stay dates the app reads from that calendar are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Cleaners: the cleaner name and the optional email address you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Marks: the day a job was marked done and the time it was marked are stored on your site.
  • Calendar: the app's backend fetches the calendar link you save in order to read the stays. It sends nothing to that calendar host except the request for the calendar itself.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a property removes its stored stays. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
