Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the hours and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the hours box?

Installing the app adds it to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Business Hours.

Where do the hours come from?

If your Business Info has opening hours, the widget uses them and follows every change you make there. If it has none, click the widget in the editor, then Settings, and type your hours per day, like 09:00-17:00. A day left empty shows as closed.

How do I enter a lunch break or a late night?

Separate two times with a comma: 09:00-12:00, 13:00-17:00. A closing time after midnight works too: 18:00-02:00 shows as open until 2 AM.

Open now shows the wrong answer

The widget uses your site's time zone, set in your site settings. If that is wrong, the status is off by the difference. Fix the time zone there and reload the page.

How do I show a holiday?

On Premium, special hours you save in Business Info show on their dates, or type them in Settings under Special days, one per line: 2026-12-25 closed Christmas Day.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The week table and the open or closed line keep working. Special days, the badge and today-only layouts, other languages, the clock choice and your own status words switch off.

Privacy
