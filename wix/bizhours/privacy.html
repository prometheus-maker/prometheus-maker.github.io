This policy describes what the Business Hours app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a box to pages of your Wix site that shows your opening hours and whether you are open right now.

Data the app handles

  • Opening hours: the app's backend on Wix reads the opening hours, special hours and time zone saved in your site's Business Info, plus the site's language and country (to pick a 12 or 24-hour clock). These are the hours you already show to the public; nothing else from Business Info is read or shown.
  • Widget settings: hours you type in, special days, your text, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last hours it received from the backend, so the box shows the right status at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows your hours and the current status.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your hours, your settings or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
