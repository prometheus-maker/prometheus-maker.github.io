These terms cover your use of the Store Backup: Auto Export app ("the app") from the Wix App Market.

What you get

The app writes your store's products, collections and orders into a ZIP you download from your dashboard, plus a README that states the window covered. The free plan covers the last 30 days of orders and all products and collections. Premium ($6 per month, or $5 per month billed yearly) covers 12 months of orders and can e-mail the ZIP weekly to up to three addresses.

What the app does not do

The app reads your store data; it does not change, restore or delete anything in it, and it is not a restore tool or a guarantee of data recovery. Keep your downloads somewhere safe: the e-mail link stops working after a week.

Billing and cancellation

Billing is handled by Wix as part of your site's plan. Cancel Premium at any time in your Wix account; the free plan keeps working.

Limits

One backup holds up to 5,000 products and 5,000 orders; the README says when that cap was hit.
