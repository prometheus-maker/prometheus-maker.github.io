Privacy Policy: Store Backup: Auto Export

Effective date: 2026-11-18

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Store Backup: Auto Export app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard that makes a dated ZIP of your store: products, collections, orders, and a README that says what the backup covers. You download the file yourself; on Premium the app can also e-mail it on a weekly rhythm.

Data the app handles

  • Products and collections: the app's backend reads name, variants, stock, price and image links so they can go into products.csv and collections.csv.
  • Orders: the backend reads the orders inside the backup window (30 days on Free, 12 months on Premium), including buyer details, options and refunds, because a store backup that leaves them out does not restore anything.
  • E-mail addresses: the addresses you add for the weekly mail are stored in the app's data collection on your Wix site and used only to send that mail.
  • Plan status: the dashboard asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.

The backup file goes from the app to your browser and nowhere else, except the weekly mail (sent through Wix's own e-mail service to the addresses you entered). The app keeps no logs of visitors and shares no data with third parties.

Your choices

Remove an e-mail address in the dashboard at any time; removing the app removes its data from your site. Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with any question about this policy.
