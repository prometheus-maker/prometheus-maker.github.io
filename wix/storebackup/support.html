Support: Store Backup: Auto Export

The Store Backup: Auto Export app makes a dated ZIP of your store's products, collections and orders, and can e-mail it weekly on Premium.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Store Backup" in your Wix dashboard. Press "Download backup" and the ZIP downloads at once: README.txt, products.csv, collections.csv and orders.csv. Open the README first; it states the window covered and the row counts.

Questions people ask

  • What is in the ZIP? Products with their variants, stock and image links; collections; orders with buyer details, options and refunds; and a README describing the backup.
  • How far back do orders go? The last 30 days on the free plan, 12 months on Premium.
  • How do I get the weekly e-mail? On Premium, add up to three addresses in the weekly card, switch it on and Save. Store apps have no background timer, so the mail goes out with the first order after seven days, or when you open the app.
  • The link in the e-mail stopped working. The link expires after a week; download a fresh backup from the dashboard.
  • My store has no orders yet. The ZIP then holds the README, products.csv and collections.csv, which is expected.
  • How big can a backup be? Up to 5,000 products and 5,000 orders; the README says when that cap was hit.

Something wrong

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with the date of the backup, what you expected and what you saw. We answer within two working days.
