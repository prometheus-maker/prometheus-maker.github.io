1. What you get

Enrollment is a Wix app that puts your school's programs on your site as a list of cards. Each card shows the age range, term dates, the fee note and places left, with an Apply button. You install it from the Wix App Market and keep the programs in the app's dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one program on your site, with the spots number you type and an Apply button that opens a page or email address you set. The paid plan shows every program, counts places down from the applications you approve, marks a program Full and keeps a waitlist in joining order, adds a built-in application form with up to two documents, and lists applications with statuses and a CSV download. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. What the app does not do

The app does not take payments, check eligibility, rank applicants or make admission decisions. Approving an application marks it approved, counts one place and, on the paid plan, files the guardian as a contact with the label "Enrollment applicant". The number of places left is only as correct as the applications you approve and decline, so keep those up to date. A form that cannot be saved (for example Wix is slow or a request fails) shows an error and the family can try again.

4. Your data

Programs, applications, waitlist entries and settings are kept in your own site's Wix collections, and uploaded documents in your Media Manager. See the Privacy Policy: https://prometheus-maker.github.io/wix/enrollment/privacy.html
