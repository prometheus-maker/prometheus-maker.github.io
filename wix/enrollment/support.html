Support: Enrollment: School Admissions

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what you saw. A screenshot helps.

Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I add a program?

In your Wix dashboard open Apps > Enrollment > Programs and press + Add a program. Fill in the title, grade band, ages, start and end dates, places and a fee note such as "$640 a month". Save. The widget on your site lists it in the order shown there; use the up and down arrows to reorder.

Why does only one program show on my site?

The free plan shows the first program in your list. Premium shows every program. The first program is the one at the top of the Programs tab.

How are places left counted?

On Premium, places left is the program's number of places minus the applications you approved. Approve or decline applications on the Applications tab and the number follows. If you type a number in "Spots left" for a program, that number replaces the count and shows as typed.

A program is full. What happens?

The card says Full and its button becomes Join the waitlist, if the waitlist is on in the widget's Settings. Families leave a name and email, and the Waitlist tab shows their place in line, in joining order.

How do I turn on the application form?

Press the widget on your page, open Settings, and turn on Built-in application form. The form asks for the child's name and date of birth, the program, the guardian's name, email, phone, notes and up to two documents of 5 MB each. Applications appear on the Applications tab.

Where do applications and documents go?

Applications are saved in your site's own database and listed on the Applications tab, with a status. Documents are uploaded to your site's Media Manager and linked from each application. On Premium each guardian is also saved as a contact with the label "Enrollment applicant", so you can send confirmations with Wix Automations.

Can I get the applications into a spreadsheet?

Yes. On the Applications tab press Download CSV. It includes the date, program, child, guardian, contact details, notes, document links and status.

Can I add many programs at once?

On the Import tab choose a CSV with a header row. Columns can include title, grade band, age from, age to, start date, end date, places, fee, spots left and photo URL. A file exported from a spreadsheet with "title" and "start date" columns is enough to start.

The Apply button opens the wrong page.

On the free plan, Settings > Applying > Application page link sets where Apply goes: a page on your site such as /admissions-form, or mailto:you@yourschool.org. On Premium with the built-in form on, the form opens instead and that link is not used.

What is the small "Made with Enrollment" line?

It is the free plan's credit link. Premium removes it.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. In the widget's Settings, under Look, set the accent, card and text colours to match your site. Choose round, slightly round or square corners, and turn the fee note on or off.

How do I remove the app?

Uninstall it in your Wix dashboard under Apps. Your collections and uploaded documents stay in your site until you delete them.
