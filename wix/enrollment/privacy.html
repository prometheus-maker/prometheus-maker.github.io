This policy describes what the Enrollment app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app shows a school's programs on its Wix site as a list of cards with the age range, term dates, fee and places left, and an Apply button. The site owner keeps the programs in the app's dashboard. On the paid plan the app also takes applications with a form on the site, keeps a waitlist for programs that are full, and files each guardian as a contact in the site owner's own Wix account.

Data the app handles

  • Programs: title, grade band, age range, start and end dates, places, a fee note, a photo and the order they show in. Stored in a collection in your site's own database (CMS).
  • Applications (paid plan): the child's name, date of birth and chosen program; the guardian's name, email, phone and notes; up to two documents the guardian uploads (5 MB each); the time, the status (new, approved or declined) and the ID of the matching Wix contact. Stored in a collection in your site's CMS. The uploaded documents are stored in your site's Media Manager; the application item holds their links.
  • A rate-limit key for each application: the visitor's IP address hashed with a secret and cut short, so the stored value cannot be turned back into the address. It is used only to limit repeated submissions.
  • Waitlist (paid plan): name, email, chosen program, time of joining, place in line and status. Stored in a collection in your site's CMS.
  • Contacts (paid plan): the guardian's name and email are saved as a contact in your site's Wix Contacts, with the label "Enrollment applicant". Only in your own Wix account.
  • Settings: which programs are seeded, a signed copy of your plan status with its expiry time, and the ID of the contact label the app created. Stored as one item in your site's CMS.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.

Who is responsible for the data

A school's site owner decides what to collect and why. The data stays in that site's Wix account. The developer cannot see it and holds no copy. If you are a parent or guardian, ask the school about the application you sent.

Data the app does not collect

The app has no servers or database of its own; its pages and checks run on Wix's platform. It does not send data to the developer or any third party. It uses no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts and sets no cookies on visitors.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data. The only parties involved are you, your site's visitors and Wix. Wix processes data under its own privacy policy (https://www.wix.com/about/privacy). Payments for the paid plan are handled by Wix; the developer does not receive card details.

Children

Application forms carry a child's name and date of birth because a school needs them to consider an application. That information is entered by the guardian and stored only in the school's own account. The app is sold to schools and other organisations, not to children.

Retention and deletion

Programs, applications, waitlist entries and settings stay in your site's collections until you delete them in the app's dashboard or the CMS, or uninstall the app. Uploaded documents stay in your Media Manager until you delete them there. The developer holds nothing, so there is nothing for the developer to delete.

Changes

If the app changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated before the change is released and the effective date will change.
