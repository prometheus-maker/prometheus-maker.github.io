What it does

Enrollment puts your school's programs on your site as a clear list of classes. Each card shows the grade band, the ages it takes, the term dates, the fee and how many places are left, with an Apply button. Families can see what is open and what is full without emailing to ask.

You keep the programs in the dashboard: title, grade band, ages, start and end dates, places, fee note and an optional photo, in the order families should see them. A new site starts with sample programs, so the widget looks finished the moment it is added; edit them or delete them.

The free plan shows your first program, with the spots number you type and an Apply button that opens your own form page or an email address. Premium lists every program and counts places down as you approve applications. A program with no places left says Full and offers a waitlist in the order families joined.

Premium also adds a built-in application form with the child's details, the guardian's contact, notes and up to two documents. Each guardian is filed in your contacts with a label, so an automation can send the confirmation email. Applications appear in the dashboard with a status, and the list downloads as a CSV.
