Support: Timegrid

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the timetable and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds a sample week to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another timetable, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Timegrid. Each widget has its own classes.

How do I change the classes?

Click the widget in the editor, then Settings. Pick a class at the top, then edit its day, start and end time, name and teacher. Use Add, Copy and Remove for more or fewer classes.

Can I paste my whole week at once?

Yes. In Settings open "Edit all as text" and write one class per line: day | start-end | class | teacher, for example "Mon | 18:00-19:00 | Yoga | Anna". Times like 6pm or 18.30 work too. Click Apply.

Does it take bookings?

No. Timegrid shows your timetable. On Premium each class can link to the page where people book or sign up: your booking page, a form, WhatsApp or email.

Why 12-hour or 24-hour times?

By default times follow the language of your site. Pick 12-hour or 24-hour in Settings to choose yourself.

How does it look on phones?

Phones and narrow boxes show the week day by day, with the time on the left of each class. Premium can show day tabs instead.

Why only 8 classes?

The free plan shows up to 8 classes per widget. Premium has no limit.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working. The first 8 classes show; type colours, the filter, the Today mark, day tabs, class links and the week start switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget shows your text and collects nothing from visitors.
