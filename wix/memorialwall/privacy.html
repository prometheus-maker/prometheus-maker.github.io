Privacy Policy: Memorial Wall: Light a Candle

Effective date: 2026-11-13

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Memorial Wall: Light a Candle app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a memorial to a page of your Wix site: a photo, a name, dates and a few words that you write, a "Light a candle" button with a running count, and memories that visitors share and you approve.

Data the app handles

  • Memorials: what you type in the app's dashboard (name, dates, photo link, text). Stored in your own site's CMS, in collections the app creates.
  • Memories: what a visitor types in the form (their name, how they knew the person, the memory, and an email address only if they choose to give one). Stored in your site's CMS. The email is shown only to you in the dashboard and is never shown on the site.
  • Candles: one count per memorial per day. To allow one candle per visitor per day, the app keeps a one-way code made from the visitor's IP address, that day's date and a secret key. The code changes every day, cannot be turned back into an IP address and is shown to nobody.
  • Limits: to stop spam, the same kind of one-way code counts how many memories a visitor sent in the current hour (at most 3).
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan and keeps the answer for up to 6 hours.

Site visitors

The widget sets no cookies, does not track visitors and loads no third-party scripts.

Where data lives

Everything above is stored in your own site's CMS collections. The app's backend reads and writes them only to show the memorial and save candles and memories. It keeps no other copy.

Deleting data

You can delete any memory or memorial in the app's dashboard at any time, for example when a visitor asks you to remove what they wrote (see your site's own privacy page for how you handle such requests). Uninstalling the app stops all processing; the collections stay in your CMS until you delete them.

Changes
