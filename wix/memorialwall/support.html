Support: Memorial Wall: Light a Candle

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and the page with the memorial. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do I add the person's details?

In the app's dashboard (Apps, then Memorial Wall): Add memorial, then the name, dates, photo and a few words. The widget on your site shows it at once.

Where is the widget?

It is added to your site when you install the app. To place it on another page, open the Editor, click Add Apps, then Manage Apps, and add Memorial Wall. Publish the site to show it to visitors.

Why don't new memories show on the site?

Each memory waits in the dashboard under "Waiting for approval" until you press Approve. To show them at once, edit the memorial and switch on "Show new memories without my approval".

How do I show a different person on each page?

On Premium, add a memorial per person. Then either pick the memorial in the widget's Settings, or leave it on Automatic and give the page an address ending in the memorial's slug, for example /obituaries/eleanor-whitcombe. A dynamic obituary page works the same way.

Someone asked me to remove what they wrote. How?

Open the dashboard, find the memory under On the wall, and press Delete. It is gone from the site and your CMS at once.

How do I make the keepsake book?

Premium: in the dashboard, under Keepsake book, pick the memorial and press the PDF button. It holds the photo, dates, candle count and every approved memory, oldest first.

What is a remembrance wall?

A Premium memorial kind for services such as a December remembrance evening: visitors add the name of someone they miss and light a candle, and the names show as a list.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: memorials and memories are stored in your own site's CMS; visitors are never tracked; emails are optional and only you see them.
