1. What you get

Program Enroll is a Wix app that adds people to your online programs after an event registration or check-in, a paid store order, or in bulk from a contact label or a list of emails. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan runs one rule, adds up to 25 people at a time and shows the last 20 actions. The paid plan runs any number of rules, adds any number of people, removes buyers on a full refund and keeps the full history with a CSV download. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. Your responsibilities

You choose who is added to which program. Make sure you are allowed to give those people access, and check your rules before you switch them on. Your site's own Online Programs plan limits how many active participants it can have; the app can't add people beyond that limit.
