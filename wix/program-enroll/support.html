Support: Program Enroll

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the rule or the group you used, and what you saw. A screenshot of the Activity tab helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I start?

Open the app, press Add rule, choose what happens (someone registers for an event, checks in, or buys a store product), tick the events or products, choose the program and press Save rules.

Who counts as registered?

Guests who RSVP yes or get a ticket. RSVP no and waitlisted guests are not added; a waitlisted guest is added when they get a spot.

When are buyers added?

When the order is paid. For offline payments, that is when you mark the order as paid.

Someone shows "Waits for sign-up"

Online programs need a member account. The person is added automatically as soon as they create one with the same contact.

Someone shows "Failed: participant limit reached"

Your site's Online Programs plan allows a set number of active participants. Upgrade the site plan or remove inactive participants; the next time that person's event arrives they are tried again, or add them with Add people.

Can I add a past event's guests?

Yes. Events gives each event's guests a contact label. In Add people, choose that label and the program.

Does a refund remove the person? (Premium)

Tick "Remove them from the program when the order is fully refunded" on a store rule. Partial refunds leave them in.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then Program Enroll.

Privacy
