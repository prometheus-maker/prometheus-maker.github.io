The service

The app reads the portfolio projects and items of your site, keeps dated copies in the app's own storage and gives you CSV files and, on the paid plan, a full JSON copy plus a weekly automatic backup.

Your responsibilities

  • You keep your site's portfolio as the source of truth; the backup is a copy the app keeps for you.
  • The app does not restore projects into your portfolio: the portfolio service has no restore call, so rebuilding is done by hand from the CSV or JSON.
  • You use the app for data you have the right to read and store.

Plans and billing

The app is provided as it is. It is not a disaster-recovery service: take your own backups as well. The developer is not liable for lost data or lost revenue, to the extent the law allows.
