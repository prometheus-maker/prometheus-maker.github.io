Privacy Policy: Portfolio Backup: CSV Export

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Portfolio Backup: CSV Export app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where it reads your site's portfolio projects and their items with the portfolio service and keeps dated backups you can download as CSV and, on the paid plan, as a JSON file.

Data the app handles

  • Projects: the app reads each project's id, title, description, detail fields, slug, hidden state, cover image reference and collection ids, and stores the backup it makes in the app's own data collections on your site.
  • Items: the app reads every project item's id, title, description, type and image reference, and stores them in the same backup.
  • Backups and settings: the backups, their dates and your settings are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Removing a backup deletes its stored rows. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
