Support: Free Shipping Bar

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the bar and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the bar?

Installing the app adds the bar to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, for example on product pages or the cart page, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Free Shipping Bar.

Where do I set the amount?

Click the bar in the editor, then Settings, and type it under "Free shipping from". Use the same amount as your free shipping rule in your store's shipping settings: the bar shows the message, your shipping rule gives the free shipping.

The editor shows a half-full bar

That is a sample cart so you can see the look. On your live site each shopper sees their own cart.

Which items count?

The cart subtotal after item discounts. Items that don't ship, such as digital downloads and gift cards, are left out.

Does it update without a reload?

Yes. The bar follows the site's own cart: adding, removing or changing the quantity of an item updates it.

Shoppers pay in another currency

The amount left is shown in the shopper's currency. Type the goal in your store's main currency.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The bar keeps working with one goal and your messages. The second goal, dates, hiding on an empty cart, the strip style and your own background and text colours switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the bar reads only the shopper's cart total in their own browser and sends nothing to the developer.
