This policy describes what the Free Shipping Bar app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a bar to pages of your Wix site that tells each shopper how much more they need to add to their cart to get free shipping.

Data the app handles

  • The shopper's cart: the widget runs in the shopper's own browser and asks the Wix eCommerce service for that shopper's current cart. It uses only the cart subtotal, the currency, the number of items and whether each item ships. It does not read names, addresses, e-mail addresses or payment details, and it sends nothing about the cart to the developer.
  • Widget settings: your free shipping amount, messages, colours, font, dates and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status and currency: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and which currency your store uses. The backend asks Wix and answers with those facts and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the shopper's browser keeps the last cart total it saw for the current browsing session, and the plan and currency, so the bar shows the right line at once on the next page.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows an amount and a progress bar. If you turn on the "View cart" link, clicking it opens your site's own cart.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your shoppers' carts or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
