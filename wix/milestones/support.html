Support: Milestones

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds an "Our story so far" section with four sample milestones to the page you were on. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Milestones. Each widget has its own milestones.

How do I change the milestones?

Click the widget in the editor, then Settings. Pick a milestone in the list and edit its date, title, big number and text. The buttons under the list add a milestone, move it up or down, or remove it. The widget changes as you type.

How should I write the date?

Any way you like: 2014, March 2021, Spring 2025 or Q3. The widget shows it exactly as written and keeps the order of your list.

What is the big number?

An optional figure shown large above the title, like 10,000 or 40 cafés. Leave it empty for milestones without one.

Why do my side-by-side milestones stack on phones?

Several columns of text don't fit on a phone screen, so in narrow boxes the milestones always sit one below the other, with the line running down the side. The zig-zag layout does the same.

Can I remove the heading or the line under it?

Yes. Clear the field in Settings and it disappears.

What is a planned milestone?

A milestone that is still to come, like a shop opening next spring. It gets a hollow dot and a small "Planned" tag (you can change the word). It is part of Premium.

Is it accessible?

The milestones are a list of ordinary page text with a heading for each one, so screen readers announce them in order. The scroll animation switches off for visitors who ask their system for reduced motion.

Why can I add only 5 milestones?

The free plan shows up to 5 milestones per timeline. Premium allows 30.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working. The first 5 milestones stay; the zig-zag layout, icons, links, planned tags, the extra line styles and the scroll animation switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget shows your milestones and collects nothing from visitors.
