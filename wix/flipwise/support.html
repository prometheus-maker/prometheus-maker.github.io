Support: Flipwise

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the cards and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where are the cards?

Installing the app adds three sample cards to the page you were on, in their own section. To add more, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Flipwise. Each widget has its own cards.

How do I change the text?

Click the cards in the editor, then Settings. Pick a card at the top, then edit its front (icon, small line, title) and its back (text, button text, button link).

The cards don't flip in the editor

The editor is for editing, so pointing at a card there may not turn it. Open Preview or the live site and point at a card, or tap it on a phone.

How do visitors on phones flip a card?

They tap it. There is no hover on phones. On narrow screens the cards sit in one row that visitors can swipe.

Can I use a photo on the front?

Yes, on Premium. Copy the image address (it starts with https://), for example from your Media Manager, and paste it into "Image instead of colour".

Why only 3 cards?

The free plan shows up to 3 cards per widget. Premium allows 12.

Can it use my site's font?

Yes, that's the default. Use Change font in Settings to pick a different one, and "Use site font" to go back.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The cards keep working. The first 3 cards show with the shared colours and a sideways flip; image fronts, card colours, 3D and auto-flip switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the cards show your text and collect nothing.
