What it does

A row of plain boxes says the same thing a flip card does, but nobody stops to read it. Flipwise puts flip cards on any page of your site: a bold front with an icon and a title, and a back with your text and a button.

Visitors point at a card and it turns over. On phones there is no hover, so a tap turns it, and the cards sit in a row you can swipe. Keyboard users can flip a card too. People who ask their device for less motion get a soft fade instead of a turn.

Click the cards in the editor, open Settings and write the front and back of each one. Link the button to a page, an email or a phone number. Pick the colours for the front, the back and the button, round or square corners, and your font. The editor shows every change at once.

New cards start with sample text and a clean dark look, so the page never shows an empty box. Both sides stay in the page for search engines and screen readers. The script is small and loads after your page.

The free plan gives you 3 cards with no branding. Premium adds up to 12 cards, image fronts, a colour per card, top-to-bottom and 3D flips, cards that turn by themselves, and a fixed number of cards per row.
