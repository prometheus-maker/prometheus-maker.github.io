Support: Gift Finder: AI Product Finder

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, what the shopper typed and what came back. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the search box?

It is added to your site when you install the app. To place it on another page, open the Editor, click Add Apps, then Manage Apps, and add Gift Finder. Publish the site to show it to shoppers.

Which products can it show?

Your visible, in-stock Wix Stores products. Hidden and sold-out products are left out. It reads them fresh for every search, so new products show up at once.

What is a smart search?

A search where the AI model reads the shopper's words and picks the best fitting products from your shop. The free plan has 30 a month, Premium 1,500. After that, and if a shopper searches more than 10 times in an hour, the box shows plain word matches instead.

Can it suggest something I don't sell?

No. The model only sees a numbered list of your own products, and every pick is checked against your catalog before it is shown.

How do I change the look?

Click the widget in the Editor, then Settings: heading, line under it, hint text, button text and colour, example searches and font.

Where do I see what shoppers searched?

In the app's dashboard: this month's count and the last searches. On Premium it also lists what shoppers asked for that your shop doesn't have.

Do you also have a chat box for questions?

Yes, our separate AI Chatbot app answers questions about shipping, opening hours and your products in a chat bubble.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: shoppers' search words and your product shortlist go to the AI provider to pick products; the last 200 searches are kept as text, with no names, emails or IP addresses.
