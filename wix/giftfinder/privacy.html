This policy describes what the Gift Finder: AI Product Finder app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a search box to a page of your Wix site. A shopper describes who a gift is for, and the app shows matching products from your Wix Stores catalog.

Data the app handles

  • Store products: for each search the app's backend reads your visible, in-stock store products (name, price, sale price, main image, product page link and the first 160 characters of the description). It does not keep a copy.
  • Searches: the words a shopper types, the "for" and budget buttons they choose, your product shortlist (names, prices, short descriptions) and the page language are sent to the AI provider to pick products. The AI provider is DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com).
  • Search log: the last 200 searches per site are kept as text on the app's backend storage, with the time, the number of results and, if the AI noted it, what the shopper asked for that your shop does not have. You see them in the app's dashboard. Names, emails and IP addresses are not logged. Shoppers should not type personal details into the box.
  • Limits: to enforce search limits the backend counts searches per site per month, and per shopper per hour. The per-shopper count uses a one-way hash of the shopper's IP address (or the anonymous Wix visitor id), is kept for one hour and is never shown to anyone.
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan and keeps the answer for up to 6 hours.

Site visitors

The widget sets no cookies and does not track visitors. It keeps the plan answer in the browser tab's session storage for the visit.

AI provider

DeepSeek is a processor that receives the data above only to pick products. DeepSeek is based in China; see its privacy policy at https://platform.deepseek.com. If your shoppers' searches must stay in a particular region, do not use the app.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app stops all processing. Ask prometheus.maker@proton.me to delete your site's search log and counters; we do it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
