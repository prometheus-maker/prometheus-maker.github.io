1. What you get

Gift Finder: AI Product Finder is a Wix app that adds a gift search box to your site. It shows products from your own Wix Stores catalog, picked by an AI model from the shopper's description.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan includes 30 smart searches per site per calendar month and shows 3 products per search, with a small credit link. The paid plan includes 1,500 smart searches per calendar month, 6 products with a short reason each, who-for and budget buttons, an order-by note, the not-stocked report and no credit link. When a limit is reached, shoppers get plain word matches from your products instead. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.
