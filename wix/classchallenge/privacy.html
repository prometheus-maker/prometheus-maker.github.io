This policy describes what the Leaderboard: Class Challenge app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that ranks classes, teams or groups of a fundraiser or membership drive by the money raised or the number of supporters, using your site's paid orders.

Data the app handles

  • Orders: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's paid eCommerce orders. From each order it uses the product name, the product options and custom text fields (for example "Teacher's name: Ms. Lee"), checkout custom fields, the amount paid, refunds, the currency, the payment date, and the contact ID or e-mail only to count each supporter once.
  • What reaches the page: only group names (for example "Ms. Lee"), the money raised and the number of supporters per group, and the totals. Buyer names, e-mail addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers and contact IDs are never sent to the page.
  • Widget settings: source, group field, goals, hidden or merged group names, colours, texts and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Short-lived memory: the backend keeps the orders it read in memory for up to 60 seconds, so a busy page does not read them again for every visitor. Nothing is written to disk or a database.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last board it saw (group names and totals only) so the board shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

Group names

Group names come from what buyers type or pick at checkout. If a buyer typed something that should not be public, add it to "Leave off the board" in the widget's settings and publish.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your buyers' details or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
