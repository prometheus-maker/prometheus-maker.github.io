What it does

Put a live class leaderboard on your fundraiser or membership drive page. Families pick their child's teacher when they buy, and the board ranks every class by the money raised or by the number of families: "Ms. Lee · $1,240 · 31 supporters". A paid order moves its class up the board within a minute.

It reads the orders your site already takes. Give your membership, ticket or fundraiser product a custom text field such as "Teacher's name" or an option such as "Grade", and the board groups the paid orders by it. Taking donations instead? Each campaign becomes one team. "Mrs. Lee" and "mrs lee" count as one class, and a hide list keeps test orders off the board.

Only class names and totals reach the page, never who bought. The leader gets a crown and a goal bar shows how close the school is. You choose the heading, the word for supporters (families, members, readers), a start date and the button link. Colours and font follow your site.

The free plan shows your top 5 classes as ranked bars. Premium shows up to 30 classes, adds a podium for the top 3, a goal per class, a merge list for other spellings and a last 7 days view, and removes the credit link.
