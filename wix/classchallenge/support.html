Quick answers

Where is the board?

Installing the app adds the board to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Class Challenge.

How does it know the class?

Give your product a custom text field (for example "Teacher's name") or an option (for example "Grade"). Every paid order then counts for the class the buyer typed or picked. In Settings, "Group by" picks that field; Automatic finds a field named like Class, Teacher, Grade or Team. A custom field on the checkout page works too.

I take donations, not store orders

In Settings, set "Count from" to Donation campaigns. Each campaign becomes one team on the board.

The board says "The challenge has just started"

There are no paid orders with the class field yet, or the start date in Settings is later than your orders.

One class shows twice

Case, dots and accents are already ignored ("Mrs. Lee" = "mrs lee"). For other spellings ("Lee" and "Ms. Lee") use the merge list on Premium, or leave one off the board.

Does it show who bought?

No. Only class names and totals reach your page.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The board keeps working with your top 5 classes as ranked bars. The podium, goal per class, merge list and last 7 days view switch off, and the small credit link comes back.
