This policy describes what the Product Enquiry & Q&A app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where customer questions appear, and a public form a customer opens from a product page or a link you share.

Data the app handles

  • Questions: the name, e-mail address, product and question text a customer enters on the public form are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The answer you write in the dashboard is stored there too, together with the question status. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Settings: the shop name, button colour and saved answers you set are stored in the app's settings collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the form asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Products: when a question arrives from a product page, the app reads the product's name and link so the question shows what it is about. Nothing about the product is stored beyond that.

The app keeps no visitor logs, sends no e-mail itself and shares no data with third parties. Your reply opens in your own e-mail program, so the message travels from your own address.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
