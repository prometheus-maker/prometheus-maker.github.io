Support: Product Enquiry & Q&A

The Product Enquiry & Q&A app puts a question form on your product pages and keeps every question in one dashboard page.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Product Enquiry & Q&A" in your dashboard. The inbox starts empty. Open Form links to get the button code for a product, or the shop-wide link for your contact page, and put it where customers will see it.

A customer writes a name, an e-mail address and a question. It appears in the inbox with the product attached. Write your answer and press Answer: your e-mail program opens with the reply filled in, addressed to the customer, so you send it from your own address. Premium also records the status (new, answered, closed) and adds your saved answers to the form.

Questions people ask

  • How do customers find the form? Paste the button code on a product page, or share the shop-wide link anywhere on your site.
  • How many questions do I get? Twenty in each calendar month on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? Unlimited questions, statuses, saved answers shown on the form, and a CSV export of the inbox.
  • Does the app send e-mails? No. Your reply is sent from your own e-mail program.
  • Where is the data? In the app's collections on your site; removing the app removes it.
