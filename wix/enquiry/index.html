What it does

A shopper wants to know whether the vase is dishwasher safe, and there is no phone number to call. So they close the tab. This app puts a question form on any product page and sends what they write to one page in your dashboard.

Open Form links, pick a product from your catalogue, and copy the prepared button. Paste that button code on the product page and the question arrives with the product attached. There is also a shop-wide link for your contact page or footer, for questions that are not about one item.

Each question reaches the inbox with the name, the reply address, the product and the text. Write your answer, press Send reply and the customer gets your reply by e-mail from your own address, so the thread stays in your mailbox.

Premium adds statuses (new, answered, closed), saved answers that appear on the form before a customer writes, and a CSV export of the whole inbox. It also removes the monthly cap.

The free plan takes 20 questions in each calendar month; after that the form tells the customer to e-mail you directly and nothing is lost with them. Premium is unlimited, for $6 per month or $5 per month billed yearly.

The form needs no account and works on mobile.
