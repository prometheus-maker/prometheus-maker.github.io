What the app does

The app keeps contract templates for your rentals and creates a rental contract for each booking: the guest, the property, the dates, the house rules and the deposit. It adds a signing element to your site where the guest reads and signs the contract, and a dashboard where you see every contract, its status and a PDF copy.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the wording of your contracts and to the data you enter. The app stores it on your site. The app is not a party to the contract between you and your guest, and it does not give legal advice. Have your contract wording checked by someone qualified if you rely on it in a dispute.

Plans

The free plan holds one contract template and shows the signing page as a preview; it does not collect signatures. Premium keeps a template for each property, collects signatures and exports signed contracts to CSV. Premium is billed by the platform at $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.
