This policy describes what the Rental Agreement: Contracts app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a dashboard page to your site where you keep contract templates and create a contract for each booking, and a signing element on your site where the guest reads and signs the contract.

Data the app handles

  • Templates: the title, the contract text, the house rules, the deposit amount and currency, and the check-in and check-out times you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Contracts: the guest name and email address, the property, the check-in and check-out dates, the nights, the deposit and the contract number you enter are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Signatures: the signature image the guest draws, the text of the contract as the guest read it, a SHA-256 seal of that text, the date and time, and the browser's user-agent string are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Public element: the signing element shows the contract for the booking named in its link: the guest name, the property, the dates and the contract text. It shows no other booking and no template list.

The app keeps no logs of visitors beyond the values listed above, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. A record is marked for deletion two years after the stay it belongs to. Deleting a contract removes its signature and seal; deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
