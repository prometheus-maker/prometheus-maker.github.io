What it does

Every holiday let and equipment hire needs a contract for each booking: who stays, which property, the dates, the house rules and the deposit. Most owners edit a document by hand for each stay.

This app keeps a template of your wording and creates a contract for each booking. The template holds placeholders for the property, the guest, the dates, the nights, the house rules and the deposit, so one template serves every stay.

You enter the guest, the property and the dates, and the app makes a numbered contract with a signing link. The guest opens the link, reads the filled contract and signs with a finger or the mouse. The signature, the text they read and a SHA-256 seal are stored with the date.

The desk shows every contract as signed, not signed, deposit due or needs re-signing, which appears when you change the wording after a guest signed. Signed contracts export to CSV and open as a PDF with the signature and the seal.

The free plan holds one template and previews the signing page. Premium keeps a template for each property and collects signatures. Premium is $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly.
