Common questions

How do I make a contract for a booking?

Create a template first, under Templates, with your contract text and the placeholders. Then open Bookings, enter the guest, the property, the check-in and check-out dates, and press Create contract. The app makes a numbered contract and a signing link to copy and send.

Where do I set the signing page?

On the Bookings tab, type the address of the page on your site that holds the signing block, for example https://your-site.com/agreement. Save it once; every link uses it.

The guest cannot sign

Signing needs the Premium plan. On the free plan the page shows the contract as a preview. Also check that the link is complete and that the contract has not been signed already.

Why does a contract ask for a new signature?

You changed the wording of its template after the guest signed. The app keeps the version the guest agreed to; open Contracts, find the contract listed for a re-sign, copy the re-sign message and send it to the guest.

Where is the PDF?

On the Contracts tab, press PDF next to a contract. The free plan gives a watermarked sample; Premium gives the signed contract with the signature, the dates and the seal.
