This policy describes what the RFQ: Store Quote Requests app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a quote request form to your site. A visitor sets quantities for the products you list, leaves a name, an email address and an optional note, and the request is stored for you to read and answer.

Data the app handles

  • Products: the name, unit and optional guide price you enter in the editor are stored in the app's settings on your site and shown in the form.
  • Requests: the reference, the visitor's name, email address, note, the lines and quantities and the estimated total are stored in the app's data collection on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Email: when a request arrives the app asks the platform to send one notification to the store owner's address set on the site. The visitor's email address is included so you can answer.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sets no cookies of its own and shares no data with third parties. It does not handle payments.

Keeping and removing data

Requests stay on your site until you delete them or remove the app. Removing the app removes its collections and everything in them.
