Common questions

The form shows no products

Open the widget's settings in the editor and add each product with a name, a unit and an optional guide price. The form shows what you save there.

A visitor says the request did not arrive

Check the request list in the dashboard first; every request is stored there even if the email notification failed. If the list is empty, the visitor may have pressed Send before the page finished loading.

The guide total looks wrong

The total is quantity times the price you entered, summed across the lines. Change the price in the editor and the form updates. The total is a guide; you send the final quote yourself.

I reached the daily limit

The free plan accepts five requests per site per day. Premium removes the limit and allows up to twenty products.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
