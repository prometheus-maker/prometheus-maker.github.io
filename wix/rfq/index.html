What it does

Stores that sell to businesses often reply to every inquiry by email: the customer asks for a price on 200 units, the owner checks stock and sends a number, and the thread is lost by next week. This app puts a quote request form on your site and keeps every request in one list.

In the editor, list the products you quote with a name, a unit and an optional guide price. On your page, the visitor sets quantities and sees a guide total while choosing, then leaves a name, an email and an optional note and sends the request. The visitor does not pay and no order is created, so your checkout stays untouched.

Each request appears in the dashboard with its reference, customer, total and date, newest first. Open one to see the lines, the note and the contact address, and reply by email from the same screen.

The free plan covers five products, five requests a day and the request list. Premium raises the list to twenty products, removes the daily cap and adds status tracking, so a trade counter can follow every quote from new to answered.

Premium is $6 a month, or $4.83 a month billed yearly, with a 14-day free trial.
