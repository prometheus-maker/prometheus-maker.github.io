What it does

Membership Card gives every member of your site a card for the plan they bought from you. A member logs in and sees their plan name, a green Active chip, the month they joined, the date the plan renews or ends, and a short card number. It looks like a wallet card, easy to show at the desk.

Nothing to type: the card reads the member's own orders in Pricing Plans. When a plan renews, is paused or ends, the card changes with it. Each member only ever sees their own card. Visitors who are not logged in see a short prompt and a link to your plans page, and a member without a plan gets the same link.

You pick the title, card colour, style, corners and font in the editor, and every word on the card can be changed for sites in other languages.

Premium adds a QR code to each card. Staff scan it with a phone that is logged in to your dashboard, or type the card number, and the app's check page says at once whether the membership is active today, with the member's name and plan. Premium also shows member photos and your logo instead of the title, and removes the small credit link that the free plan shows under the card.

Made for gyms, studios, spas and clubs.
