What it does

Membership Card gives every member a card for their plan. A member logs in and sees the plan name, a green Active chip, the month they joined, when it renews or ends, and a short card number, like a wallet card to show at the desk.

Nothing to type: it reads the member's own Pricing Plans orders and follows a renewal, a pause or an end. Each member sees only their own card; logged-out visitors get a short prompt and a link to your plans page.

You pick the title, colour, style, corners and font in the editor; every word on the card can be changed.

Premium adds a QR code staff scan with a phone logged in to your dashboard, or they type the card number: the check page says at once whether the membership is active today, with name and plan. Premium also shows member photos and your logo and removes the credit link.

Pro adds member self-renewal: a member whose plan ends soon sees "Ends in 5 days - Renew now" under their card, so they renew before it lapses. The Renewals tab lists members ending in 14 days and those who lapsed in the last 30, with ready-to-send messages and CSV. Pro also keeps the last 500 staff checks and a card design per plan.

For gyms, studios, spas and clubs.
