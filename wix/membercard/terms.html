1. What you get

Membership Card is a Wix app with a site widget that shows each logged-in member a card for their own Pricing Plans membership, and a dashboard page for checking cards. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows the card with the plan name, status, member since, renewal or end date and card number, with your title, card colour, style, corners and font, and a small credit link. The paid plan adds a QR code on every card, the staff check page, member photos and your logo, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
