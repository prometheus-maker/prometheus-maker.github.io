This policy describes what the Membership Card app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows each logged-in member a card for their own pricing plan, and a dashboard page where your staff can check whether a card is active.

Data the app handles

  • The logged-in member's own membership: when a member opens a page with the card, the app's backend on Wix learns who is asking from the member's Wix session (never from anything the browser sends), reads that one member's Pricing Plans orders (plan name, status, start, end and renewal dates, order ID) and their name and profile photo from Members, and sends the card back to that member's browser only. Other members' data is never sent to a visitor. The app never reads or shows email addresses.
  • Staff check page: in your dashboard, the check page looks up the order behind a card number or QR code and shows the member's name, plan and whether it is active today. Only people with access to your site dashboard can open it.
  • The QR code holds a link to that dashboard page with the order ID. It contains no name, email or other personal detail.
  • Widget settings: title, colours, style, font, words and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan of this app.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of members or visitors. The widget sets no cookies and stores nothing in the browser.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your members, their plans or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
