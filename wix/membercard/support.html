Support: Membership Card

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the card and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the card?

Installing the app adds the card to the page you were on, in its own section. A members-only page or your member area is a good home for it. To add it to another page, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Membership Card.

What do visitors see?

A logged-in member sees their own card. Someone who is not logged in sees "Log in to see your membership card" and a button to your plans page. A member without a plan sees "You have no membership yet" and the same button. In the editor you see a sample card; visitors never see it.

Where do the plan and dates come from?

From the member's orders in Pricing Plans. The card shows the active plan first, then a paused one, then one that starts later, then the last one that ended. Renewals, pauses and cancellations show up by themselves.

Can a member see someone else's card?

No. The app works out who is asking from the member's own login on your site and only ever reads that member's orders.

How does the QR code work? (Premium)

It opens the app's page in your dashboard with that card filled in, so staff need a phone that is logged in to your site dashboard. The page says Active today, Paused, Not started yet or Ended, with the member's name and plan. Staff can also type the 8-character card number instead.

The card is in English, my site is not

Open Settings, then Words on the card, and change any label. Dates follow the language of your site.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The card keeps working with plan, status, dates and card number, your colour, style and font, and a small credit link. The QR code, check page, photos and logo switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: each member only sees their own card, and the app stores nothing.
