1. What you get

Christmas Gift Guide is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your store's products in tabs by price. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows three price tabs with up to eight products each, with your own heading and price limits, and a small credit link under the guide. The paid plan adds a fourth price tab or tabs by store category, up to 24 products per tab, an order-by note, sale badges with old prices, more colour themes and your own accent colour, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

The guide shows the products, prices and photos from your store. Keep them correct, and make sure any order-by date you write matches your real shipping times.
