What it does

Christmas Gift Guide turns your store into a gift guide shoppers can browse by budget. It reads the products you already sell and sorts them into tabs like "Under $50", "Under $100" and "Luxury". Add the widget to a page and it fills itself.

The price bands fit your own prices. A candle shop gets "Under $25" and "Under $50", a watch shop gets "Under $300" and up. If you'd rather pick the limits yourself, type them (25, 50, 100) and the tabs follow. Each card shows the product photo, name and price, and links to its product page. Sold-out products drop out by themselves. New products and price changes show up on their own.

You can change the heading and the line under it, rename the top tab, choose how many gifts each tab shows and pick a font. On phones the cards sit two across and the tabs scroll sideways.

The free plan gives three price tabs with eight gifts each and a small credit link under the guide. Premium adds a fourth price tab or tabs by your store categories (For her, For kids), up to 24 gifts per tab, an order-by note for your last shipping day, sale badges with old prices, three more colour themes, and removes the credit link.
