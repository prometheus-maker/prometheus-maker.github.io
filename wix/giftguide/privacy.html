This policy describes what the Christmas Gift Guide app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a gift guide to pages of your Wix site. It shows your store's products in tabs by price (or by store category).

Data the app handles

  • Your store products: the app's backend asks Wix for the site's visible products and passes on only what the guide shows: each product's name, price, sale price, main image, product page link and the IDs of its categories. Hidden and out-of-stock products are left out. Orders, customers and inventory numbers are not read.
  • Your store categories: their names, for the category tabs.
  • Widget settings: your heading, texts, price limits, theme, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status and currency: the backend also tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan and which currency your store uses, together with the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last answer (plan, currency and the products above) so the guide shows at once on the next page.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows products and their prices. Clicking a product opens its product page on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your products, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
