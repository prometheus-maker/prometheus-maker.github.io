Support: Christmas Gift Guide

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the guide and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the guide?

Installing the app adds the guide to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Christmas Gift Guide.

Which products show?

Every visible product in your store that is in stock. Hidden products and sold-out products are left out, and so are products that are out of stock but open for pre-order, since they may not arrive in time. A product with options shows its lowest price as "From $20".

How are the price tabs chosen?

The guide splits your products into groups of about the same size and puts each limit at a round number just above the group, for example "Under $40", "Under $100", then "Luxury" for the rest. To set the limits yourself, type them in Settings under Price bands, for example 25, 50, 100. On the free plan the first two limits are used (three tabs).

The editor shows a candle and wool socks I don't sell

That is a sample so you can see the look before your store has products. Your live site shows only your own products, and hides the guide when the store has none.

Can I group gifts by who they are for?

Yes, on Premium. Create store categories such as "For her" and "For kids" in your dashboard, add products to them, then choose Tabs: By category in Settings.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The guide keeps working with three price tabs and eight gifts per tab in the pine and gold look. Category tabs, the order-by note, sale badges and the other themes switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the guide reads your products' names, prices, photos and links, and sends nothing to the developer.
