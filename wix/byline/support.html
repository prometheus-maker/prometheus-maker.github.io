Quick answers

How do I add the box?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Byline, and click Add to blog post page. Then publish your site once. You can also add it in the editor: open your blog post page and add the Author Box plugin.

Where do the photo, name and bio come from?

From the writer's member profile: display name, profile photo, title and the text of their About section. For a writer with an empty profile, click the box in the editor, then Settings, pick the writer and type a bio. What you type wins over the profile.

Can a post have more than one author?

Yes, on Premium. In the box's Settings, under Co-authors, pick a post and tick up to three more writers. You can also add writers who are not site members.

Does it help with search engines?

On Premium the box adds schema.org author data (name, title, bio, photo, links) to each post, which search engines read to know who wrote it.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The box keeps showing in the card look with photo, name, title and bio, plus the small credit link.
