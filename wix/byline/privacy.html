This policy describes what the Byline: Author Bio Box app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds an "About the author" box under the posts of your Wix Blog: the writer's photo, name, title and a short bio. A dashboard page lets you add the box to your blog post page and lists your writers.

Data the app handles

  • Blog posts: to show a box, the app's backend asks Wix for the post (its title, link and the member ID of its writer) and the writer's three newest posts.
  • Writer profiles: the backend reads the writer's public member profile (display name, profile photo, title) and the text of their "About" section. A title and About text are not shown when the member's profile is set to private. E-mail addresses, phone numbers and other contact details are never read or passed on.
  • Your settings: the look, heading, colour, and any name, title, bio, photo link, links and co-authors you type for your writers. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the box and its settings panel whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID. This answer is kept in the server's memory for up to ten minutes; writer details for a post are kept there for up to five minutes.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors, keeps no copy of your posts or members and writes nothing to a database.

Site visitors

The box does not set cookies and does not track anyone. It shows the same writer details your blog already shows on the post and the writer's profile page.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your posts, members or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
