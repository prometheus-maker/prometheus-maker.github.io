This policy describes what the Comment Vault: Comments Backup app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where it reads the comments left on your blog and keeps dated copies of them, which you can download as CSV files.

Data the app handles

  • Comments: the comment text, author name, status, reply parent, rating and dates are read from your site through the platform's Comments API and stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Posts: the app reads blog post titles and IDs to label each comment, and stores those titles with the backup rows.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Backups stay on your site until you delete them, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
