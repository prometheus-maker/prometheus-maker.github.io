Common questions

The app shows no comments

It reads the comments on your blog. If your blog has no comments yet, there is nothing to back up. If you use a different comment system on your pages, those comments live outside the app's reach.

The backup left some comments out

The free plan backs up the 50 newest comments. Premium backs up every comment and reply, however old.

A comment is missing from a backup

A comment deleted on your site after a backup stays inside that earlier backup. Press Back up now and the new backup lists it under the comments deleted since the previous backup, so it is not lost without a trace.

The CSV opens with garbled characters

The file is saved in UTF-8 with a byte-order mark, which Excel and Sheets read correctly. If your program asks for an encoding, choose UTF-8.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
