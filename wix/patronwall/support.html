Support: Patron Wall: Fan Supporters

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the wall and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the wall?

Installing the app adds the wall to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Patron Wall.

Where do the names come from?

From your Wix Pricing Plans: every member with an active plan counts. New patrons show by themselves within about five minutes, and patrons whose plan ended or was cancelled leave the wall.

Someone is counted but not named

Only members with a public site profile are named. Members with a private profile still count in the total ("and 12 more patrons").

The wall says "Be our first patron"

No member holds an active plan yet, or the plans you ticked under "Plans that count" have no members. Wix Pricing Plans and Members must be on the site.

I sell a class pass too. Can I leave it out?

Yes. Click the wall in the editor, then Settings, and untick that plan under "Plans that count".

How do I remove one name?

In Settings, add the name as it shows on the wall (for example "Maya L.") to "Hide these names". Publish.

Some of my fans pay me on Patreon. Can they be on the wall?

Yes, on Premium: type them under "Extra names", one per line, optionally with a comma and the plan name ("Chris M., Gold").

Where does the button go?

To /plans-pricing, the page Wix Pricing Plans adds. Change the link in Settings, or clear it to hide the button.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The wall keeps working as one wall of up to 100 names. Sections, the goal bar, photos, years, extra names and cards switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: only first names, last initials and plan names of members with a public profile reach your page.
