What it does

Thank the fans who pay for your work by name, on your own site. Patron Wall reads the members who hold an active paid plan on your site and puts them on a wall: "142 patrons", then their names. A fan joins your fan club at night, and by morning their name is on the page. Cancel, and they leave the wall. Nobody types a list.

The wall is careful with people. Only members whose profile is public are named, as a first name and last initial, never a full name or e-mail. Members with private profiles still count in the total. You can hide any name, show first names or initials only, and leave out plans that are not fan tiers, like a class pass.

Pick a layout: a name cloud or a list. Call them patrons, supporters, members or fans. A "Become a patron" button links to your plans page. Colours, font and card match your site, on phones too.

The free plan shows one wall with up to 100 names and a small credit link. Premium adds a section for each plan (Gold patrons first), a goal bar such as "142 / 200 patrons, help us fund the next album", cards with profile photos, the year each fan joined, typed extra names for fans who support you elsewhere, up to 500 names, and removes the credit link.
