This policy describes what the Patron Wall: Fan Supporters app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that lists the site members who hold an active paid plan from Wix Pricing Plans, so you can thank your patrons in public.

Data the app handles

  • Plan orders: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's active Wix Pricing Plans orders. From each order it uses the buyer's member ID, the plan ID, plan name and plan price, and the start date, to count each patron once and to sort plans by price.
  • Member profiles: for those buyers only, the backend reads the public part of their site member profile (display name and profile photo), and only for members whose status is approved and whose profile is public.
  • What reaches the page: the first word of the display name (up to 24 characters), the first letter of its last word, the plan name, and the number of patrons. On the paid plan also the year the patron started and their public profile photo, which show only if you switch them on. E-mail addresses, full last names, member IDs and contact IDs are never sent to the page. Members with a private profile are only counted.
  • Widget settings: plans that count, name style, hidden names, extra names you type in, colours, texts and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Short-lived memory: the backend keeps the orders and profiles it read in memory for up to 5 minutes, so a busy page does not read them again for every visitor. Nothing is written to disk or a database.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last wall it saw (the same display fields as above) so the names show at once on the next visit.

Your patrons

Only members who made their profile public are named. If a patron asks to be removed, add their name as it shows on the wall to "Hide these names" in the widget's settings and publish. Consider telling fans on your plans page that their first name may appear on your site.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your members' details or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
