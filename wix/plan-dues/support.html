Support: Plan Dues

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the member and plan, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Which memberships show up?

Plans sold offline: orders you made in the Pricing Plans dashboard with "sell offline", members you added with New cash member, and plans given from a request. Plans bought online by card are paid by card and are left out.

How are due dates worked out?

Each billing cycle is due on the day it starts. A monthly plan that started on 31 August is due on 30 September, 30 October and so on, the same dates your pricing plans use. A free trial moves the first due date to the end of the trial.

When is a cycle overdue?

After the number of days you set in Settings (7 by default). Until then it shows as Due.

Why don't I see older unpaid cycles?

Settings has a "Follow dues from" date, set to the first day of last month when you first open the app. Move it earlier to see older cycles. Payments you record are always kept.

What does Pause do?

It pauses the member's plan, so they lose what the plan gives until you resume it. The plan's end date moves later by the paused time.

How do plan requests work? (Premium)

Make a form with name, email, a plan choice and a payment method choice (for example Bank transfer and Cash) and put it on a page. In Settings, pick the form and write your payment instructions. Each answer then shows in Plan requests. Email opens your email program with the instructions filled in. When the money arrives, press Paid: give plan.

The request says "Not a site member yet"

A plan always belongs to a site member. Ask the person to sign up on your site with the same email, then press Check for new requests.

How does auto-pause work? (Premium)

Set the number of days in Settings. When a cycle stays unpaid that long, the membership is paused and listed in Activity. The check runs when you open the app and whenever a billing cycle starts on your site. Each late cycle pauses a membership once; if you resume it by hand, it stays resumed.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Apps, then Plan Dues.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: your records stay in your site's collections, the app uses only Wix's own APIs, and the developer receives nothing.
