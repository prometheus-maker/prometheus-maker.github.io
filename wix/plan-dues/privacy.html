This policy describes what the Plan Dues app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app is a page in your Wix dashboard. It reads the pricing plan orders on your site that were sold offline, lists each billing cycle with its due date and amount, and lets you record the payments you receive in cash, by bank transfer or by cheque. It can pause and resume those orders and give a member a plan. On the paid plan it also reads the answers to one form you choose, so people can ask for a plan and pay offline.

Data the app handles

  • Pricing plan orders: plan, buyer's member ID, start date, billing cycle, price, status and payment status. Used to build the dues list and to pause, resume or create orders when you ask.
  • Members: name and login email, to show who each order belongs to and to find the member who sent a plan request.
  • Form answers (paid plan, the form you choose only): name, email, chosen plan and payment method.
  • Your records, stored in collections on your own site: payments you record (order ID, cycle, due date, amount, payment date, method, reference, note, member name, plan name), the status of each plan request, the app's settings, and an activity log of pauses, resumes and plans given.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro and review boxes, kept in your browser's local storage.

Where the data goes

Everything stays in your site. The app talks only to Wix's own APIs, and its automatic pause check runs as app code hosted by Wix. CSV files you download are made in your browser. "Email instructions" opens your own email program with a draft; the app sends no email. The developer runs no server of its own, never receives, stores or sees your members, orders or payments, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app stops all reading and changes. The payment records, requests, settings and log live in your site's collections until you delete them. Orders you created or paused stay in your pricing plans.
