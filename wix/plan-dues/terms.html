1. What you get

Plan Dues is a Wix app that tracks dues for pricing plans you sell offline: one line per billing cycle, payments you record, an overdue list, pause and resume, and plan requests from a form. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan follows up to 15 offline memberships, records payments with date and amount, lists overdue dues, pauses and resumes memberships and gives plans to members who pay you directly. The paid plan follows every offline membership and adds plan requests from a form, automatic pausing, payment method and reference per cycle, and a CSV export. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. Your responsibilities

The app records what you tell it. You collect the money, check that a payment arrived before you mark it paid, and decide when to pause or resume a membership. You are responsible for handling your members' data lawfully and for the payment instructions you send.
