This policy describes what the E-Invoice: XRechnung & PDF app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard. It reads your store orders, turns an order into a XRechnung 3.0 XML invoice (and, on the paid plan, a PDF with the XML attached and a monthly ZIP), and keeps a record of the invoices you issue.

Data the app handles

  • Order data: the order number, date, currency, line items and totals, and the buyer's name, address, e-mail and VAT ID, as they are already stored by your store. The data is read from your site when you open the page and is never written back to an order.
  • Invoice data: the invoices you issue (number, date, buyer, VAT ID, Leitweg-ID, amounts), your business details (name, address, VAT ID, contact, bank details if you enter them) and saved buyer profiles. These are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks the platform whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and that one fact decides what the page offers.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app sends no e-mail, keeps no visitor logs and shares no data with third parties. Everything it produces is made in your browser and downloaded by you.

Keeping and removing data

Invoice records, settings and saved buyers stay on your site until you delete them or remove the app. Removing the app deletes its collections and everything in them.
