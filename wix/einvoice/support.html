Support: E-Invoice: XRechnung & PDF

The E-Invoice app turns a store order into a XRechnung 3.0 XML invoice inside your dashboard, so a business buyer gets the file they asked for.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "E-Invoice: XRechnung & PDF" in your site's dashboard. Fill in your business details once at the top: name, address, VAT ID and contact. The page lists your store orders; press "Make invoice" on one, check the buyer details, type the Leitweg-ID or buyer reference if the buyer gave one, and download the XML. On the paid plan the same page offers the PDF and the monthly batch.

Questions people ask

  • Do I have to upload the XML anywhere? No. The app makes the file; you send it to the buyer or their portal by e-mail or however they accept invoices.
  • Is it XRechnung 3.0? Yes: CustomizationID xrechnung_3.0, ProfileID Peppol BIS Billing 3.0, InvoiceTypeCode 380, with per-rate VAT subtotals.
  • My buyer's portal rejected the file. What do I check? The buyer's VAT ID, your VAT ID and the tax rate on each line come from the order and your settings; the Leitweg-ID comes from the buyer. Compare those four with what the buyer sent you.
  • Does the app write anything into my orders? No. Orders are only read.
  • What does Free cover? 10 invoices a month, counted by issue date. A new month starts at zero.
  • What does Premium add? Unlimited invoices, the PDF with the XML attached, saved buyer profiles and a monthly ZIP of every invoice issued that month.
  • Where are invoices stored? In the app's own data collections on your site.
