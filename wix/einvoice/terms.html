What you get

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard that turns your store orders into XRechnung 3.0 XML invoices. The free plan makes 10 invoices a month. The paid plan lifts that limit and adds PDF invoices, saved buyer profiles and a monthly batch export.

Your responsibility for the documents

The app builds the invoice from the data in your order and the details you enter. You are responsible for what the invoice says: check the buyer's VAT ID, your own VAT ID and tax rates, and the Leitweg-ID, before you send it. The app produces the file; it does not send it to any tax authority, PEPPOL network or buyer portal, and it does not certify tax compliance in any country.

The PDF is a PDF with the invoice XML attached. It is not a certified PDF/A-3 document.

Payment

The paid plan is billed through the platform's own billing on a monthly or yearly basis, and you can cancel at any time. A cancelled or downgraded site falls back to the free plan's 10 invoices a month.
