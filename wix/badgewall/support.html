Support: Badge Wall: Member Badges

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the wall and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the wall?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Badge Wall.

How do I create a badge?

In your site's dashboard: Contacts, then Manage Member Access, then Create Badge. Give it a name and colours, then assign it to members on its Members tab. The wall uses the badge's colours.

Which badges does it show?

Until you choose, it shows your badges that don't open members-only pages (the free plan shows the first of them). Click the widget in the editor, then Settings, and tick the badges you want.

I gave a member a badge. Do I need to publish again?

No. Badges are read when a visitor loads the page, so the member shows up the next time the page is opened.

A member with the badge does not show

Only members whose profile is public are listed. A member whose profile is private, or who is still waiting for approval, is left out.

Can it hide last names?

Yes. In Settings, under Names, choose "First name and last initial" or "First name only".

What happens if the paid plan ends?

The widget keeps working with one badge and up to 24 members. Extra badges, tabs, the list layout, the year, sorting, search and profile links switch off, and the small credit link comes back.
