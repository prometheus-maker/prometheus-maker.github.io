What it does

Give a member a badge in your dashboard and they appear on your page under it: "Black Belt", "Board of Directors", "Competition Team 2025-26", "Life Members". Take the badge away and they drop off. No more retyping lists of names.

Each badge shows as a label in its own colours, with how many members hold it. Under it are the members: profile photo (or initials) and name. Names can appear as on the profile, as first name and last initial ("Maya L."), or as first name only.

Only members whose profile is public are shown. E-mail addresses and contact details are never read.

Pick the badges to show in the widget's settings. Until you do, it shows your badges that don't open members-only pages. Set the heading, colours and font to fit your site.

Martial arts clubs use it for black belts, dance studios for their competition team, associations for the board, and clubs for coaches and life members.

The free plan shows one badge with up to 24 members and a small credit link. Premium shows every badge with no member limit, as sections or tabs, adds a list layout, the year each member got the badge, sorting, search and profile links, and removes the credit link.
