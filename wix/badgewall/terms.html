1. What you get

Badge Wall: Member Badges is a Wix app with a site widget that lists your site's members grouped by their member badges. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one badge with up to 24 members, your heading, colours and font, and a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan shows every badge with no member limit, as sections or tabs, adds a list layout, the year each member got the badge, sorting, a search box and links to member profiles, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
