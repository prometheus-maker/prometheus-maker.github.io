Effective date: 2026-11-10

This policy describes what the Badge Wall: Member Badges app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that lists your site's members grouped by their member badges (for example "Black Belt" or "Board"), with each member's profile photo or initials, name and, if you turn it on, the year they got the badge.

Data the app handles

  • Badges: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's member badges (name, description, colours, icon and whether a badge opens members-only pages) and which members hold each badge, with the date it was given.
  • Members: for the members who hold a shown badge, the backend asks Wix only for members whose profile is public and who are approved, and reads their public profile: nickname, profile photo and profile address. It never reads e-mail addresses, phone numbers, addresses or other contact details. Members whose profile is private are never listed.
  • Widget settings: the badges you picked, how names appear, layout, wording, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last wall it saw (badge names and colours, member names, photo addresses and badge years) so it shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows the same public profile details that your site's Members Area already shows.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your members' details or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
