Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the list and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the list?

Installing the app adds the list to the page you were on, in its own section. To put it somewhere else, like next to your blog posts, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Most Read.

Where do the numbers come from?

From Wix Blog: the same view, like and comment totals you see in your blog's dashboard. Most Read reads them and sorts your posts.

How often does the order change?

The list is refreshed every 10 minutes. A visitor who comes back first sees the list from their last visit, then the fresh one.

All my posts have 0 views

Then the list shows your newest posts. As soon as people read your posts, the most read ones move to the top.

Which posts are counted?

Your up to 100 newest published posts. Drafts and scheduled posts are not shown.

Can I show one list per topic?

Yes, on Premium: pick a category in Settings. Add the widget several times to show several categories.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The list keeps working with your top 3 posts by views. More posts, likes or comments ranking, the category filter, counts and the numbered and card styles switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget shows post titles, links and pictures and does not track visitors.
