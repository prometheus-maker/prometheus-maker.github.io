This policy describes what the Most Read app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a list of your blog's most popular posts to pages of your Wix site, ranked by the view, like or comment totals your Wix Blog already keeps.

Data the app handles

  • Blog posts: the app's backend on Wix reads your up to 100 newest published posts with their view, like and comment totals, their link and cover picture. It sends visitors only the title, link, cover picture and one number per listed post. The ranked list is kept in the backend's memory for up to 10 minutes so the blog is not asked again on every page view; it is never written to storage.
  • Blog categories: the settings panel lists your categories by name so you can pick one. This list is only answered for the site's owners and collaborators, not for visitors.
  • Widget settings: your title, the ranking, number of posts, category, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last list it saw so the widget shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The app keeps no database and no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not record what they read. View totals are counted by Wix Blog itself, not by this app.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your posts, your totals or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
