1. What you get

Popular Dishes: Most Ordered is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your most ordered dishes, counted from your site's own online orders. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows your top 3 dishes of the last 7 days as a grid, list or sliding row, with a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan shows up to 10 dishes, ranks the last 30 days or 12 months, adds an "ordered N times" line, can count shop products, lets you hide items, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

You decide where the widget appears and where its Order button links to. Make sure the dish names, photos and prices in your menu are correct; the widget shows them as they appear in your orders.

4. Accuracy

The ranking is counted from your orders as Wix records them, refreshed about once an hour. Canceled and fully refunded orders are left out. Very busy sites are ranked from their newest 1,500 orders in the chosen period.
