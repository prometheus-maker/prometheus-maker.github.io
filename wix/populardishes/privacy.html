This policy describes what the Popular Dishes: Most Ordered app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows your most ordered dishes, for example "Popular this week: Margherita Pizza, Italian Sandwich, Harvest Salad", each with photo, price and an Order button.

Data the app handles

  • Order line items: the app's backend reads your site's own online orders from the last 7 days (on the paid plan up to 12 months) and counts how many times each dish was ordered. From each order it uses only the items: dish name, photo, price and quantity.
  • The app does not use the buyer's name, email, phone, address or payment details, and never shows or stores them.
  • Plan: the backend reads whether your site has the free or the paid plan.

What the app stores

  • Nothing in your site and nothing in a database of ours. The backend keeps the ranked list (dish name, photo address, price, count) in memory for about an hour so pages load quickly.
  • In your visitors' browsers the widget remembers the last ranked list, so it shows at once on the next page. It sets no cookie and adds no tracking script.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, share or use any data for advertising.
  • It keeps no logs of visitors or buyers.

Retention and deletion

The in-memory list expires within an hour. Removing the app stops all reads. The list in a visitor's browser storage is replaced on the next visit and can be cleared with the browser's site data.
