Support: Popular Dishes: Most Ordered

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on. To add it somewhere else in the editor: Add Elements, then App Widgets, then Popular Dishes.

I see sample dishes

Your site has no online orders in the chosen period yet. Only you see the samples, in the editor. Your visitors see nothing until the first orders come in; then your own dishes show by themselves.

Which orders count?

Online orders of your Wix Restaurants menus from the last 7 days (paid plan: 30 days or 12 months), without canceled or fully refunded ones. On the paid plan you can also count products from your Wix store.

A new order does not show yet

The ranking refreshes about once an hour.

Where does the Order button go?

To /online-ordering, the page Wix Restaurants Orders adds to your site. If your ordering page has another address, type it under Settings, Button link.

How do I keep drinks or add-ons out of the list?

On the paid plan, type their names under "Never show these dishes", separated by commas.
