What it does

Advent Deals turns a page of your shop into an advent calendar with a real deal behind each door, so visitors come back every day to see what opens next.

In the app's dashboard you pick a product and a deal for a door: percent off, amount off, a special price or free shipping. The app makes a normal store coupon that works only on that day. When the day comes, the door opens on your site with the product photo, the new price next to the old one, the code with a copy button, and a button to the product. Past doors say Ended. Future doors stay locked with their date, and a small star shows that a deal waits behind them. Nobody can read a code before its day.

Doors without a deal show one of your products, so the calendar is never empty. "Fill empty doors" sets a deal on every open day in one step.

You can change the heading, the line under it, the button text and the font.

The free plan has the Advent calendar (December 1 to 24) with deals behind up to 6 doors and a small credit link. Premium puts a deal behind every door, adds the 12 Days of Christmas, Hanukkah and New Year calendars, four themes, your own door colour and a today-only layout, and removes the credit link.
