This policy describes what the Advent Deals app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds an advent calendar block to pages of your Wix site. Each door belongs to one day. Through the app's dashboard page you can put a deal behind a door; the app then creates a normal coupon in your store that works only on that day.

Data the app handles

  • Your store products: the app's backend asks Wix for the site's visible products and passes on what the calendar shows: each product's name, price, main image, product page link and whether it is sold out. Orders, customers and inventory numbers are not read.
  • Your coupons: the dashboard page creates, changes and deletes the coupons it made for the doors (their names start with "ADV:"). The backend reads only those coupons, and sends the calendar the code of the deal that runs today; codes of future days are never sent to visitors. Coupons you made yourself are not read or changed.
  • Widget settings: your calendar choice, heading, texts, theme, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status and currency: the backend also tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan and which currency your store uses, together with the app instance ID.
  • Short-lived copies: the backend keeps the product list in memory for up to five minutes and the door list for one minute so pages load faster, and the visitor's browser keeps the product list (never codes) so the calendar shows at once on the next page. Nothing is written to a database.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows doors, products, prices and today's code. The copy button puts the code on the visitor's own clipboard. Clicking a product opens its product page on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your products, your coupons, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
