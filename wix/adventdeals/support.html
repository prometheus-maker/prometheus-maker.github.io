Support: Advent Deals

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the calendar and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the calendar?

Installing the app adds it to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Advent Deals.

How do I put a deal behind a door?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Advent Deals. Next to a door, click Add a deal, pick the deal (percent off, amount off, special price or free shipping), the product and the code, and save. "Fill empty doors at once" puts the same kind of deal on every open day with a different product each day.

When does a code work?

From midnight to midnight on its door's day, in the time zone of the computer you used to set it (the dashboard shows which one). Before and after that day the coupon does not work, and the calendar does not show its code.

Can anyone use the code?

Yes. Today's code shows to everyone who visits the page, so treat it as a public offer for that day.

What do doors without a deal show?

One of your products, a different one each day. You can switch this off in the widget's settings; the door then just says it is open.

The editor shows door 5 open although it is November

The editor shows an open door so you can see the look. Your live site opens the doors on their real dates.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The calendar switches back to the Advent calendar with the deals of its first 6 doors, the default theme, and the small credit link. Your coupons stay in your store.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the calendar reads your products' names, prices, photos and links and the coupons the app made, shows a code only on its day, and sends nothing to the developer.
