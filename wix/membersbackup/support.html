Common questions

The page says it could not read my members or badges

The app reads members and badges with your signed-in session. Reload the dashboard page and try again. If the message stays, write to the address above with your site name and the time you tried.

How much does the free plan back up

The free plan backs up the 50 newest members and keeps the two most recent copies. The page shows how many members and badges your site has, and how many older members are outside the free plan, before you take a copy.

What is in the CSV

One row per member, with the member and contact id, login e-mail and its verification state, name, status, privacy, phone, city and country, join date, last login, profile link, badge titles and every custom-field value under its display name. It opens in any spreadsheet.

Roles are missing from the CSV

Member roles have no public interface, so the app cannot read them. The member id and contact id are in the CSV, so roles can be assigned by hand.

The weekly copy did not run

Apps cannot run a timer. The weekly copy is taken the first time you open the page after seven days, on Premium, and the page shows the time of the last copy.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
