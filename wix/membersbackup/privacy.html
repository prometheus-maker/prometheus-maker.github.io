Privacy Policy: Members Backup: CSV Export

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Members Backup: CSV Export app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where it reads the members of your site and stores dated copies of their member profiles, which you download as a CSV file.

Data the app handles

  • Members: the member id, contact id, login e-mail and its verification state, first and last name, status, privacy status, activity state, phone, city and country, join date, last login, profile link, badge titles, and custom-field values by their display names are written to a backup in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Badges: the badge names and the assignments of badges to members are read to join badge titles to each member.
  • Settings: the time of the last backup and the weekly backup state are stored on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

Members and badges are read when you press Back up now, or when the app checks whether the weekly copy is due. Profile photos are not copied: the backup records the picture link, never the image. The copies stay inside the app on your site; the app sends them nowhere, keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Downloads

A backup CSV is generated in your browser and saved to your computer. You are responsible for the file you download, and the member data in it.

Your rights

You can delete a backup and its rows from the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes its data collections from your site. Write to the address above for any question about your data.

Changes

The developer may change this policy; the current version is always on this page.
