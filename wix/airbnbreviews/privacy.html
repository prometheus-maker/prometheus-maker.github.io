Privacy Policy: Guest Reviews: Import

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

This policy describes what the Guest Reviews: Import app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you paste or upload the reviews from your own listing and keep them in one list.

Data the app handles

  • Reviews: the guest name, rating, review text and date you paste or upload are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Import labels: each review keeps the source line and the date it was imported with, stored on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app fetches nothing from any other service. It keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail, sets no cookies of its own and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Removing all reviews empties the list. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
