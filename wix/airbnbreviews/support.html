Common questions

My rows are not read

The app reads one review per line. The first line can be a header naming the columns (guest name, rating, review text, date). Separate the columns with commas, semicolons or tabs. Quote a review text that contains a comma.

A rating is shown as zero stars

Ratings can be 1 to 5, as a number, or as stars. A row with a rating outside that range is listed as not ready in the preview, so you can fix it before saving.

The same review appears twice

Rows already stored are skipped, and a review that appears twice in one paste is kept once.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.

How many reviews can I import

The free plan holds 10 imported reviews. Premium removes the limit. Nothing is deleted when you reach the cap; you just cannot add more until you upgrade or remove some.
