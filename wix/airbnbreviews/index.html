What it does

You already have reviews from past guests, but they sit wherever the bookings came from. This app brings them into your site's dashboard, so they stay together and every one is labelled.

Paste the reviews from your own listing export, or upload the file. The app reads the columns for guest name, rating, review text and date, shows a preview with a verdict for every row, and skips duplicates. Nothing is fetched from another service: only the text you provide is imported, and each row keeps the source line and date it came with.

The list shows the star average, a breakdown from five stars to one, and how many reviews are stored. Remove a single review or all of them whenever you like.

Free holds 10 imported reviews. Premium removes that cap, so you can keep every review you have. Premium is $4 a month, or $3 a month billed yearly.
