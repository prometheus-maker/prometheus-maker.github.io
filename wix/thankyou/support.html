Support: Thank You Page: Post Purchase

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the order number you tested with and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I add the card?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Thank You Page, and click Add to Thank You page. Then publish your site once. You can also add it in the editor: open the Thank You page, select the order section and add the Thank You Card plugin.

How do I set the coupon?

In the same dashboard page, pick "Same code for everyone" and type a code you created in Marketing, Coupons, or (Premium) "A unique one-time code for every order" with the discount, days valid and minimum order. Save.

Why don't I see a code on an old order?

Codes show only for orders placed in the last two hours, so an old Thank You page link does not make new codes.

Will a buyer get two codes if they reload the page?

No. The code for an order is made once and shown again on reload.

What do donors see?

A thank-you for their gift with the campaign name and buttons to share your cause. No coupon.

How do I change the texts or colour?

In the editor, open the Thank You page, click the card, then Settings.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

New orders get the free plan's card: your single code for everyone (if you set one) and the small credit link. Codes already made stay in your store and keep working until they expire.
