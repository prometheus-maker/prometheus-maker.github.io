What it does

Right after checkout, your buyer lands on the Thank You page. This app adds a card there, under the order items, that thanks them by first name and hands them a coupon for their next order, with a Copy code button.

You pick the offer in the app's dashboard. On the free plan every buyer sees the same code, one you made in your store's coupons, such as THANKS10. On Premium the app makes a fresh code for each order: percent off, an amount off or free shipping, valid for the number of days you choose, usable once, with an optional minimum order. Reloading the page shows the same code again instead of making a second one, and codes only appear for orders placed in the last two hours.

Donation orders get a different card: a thank-you for the gift with the campaign name, and buttons to share the cause on Facebook, X, WhatsApp or by email.

In the editor you can change the texts and the accent colour, and add Follow us links for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and X.

The card shows the buyer's first name and the order total, nothing else. No emails, phone numbers or addresses leave your store.

The free plan shows a small credit link under the card. Premium removes it.
