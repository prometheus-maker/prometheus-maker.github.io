This policy describes what the Thank You Page: Post Purchase app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a card to your store's Thank You page, the page buyers see right after checkout. The card thanks the buyer and shows your coupon for their next order. For donation orders it shows a thank-you and share buttons instead.

Data the app handles

  • The order on the Thank You page: the page gives the card the order's ID. The app's backend asks Wix for that order and passes on only what the card shows: the buyer's first name, the order total, whether it is a donation and the campaign name. Email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, payment details and the other order fields are not passed on, stored or logged.
  • Your coupons: the dashboard saves your offer as one inactive coupon in your store (its name starts with "TYP:"; nobody can use it). On the paid plan the backend creates one single-use coupon per order (its name starts with "TYO:" and contains the order ID) and reads it back so a reloaded page shows the same code. Coupons you made yourself are only read when you type their code into the offer form, to check it exists.
  • Card settings: your texts, follow links and colour. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the card and its settings panel whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID. This answer is kept in the server's memory for up to ten minutes.

The backend keeps no logs of buyers or visitors and writes nothing to a database.

Site visitors

The card does not set cookies and does not track anyone. The Copy code button puts the code on the buyer's own clipboard. Share buttons open the chosen network's own share page in a new tab, only when the buyer clicks one.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your orders, buyers, coupons or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
