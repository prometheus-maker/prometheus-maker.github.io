What it does

Since December 2024 a product sold to EU consumers has to name its manufacturer, and when the seller sits outside the EU, an EU responsible person, along with the warnings that product needs. Keeping that right by hand in the product description, for fifty products and three languages, is where a catalogue goes stale.

This app keeps the details in one place. It reads the products in your store and shows each one with what is still missing: manufacturer details, the EU contact, model or batch number, safety warnings, and links to declarations or test reports. You fill a product once and the checklist says what is left. Copying a manufacturer and its documents from another product takes one click, so a family of items is done in a minute.

The free plan covers three products with every field. Premium removes that limit, keeps the warning text in up to eight languages, and prints a one-page EU Declaration of Conformity as a PDF you can download, sign and publish with the product.

All of it stays on your site. The app formats the details you enter; it does not judge whether a product complies, and it is not legal advice.
