Effective date: 2026-11-18

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

This policy describes what the GPSR: Product Safety Info app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard where you store the product safety details that the EU General Product Safety Regulation asks for: manufacturer and EU responsible person, model and batch number, warnings, and links to declarations or test reports. It can check each store product against those details and print a declaration of conformity as a PDF.

Data the app handles

  • Products: the app's backend reads the name, image and ID of the products in your store, so the dashboard can list them and show which ones still need details. Nothing else about a product is read.
  • Safety details: what you type into the dashboard (manufacturer and EU contact, model and batch, warnings, document links, translations) is stored in the app's data collection on your Wix site. It stays with your site; the app's backend stores no copy of its own.
  • PDF: the declaration of conformity is generated in your browser from the details you entered. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Plan status: the dashboard asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties.

Your choices

Delete a product's details in the dashboard at any time; removing the app removes its data from your site. Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with any question about this policy.
