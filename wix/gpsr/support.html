Support: GPSR: Product Safety Info

The GPSR: Product Safety Info app keeps the safety details of your store products in one place and prints an EU declaration of conformity.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "GPSR: Product Safety Info" in your Wix dashboard. Every product in your store appears with a status: Not started, a number of fields still to add, or Complete. Press "Fill in" on a product and enter the manufacturer, the EU responsible person (tick "Same as the manufacturer" to reuse the manufacturer's address), model and batch, one safety warning and any document links. Save, and the status turns Complete.

Questions people ask

  • How many products can I fill in? Three on the free plan, all of them on Premium.
  • Where is the PDF? On Premium, open a product and press "Declaration of conformity (PDF)". It prints one A4 page with the details you entered.
  • More than one language? On Premium the same product carries several language tabs, up to eight.
  • My store shows no products. The app reads the products of the site it is installed on. Add a product in Wix Stores, then reload the dashboard page.
  • Can I remove a product's details? Yes, clear the fields and save, or delete the product from the store.

Something wrong

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with the site address, what you did and what you saw. We answer within two working days.
