This policy describes what the Kudoline app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a testimonials widget to pages of your Wix site. It shows the quotes, names, ratings and colours you enter in its settings panel.

Data the app handles

  • Widget settings: the testimonials you type (quote, the person's name, role and star rating), photo links, colours, font and layout options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element. You decide which names appear; only publish testimonials you have permission to use.
  • Review schema (Premium, if you switch it on): the widget adds schema.org markup with the same quotes, names and ratings to your page, so search engines can read them. Nothing new is collected.
  • Plan status: the widget and its settings panel ask the app's backend on Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID. It stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro box on the app's dashboard page, kept in your browser's local storage.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It only shows your testimonials.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your settings, your testimonials or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
