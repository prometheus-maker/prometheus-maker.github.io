Support: Kudoline

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the testimonials and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds three sample testimonials to the page you were on, in their own section. To add another set, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Kudoline. Each widget has its own testimonials.

How do I change the testimonials?

Click the widget in the editor, then Settings. Pick a testimonial at the top, then edit the quote, name, role and stars. Use Add and Remove for more or fewer, and the arrows to change the order.

Can I import reviews from Google?

No. Kudoline shows testimonials you type or paste in. It doesn't connect to Google, Facebook or other review sites.

Can I show a photo of the customer?

Yes, on Premium. Copy the image address (it starts with https://), for example from your Media Manager, and paste it into Photo. Without a photo the card shows the person's initials.

Why only 5 testimonials?

The free plan shows up to 5 per widget. Premium allows 40.

Will Google show stars for my testimonials?

Kudoline can add review schema markup (Premium), and every quote is plain text in the page either way. Whether a search engine shows stars is its own decision; Google usually doesn't show stars for reviews a business publishes about itself.

Can it use my site's font?

Yes, that's the default. Use Change font in Settings to pick a different one, and "Use site font" to go back.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working. The first 5 testimonials show as a slider or grid; photos, the masonry and moving-row layouts, the rating summary and the schema switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget shows your text and collects nothing from visitors.
