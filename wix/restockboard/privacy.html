This policy describes what the Restock Board: Back in Stock app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a board to pages of your Wix site. It shows store products that came back in stock in the last days, newest first, and on the paid plan a row of products that are sold out right now.

Data the app handles

  • Stock changes: when Wix tells the app that a product variant is back in stock, the app saves one row in a data collection on your own site: the inventory item ID, the product ID, the variant ID, the time it came back and its stock at that moment. When the item sells out again, the row is deleted. Rows older than 60 days are deleted too. You can see this collection in your site's CMS.
  • Your store products: the app's backend asks Wix for the visible products on the board and passes on only what the board shows: each product's name, price, main image, product page link and the IDs of its categories. Hidden products are left out. Orders, customers and buyers are not read.
  • Your store categories: their names, for the hidden categories setting.
  • Widget settings: your heading, texts, layout, theme, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last answer (plan and the products above) so the board shows at once on the next page.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows products. Clicking one opens its product page on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The restock rows stay in your site's own data collection. The developer never receives your products, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
