What it does

Restock Board puts the products that just came back in stock on your homepage, newest first. When a sold-out product gets stock again, it appears on the board by itself with a label like "Back today" or "Back since Tue", its photo, price and a Shop now button. When it sells out again, or after the days you chose, it leaves the board.

Many shops type "Back in stock" into a ribbon by hand and forget to take it off weeks later. The board follows your store stock instead, so the list stays current and you never edit it.

You choose how long a product stays (7, 14 or 30 days), how many show, the stock a restock needs to count and the wording of the label and button. On phones the row swipes. While nothing is back in stock, the board stays hidden on your site.

The free plan shows restocks of the last 7 days, up to 6 products in one row, with a small credit link. Premium keeps products up to 30 days and shows up to 24, adds a "Coming back soon" row of sold-out products linking to their Notify When Available button, grid and carousel layouts, hidden categories, warm and dark themes, and removes the credit link.

It needs the new product catalog of the store and reads no customer data.
