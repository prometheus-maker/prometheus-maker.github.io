1. What you get

Restock Board: Back in Stock is a Wix app with a site widget that shows the store products that came back in stock in the last days, with a "Back since" label, price and a Shop now button. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows restocks of the last 7 days, up to 6 products in one row, with your own heading, label and button wording, and a small credit link under the board. The paid plan keeps products up to 30 days, shows up to 24, and adds a row of sold-out products, grid and carousel layouts, hidden categories, more colour themes and your own label colour, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

The board shows the products, photos, prices and stock status from your store. Keep them correct, and only show products you have the right to show.
