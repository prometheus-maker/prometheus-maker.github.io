Support: Restock Board: Back in Stock

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the board and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the board?

Installing the app adds the board to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Restock Board.

Why is the board empty on my site?

It lists products that come back in stock after you install the app, so it starts empty. Give a sold-out product stock again (in Store Products, or by import) and it appears with "Back today". While nothing is back in stock the board hides itself, so your page never shows an empty box.

The editor shows products I don't sell

Those are samples so you can see the look before anything is restocked. Your live site shows only your own products.

My store uses the older catalog

Restock news comes from the new Wix Stores product catalog. On a store that still uses the older catalog, the settings panel says so and the board stays hidden until the store moves to the new catalog.

One returned item put a product on the board

Set "Count as restocked from" in Settings to the stock you want, for example 5 in stock. Products that don't track stock always count.

Can I change "Back since Tue" and "Shop now"?

Yes, on every plan: Settings, Text. Write {day} where the day goes. With your own words, the day follows your site language.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The board keeps working with the last 7 days, up to 6 products in one row and the white look. The Coming back soon row, grid and carousel layouts, hidden categories and other themes switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app saves which product variants came back in stock and when, in a collection on your own site, reads your products' names, prices, photos and links, and sends nothing to the developer.
