What it does

Privacy laws like the GDPR (Europe) and the LGPD (Brazil) give your visitors the right to ask what personal data you hold about them, and to have it corrected, deleted or sent to them. They also expect a clear privacy contact. This app gives your site both, in your visitors' language.

The widget fits on your privacy policy page. It shows who is responsible for the data and how to reach your privacy contact or data protection officer, next to a short request form. The list of request types follows the law you choose: GDPR, LGPD, or both. Texts are ready in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian, and the title and intro can be your own.

Every request lands in a private inbox in your dashboard with a reference number and an answer-by date: 15 days for LGPD and one month for GDPR by default, or your own number of days. Colour chips flag what is due soon or late. Notes and statuses keep a record of how you handled each one.

Premium emails you the moment a request arrives, exports your request log as a CSV file and lets the widget follow the page language on multilingual sites. Requests stay on your own site. The app is not legal advice.
