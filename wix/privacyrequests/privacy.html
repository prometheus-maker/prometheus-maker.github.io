This policy describes what the Privacy Requests app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site with your privacy contact details and a form where visitors can send privacy requests (for example a request to see or delete their data). Requests appear in a private inbox on the app's dashboard page with an answer-by date.

Data the app handles

  • Widget settings: the law, language, business name, address, privacy contact, email, phone, policy link, texts and colours you enter. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Requests from your visitors: name, email address, request type, the details they type, the widget language and the law, plus the date. They are stored in an app collection in your own site's CMS. You, as the site owner, are the controller of this data; the app processes it only to show it to you.
  • Your own notes, statuses and answer-by dates on each request, stored in the same collection.
  • App settings: whether email alerts are on, the alert addresses and the number of days to answer, stored in a second app collection on your site.
  • Email alerts (paid plan): when a request arrives, the app's backend on Wix sends an email with the request to the addresses you chose, using Wix's own email service.
  • Plan status: the widget asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or paid plan. The backend stores nothing else.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro box on the dashboard page, kept in your browser's local storage.

Site visitors

The widget sets no cookies and does not track visitors. It only sends what a visitor types into the form, after they tick the consent box and click Send. Visitors who want their request data removed can ask you; you can delete any request in the inbox.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. Requests stay in your site's collections and in the alert emails you choose to receive. The developer never receives the requests, your settings or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Retention

Requests stay in your site until you delete them, or until you remove the app and its collections.
