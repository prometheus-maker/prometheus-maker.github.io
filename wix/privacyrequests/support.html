Support: Privacy Requests

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. Most sites move it to the privacy policy page. To add it again, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets, then Privacy Requests.

How do I put in my details?

Click the widget in the editor, then Settings. Under "Who you are", enter your business, address, privacy contact, email, phone and a link to your privacy policy. Publish your site afterwards.

GDPR, LGPD or both?

The law sets which request types visitors can pick and the default answer time: one month for GDPR, 15 days for LGPD. Pick "Both" if you have visitors from the EU and Brazil. You can set your own number of days in the dashboard.

Where do requests go?

To the app's dashboard page (Apps > Privacy Requests in your site's dashboard), with a reference number and an answer-by date. On Premium you also get an email for each new request.

The form says it could not send

Your site needs the free CMS (Content Manager) to store requests. If it was removed, add it again from the App Market, then try again. If it still fails, email us the site address.

Does this make my site compliant?

No app can do that alone. This one gives visitors a clear way to reach you and helps you keep track of deadlines. It is not legal advice.

How do I delete a person's data from my site?

Use your site's own privacy settings for contacts and members. In this app you can delete the request itself from the inbox.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

Requests keep arriving in your inbox. Email alerts and CSV export stop, and the widget shows English if it was set to automatic language.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: requests stay on your own site and the developer never sees them.
