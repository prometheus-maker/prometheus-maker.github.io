This policy describes what the Volunteer Shifts: Signup Sheet app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where the organiser keeps weekly shift templates and the people signed up for each date, and a widget for a page of your site where volunteers take a place themselves.

Data the app handles

  • Shifts: the shift title, weekday, start and end time, number of places and whether it is active are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Sign-ups: the volunteer's name, email address, the shift and date they chose, the status (going, cancelled, no-show) and a random cancel token are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Sign-up from the site: when a volunteer signs up, the app's backend writes that row and sends a confirmation email to the address given. The email goes through the site's own email service.
  • Reminders: a reminder email goes to each person who is going, two days before the shift, with a cancel link.
  • Cancel page: the link opens a page served by the app's backend, which reads the cancel token and updates that one row. It asks for nothing else.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors and shares no data with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a sign-up removes the person's name, email and token. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
