These terms cover the Volunteer Shifts: Signup Sheet app ("the app") on your site.

What the app does

The app keeps weekly volunteer shifts on a board in your site's dashboard, lets people sign up for a date from a widget on your site, sends them a confirmation and a reminder, and lets the organiser mark who turned up.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the data you enter. The app stores it on your site. You are responsible for the people you add and for the emails the app sends on your behalf: tell volunteers that their name and email are kept for the shift roster.

Plans

The free plan holds two shift templates and one week of board, with sign-ups added by hand. Premium adds unlimited templates, the four-week board, sign-up from your site, reminder emails, no-show marks and the hours CSV. Premium is billed by the platform at $9 a month, or $7.20 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.

Email

The app sends a confirmation when someone signs up and one reminder two days before a shift. It sends nothing else, and every message carries a cancel link.

Availability

The app is provided as it is. Reminder emails depend on the site's email service and can be delayed or filtered, so check the board before a shift as well.

Changes

The developer may change these terms; the current version is always on this page.
