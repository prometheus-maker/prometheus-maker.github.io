Common questions

The register is empty after I install it

The app starts with no assets. Press Add 6 sample assets to see how a filled register looks, or Add asset to enter your first item. The samples are ordinary assets and can be edited or deleted.

An asset will not save

Name is the only required field. Check that the purchase price is a number and that the dates use the date field's own picker; any other field can be left blank. If saving still fails, the message at the top of the form names the field.

The free plan will not take another asset

Free holds 10 assets and 5 service entries. Premium removes both limits and adds CSV import and export. The register shows how many of the 10 are used, and the upgrade panel appears at the cap.

A warranty or service flag looks wrong

The warranty flag is calculated from the warranty end date, and the service flag from the next due date of the latest service entry: overdue after that date, soon within 60 days of it. Correct the date in Edit, or add a newer service entry, and the flag follows.

The CSV import skipped some rows

The first row must be the header: Name, Category, Serial, Location, Status, Purchase date, Price, Currency, Supplier, Warranty end, Notes. Only Name is required; the import preview counts the valid rows and names the ones with errors before anything is saved.
