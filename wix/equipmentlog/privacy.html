Privacy Policy: Equipment Log: Asset Register

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Equipment Log: Asset Register app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you keep a register of the equipment you own and the services done on it.

Data the app handles

  • Assets: the name, category, serial number, location, status, purchase date, purchase price, currency, supplier, warranty end date and notes you enter are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Service entries: the date, kind, cost, note and next due date you log against an asset are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting an asset removes its service entries. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
