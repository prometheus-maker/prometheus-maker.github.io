This policy describes what the Donor Wall: Nonprofit Donors app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that lists the newest gifts your site received through Wix Donations, so you can thank donors in public.

Data the app handles

  • Donation orders: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's paid donation orders (eCommerce orders that hold a Wix Donations line). From each order it uses the donor's first name and last name, the campaign name, the payment date, the amount, whether the gift is recurring, the donor's checkout message, and the contact ID or e-mail only to count each donor once.
  • What reaches the page: only the first name (up to 24 characters), the first letter of the last name, the campaign name, the date and the recurring flag. On the paid plan also the amount, the total raised and the donor's message, which show only if you switch them on. Full last names, e-mail addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers and contact IDs are never sent to the page.
  • Widget settings: name style, hidden donors, start date, colours, texts and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Short-lived memory: the backend keeps the orders it read in memory for up to 60 seconds, so a busy page does not read them again for every visitor. Nothing is written to disk or a database.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last donor list it saw (the same display fields as above) so the list shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

Your donors

Wix Donations does not let donors choose to stay anonymous, so the wall shows a first name and a last initial by default and hides amounts. You decide what shows: first names only, initials, no names, a list of donors to hide, and a start date. If a donor asks to be removed, add their name to "Hide these donors" in the widget's settings and publish. Consider telling donors on your donation page that their first name may appear on your site.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your donors' details or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
