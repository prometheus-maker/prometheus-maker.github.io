1. What you get

Donor Wall: Nonprofit Donors is a Wix app with a site widget that lists the newest gifts your site received through Wix Donations. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows your newest 10 donors as a list with a donor count, a Donate button and a small credit link under the list. The paid plan adds amounts and the total raised, tiers, a campaign filter, a Today view, donor messages, tile and ticker layouts, up to 50 donors, your own heading, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
