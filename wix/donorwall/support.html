Support: Donor Wall: Nonprofit Donors

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the wall and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the wall?

Installing the app adds the wall to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Donor Wall.

Where do the names come from?

From the gifts your site takes with Wix Donations. Every paid gift shows by itself within about a minute, newest first. Fully refunded gifts are left out.

The wall says "Be the first to give"

There are no paid donations yet, or the start date in Settings is later than your gifts. Wix Donations must be on the site.

Can donors stay anonymous?

Wix Donations does not offer that at checkout, so the wall is careful by default: first name and last initial, no amounts. In Settings you can show first names only, initials or no names, and hide any donor by name.

How do I remove one donor?

Click the wall in the editor, then Settings, and add their name as it shows on the wall (for example "Sarah K.") to "Hide these donors". Publish.

Can I show amounts?

Yes, on Premium: switch on "Show amounts". Tiers (for example 250 Gold, 100 Silver) can show a label even with amounts hidden.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The wall keeps working with your newest 10 donors as a list. Amounts, tiers, filters, messages and the other layouts switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: only first names, last initials, campaigns and dates reach your page, unless you switch on amounts or messages.
