1. What you get

Attendance: Class Milestones is a Wix app with a site widget that shows the members who reached a class milestone (for example 50 classes), counted from your site's Wix Bookings. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one milestone with up to 12 members, the classes taken this year, a button and a small credit link under the wall. The paid plan celebrates several milestones at once, adds a most-classes-this-month list and a service filter, shows up to 48 members and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

You decide which member names your site shows and you are responsible for having your members' consent where the law requires it. Remove anyone who should not be shown with the "Leave off the wall" setting, or show first names only.
