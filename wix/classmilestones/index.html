What it does

Put a class milestone wall on your studio's site. When a member reaches 50 classes, their name shows up with a badge: "Maya L. · 50 classes · Nov 8". Nobody has to keep a punch card or update a page by hand, because the wall counts the class bookings your site already takes.

If you mark attendance in your booking calendar, only classes marked attended count and no-shows never do. If you don't, every confirmed booking counts once its start time has passed. A count-from date lets your 100 Club start on a day you pick.

Members show by first name and last initial, or first name only. Last names, e-mails and phone numbers never reach the page, and a hide list keeps off anyone who asked not to be shown. Above the wall, a line shows how many classes your studio taught this year. Fresh milestones get a New tag for 30 days. You choose the heading, the word for classes (sessions, workouts, rides), the button to your booking page, colours and font.

The free plan celebrates one milestone and shows up to 12 members. Premium celebrates several at once (25, 50, 100, 250), adds a most-classes-this-month list, counts only the services you pick, shows up to 48 members and removes the credit link.
