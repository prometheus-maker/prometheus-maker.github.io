Support: Attendance: Class Milestones

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the wall and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the wall?

Installing the app adds the wall to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Class Milestones.

What counts as a class?

If you mark attendance in your booking calendar or the Wix app, only classes marked attended count, and no-shows never do. If you don't mark attendance, every confirmed booking counts once its start time has passed. In Settings, "What counts" lets you pick one of the two yourself.

The wall says "Our first members are on their way"

Nobody has reached the milestone yet. Pick a smaller milestone in Settings, or check the count-from date.

Can I hide someone?

Yes. Add their name to "Leave off the wall" in Settings (as it shows on the wall, or their full name) and publish. You can also show first names only.

Does it show last names or e-mails?

No. Only a first name and a last initial (or the first name only) and the number of classes reach your page.

Does it count classes from before I installed it?

Yes, every confirmed booking still in your Wix Bookings counts, up to the newest 3,000. Set a count-from date to start later.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The wall keeps working with one milestone and up to 12 members. Several milestones, the most-classes-this-month list and the service filter switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: only short first names and class counts reach your page.
