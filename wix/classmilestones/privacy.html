This policy describes what the Attendance: Class Milestones app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows the members who reached a class milestone (for example 50 classes), counted from your site's Wix Bookings.

Data the app handles

  • Bookings: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's confirmed bookings with their attendance status. From each booking it uses the client's first name, last name, contact ID or e-mail (only to count each person once), the class start time, the service name and whether the client attended.
  • What reaches the page: only a shortened name (first name and last initial, or first name only), the milestone, the date it was reached and class counts, plus the studio's totals. Last names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and contact IDs are never sent to the page.
  • Widget settings: milestones, what counts, count-from date, hidden names, colours, texts and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Short-lived memory: the backend keeps the bookings it read in memory for up to 10 minutes, so a busy page does not read them again for every visitor. Nothing is written to disk or a database.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last wall it saw (short names and counts only) so the wall shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

Member names

You decide whether your members' names are shown. Use "First name only" or add a name to "Leave off the wall" in the widget's settings and publish, for example when a member asks not to be shown.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your clients' details or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
