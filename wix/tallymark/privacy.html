This policy describes what the Tallymark app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a counter to pages of your Wix site that shows how many submissions one or more of your Wix Forms have received.

Data the app handles

  • Submission counts: the app's backend on Wix asks the Wix Forms service how many submissions your chosen forms have (in total and, if you turn it on, in the last 7 days). It reads counts only. It never reads, copies or shows the content of a submission: no names, emails or answers.
  • Form names: the settings panel lists your forms by name and count so you can pick one. This list is only answered for the site's owners and collaborators, not for visitors.
  • Widget settings: the form you picked, your text, goal, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last count it saw (one number) so the counter shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows a number. If you add a button, visitors who click it go to the link you entered.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your counts, your forms or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
