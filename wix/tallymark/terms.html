1. What you get

Tallymark is a Wix app with a site widget that shows how many submissions your Wix Forms have received. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan counts one form and shows a big number or a goal bar with your text, colours, font and a button. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
