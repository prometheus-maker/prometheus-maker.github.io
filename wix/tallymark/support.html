Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the counter and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the counter?

Installing the app adds a counter to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Tallymark.

Which form does it count?

Your newest form, until you pick one. Click the counter in the editor, then Settings, and choose the form from the list. The list shows how many submissions each form has today.

It shows "Be the first to sign up"

That line shows while the form has no submissions yet (or, on Premium, until the count reaches your "show from" number). You can change the text in Settings.

How fast does a new sign-up show?

The count is read when a visitor loads the page, so a new submission shows the next time the page is opened.

My site has no form yet

Add one in the editor (Add Elements, then Contact & Forms). Tallymark counts submissions of the site's forms; it does not make a form of its own.

Can I add sign-ups I collected on paper?

Yes, on Premium: type them under "Start counting from" and they are added to the live count.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The counter keeps working with one form. Extra forms, the starting number, "this week", "show from" and the circle style switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the counter shows a number and never shows names, emails or answers.
