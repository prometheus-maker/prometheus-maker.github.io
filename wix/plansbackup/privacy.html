Privacy Policy: Pricing Plans Backup: Export

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Pricing Plans Backup: Export app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where it reads your pricing plans and plan orders and stores dated copies of them, which you download as a ZIP file.

Data the app handles

  • Plans: the plan name, price, currency, billing terms, trial days, fees, perks and state (live, hidden or archived) are written to a snapshot in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Orders: the order id, plan, status, payment status, start and end date, total, currency, type, and the buyer's name and e-mail are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Subscribers: a list derived from the orders (name, e-mail, plans, order counts, totals, first and last order) is stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Settings: the time of the last backup and the weekly backup state are stored on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

Plans and orders are read when you press Back up now, or when the app checks whether the weekly copy is due. The copies stay inside the app on your site; the app sends them nowhere, keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Downloads

A backup ZIP is generated in your browser and saved to your computer. You are responsible for the file you download.

Your rights

You can delete a snapshot and its rows from the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes its data collections from your site. Write to the address above for any question about your data.

Changes

The developer may change this policy; the current version is always on this page.
