What it does

A pricing plan archive disappears in one click: a plan you archive can never be made active again or edited, and the native order export is a manual file of the orders you tick. This app keeps a dated copy of every plan and every plan order inside your dashboard.

Press Back up now and the app reads your plans and orders from your site and stores them in app storage. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Each backup is a ZIP download with plans.csv and orders.csv, and Premium adds subscribers.csv. A plan row carries the price, billing terms, trial days, fees, perks and state, archived and hidden plans included. An order row carries the plan, buyer, e-mail, status, start, end, total and order id.

Before you pay, the page shows your numbers: how many plans, orders and subscribers your site has. The Free plan backs up every plan and the newest 20 orders, and it names exactly how many older orders are left out. Premium backs up every order all time, adds the full subscriber list with order counts and totals, keeps 12 months of history and takes a weekly automatic copy on the first page open after seven days.

Premium is $6 a month, or $5 a month billed yearly, with a 14-day free trial.
