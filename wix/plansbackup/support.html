Common questions

The page says it could not read my plans or orders

The app reads plans and orders with your signed-in session. Reload the dashboard page and try again. If the message stays, write to the address above with your site name and the time you tried.

How much does the free plan back up

The free plan backs up every plan and the newest 20 orders, and keeps the two most recent copies. The page shows how many plans, orders and subscribers your site has, and how many older orders are outside the free plan, before you take a copy.

What is in the ZIP

A plans file with one row per plan (price, billing terms, trial days, fees, perks, state) and an orders file with one row per order (plan, buyer, e-mail, status, dates, total), plus a subscribers file on Premium. All are plain CSV that opens in any spreadsheet.

The weekly copy did not run

Apps cannot run a timer. The weekly copy is taken the first time you open the page after seven days, on Premium, and the page shows the time of the last copy.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
