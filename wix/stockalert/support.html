Support: Stock Alert Pro: Reorder List

The Stock Alert Pro: Reorder List app keeps one list of the products in your Wix shop that are running low, with how many to order and who to order them from.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Stock Alert Pro: Reorder List" in your Wix dashboard. Set the shop-wide threshold in units, such as five. Every product with stock at or under it appears in the table, the most urgent first and marked Out, Critical or Low.

Type a supplier under a product, or give a product its own threshold, and press Save. Those values stay. Each row shows the quantity to reorder, worked back up to twice the threshold; set a fixed quantity for a product if you order the same amount every time.

Download the reorder list as a CSV with the button under the table, or, on Premium, take one sheet per supplier and print it as a purchase order.

Questions people ask

  • Does it change my stock in Wix? No. It reads your products and inventory and writes only your settings.
  • What counts as low? Whatever threshold you set, per shop or per product.
  • How many products are covered? 25 on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? Products with options are split into one row per variant, and the supplier sheets and printable purchase order are included.
  • Does it send me e-mails? No. Open the list in your dashboard whenever you need to order.
  • Where is the data? In the app's collection on your Wix site; removing the app removes it.
