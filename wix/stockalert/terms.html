These terms cover the Stock Alert Pro: Reorder List app ("the app") for Wix sites.

The service

The app gives a shop a dashboard page that lists the products at or under its stock threshold, with the quantity to reorder and the supplier of each. It reads stock from your Wix shop and stores only your own settings.

Plans

The free plan tracks up to 25 products. Premium lifts the limit, splits products with options into per-variant rows and adds the supplier reorder sheets. Plans are billed by Wix through the App Market; the price shown on the app's page at the time of purchase applies. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the free plan's limit applies again from then.

Your part

You use the app only on sites you are allowed to use it on, and you check the numbers before ordering stock. The app reads what Wix holds and does the arithmetic; it does not place orders, change your inventory or contact your suppliers.

Ending it
