Effective date: 2026-11-19

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

This policy describes what the Stock Alert Pro: Reorder List app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard that lists the products in your shop whose stock is at or under your threshold, together with the quantity to reorder and the supplier of each product.

Data the app handles

  • Products and stock: the app reads your shop's product names, SKUs, variants and inventory counts to build the list. It reads them each time the page opens and keeps no separate copy.
  • Settings: the shop-wide threshold, and each product's threshold, supplier and fixed reorder quantity, are stored in one settings collection in the app's data storage on your Wix site.
  • Plan status: the app asks whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. What you save stays on your Wix site.

Keeping and removing data

Your settings stay on your Wix site until you change them or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its collection and everything in it.
