What it does

A paper punch card for your regulars, on your own site. Each paid order adds one stamp to the customer's card: store orders, food orders and paid bookings all count, and you choose which ones. Nobody types anything in, and one order never stamps twice. A canceled order drops off the card by itself.

Members open the page where you put the widget and see their card: 7 of 10 stamps, and the reward you wrote, such as a free coffee or a free class. Visitors who are not logged in see an empty card and a log in button.

You set how many stamps a card needs (3 to 20), the reward, a minimum order total and the date counting starts. The dashboard lists every customer with stamps and shows whose reward is ready. On the free plan the full card says "Reward ready, show this card at the counter" and you press Mark given once you have handed it over.

Premium turns a full card into a one-use coupon code that shows on the member's card, and the next card starts by itself. For walk-in visits without an online order, your staff can add a stamp on the customer's phone with a PIN. You can also download all cards as CSV.

The card uses your site's font. You pick the stamp shape and colours.
