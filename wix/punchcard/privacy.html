This policy describes what the Punch Card app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a stamp card widget to your Wix site. Each logged-in member sees their own card, with one stamp for each paid order. Your dashboard shows the program settings and a list of customers with stamps.

Data the app handles

  • Orders: to count stamps, the app reads your site's paid eCommerce orders (order ID, number, date, payment status, total, which app sold the items, and the buyer's contact ID, email and billing name). It reads them when a member opens their card and when you open the dashboard. It does not copy them anywhere.
  • The logged-in member: the app's backend on Wix learns who is asking from the member's Wix session (never from anything the browser sends) and looks up that member's contact ID. The widget only ever receives that one member's stamp count and reward codes. Other customers' data is never sent to a visitor.
  • Stored in your site's own collections (Punch Card settings, Punch Card stamps and rewards): your program settings, the staff PIN, staff stamps (contact ID and time), rewards given (contact ID, card number, time, coupon code). You can see and delete them in your site's CMS.
  • Coupons (paid plan): when a card is full the app creates one single-use coupon in your site.
  • Widget settings: title, colours, stamp shape and words. Wix stores them with your site.
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan of this app.

The widget sets no cookies and stores nothing in the browser.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. Everything stays in your Wix site. The developer never receives your customers, their orders or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
