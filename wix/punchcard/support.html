Support: Punch Card

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what you saw. A screenshot helps. Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the card? Installing the app adds the card to the page you were on. A members page or your member area is a good home for it. To add it elsewhere, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets, then Punch Card.

What gives a stamp? One paid order. In the dashboard you choose which orders count: store orders, food orders, paid bookings. You can set a minimum order total and the date counting starts (the day you installed the app, unless you change it). Canceled orders drop off the card.

Why does a customer have no stamps? The card belongs to a site member. The customer must be logged in, and the order must be placed with the same contact (the same email). Guest orders under another email land on another contact.

What happens when a card is full? On the free plan the card says the reward is ready; hand it over and press Mark given in the dashboard. On the paid plan the member gets a single-use coupon code on their card and the next card starts by itself.

How do staff stamps work? (paid plan) Set a PIN of 4 to 8 digits in the dashboard. The customer opens their card on their phone and taps Staff stamp; your staff type the PIN. One staff stamp per customer every 2 minutes, and 5 wrong PINs lock it for 15 minutes.

Can a member see someone else's card? No. The app works out who is asking from the member's own login and only reads that member's orders.
