Getting help

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with the name of your site and what you tried. We answer within two business days.

Common questions

  • The widget shows nothing: open the dashboard, write the document, then check the widget's settings on the page: it shows the document you pick there.
  • The text is out of date: press Write again after changing an answer; the page shows the newest version you saved.
  • A document is locked: the free plan writes the privacy policy; the other four documents come with Premium.
  • Plans and billing: subscriptions are handled by the platform in your site's dashboard under Apps. Cancelling stops the next charge and keeps the free plan.

Data

Deleting the app removes the documents and answers the app stored on your site. See the privacy policy for details.
