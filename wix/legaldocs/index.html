What it does

Any site that takes payments, runs a newsletter or counts visits needs a privacy policy, and often a cookie and returns notice too. Writing them by hand takes an afternoon. This app turns a short questionnaire into five readable documents: privacy policy, cookie policy, terms of sale, returns and withdrawal policy, and imprint.

Fill in the business name, address, contact e-mail and country, tick what your site does, and set the days for returns. Press Write and the app writes it from its plain-word clause templates, using your answers throughout.

Add the widget to a page and choose the document it shows. It reads the published text from the app's backend, so a change shows on the live page without republishing the site.

The free plan gives you the privacy policy. Premium writes all five documents, keeps the version history, adds document titles in German, Spanish, French and Portuguese, and notes when the templates change. Premium costs $6 a month, or $5 a month billed yearly.

These are templates, not legal advice: each ends with a line asking you to have it checked for your country and business. Your answers stay in your own site.
