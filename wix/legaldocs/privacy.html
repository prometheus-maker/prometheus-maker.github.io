This policy describes what the Legal Docs: Policies & Terms app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you answer a short questionnaire and write legal documents, and a site widget that shows the published document on one of your pages.

Data the app handles

  • Answers: the business name, address, contact e-mail, country, the days for returns, and the VAT and register numbers you enter are stored in the app's data collection on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Documents: the text the app writes from those answers, with its version history, is stored in the same collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the widget asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your site's account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no visitor logs, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. A visitor to your page sees only the document you published.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Removing the app removes its collection and everything in it.
