Effective date: 2026-11-22

This policy describes what the Reviews Backup: Review Export app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where it reads your store's product reviews with the store's review service and keeps dated backups you can download as CSV files.

Data the app handles

  • Reviews: the app reads the review id, product, rating, full text, author name, reply, moderation status, helpful votes and dates through the review service, and stores the backup it makes in the app's own data collections on your site.
  • Product names: the app reads product names and ids through the store service so each backup row names its product.
  • Backups and settings: the backups, their dates, the restore ledger and your settings are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Removing a backup deletes its stored rows. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
