Support: Ingredient Stock: Recipe Cost

The Ingredient Stock: Recipe Cost app keeps one list of the ingredients you buy, with their cost and stock, and works out what each dish costs.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Ingredient Stock: Recipe Cost" in your Wix dashboard. Add each ingredient with its unit, cost per unit, current stock and reorder level, or press "Paste a list" and drop in the rows from a supplier sheet: name, unit, cost, stock, reorder level, tab or comma separated.

The Ingredients tab shows every row with an In stock or Reorder chip. Low stock lists the ones at or below their reorder level with the shortfall and how much to order.

On Premium, open Recipes & costing, press New recipe, pick one of your store products and add the ingredients it uses. The app shows the dish cost and the margin against the product price. When an order is paid, it subtracts the recipe from stock and records the deduction; the printable reorder sheet lists what to buy.

Questions people ask

  • Does it change my stock in Wix? No. It keeps its own ingredient list and reads product stock only where a recipe needs the product.
  • What counts as low? Stock at or below the reorder level you set for that ingredient.
  • How many ingredients are covered? 15 on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? Recipe costing, automatic deduction from paid orders and the printable reorder sheet.
  • Does it send me e-mails? No. Everything shows in the dashboard.
  • Where is the data? In the app's collections on your Wix site; removing the app removes them.
