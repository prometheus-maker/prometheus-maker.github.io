Effective date: 2026-11-20

This policy describes what the Ingredient Stock: Recipe Cost app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard that keeps your ingredient list with cost, stock and reorder level, the recipe of each product, and the stock left after paid orders.

Data the app handles

  • Ingredients, recipes and stock log: the names, units, costs, stock counts, reorder levels and recipe lines you enter are stored in the app's collections on your Wix site. Each deduction adds one row to the stock log.
  • Products: the app reads your shop's product names and prices to cost a dish and to match an order line to its recipe. It keeps no separate copy of them.
  • Orders: when an order is paid, the app reads its line items (product and quantity) to subtract the recipe's ingredients. It stores the deduction and its note, not the customer's details.
  • Plan status: the app asks whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. What you enter stays on your Wix site.

Keeping and removing data

Your ingredients, recipes and stock log stay on your Wix site until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
