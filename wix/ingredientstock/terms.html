These terms cover the Ingredient Stock: Recipe Cost app ("the app") for Wix sites.

The service

The app gives a shop a dashboard page with its ingredient list, low stock list and, on Premium, recipe costing and automatic deduction of ingredients from paid orders. It reads products and orders from your Wix shop and stores only your own records.

Plans

The free plan keeps up to 15 ingredients with cost, stock and a low stock list. Premium lifts the limit and adds recipe costing, automatic deduction and the printable reorder sheet. Plans are billed by Wix through the App Market; the price shown on the app's page at the time of purchase applies. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the free plan's limit applies again from then.

Your part

You use the app only on sites you are allowed to use it on, and you check the numbers before ordering stock or relying on a food cost. The app does the arithmetic from what you enter; it does not place supplier orders or change your product stock in the shop.
