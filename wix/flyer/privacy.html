This policy describes what the Flyer: Weekly Ad & Specials app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app shows a store's current weekly flyer on pages of its Wix site. The store's owner uploads the flyer page images to the site's Media Manager from the app's dashboard page, together with a title, the dates the flyer is valid and, on the paid plan, a list of items with prices. Visitors see the flyer while it is valid, and can open a print view of it.

Data the app handles

  • Flyer details: the title, the dates it runs, the list of page images and, on the paid plan, the item names, prices and notes the store typed in. They are kept in the app's own collections in the site's Wix account, so the site can show its flyer and keep past flyers.
  • Store details: the store name and the print header line the owner types, kept the same way.
  • Page images: the app refers to images in the site's Media Manager by address. It never copies, stores or changes the image files themselves; visitors load them from Wix's own image servers.
  • Plan status: the widget, the print view and the dashboard ask the app's backend whether the site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the dashboard page remembers whether the owner closed its short intro box and its review prompt. Nothing is kept in the visitor's browser.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. The print view carries a signature in its address, so the item list is only served to someone the store gave that link to; it shows the flyer the store published and nothing about the person reading it.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives the store's flyer data, its images or anything about its visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
