Support: Flyer: Weekly Ad & Specials

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the flyer?

Installing the app adds the flyer to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Weekly Flyer.

How do I put my flyer on the site?

Open the Weekly Flyer dashboard page, click New flyer, and upload the page image (the poster you already made) from the Media Manager. Give it a title, pick the date it is valid through, and save. Publish the site once; after that the flyer shows on the live page.

Why does the flyer not show on my site?

The flyer shows between its start date and its valid-through date. Check that it is switched on and that today falls inside that range. With no live flyer, visitors see a short "no flyer this week" line instead.

What happens when the week ends?

The flyer comes down by itself on the day after its valid-through date, so last week's prices never stay on the site. On Premium, it also moves to your archive page.

How do I print the flyer?

Click Print under the flyer. The print view opens with your store name, the flyer pages and (on Premium) the item list, laid out for A4 paper. Use your browser's print command, or Save as PDF, to keep a copy.

Can I put more than one page in a flyer?

Yes, on Premium: add up to six page images to one flyer. On a phone visitors swipe between them.

Can I add the prices as text?

Yes, on Premium: add an item name, a price and an optional note for each special, and they appear in a list under the flyer and in the print view.

Can I prepare next week's flyer now?

Yes, on Premium: set a start date in the future and the flyer stays hidden until that day. The one running now stays up until its end date.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps working with one flyer, one page image and the valid-through date, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app shows the flyer you uploaded, keeps nothing about your visitors, and sets no cookies.
