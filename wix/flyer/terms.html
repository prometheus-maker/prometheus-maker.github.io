1. What you get

Flyer: Weekly Ad & Specials is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your store's current flyer, a print view of it, and a dashboard page where you upload the flyer pages and set the dates. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the editor and the dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one flyer with one page image and its valid-through date, with a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds up to six pages per flyer, the item list with prices and notes, a start date and scheduled dates, an archive page of past flyers, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
