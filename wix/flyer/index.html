What it does

Your weekly specials already exist as an image — the one you post, print or hand out. This app keeps it on your site for the week it is valid, then takes it down.

Upload the flyer page from the Media Manager, give it a title and the dates it runs. The widget shows the page with a quiet "Valid 12–18 Jan" line under it, and on a phone the pages swipe. Below it you can list this week's items with prices, so shoppers can read the deals without zooming into the picture.

The Print button opens a plain page with your store name, the flyer pages and the item list, laid out for A4. Print it or save it as a PDF for the fridge door and the shop window.

The dates do the housekeeping. A flyer goes up on its start date and comes down when the week ends, so last week's prices never sit on the site. Premium keeps every past flyer on a page of your site and lets you schedule next week's ad now.

It suits any shop that prints a weekly ad: a grocery, a hardware store, a butcher, a farm stand, a pharmacy.
