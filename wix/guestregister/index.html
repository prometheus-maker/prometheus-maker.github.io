What it does

Holiday lets in Spain, Portugal, Italy and Morocco have to send a guest register after check-in. This app turns that into a private link the guest fills in before arrival, and a file you submit yourself.

Create the stay in the dashboard and send the guest the link. The guest types name, date of birth, nationality, document type and number, and home address on one page made for a phone. The stay flips to complete and the register row is ready.

Export the register as CSV, or one stay as a PDF. The app never connects to a police system and sends nothing for you.

Privacy is the design: details are stored only in your site's own data collections, never on our servers, with no copies and no analytics. Each row gets a delete date (90 days by default) and expired rows are removed automatically.
