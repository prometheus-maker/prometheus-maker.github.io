How to set it up

1. Install the app and open Guest Register in your dashboard.

2. In Properties, add the place you rent. The free plan covers one.

3. In Stays, add a stay: property, guest name, arrival and departure.

4. Send the guest the check-in link or the ready-made message. The guest fills in the register details before arrival.

5. When the stay is complete, press CSV for the register row; on Premium, press PDF for the per-stay form and CSV of all stays for the whole register.

Common questions

  • The link says it is not complete or expired: the stay may already be checked in or deleted. Create a new link from the stay row.
  • I cannot add another property: the free plan covers one property and the last ten stays. Premium removes both limits.
  • Where is the guest data kept: in your site's own data collections, never on our servers, and rows are deleted automatically on their delete date.
  • Can the app send the register to the police: no. You export the file and submit it yourself.
  • Is the country text legal advice: no. It is a starting point you can edit; check your local requirement.
