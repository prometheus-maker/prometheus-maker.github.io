Plans

The free plan covers one property and the last ten stays, with the check-in link, per-stay CSV and automatic deletion at 90 days. Premium covers unlimited properties and every stay, adds PDF export, the full CSV and a retention window from 1 to 3650 days. Premium is billed by the platform at $12 a month, or $9.60 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends. Free rows past the tenth cannot be created; nothing is silently deleted.
