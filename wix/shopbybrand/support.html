Support: Shop by Brand: Brand Filter

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the wall and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the brand wall?

Installing the app adds the wall to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Shop by Brand.

Where do the brands come from?

From the Brand field of your store products. In your dashboard open Store Products, edit a product and pick or type its Brand. The brand shows on the wall by itself, with how many products it has.

The editor shows brands I don't sell

Those are samples so you can see the look before any product has a brand. Your live site shows only your own brands, and hides the wall while there are none.

Can I leave a brand off the wall?

Yes, on every plan: Settings, Your brands, untick it.

How do I add brand logos?

On Premium: Settings, Your brands, Logo next to the brand, then pick the logo from your media (or paste an image link). Brands without a logo show their name.

Can a menu item open one brand?

On Premium: add ?brand= and the brand to the page address, for example /brands?brand=lion-brand, and point the menu item at it.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The wall keeps working with up to 12 brands and 8 products per brand in the light look. Logos, the A to Z letters, the list and carousel layouts, brand links and the other themes switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the wall reads your brands and the products of a brand a visitor opens, and sends nothing to the developer.
