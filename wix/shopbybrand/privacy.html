This policy describes what the Shop by Brand: Brand Filter app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a brand wall to pages of your Wix site. It shows the brands of your store products with how many products each has, and when a visitor clicks a brand, that brand's products.

Data the app handles

  • Your store brands: their names and how many products each has.
  • Your store products, for the brand a visitor opens: the app's backend asks Wix for that brand's visible products and passes on only what the wall shows: each product's name, price, sale price, main image, product page link and whether it is sold out. Hidden products are left out. Orders, customers, buyers and inventory numbers are not read.
  • Widget settings: your heading, texts, which brands show, logos you choose, layout, theme, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last list of brands and the plan so the wall shows at once on the next page.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows brands and products. Clicking a product opens its product page on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your products, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
