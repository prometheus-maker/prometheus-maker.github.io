What it does

Shop by Brand puts every brand you sell on one wall, with how many products each one has. A shopper clicks a brand and its products open right inside the wall: photo, name, price, sale price and a sold out mark, each linking to its product page. No page or category per brand to build.

The wall reads the Brand field of your store products. Give a product a brand in your dashboard and it shows up under that brand by itself. A new brand appears on the wall the same way, and counts follow your products.

You choose which brands show, the order (A to Z or most products first), how many brands, the heading, whether prices and counts show, columns and font. While no product has a brand, the wall stays hidden on your site, and the editor shows sample brands so you can see the layout.

The free plan shows up to 12 brands with counts, 8 products per brand and a small credit link. Premium shows every brand and every product with Show more, adds your brand logos, A to Z letters for long lists, a list or carousel layout, links that open one brand for your menu (?brand=lion-brand), light, warm and dark themes, an accent colour, and removes the credit link.
