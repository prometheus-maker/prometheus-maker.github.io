Support: Laurel

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the board and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the board?

Installing the app adds a board to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Laurel.

The board is empty

Laurel shows members of your site's loyalty program who have points. Turn the program on in your dashboard (Loyalty Program) and members appear once they earn points. Until then the board shows the line you set under "When nobody has points yet".

How fast do new points show?

Within about 10 minutes. The board is rebuilt from your program at most every 10 minutes.

How are names shown?

First name and the first letter of the last name (Ana P.) unless you choose otherwise in Settings: first name only, or no names at all (Member #1). Emails are never shown.

A member does not want to be on the board

Choose "No names (Member #1)" in Settings, so nobody is named.

Why do the totals say "stars" (or "beans")?

Laurel uses the name you gave your points in the loyalty program settings.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The board keeps working with the program totals and the top 3. More members, tier badges, ranking by points held now and the podium switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: names are shortened on the server, emails never reach the page, and the app stores nothing.
