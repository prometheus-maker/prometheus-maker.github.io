This policy describes what the Laurel app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a leaderboard to pages of your Wix site that shows the top members of your loyalty program by points, and the program's totals.

Data the app handles

  • Loyalty accounts: the app's backend on Wix asks the Wix Loyalty service for the accounts with the most points, the program totals (points earned, points redeemed, number of members), your tier names and the name you gave your points.
  • Member names: to build the board, the backend reads each listed member's contact name and shortens it before anything is sent to the page: first name and the first letter of the last name, first name only, or no name at all, as you choose in Settings. Emails, contact IDs, phone numbers and full last names are never sent to the page.
  • Widget settings: your name choice, title, number of members, layout, colours and font. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Short-term memory: the backend keeps the last board it built for your site in memory for up to 10 minutes, so busy pages don't ask Wix again on every visit. It is not written to disk.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last board it saw (the same short names and points shown on the page) so it appears at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The app keeps no database and no logs of visitors.

Your members

Showing members' first names on a public page is your decision as the site owner. If your members have not agreed to that, choose "No names (Member #1)" in Settings.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your members' data, your points or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

AI assistant tool

If you use the site's AI assistant in your dashboard, it can ask the app for your top members and program totals. That answer goes only to you, in your dashboard, and contains names, points and tiers, never emails.
