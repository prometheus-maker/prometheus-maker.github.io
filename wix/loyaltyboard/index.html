What it does

Laurel puts a loyalty leaderboard on any page of your site. It shows the members with the most points in your loyalty program, and a strip with the program's totals: points earned, points redeemed and members.

The board reads your program directly, so you never type anything in. When a member earns points, the board shows it within about ten minutes. It uses the name you gave your points, so a cafe that hands out beans sees "beans earned".

Privacy comes first. Visitors see a first name and the first letter of the last name, such as Ana P., or only the first name, or no names at all, such as Member #1. Names are shortened on the server before they reach the page, and emails or contact details are never sent. These choices are free on every plan.

Before anyone has points, a short line you write shows in place of an empty list. You can set the title, colours and font, and switch the totals or the board off.

The free plan shows the totals and the top 3 members. Premium shows up to 10 members, adds tier badges such as Gold or Silver, can rank by the points members hold now, and adds a podium for the top 3.
