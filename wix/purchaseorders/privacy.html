This policy describes what the Purchase Orders: Reorder app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard. It keeps a list of your suppliers, a reorder point and order quantity for each product, and the purchase orders you place. It reads your products, their stock and your orders from your store, and on the paid plan it can add received quantities back to your stock.

Data the app handles

  • Supplier data: the names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, lead times and notes you enter. These are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Product rules: the reorder point and order quantity per product, and which supplier each product comes from.
  • Purchase orders: number, date, status, lines with SKU, quantity and unit cost, and the received quantities and last price paid.
  • Store data: product names, SKUs, stock counts and your orders, read from your store when the page opens. Received quantities are written back to inventory only when you press Receive goods on the paid plan.
  • Plan status: the app asks the platform whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and that one fact decides what the page offers.
  • Settings: the shop-wide reorder point, default order quantity and currency.

The app sends no e-mail, keeps no visitor logs and shares no data with third parties. Everything it produces is made in your browser: CSV files and order sheets are downloaded by you.

Keeping and removing data

Suppliers, product rules, orders and settings stay on your site until you delete them or remove the app. Removing the app deletes its collections and everything in them.
