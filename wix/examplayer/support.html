Support: Exam Player: Timed Quizzes

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan. Paid sites get priority replies.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the quiz involved, and what you saw. A screenshot of the dashboard page helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days; priority support aims for one business day.

Quick answers

How do I start?

Install the app from the Wix App Market, add the Exam Player widget to a members-only page in the Wix editor, open the widget settings and enter your quiz's ID, the minutes and how many questions to draw, then publish. Signed-in members see the exam; signed-out visitors see a prompt to sign in.

Where do the questions come from?

From a quiz in your Wix Online Programs. The app draws a random set of questions from that quiz each time an exam starts.

How is it graded?

On the server, against the questions stored when the exam started. Late submissions score zero. The member's browser never sends a score.

A member ran out of time

When the countdown reaches zero the exam submits itself and shows the result. An attempt left open past its deadline is closed and marked late.

What does the dashboard show?

Every attempt with the member's name, score, pass or fail, whether it finished in time, and the mode. It refreshes when you open it.

Nothing shows on the page

The widget is for signed-in members. Check that the page is members-only and that the quiz ID in the settings is correct.

How do I cancel or change plan?

Manage the subscription from your Wix dashboard under Manage Apps; Wix handles billing and refunds.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then Exam Player: Timed Quizzes.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app runs on Wix, and the developer receives nothing.
