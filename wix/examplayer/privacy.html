Privacy Policy: Exam Player: Timed Quizzes

Effective date: 2026-11-28 Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io) Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Exam Player: Timed Quizzes app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app turns a quiz in your Wix Online Programs into a timed exam: it draws a random set of questions from the quiz, shows them one at a time in a widget on your site with a countdown, and grades the answers on the server. The site owner opens a dashboard page in the Wix dashboard to see the attempts and their scores.

Data the app handles

  • Quiz content: the quiz and its questions, read through Wix's Online Programs APIs so the app can draw and grade them.
  • Member identity: the signed-in member's Wix member ID, used to tie an attempt to the member who started it, and the member's name read from Wix Members for the owner's dashboard list.
  • Attempts: one row per attempt, stored in the site's own Wix Data collection: member ID, quiz ID, the drawn questions and the member's answers, the start time and server deadline, when it was submitted, whether it was late, and the score. These rows stay on your site's Wix data and are visible to you as the site owner; the app deletes nothing on its own.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether the site is on the free or the paid plan, and counts the site's attempts in the current month to apply the free plan's limit.
  • Browser settings: the member's progress through the current exam is kept in the page while it is open.

The app does not collect payments, card details, passwords or email addresses. It stores nothing on a server of the developer's.

Where the data goes

Everything is read and written through Wix's own APIs by the app's code running on Wix's platform. The developer has no other server and never receives your quizzes, members or attempt data. Nothing is shared with anyone else. Removing the app stops all processing.
