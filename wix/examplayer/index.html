What it does

Give every learner a fair timed sitting on your Online Programs quiz. Paste a quiz ID, set the minutes and how many questions to draw, and each member gets a personal paper from the pool. The exam runs one question at a time with no going back and a countdown always in view, so opening another tab cannot buy extra time. When the clock hits zero, the paper is handed in for them.

The score is worked out on our server from the questions that were actually drawn, never from the browser, and the result appears in your dashboard attempt log with the date, the length and pass or fail. The attempt is also written back as a quiz submission, so your program keeps its own record.

Set a pool of 30 questions, draw 10, allow 90 minutes: every sitting is a little different, and on Pro a learner who repeats the exam does not get the same paper twice. Free runs one exam in order with ten attempts a month; Pro adds random pools, practice mode and a history export.
