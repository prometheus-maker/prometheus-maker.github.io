1. What you get

Exam Player: Timed Quizzes is a Wix app for Online Programs. It turns a quiz in your programs into a timed exam: a random set of questions drawn from the quiz, shown one at a time with no going back, a countdown from a server-side deadline, automatic submission when time runs out, and server-side grading against the stored questions. The site owner gets a dashboard page listing every attempt with its score, pass or fail, and whether it was finished in time. You install it from the Wix App Market, add the widget to a members-only page and set the quiz, the minutes and the number of questions.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan gives one exam setup and 10 attempts a month per site. The paid plan adds unlimited exams, random question pools, practice mode and attempt-history export. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. There is no free trial.

3. Exam integrity

The score comes from the server, which grades the member's answers against the questions it stored when the exam started; the browser never sends a score. Answers submitted after the deadline score zero. The app shows the member their result but does not keep an answer key in the page.

4. Your responsibilities

You decide which programs, quizzes and members are on your site and where the widget appears. The questions and passing grade the app uses come from the quiz Wix holds for your program; a quiz change while an exam is running affects only later exams.
