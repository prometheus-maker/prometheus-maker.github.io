1. What you get

Visitor Counter: Live Stats is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your site's visitor numbers, read from your site's Wix Analytics. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one counter (unique visitors or visits, for the last 30 days or this month) as a card or one line, with a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds the all-time total with a starting number, today, the last 7 days and this year, up to three numbers side by side, a goal bar, a "contacted us" line, badge and odometer styles, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

You decide which numbers are shown on your site. The starting number you type is shown as part of the all-time total; make sure it is true.

4. Accuracy

The numbers come from Wix Analytics, as Wix counts them, refreshed about once an hour. The app does not count visitors itself and cannot correct numbers Wix reports.
