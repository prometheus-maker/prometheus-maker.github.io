Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the counter and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the counter?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on. To add it somewhere else in the editor: Add Elements, then App Widgets, then Visitor Counter.

Why is my number different from my old counter?

The counter shows what your site's Wix Analytics counts. Old hit counters counted every page load with their own script, so their numbers are usually higher. In the settings you can switch between unique visitors and visits.

My number did not change after a visit

The totals refresh about once an hour, and Wix Analytics itself can take a while to count new visits.

Can I keep the total from my old counter?

Yes, on the paid plan: choose the period "All time" and type your old total as the starting number. It is added to everything counted from the first day the app knows about.

My site is new and the number is small

Use "Hide the counter below" in the settings. The counter stays hidden until the number reaches what you typed.
