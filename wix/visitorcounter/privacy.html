This policy describes what the Visitor Counter: Live Stats app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows how many people visited the site, for example "12,480 visitors in the last 30 days".

Data the app handles

  • Site analytics totals: the app's backend reads daily site-wide totals from your site's Wix Analytics: unique visitors, visits (sessions), clicks to contact and forms sent. These are counts for the whole site. The app never reads anything about a single visitor: no IP address, no device, no location, no pages they viewed.
  • Monthly totals: the app stores one row per month (month, visitors, visits, first day counted) in a data collection on your own site, so the all-time total survives after Wix Analytics drops days older than about two months. You can see these rows in your site's CMS.
  • Plan: the backend reads whether your site has the free or the paid plan.

What the app does not do

  • It adds no tracking script and sets no cookie for your visitors. The widget only loads the totals and remembers the last ones in the visitor's browser storage so they show at once on the next page.
  • It does not sell, share or use any data for advertising.
  • Our backend keeps the totals in memory for about an hour to answer quickly, and keeps no logs of visitors.

Retention and deletion

The monthly rows stay in your site's data collection until you delete them or remove the app. Totals cached in memory expire within an hour.
