Support: Raffle: Giveaway & Prize Draw

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plans.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the raffle title and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I put the raffle on my page?

In the Wix editor, open Add Elements, choose Apps, pick Raffle: Giveaway & Prize Draw and drop the widget where you want it. The dashboard holds the raffle itself; the widget shows it.

How do I make a raffle?

Open the app from your Wix dashboard and press New raffle. Give it a title, the prize, a close date and, on the paid plans, how many entries each email may have. Save it; it appears on your site as soon as it is saved.

What does a visitor do?

They type a name and an email and tick the consent box. They are entered once; the form then says so. One entry per email by default, so the same address cannot fill the list.

What happens at the close date?

The widget stops taking entries at the minute you set and says entries are closed. On the paid plans it shows the digest of the frozen entry list at that point.

How do I draw a winner?

Open the app, find the raffle, press Draw. The winner appears on your page with the time of the draw. On the paid plans the seed and the digest are published with it.

How can an entrant check the draw? (paid plans)

Each entry is hashed as SHA-256(seed + ":" + entry id) and the smallest hash wins. The seed and the digest are on the page, so anyone can run the same formula and land on the same name.

Can I add entries for tickets sold at a stall? (Premium)

Yes. On the Bonus entries tab, paste one ticket per line as code, email, or import a spreadsheet. A ticket number that is already counted is never added twice. Nobody is charged: the ticket is your own.

Where are the entries kept?

In your own site's collections. Deleting the raffle deletes its entries and its draw with it. You can download the entries as CSV on the paid plans.

Is entry ever paid?

No. The app never takes a payment and never asks a visitor for payment details. If your raffle takes money anywhere, it does so outside this widget and under rules you are responsible for.
