1. What you get

Raffle: Giveaway & Prize Draw is a Wix app: a widget that runs a free-entry prize draw on your page, and a dashboard where you set the prize, the close date and the entries per email, read the entries and make the draw. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix site.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan runs one raffle, shows the winner you draw, and adds a small credit link on the widget. The paid plans run several raffles, add entries per email, the ticket-number bonus, CSV export, the published seed and digest, and remove the credit link. 4. How the draw works

Each entry is turned into SHA-256(seed + ":" + entry id), and the smallest hash wins. The seed is random, and on the paid plans it is published with the entries digest, before the winner is read, so a visitor can repeat the pick. The app shows what it computed; it cannot prove what happened outside your site, and a fair result still depends on you not changing entries after the draw.
