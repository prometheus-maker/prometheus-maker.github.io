What it does

Run a raffle without spreadsheets or ticket stubs. Put the Raffle widget on your page, then set the prize, a close date and entries per email in the app. Visitors see the prize, a countdown and an entry form; at closing time the page says so. The winner appears when you draw.

Entry is free: name, email and a consent tick. One entry per email by default, with quiet bot protection: a hidden field or a too-fast form is ignored. If your fair sells tickets anywhere, not only online, Premium adds a bonus entry for each ticket number you paste or import; a number already counted is never added twice.

The draw is public and repeatable: a 32-byte seed and the entry list hashed with SHA-256, both published with the winner. Anyone can repeat the pick: each entry is hashed as SHA-256(seed + ":" + entry id), smallest hash wins. Premium publishes the digest at close, before the draw, so the list cannot change afterwards.

Free: one raffle, a winner you draw yourself, a small credit link. Premium: every raffle, entries per email, the ticket-number bonus, CSV export, the published proof and no credit line. Pro: several winners per draw, bulk ticket import, export of every entry and draw.
