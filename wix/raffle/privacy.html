This policy describes what the Raffle: Giveaway & Prize Draw app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app puts a raffle on your own Wix site: a widget on your page shows the prize, a close date with a countdown and an entry form, and closes itself at the minute you set. In your Wix dashboard you see the raffles, the entries and the draw, and you can draw a winner whose seed and entry-list digest anyone can check.

Data the app handles

  • Your raffle settings: title, prize, description, close date, entries per email, number of winners and whether the credit link shows, stored in a collection on your site.
  • Entries: the name and email a visitor types, the time they ticked the consent box, whether the entry came from the form or from a ticket number you added, the ticket number, and a hash of the visitor's IP address that is used only to ignore repeated submissions. Stored in a collection on your site.
  • Draws: the seed, the digest of the entry list, the winners and the time of the draw, stored in a collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or a paid plan.
  • Browser settings: a small marker in the visitor's browser so the form shows "you are entered" when they come back. It holds no personal data and is not sent anywhere.

Where the data goes

The widget and the dashboard talk only to Wix's own APIs and to the app code hosted by Wix. Entry lists and draws stay in your site's collections; the developer runs no server of its own, never receives, stores or sees your entries, emails, ticket numbers or draws, and nothing is shared with anyone else. No payment is taken from a visitor at any point.

The choice you make about the data

You decide what the entry form says, whether you tick the consent box requirement in your own notices, and how long you keep entries before deleting the raffle. Deleting a raffle in the dashboard deletes its entries and its draw with it.
