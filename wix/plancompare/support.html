Support: Plan Compare

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the table and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the table?

Installing the app adds the table to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Plan Compare.

Where do the plans come from?

From Pricing Plans in your dashboard. The table shows your public plans with their names, prices, billing period, free trial, fees and benefits. Edit a plan there and the table follows; there is nothing to type in the widget.

The editor shows sample plans

Your site has no public plans yet. Create plans in Dashboard > Pricing Plans and they replace the samples. Visitors never see the samples: without plans, the live site shows nothing.

Why are two benefits in different rows?

Rows match benefits by their wording. "Unlimited classes" and "Unlimited classes." share a row; "Unlimited classes" and "All classes" do not. To show a value per plan, write benefits as "Classes: 8" and "Classes: Unlimited": they share the row "Classes".

Which plan gets the ribbon?

The plan you made the highlighted plan in Pricing Plans. On Premium you can pick another plan, change the ribbon text or turn it off.

Where does the button go?

To the checkout of that plan. If the checkout can't open from that page, the button opens your Plans & Pricing page, or a page you choose in Settings.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The table keeps working with up to three plans, your button text, accent colour and font, and a small credit link. The other plans, your highlight choice, hidden rows, row order, styles and the swipe layout switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the table shows the plans your site already shows to every visitor and stores nothing.
