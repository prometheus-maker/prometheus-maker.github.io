1. What you get

Plan Compare is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your site's public pricing plans side by side in a comparison table. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows up to three plans in your order with rows for plan length, free trial, fees and benefits, your button text, accent colour and font, and a small credit link. The paid plan shows every public plan and adds the choice of highlighted plan and ribbon text, hidden and reordered rows, table styles and colours, a swipe layout on phones, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
