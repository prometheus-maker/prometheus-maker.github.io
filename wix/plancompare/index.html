What it does

Plan Compare puts your pricing plans side by side in one table, so a visitor sees at a glance what each plan adds. Plans are columns, benefits are rows, and every cell shows a tick, a dash or the plan's own value, such as "8 a month" against "Unlimited".

You type nothing: the table reads the plans you already sell with Pricing Plans: names, prices, billing period, free trial, joining fee and benefits. Change a price or add a benefit in your dashboard and the table on your site changes with it. Benefits worded the same line up in one row, and "Classes: 8" next to "Classes: Unlimited" share the row "Classes".

The plan you highlighted as most popular gets a coloured column and a ribbon. Each button opens that plan's checkout. On phones the table turns into one card per plan, with the same ticks and dashes.

The free plan shows up to three plans you pick, in your order, with your button text, accent colour and font, and a small credit link under the table. Premium shows every plan, lets you choose the highlighted plan and its ribbon text, hide or reorder rows, pick striped or borderless styles and your own colours, keep the grid swipeable on phones, and removes the credit link.
