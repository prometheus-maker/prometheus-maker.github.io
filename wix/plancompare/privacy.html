This policy describes what the Plan Compare app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a table to pages of your Wix site that compares your pricing plans side by side.

Data the app handles

  • Your public pricing plans: the app's backend on Wix reads the plans your site already shows every visitor on its Plans & Pricing page (name, description, price, currency, billing period, plan length, free trial days, fees, benefits and which plan is highlighted) and passes them to the widget. Private plans, orders, buyers and members are never read.
  • Widget settings: the plans you pick and their order, texts, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan of this app. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last plan list it saw so the table shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. When a visitor clicks a plan's button, Wix opens that plan's checkout (or your Plans & Pricing page); the purchase itself is handled by Wix Pricing Plans, not by this app.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your plans, your buyers or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
