Support: Headcount

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the counter and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the counter?

Installing the app adds a counter to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Headcount.

Who is counted?

Every approved member of your site: the people in your dashboard's Site Members list. Members waiting for approval and blocked members are not counted.

It shows "Be one of our first members"

That line shows while the site has no members yet (or, on Premium, until the count reaches your "show from" number). You can change the text in Settings.

How fast does a new member show?

The count is read when a visitor loads the page, so a new member shows the next time the page is opened (within about a minute).

My site has no members yet

Members join through the Members Area (sign-up and log-in). Add it from the editor if your site does not have it; Headcount counts the members it creates.

Whose names appear on Premium?

Only members whose profile is public, and only the first word of their public name, at most three. E-mails, last names and photos are never shown. Turn the setting off and no names appear.

Can I add members I had before the site?

Yes, on Premium: type them under "Start counting from" and they are added to the live count.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The counter keeps working with the live count and the weekly line. Names, the monthly line, the starting number, "show from" and the circle style switch off.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the counter shows numbers and, only if you turn it on, up to three first names from public profiles.
