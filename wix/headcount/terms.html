1. What you get

Headcount is a Wix app with a site widget that shows how many members your Wix site has. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows the live member count as a big number or a goal bar, a "joined this week" line, and your text, colours, font and a button. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.
