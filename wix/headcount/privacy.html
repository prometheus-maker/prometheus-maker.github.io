This policy describes what the Headcount app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a counter to pages of your Wix site that shows how many members your site has, and how many joined recently.

Data the app handles

  • Member counts: the app's backend on Wix asks the Wix Members service how many approved members your site has, and how many joined in the last 7 days (or 30 days, on the paid plan). It asks for counts; it does not copy or keep member records.
  • First names (paid plan, only if you turn it on): the backend reads the public profile names of the newest members whose profile is public and passes on the first word of each name, at most three. It never passes on e-mail addresses, last names, photos, member IDs or any other member data. Members with a private profile are never named.
  • Widget settings: your text, goal, starting number, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last counts it saw so the counter shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows numbers (and, if you turn it on, up to three first names). If you add a button, visitors who click it go to the link you entered.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your counts, your members' names or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
