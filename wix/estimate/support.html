Common questions

The calculator shows a service I deleted

Open the settings panel and press Save. The page shows what the panel saved; a change made in the editor is live once the page is published.

The total is wrong

Check the price and the unit on that line. A price per hour is multiplied by the number of hours the visitor sets, and a fixed price is a tick, not a quantity. Prices are entered in the smallest unit the panel accepts; the panel shows the price visitors will see under each field.

The send form does not appear

The form is part of the Premium plan. Open the app again from the dashboard to see the current plan; if it shows free after you paid, write to the address above with your site name.

A request did not arrive by email

The request is saved in the app's dashboard page even when the email is delayed. Open the page and check the list; if the request is there and the email is not, check the address set in the panel.

Can I show more than three services?

Three on the free plan, twelve on Premium. The panel warns when the site's plan cannot show every item you added.
