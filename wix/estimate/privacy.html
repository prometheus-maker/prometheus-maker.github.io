This policy covers the Estimate: Quote Calculator app ("the app") on your site.

What the app stores

The services, prices and texts you enter in the settings panel are stored with your site so the calculator can show them.

On the Premium plan, when a visitor sends an estimate, the app stores the name, email address and optional note the visitor typed, together with the items and the total, in a data collection on your site. The visitor sees the app's form before sending and can choose not to.

Email

Each sent request produces one email to the address you give in the app's settings. The email carries the visitor's details and the estimate. The app does not send marketing email and does not email the visitor.

What the app does not do

The app does not use cookies of its own, does not track visitors, and does not send data to the developer or to any other company. It reads your site's plan to know whether the Premium form is available.

Your choices

You can delete a request from the app's dashboard page. Removing the app removes the app's stored settings; requests remain in the site's data collection until you delete them.

Questions

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me.
