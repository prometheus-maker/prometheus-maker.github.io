1. What you get

Walk-in Queue: Live Wait Time is a Wix app that gives your staff a walk-in line in your dashboard and shows the live line on your site: the number being served, the next numbers, how many people are waiting and an estimated wait. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in your Wix dashboard and editor.

2. Your responsibility

You decide who is added to the line and in what order people are served. The estimated wait is a guess from your own service times; it is not a promise to your customers.

3. Plans and payment

The free plan takes 40 walk-ins a day and shows a small credit link under the board. The paid plan removes the daily limit and the credit link, adds a TV screen layout and downloads your walk-ins as CSV. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the app keeps working with the free plan's limits.
