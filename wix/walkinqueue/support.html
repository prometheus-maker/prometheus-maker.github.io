Support: Walk-in Queue: Live Wait Time

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what the board or the dashboard showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the board?

It was added to your home page when you installed the app. In the editor, drag it where people look first (your opening hours or contact page), click it and open Settings to change the heading, the paused and closed texts and the colours.

How do I run the line?

Open Apps, then Walk-in Queue, in your dashboard (a tablet at the counter works well). Add walk-in gives the next number; Call next brings up the next person; Done and No-show close a turn. Adding the first walk-in opens the line.

How is the wait worked out?

People waiting × minutes per person ÷ people served at once. Until three people have been served it uses the minutes you set; after that it uses the average of your last 20 real service times (from Call to Done).

Two of us serve at the same time.

Set "People served at once" to 2. Call next then no longer finishes the person being served; press Done for each.

Do numbers reset?

Yes, at midnight in the time zone of the device that first set up the line. The board shows an empty line on a new day until someone is added.

What does the free plan's daily limit count?

Every walk-in added that day. After 40 the Add button stops until midnight; the people already in the line can still be called.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The 40-a-day limit and the small credit link come back, and a TV screen board turns back into the normal board.
