This policy describes what the Walk-in Queue: Live Wait Time app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

Your staff run a walk-in line from the app's dashboard page: they add each walk-in (a first name and a note are optional), call the next number, and mark each turn done or a no-show. A board on your site shows the number being served, the next numbers, how many people are waiting and the estimated wait.

Data the app handles

  • Walk-ins: each walk-in is stored in a data collection on your own site with its number, the first name and note your staff typed (both optional), its status and the times it joined, was called and finished. Only you and the app can read it; you can delete rows in your CMS at any time.
  • Queue settings: open, paused or closed, your minutes per person, how many people you serve at once, your time zone, the current public board and a cached copy of your plan status are stored in a second data collection on your site.
  • The app does not ask for or store phone numbers, email addresses or any other contact details.

Site visitors

The board shows ticket numbers and the first letter of each first name (for example "15 J."), never full names or notes. It reads the board from the app's backend every 10 seconds while the page is open. It sets no cookies and does not track anyone.

Where the data goes

Everything stays inside your Wix site. The developer never receives your walk-in list, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The app sends no emails or text messages.
