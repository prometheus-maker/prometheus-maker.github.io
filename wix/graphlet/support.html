Support: Graphlet

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the chart and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the chart?

Installing the app adds a sample chart to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Graphlet. Each chart has its own data.

How do I enter my numbers?

Click the chart in the editor, then Settings. Under Data, type a label and a value on each row. Add row adds one more; the bin icon deletes a row.

Can I paste from a spreadsheet?

Yes, on Premium. Copy the cells from your spreadsheet, with the labels in the first column and the series names in the first row, and paste them into "Paste from a spreadsheet".

My numbers disappear when I publish

They shouldn't, on any plan. Click Publish in the editor after your changes and reload the live page. If they still don't show, email the site address and we'll look.

Why only 8 rows?

The free plan draws up to 8 rows and one data series per chart. Premium allows 100 rows and 8 series.

Can it use my site's font?

Yes, that's the default. Use Change font in Settings to pick a different one, and "Use site font" to go back.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The chart keeps working. Premium chart types fall back to columns or a line, and only the first 8 rows and the first series show.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the chart shows your numbers and collects nothing.
