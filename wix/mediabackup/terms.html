These terms cover the Media Library Backup app ("the app") on your site.

What the app does

The app reads the files and folders in your site's media library and keeps dated backups of their details: name, folder, size, upload date and link. A backup downloads as a CSV, and Premium backups also download as a ZIP with a manifest and a link for each file.

What a backup is

A backup is a record of your media library, not a copy of the files. File contents stay in your media library and are not downloaded, stored or sent anywhere by the app. A backup helps you see what you have and what has changed; restoring a deleted file means re-uploading it from your own copy.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the data you enter. The app stores its records on your site. You are responsible for keeping access to your own files.

Plans

The free plan backs up the newest 100 files and keeps two backups. Premium backs up every file in every folder, keeps twelve months of history, adds the ZIP download, the removed-files list and the automatic weekly copy. Premium is billed by the platform at $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.

Availability

The app is provided as it is. The automatic copy is taken the first time the dashboard page is opened after seven days, because apps cannot run a timer. The developer is not liable for data loss, for files deleted from your media library, or for losses that follow from an incomplete backup.
