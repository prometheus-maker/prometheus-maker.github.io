Privacy Policy: Media Library Backup

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Media Library Backup app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you take dated backups of your media library: it lists every file with its name, folder, size, upload date and link, keeps a history of these backups, and lets you download a backup as a CSV or a ZIP.

Data the app handles

  • Media library: the app reads the files and folders in your site's media library through your dashboard session. It records each public file's name, folder path, media type, size, upload date and link. It never reads or copies the file contents themselves, and it doesn't read private files.
  • Backups: the records above are stored in the app's own data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy and sends nothing to any other service.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page, and a visitor to your site never sees the app or this data.

Where the data lives

All records are stored in your site's own data collections, under the app's namespace. Removing the app removes them with the app's data.

Your rights

You can delete any backup or the app itself at any time from your dashboard. For a question about the data, write to prometheus.maker@proton.me.

Changes

The developer may change this policy; the current version is always on this page.
