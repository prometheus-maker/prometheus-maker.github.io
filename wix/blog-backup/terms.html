Blog Backup & Export is a Wix app that downloads your blog posts as Markdown, HTML or JSON files in a ZIP. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard.

4. Your data

The app converts post content into Markdown or HTML. Some embedded elements (for example polls, forms or third-party widgets) are kept as links or left out. JSON keeps every field as stored. Check a backup before you rely on it.
