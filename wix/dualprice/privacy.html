This policy describes what the Dual Price: Net & Gross app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds one line to the product pages of your Wix store, showing the net and the gross price side by side, and a dashboard page where you set the rate and the wording of that line.

Data the app handles

  • Settings: the VAT rate, the net or gross entry, labels, separator, currency, rounding and product list you save are stored in the app's settings collection on your site.
  • Product prices: when a product page loads, the app's backend reads that product's price and currency from your own store to work out the other figure. Prices are read, never changed.
  • Plan status: the app's backend asks whether your site is on the free or the paid plan and answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app reads nothing about your customers: no orders, no contact details, no payment data, and no visitor logs. It sends no e-mail and shares nothing with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

Settings stay on your Wix site until you change them or remove the app. Removing the app removes its collection and everything in it.
