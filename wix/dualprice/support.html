Support: Dual Price: Net & Gross

The Dual Price: Net & Gross app puts the net and the gross price side by side under the price on every product page of your store.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Dual Price: Net & Gross" in your Wix dashboard. Type your VAT rate, pick whether the price in your store is net or gross, and press Save. Open a product page: the line appears under the price with both figures.

Questions people ask

  • Which number is mine? Whichever your store shows: if you type prices with VAT included, pick gross; the app works out the net.
  • Can I change the words? Yes. "net" and "gross" are just labels; write what your buyers expect, in any language.
  • What about prices with odd cents? Rounding can keep to the cent, or round up to 0.05, 0.10 or 0.50.
  • Does it work with variants? Yes, the line follows the variant a customer picks.
  • Does it change what customers pay? No. The app only displays; the price, cart and checkout stay as they are.
  • How many products? The first 10 on the free plan, every product on Premium.
  • Where is my data? Your settings live in the app's collection on your own site; removing the app removes them.
