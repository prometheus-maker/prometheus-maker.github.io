1. What you get

Silent Auction: Bids & Items is a Wix app: a widget that runs a silent auction on your page, and a dashboard where you add the lots, set the opening bid and the closing time, read the bids and export the winners. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix site.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan runs three items and 20 bids and adds a small credit line on the widget. The paid plan runs up to 500 items and any number of bids, adds buy-now, reserve prices, the closing-time setting and a CSV of the winners, and removes the credit line. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. Bidding is free for the visitor: the app never takes a payment and never asks for payment details.

3. Your responsibilities

You run the auction, so the law that applies to it is yours to keep. You decide the lots, the rules, who may bid, the closing time, and whether you need a permit or published rules where you are. Do not use the app for a lottery or any game your country treats as gambling, and do not list an item you may not legally give away. Collecting the money, delivering the lots and contacting winners are yours: the app records bids and winners but takes no payment and witnesses nothing outside it.

4. How a bid is accepted

A bid is accepted only if it is at least the current bid plus the step, and only while the item is open by the server's clock. An equal bid and a bid after the closing time are refused, and the page says why. When two bids arrive at once the app keeps the one it can store against the price it read, so the price only moves forward. The dashboard shows the accepted bids; a bid a visitor thinks they placed but which was refused is not an accepted bid.
