This policy describes what the Silent Auction: Bids & Items app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app runs a silent auction on your own Wix site. A widget on your page shows the lots with their photos, current bids and closing times, and lets a visitor place a bid. In your Wix dashboard you add the items, read the bids as they arrive and export the winners.

Data the app handles

  • Your auction settings: the organisation name, currency, default closing window and brand colour, stored in a collection on your site.
  • Items: the title, description, photo link, starting bid, step, buy-now and reserve prices, closing time, and the current bid with the name, email and bidder code of the person leading it, stored in a collection on your site.
  • Bids: the bidder's name and email, the amount, the six-character bidder code, the time of the bid, and a hash of the visitor's IP address, used only to limit how many bids arrive from one connection. Stored in a collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or a paid plan.
  • Browser storage: a small marker in the visitor's browser holds their name, email and bidder code, so they can bid again and read their own bids back. It stays in their browser and is not sent anywhere else.

Where the data goes

The widget and the dashboard talk only to Wix's own APIs and to the app code hosted by Wix. Items, bids and settings stay in your site's collections. The developer runs no server of his own and never receives, stores or sees your items, bids, names or email addresses, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The app sends no emails and takes no payment from a bidder.

The choice you make about the data

You decide what the bid form says, what the auction rules are, how long you keep the bids, and who may bid. Deleting an item in the dashboard deletes it; closing an auction leaves the bids in your site for you to read and export.
