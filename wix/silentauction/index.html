What it does

Set up the lots in the dashboard: a title, a description, a photo from your Media Manager, a starting bid, the smallest step, and when bidding stops. The widget on your page shows the grid. Visitors give a name and email once, then bid from their phone; nothing else is asked of them.

Every bid is checked on the server against the stored price, so two people bidding at the same moment cannot overwrite each other, and a bid equal to the current one is refused with the next amount. When the closing time passes, the page says so and late bids are turned down. The dashboard shows bids as they arrive, with the bidder's name, email and code.

There are no accounts or passwords. Each bidder gets a six-character code on screen, and "My bids" reads their bids back on that browser; the app never emails anyone.

Free: three items, 20 bids, a small credit line. Premium: unlimited items, buy-now, reserve prices, a setting for when items close, and a CSV of every winner, with no credit line.
