Support: Silent Auction: Bids & Items

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plans.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the item title and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I put the auction on my page?

In the Wix editor, open Add Elements, choose Apps, pick Silent Auction: Bids & Items and drop the widget where you want it. The dashboard holds the lots; the widget shows them.

How do I add an item?

Open the app from your Wix dashboard and press Add item. Give it a title, a description, a photo from your Media Manager or an upload, a starting bid, the smallest step, and a closing date and time. Save it; it appears on your site as soon as it is saved.

What does a bidder do?

They type a name and an email once, then enter an amount. The page shows the next accepted bid. A bid equal to the current one is refused with the amount to type; a bid after the closing time is refused too. Each bidder gets a six-character code on screen, and "My bids" reads their bids back on that browser.

How does someone win?

When the closing time passes, the item closes itself and the highest accepted bid wins. Closed items show "Sold for" and the winning amount. On the paid plan, set a reserve price and an item without a bid at or above it closes unsold.

What is buy-now?

On the paid plan you can give an item a buy-now price. A visitor who pays it takes the item at once and bidding on it closes.

How do I read the results?

The dashboard lists every bid with the bidder's name, email and code. On the paid plan, Export winners gives you a CSV with each item, its status, the winning bid, the winner's name and email, and how many bids it took.

How do I change the currency and the brand colour?

Open Settings in the dashboard. The currency applies to new items and to every price on the page; the brand colour sets the widget's accent and buttons.
