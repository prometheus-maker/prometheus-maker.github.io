Support: Pack & Ship: Packing Slips

The Pack & Ship: Packing Slips app lists the recent orders of your Wix shop in one dashboard page and prints branded packing slips and pick lists from them.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Pack & Ship: Packing Slips" in your Wix dashboard. Your recent orders appear, newest first. Tick the ones you are packing and press Packing slip: a PDF downloads with one page per order.

Add your business name, accent colour and footer line in the branding panel and save; the next slip carries them.

Questions people ask

  • What is on a packing slip? Your business name, the order number and date, the ship-to address, the items with options and SKUs, and the customer's note. Prices are off by default.
  • What is a pick list? Every ticked order on one sheet, grouped by SKU, with total quantities and the order numbers that need them. Premium only.
  • How many orders at once? One at a time on free; up to 50 in one PDF on Premium.
  • Does the app send anything to my customers? No. You print the slip and put it in the parcel.
  • Where is my order data? It is read in your browser session to draw the PDF and is not stored by the app. Only your branding settings are saved, in the app's collection on your site.
  • Why does nothing print directly? Browsers cannot open the printer without you; the app downloads the PDF and you print it from your PDF reader.
