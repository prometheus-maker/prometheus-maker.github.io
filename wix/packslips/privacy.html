Effective date: 2026-11-19

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Pack & Ship: Packing Slips app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard that lists your store's recent orders and prints packing slips and pick lists from them, as PDF files built in your browser.

Data the app handles

  • Orders: the app reads your orders (order number, date, customer name, shipping address, items, options, SKUs, quantities, customer notes and custom-field replies) through Wix's Orders API, in your own signed-in session. The data is used to draw the PDF and is not sent anywhere else.
  • Settings: the business name, accent colour, footer line and "show prices" choice you save are stored in the app's settings collection on your Wix site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Plan status: the page asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail, and shares no data with third parties. The PDF is generated in your browser and downloaded to your computer; the app stores neither the PDF nor the order data in it.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your Wix site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
