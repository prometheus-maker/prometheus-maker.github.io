What it does

Tenant Portal gives every tenancy its own private page on your site. One link opens it: what is due this month, every payment you have recorded, and the documents for the home. No accounts, no passwords.

Set the rent, the day it is due and the lease dates once. The tenant's page then shows the month, the due date, how much has come in and what is left, with the last payments underneath. A payment you record in the dashboard appears there the next time they open the link.

The dashboard keeps your tenancies in one list: a name, a unit, the rent, the lease dates and a private note only you can see. Files come from your Media Manager or an upload, messages are written there, and an invoice carries an amount and a document. A link can be revoked or replaced at any time, and the old address stops working at once.

The page takes your site's colours and fonts, sits on any page of your site, and is kept out of search results.

Free covers one tenancy and three items. Premium is $7 a month, or $5.58 a month on yearly billing: 25 tenancies, rent tracking with payment history and reminder text, invoices, expiry dates, your own brand colour and no credit link.
