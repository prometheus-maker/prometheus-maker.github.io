What you get

The app adds a private area for each tenancy to your Wix site. It stores your tenant list, the files, messages and invoices you share, and the access links that open each tenant's page. The free plan covers one tenancy and three items. The paid plan covers up to 25 tenancies and 200 items each, rent tracking with payment history and reminder text, invoices, expiry dates, a last-opened log and no credit link.

Your responsibilities

You decide which tenants to add, what to share with them and how long a link should work. Keep your links private: anyone who has a link can open that tenant's page until you revoke it or it expires. Only share material you have the right to share, and keep your own backup of important files. The app stores files in your site's Media Manager and records in your site's CMS; Wix's terms apply to that storage.
