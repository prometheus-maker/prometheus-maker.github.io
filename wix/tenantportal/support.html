Getting started

Install the app, open Tenant Portal in your site's dashboard, and add your first tenant. Then place the Tenant Portal widget on a page of your site, copy that page's address and paste it in Settings. The address is what turns a tenant's link into a full URL you can send.

Common questions

### A tenant says the link is not valid

The link may have expired, been revoked or replaced. Open the tenant in the dashboard, copy the current link and send that. Links are long on purpose; a shortened or retyped link will not work.

### How do I share a file?

Open a tenant and add an item of kind File. Pick a document from your Media Manager or upload one (up to 15 MB; PDF, Office, image or ZIP). The tenant sees a Download button on their page.

### Can a tenant see another tenant's files?

No. A link opens one tenant's page, and the download address is checked against that tenant on the server. A link that is wrong, revoked or expired gets the same neutral message for everyone.

### How do I close a tenant's page?

Revoke the link in the dashboard, or set an expiry date (paid plan). Revoking stops the old link at once; you can issue a new one whenever you need.

### Why is there a small credit link?

The free plan shows a small "Made with Tenant Portal" line under the widget. The paid plan removes it and lets you use your own brand colour.
