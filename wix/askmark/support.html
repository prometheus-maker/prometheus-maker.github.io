Support: Askmark: Post Purchase Survey

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the order number you tested with and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I add the question?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Askmark, and click Add to Thank You page. Then publish your site once. You can also add it in the editor: open the Thank You page, select the order section and add the Askmark Survey plugin.

Where do I see the answers?

In the same dashboard page: every answer with its number of orders and share. Premium adds revenue per answer, any date range and a CSV download.

Why don't I see the question on an old order?

It shows only for orders placed in the last two hours, so an old Thank You page link cannot add answers.

What if a buyer changes their answer?

They can tap Change. One order has one answer; the new one replaces the old one.

How do I change the question or the colour?

In the editor, open the Thank You page, click the question, then Settings. Your own question and answers need Premium; the thank-you line, alignment and colour are free.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The standard question comes back, results show the last 30 days, and the credit link returns. Saved answers are kept.
