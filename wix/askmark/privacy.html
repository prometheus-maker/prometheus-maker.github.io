This policy describes what the Askmark: Post Purchase Survey app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds one question to your store's Thank You page, the page buyers see right after checkout, such as "How did you hear about us?". The buyer can tap an answer. The app's dashboard shows how many orders, and on the paid plan how much revenue, came with each answer.

Data the app handles

  • The order on the Thank You page: the page gives the question the order's ID. When the buyer answers, the app's backend asks Wix for that order to check it belongs to your site and was placed in the last two hours, and reads its order number, total, currency and date.
  • The answer: the backend saves one row per order in a data collection on your own site ("Askmark answers"): order ID, order number, the answer, any text typed under "Other", order total, currency, order date and answer time. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, payment details and line items are not read into it, stored or logged.
  • Question settings: your question, answers, texts and colour. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend tells the question, its settings panel and the dashboard whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID. This answer is kept in the server's memory for up to ten minutes.

The backend keeps no logs of buyers or visitors. The answers stay in your site's collection, where you can see and delete them in your CMS, and they are removed with the collection if you uninstall the app.

Site visitors

The question does not set cookies and does not track anyone. Nothing is sent until the buyer taps an answer.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your orders, answers or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
