Privacy Policy: Event Import: CSV Events

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Event Import: CSV Events app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you paste or upload a list of events from a spreadsheet, review the rows, and create the events on your site.

Data the app handles

  • Spreadsheet rows: the text or file you paste is read in your browser to show the preview. It is not sent anywhere except as the individual events the app creates when you press create.
  • Events: the app reads the events already on your site, so it can show which rows look like duplicates and which events exist. It creates the events for the rows you import.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app stores nothing of its own: there is no database, no copy of the spreadsheet and no log of imported events kept by the app. It sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. It has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

The events you import are ordinary events on your site, kept by the platform in the same way as events you add by hand. Delete them in your events list, or remove the app; the app keeps no separate copy to delete.
