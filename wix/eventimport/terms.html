Limits and accuracy

The app checks names, dates and end times, and marks rows that look like events already on your site. Those checks reduce mistakes but do not replace your own review of the preview, which you see before anything is written. Imported events are created as RSVP events; ticket types and prices are carried in the event description where the event form has no field for them.
