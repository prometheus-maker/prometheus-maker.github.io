Common questions

My dates came out at the wrong hour

Dates without a time zone are read as the wall clock of the event time zone you chose at the top of the page. Choose the zone your spreadsheet runs on before you read the list; a date that carries its own zone or an offset is read as written.

A row is marked with a problem

A row needs a name, a start date the app can read, and an end that comes after the start. The preview says which check failed for each row, and rows with problems are not created.

An event I expected is shown as already there

The app compares the event name and the start time with the events already on your site, and skips a row it recognises, so reading the same list twice is safe. On Premium the switch at the bottom of the preview can be turned off if you mean to create such a row again.

Only some of my rows became events

Rows that fail a check are left out; the summary after the import counts created, skipped and failed rows and the reasons, and the results file lists every row. More than ten events on the free plan are not created until the list is split or the plan is upgraded.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
