Getting started

1. Install the app from the Wix App Market and open "Repair Desk: Job Tickets" in your dashboard.

2. Add a ticket with the customer's name, the item and the fault, then save it.

3. Press Print on a ticket to print the intake receipt on A4 or Letter paper.

Common questions

  • Where are my tickets stored? In your own site's Wix Data storage, under the app's collection. Removing the app removes them.
  • How does the monthly limit work? The free plan counts new tickets from the 1st of each calendar month. Tickets saved earlier stay editable.
  • Who can see a status page? Anyone with the link. The page shows the ticket number, item, status, public note and promised date, never the customer's contact details or your internal notes.

Reporting a problem

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with the site name, what you did and what happened. If a ticket's status page does not load, send the address that fails.
