This policy describes what the Repair Desk: Job Tickets app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard where a repair shop writes a numbered job ticket for each item taken in: the customer's name and contact, the item and serial number, the fault, parts and labour, the promised date and your own notes. It prints an intake receipt, and on the paid plan it gives each ticket a status page the customer can open.

Data the app handles

  • Ticket details: everything you type into the dashboard is stored in the app's data collection on your Wix site. It stays with your site; the app keeps no copy of its own.
  • Plan status: the dashboard asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Status page: when a customer opens a ticket's status page, the page reads that one ticket using the app's own credentials. It shows the ticket number, item, status, public note and promised date. It never shows the customer's name, phone, e-mail, prices or your internal notes.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. It does not read anything else in your site.

Your choices

Delete a ticket in the dashboard at any time, or remove the app to remove its data from your site. To ask a question about this policy, write to the contact address above.
