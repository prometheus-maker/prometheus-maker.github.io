What it does

A repair shop writes a paper ticket for every item and then answers "is it ready?" all week. Repair Desk keeps the job in your dashboard instead: customer, item, serial or IMEI, the fault in the customer's words, parts and labour, and a promised date.

One click moves a ticket through Intake, In progress, Waiting for parts, Ready and Collected. Print the intake receipt on A4 or Letter for the customer's pocket; it carries the ticket number, the item, the estimated total and the promised date.

Premium adds a status page for every ticket. The ticket gets a long web address the customer opens from a phone or an e-mail. It shows the ticket number, the item, the current status and the one line you write for the customer. It never shows the customer's name, phone, e-mail, prices or your internal notes.

Free covers 25 new tickets a month. The counter starts again on the 1st and tickets you saved stay editable. Premium removes the cap and adds the status page. $7 a month, or $5.60 a month billed yearly.

Tickets live in your site's own data storage. Nothing is added to your live site.
