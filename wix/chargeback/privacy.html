Privacy Policy: Evidence: Chargeback Pack

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

This policy describes what the Evidence: Chargeback Pack app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you choose one of your orders, record the evidence for a payment dispute, and export the pack as a PDF on the paid plan.

Data the app handles

  • Orders: the app reads the orders of your site through the site's own order service when you open the order picker. It copies the order number, date, total, buyer name, payment status, shipments and refunds into a pack. The app never changes an order or a payment.
  • Packs: the dispute reason, the delivery notes, the tracking reference, the signature name and the checklist you tick are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail to your buyers and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Who can see a pack

Packs live on your site and are readable only through the app's dashboard by people who can open that dashboard. The PDF is generated in your browser and downloaded by you.

Retention

Packs stay on your site until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app.

Questions

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me.
