Common questions

No orders appear in the picker

The picker reads the orders the site already has. If your store has no orders yet, there is nothing to build a pack from. Orders also need to belong to the site the app is installed on.

The PDF export does not start

The PDF export is part of Premium. On the free plan the export button opens the upgrade offer. After upgrading, open the app again; the plan is read each time the page loads.

Can I delete a pack?

Open the pack and use Delete pack. Deleted packs cannot be restored.

A field is missing in the PDF

The PDF is built from the pack at the moment you press the export. Fill in the delivery notes, tracking and signature first, then export again.

The app shows the wrong plan

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows the wrong one, write to the address above with your site name.
