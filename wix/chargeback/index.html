What it does

When a buyer disputes a payment, the bank asks for proof: what was ordered, when it was paid, what was shipped and who signed for it. Gathering that from several screens takes time the dispute window does not leave. This app reads the order you choose and turns it into one evidence pack.

Open the app in your site's dashboard and pick an order from the list. The pack copies the order facts at that moment and adds the order timeline: placed, paid, shipment and tracking, refund if there is one. You then type what was delivered, the tracking reference and the name on the signature, and tick the checklist for the dispute reason. When the pack is ready, the PDF export gives you one document to send to the provider.

The app only reads orders; it never changes an order or a payment. Packs stay in the app's own collections on your site.

The free plan covers three packs per calendar month. Premium removes the monthly limit and opens the one-PDF export. Premium costs $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly ($48 a year), with a 14-day free trial.
