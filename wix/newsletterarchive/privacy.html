This policy describes what the Newsletter Archive app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a list of the email newsletters your site has sent to pages of your Wix site, newest first, each linking to its public web version.

Data the app handles

  • Your email campaigns: the app's backend asks Wix for the site's published email marketing campaigns and passes on only what the archive shows: each campaign's title, publish date, first image (or the preview image Wix makes of the email) and the link to its public web version. Drafts, templates, archived campaigns, automated emails and campaigns whose title starts with [private] are left out. Recipients, contact lists, email contents and statistics (opens, clicks) are not read.
  • Widget settings: your heading, texts, Subscribe link, the issues you chose to hide, theme, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the widget whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last answer (plan and the campaign details above) so the archive shows at once on the next page.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows newsletter titles and dates. Clicking one opens the newsletter's web version, a page Wix hosts for every published campaign.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your campaigns, your contacts, your visitors' data or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.
