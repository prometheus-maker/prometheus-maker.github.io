Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the archive and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the archive?

Installing the app adds the archive to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Newsletter Archive.

Which newsletters show?

Every email marketing campaign you have published, newest first: sent campaigns and campaigns published as a landing page only. Drafts, templates, archived campaigns and automated emails are left out. A new campaign appears a minute or two after you send it.

How do I keep one newsletter off my site?

Start its title with [private] in your Email Marketing dashboard; it stays off on every plan. On Premium you can also untick it under Hide issues in the widget settings.

The editor shows issues I never sent

That is a sample so you can see the look before your site has sent a campaign. Your live site shows only your own newsletters, and hides the archive while there are none.

Where does the Subscribe button go?

Wherever you point it in Settings: a page of your site such as /newsletter, or a full link to a sign-up form. Leave the link empty and there is no button.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The archive keeps working as a list of your newest six newsletters in the Paper look. Cards, the latest issue banner, search, date groups, hidden issues and the other themes switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the archive reads your campaigns' titles, dates, images and public links, never your contacts or statistics, and sends nothing to the developer.
