1. What you get

Newsletter Archive is a Wix app with a site widget that lists the email campaigns your site has sent, each linking to its web version. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows your newest six newsletters as a list, with your own heading, dates, a Subscribe button and a small credit link under the list. The paid plan shows every newsletter, adds cards with images, a latest issue banner, search, groups by year or month, hiding single issues, more colour themes and your own accent colour, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Your responsibilities

The archive shows the newsletters you published. Only publish campaigns you are happy for anyone to read, and start the title with [private] (or hide the issue on the paid plan) for any you want kept off your site.
