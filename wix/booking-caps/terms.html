1. What you get

Booking Caps is a Wix app that refuses new bookings that would go over limits you set (per day, week or month, for services, staff members or each client). You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan has 1 active limit. The paid plan has unlimited limits and a log of blocked booking attempts. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. How limits behave

If the app cannot finish a check (for example Wix is slow or a request fails), it lets the booking through rather than block it. So a limit can occasionally be exceeded; check your calendar for important limits. Bookings made before you set a limit are counted but never cancelled.

4. Your data

The app counts your bookings through Wix and keeps only your limits (and, on the paid plan, a log of blocked attempts) on your site. See the Privacy Policy: https://prometheus-maker.github.io/wix/booking-caps/privacy.html

5. Availability and changes

The app depends on Wix's platform and the Bookings APIs. If Wix changes or limits them, features may stop working until the app is updated.

8. Ending use

You can stop using the app at any time by uninstalling it in your Wix dashboard. Limits stop applying as soon as it is uninstalled.
