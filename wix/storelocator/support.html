Support: Store Locator: Your Locations

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the locator and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the locator?

Installing the app adds it to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Store Locator.

Where do the locations come from?

From Business Info in your dashboard: Settings, then Business Info, then Locations. Add, edit or remove a location there and the locator follows by itself.

A location has no pin on the map

It has no map position. Open it in Business Info, type the address again and pick it from the suggestions, then save.

A location is missing

Locations with neither an address nor a phone number are left out, and so are archived ones. On the free plan the locator shows 3 locations; Premium shows all of them. Also check "Hide these locations" in the widget's settings.

How do I hide one location?

Click the locator in the editor, then Settings, and add its name to "Hide these locations". Publish.

Is "open now" right?

It is worked out from each location's own opening hours and time zone in Business Info, including special days you set there (Premium).

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The locator keeps working with 3 locations. Search, Near me, open now and the pin colour switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the locator shows the location details you published in Business Info; a visitor's position (Near me) never leaves their device.
