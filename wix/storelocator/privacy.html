Privacy Policy: Store Locator: Your Locations

Effective date: 2026-11-10

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Store Locator: Your Locations app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows your business locations on a map and in a list, so visitors can find the one near them.

Data the app handles

  • Business locations: the app's backend on Wix reads the locations you keep in Business Info: name, address, map position, phone number, e-mail address, time zone and opening hours. This is information about your business that you chose to publish; the app reads it, it does not change it.
  • What reaches the page: the same location details. On the free plan the backend sends up to 3 locations and no opening hours.
  • Widget settings: layout, map style, hidden locations, colours, texts and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last location list it saw so the list shows at once on the next visit; the dashboard page remembers whether you closed its intro box.
  • Nothing is written to a database by the app.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies and does not track visitors. On the paid plan a visitor can press "Near me": the browser then asks the visitor for permission to share their position. If they agree, the position is used inside the page only, to sort locations by distance. It is never sent to the app's backend or anyone else.

The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org), so the visitor's browser requests map images from them, which shares the visitor's IP address and the page address with OpenStreetMap like any embedded map; see https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. The Directions link opens Google Maps only when the visitor clicks it.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share data. It is hosted on Wix's infrastructure.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app removes the widget. Edit or remove any location in Business Info, or hide it from the widget in its settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
