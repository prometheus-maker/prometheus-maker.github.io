This policy describes what the Allergen & Nutrition Label app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard where you keep the labels for the dishes or products you sell: ingredients, allergens, the nutrition figures, a serving name and an optional note. From those it builds an EU nutrition declaration or a US Nutrition Facts panel and, on the paid plan, a print-ready PDF.

Data the app handles

  • Labels: what you type into the dashboard (dish name, ingredients, allergen ticks, nutrition figures, serving size and name, free note) is stored in the app's data collection on your Wix site. It stays with your site; the app's backend keeps no copy of its own.
  • PDF: the label page is generated in your browser from the figures you entered. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Plan status: the dashboard asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app reads nothing about your store's products or orders, keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties.

Your choices

Delete a label in the dashboard at any time; removing the app removes its data from your site. Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with any question about this policy.
