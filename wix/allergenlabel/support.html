Support: Allergen & Nutrition Label

The Allergen & Nutrition Label app turns a recipe's ingredients and nutrition figures into an EU nutrition declaration or a US Nutrition Facts panel, names the allergens it finds, and prints the label as a PDF on the paid plan.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Allergen & Nutrition Label" in your Wix dashboard. Press "New label", type the dish name and its ingredients, and press "Take allergens from the ingredients" to tick the fourteen EU allergens for you. Fill the figures per 100 g, the serving size and name, then choose EU declaration or US Nutrition Facts: the preview on the right updates as you type and lists anything the checks catch. Press "Save label".

Questions people ask

  • How many labels can I keep? Five on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • Can I print the label? On Premium, open a label and press "PDF". It saves one page, A6 for the EU declaration or 3.5 by 6 inches for US Nutrition Facts.
  • Why did it tick an allergen I did not expect? The app reads the ingredient list and knows the exceptions: coconut milk is not milk, but almond milk counts as nuts. Untick anything that does not apply.
  • My site shows no labels. A new site starts empty; the empty state offers a sample dish you can save and edit.
  • How do I remove a label? Open it and press "Delete".

Something wrong

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with the site address, what you did and what you saw. We answer within two working days.
