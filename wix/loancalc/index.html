What it does

A visitor who already knows the monthly payment is ready to talk. Loan Calculator puts a working loan payment calculator on any page of your site: loan amount, interest rate and term, each with a slider and a box to type in, and the monthly payment changes as they move.

Under the payment it shows the principal, the total interest and the total paid, with a bar that makes the interest share obvious at a glance. It suits car dealers, mortgage brokers, lenders, credit unions, solar installers and anyone who sells something people pay off over time.

In the editor's Settings panel you set the starting amount, rate and term, the currency and where it sits, the number format (1,234.56 or 1.234,56), the title, the colours and the font. It works with zero setup and fits narrow phone screens. Nothing a visitor types is sent anywhere or stored, and the script is small and loads after your page.

Premium adds a yearly amortization schedule visitors can open, an extra monthly payment field that shows the interest saved and how much sooner the loan ends, and a button to your own page, such as Apply now or Book a call. Premium also removes the small credit line under the calculator.
