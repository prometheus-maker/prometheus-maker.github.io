Support: Loan Calculator

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the calculator and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the calculator?

Installing the app adds the calculator to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Loan Calculator.

How do I set my own numbers?

Click the calculator in the editor, then Settings. Under Starting numbers, set the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the term in years or months. Visitors start from these numbers.

Can I use euros, pounds or another currency?

Yes, on every plan. Type any symbol or code in Currency, choose whether it goes before or after the number, and pick a number format such as 1.234,56.

How is the payment worked out?

With the standard formula for a fixed-rate loan repaid in equal monthly payments, with interest compounded monthly. A rate of 0% simply splits the amount over the months.

How do I add a button such as "Apply now"?

On Premium, type the button text in Settings and choose a page or paste a link. The button shows only when it has a link.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The calculator keeps working. The payment schedule, the extra payment field and the button are hidden, and the small credit line comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: visitors' numbers stay in their browser and nothing is stored.
