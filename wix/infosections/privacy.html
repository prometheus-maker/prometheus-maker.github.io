This policy describes what the Info Sections: CSV Export app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app is a page in your Wix dashboard. It reads your store's products and their info sections, saves them as a CSV file on your computer, and, when you load an edited file, writes the changes back to the same products and sections.

Data the app handles

  • Products: ID, name, URL slug and the list of info sections on each product. Prices, stock, images, orders and customers are not read.
  • Info sections: name, title and text.
  • Categories: names and the products in a category, read only when you use "Apply to a category".
  • The file or pasted rows you load: read in your browser to build the dry run. The file is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Undo files: a copy of how the changed products looked before each import, kept in your browser's local storage so you can undo it. You can download or ignore it; clearing the browser's site data deletes it.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.

Where the data goes

Everything is read and written through Wix's own APIs from your browser while the page is open. The app has no server: the developer never receives, stores or sees your products or files, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
