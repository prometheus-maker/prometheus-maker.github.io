1. What you get

Program Roster: CSV Import is a Wix app for Online Programs. It lets you add a whole cohort of learners to a program from a CSV file or a pasted list. You install it from the Wix App Market, open it in your Wix dashboard, pick the program and import your list.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan imports up to 25 rows per import. The paid plan removes the row limit, allows repeat imports and places each person at a chosen starting step. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. Your responsibilities

You choose which people to import and confirm you may contact them. Check the preview before pressing Import. Your site's own Online Programs plan limits how many active participants it can have; the app adds people within that limit and cannot go beyond it. People in your file must have a valid email address; rows with bad addresses are reported and not imported.
