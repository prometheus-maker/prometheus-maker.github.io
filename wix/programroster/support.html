Support: Program Roster: CSV Import

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the program you imported into, and what you saw. A screenshot of the preview or the result table helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I start?

Open the app from your dashboard (Manage Apps, then Program Roster), choose the program, paste a list or upload a CSV, check the preview, then press Import.

What does the preview tell me?

Each row shows ready to add, already in the program, a bad email address, a repeated line, or a starting step that does not exist. Errors name the line in your file.

What columns does my file need?

Only email is required. First name, last name, group and starting step are optional; common header names work, and a template is one click away.

How many rows can I import?

The free plan imports up to 25 rows at a time. Premium imports any size.

A row says "Premium subscription required"

Your site's Online Programs plan allows a set number of active participants. Upgrade the site's plan or remove inactive participants, then import again.

Can I import the same file twice?

Yes. People who are already in the program are skipped, never added again.

How do I download the rows that failed?

The result table has a download that gives you the failed rows with their reasons, ready to fix and import again.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then Program Roster: CSV Import.

Privacy
