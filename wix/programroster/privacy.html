This policy describes what the Program Roster: CSV Import app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds people in bulk to a Wix Online Program. From your Wix dashboard you choose a program, paste a list or upload a CSV of names and email addresses, preview every row, and press Import. The app then finds or creates each person's contact and member account in your site and enrols them in the program.

Data the app handles

  • Email addresses from your file: used to find or create the person's contact and member account on your site, and to enrol them in the program you chose.
  • Names and group or starting-step values from your file, when the columns are present.
  • Online Programs data: program IDs, titles and enrollment records, read and written so people can be added.
  • Import results: how many rows were added, skipped or failed, held only in the dashboard page while it is open.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.

The app does not collect visitors, payments or card information. The only personal data it handles is the list you choose to import.

Where the data goes

Everything is read and written through Wix's own APIs, by the app's code running on Wix. The developer has no other server and never receives your files, your lists, the email addresses or your members' data. Nothing is shared with anyone else. Removing the app stops all processing; contacts and members already created stay on your site until you delete them.

Your responsibilities

Only import people who agreed to hear from you and to join your program. You are the controller of that data under GDPR and similar laws.
