This policy describes what the Shabbat Times app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows this week's candle lighting and havdalah times, the parsha, the Hebrew date and holidays for one place, and, on the Premium plan, your service times, zmanim and coming holidays.

Data the app handles

  • Widget settings: the city you pick, candle lighting minutes, havdalah custom, language, layout, service times, colours and font you enter in the settings panel. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Your place: when no city is picked, the app's backend reads the address coordinates and time zone from your site's Business Info, only to look up the times for that place.
  • Times lookup: the app's backend asks Hebcal.com (https://www.hebcal.com) for the calendar of your place. It sends the place (a city ID, or your business address coordinates rounded to about 100 metres, and the time zone) and your calendar options, nothing else. Answers are cached for 24 hours.
  • Plan status: the widget and its settings panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser settings: how many times you opened the settings panel (for one optional review link), kept in your browser's local storage.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not record what they click. It does not ask for the visitor's location. Nothing about visitors is sent to Hebcal.com or anywhere else.

Where the data goes

Your place and calendar options go to Hebcal.com as described above. The developer does not keep logs of visitors, never receives your text, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
