Support: Shabbat Times

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget, your city and settings, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds this week's times to the page you were on. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Shabbat Times.

Which place are the times for?

Your site's Business Info address, until you pick a city in the widget's Settings. If your site has no address, the editor shows sample times for New York with a note, and visitors see nothing until you pick a city.

Our candle lighting time is different

Communities light at different times. Under Settings > Times pick your minutes before sunset (Hebcal's default is 18, 40 in Jerusalem) and your havdalah custom (nightfall, or 42, 50 or 72 minutes after sunset).

When does it move to next week?

On Saturday night, once havdalah has passed in your city.

How do I write service times? (Premium)

One service per line: name | days | time. Examples: "Mincha | Fri | candles - 15", "Shacharit | Sat | 9:00", "Maariv | Sun-Thu | sunset + 20". Times can be fixed, or minutes before or after candle lighting, havdalah, sunrise, sunset, plag or nightfall; the widget works them out every week.

Can it show Hebrew?

Yes. Settings > Times > Language: Hebrew turns the widget right to left with Hebrew names and a 24-hour clock.

What does Premium add?

Your service times, today's zmanim, the next holidays, a large-type layout for a lobby screen, and no credit link.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps showing this week's times, parsha, Hebrew date and holidays. Service times, zmanim, coming holidays and the large layout switch off, and the small credit link comes back.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app asks Hebcal.com for your place's calendar and collects nothing from visitors.
