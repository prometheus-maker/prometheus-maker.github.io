What it does

Client Portal gives each of your clients a private page on your site. One link opens it, and the page shows that client's files, your messages and their invoices. No accounts or passwords.

The dashboard keeps the client list. Each client record holds a name, an email address and an optional private note; files come from the Media Manager or an upload, messages are written there, and an invoice line carries an amount, a currency and a PDF. The link is made when a client is added.

A link can be revoked or replaced, and the old address stops working at once. An expiry date closes a page at the end of a project, and Premium shows when each client last opened their page.

The widget sits on any page of your site; that page's address is kept in Settings. It takes your site's colours and fonts and fits its column.

Free covers one client and three items. Premium is $6 a month, or $4.80 a month on yearly billing: 25 clients and 200 items each, invoices, expiry dates, the last-opened log, your own brand colour and no credit link.

Files stay in your Media Manager and the rest lives in the app's collections on your site. The client page is noindex, so search engines leave it alone.
