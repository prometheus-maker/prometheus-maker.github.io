Getting started

Install the app, open Client Portal in your site's dashboard, and add your first client. Then place the Client Portal widget on a page of your site, copy that page's address and paste it in Settings. The address is what turns a client's link into a full URL you can send.

Common questions

A client says the link is not valid

The link may have expired, been revoked or replaced. Open the client in the dashboard, copy the current link and send that. Links are long on purpose; a shortened or retyped link will not work.

How do I share a file?

Open a client and add an item of kind File. Pick a document from your Media Manager or upload one (up to 15 MB; PDF, Office, image or ZIP). The client sees a Download button on their page.

Can a client see another client's files?

No. A link opens one client's page, and the download address is checked against that client on the server. A link that is wrong, revoked or expired gets the same neutral message for everyone.

How do I close a client's page?

Revoke the link in the dashboard, or set an expiry date (paid plan). Revoking stops the old link at once; you can issue a new one whenever you need.

Why is there a small credit link?

The free plan shows a small "Made with Client Portal" line under the widget. The paid plan removes it and lets you use your own brand colour.

Still stuck

Write to prometheus.maker@proton.me with your site's address and what you expected to see. Please do not send client files or personal data in the email.
