This policy describes what the Entry List: Event Guests app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that shows who has registered for one of your events: the number of entries and the guests' names, as one list or grouped by ticket type or by an answer on your registration form (for example a division or age group).

Data the app handles

  • Events: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's published events (title, date, time zone, registration status and event page address) and the questions of their registration forms (labels and options).
  • Guests: for the event shown, the backend reads its guest list: each guest's first and last name, ticket type, attendance status, registration date and form answers. It turns them into entries before anything leaves the backend: the name in the style you chose (by default first name and last initial, "Maya L."; or full name, first name only, or no name), plus the one form answer you chose to show (for example the club). E-mail addresses, phone numbers, addresses and every other answer are never sent to the browser. If you pick a consent question, guests who did not tick it are counted but not named.
  • Widget settings: the events you picked, grouping, places, name style, wording, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last list it saw (event title, date, counts and the shown names) so it appears at once on the next visit.

The backend stores nothing and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your guests' details or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
