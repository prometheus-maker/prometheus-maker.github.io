How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the list and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the list?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Entry List.

Which event does it show?

Until you choose, it shows the next event that is open for registration. Click the widget in the editor, then Settings, and pick the event.

Someone just registered. Do I need to publish again?

No. The guest list is read when a visitor loads the page, so the new entry shows the next time the page is opened.

How do I group entries by division or age group?

Add a dropdown or single-choice question to the event's registration form (Events, then the event, then Registration Form), for example "Division" with your divisions as options. Then in the widget's Settings, under Group entries by, pick that question. Grouping is part of Premium.

Can it hide last names?

Yes. Names show as first name and last initial by default. Under Names you can also pick first name only, full name, or no names (counts only).

Who is counted?

Guests who are going: each ticket holder on ticketed events, each "yes" reply on RSVP events (with their extra guests as +1, +2). Waitlisted guests and "not going" replies are left out.

What happens if the paid plan ends?

The widget keeps working with one event as one list. Groups, places per group, extra events and the consent question switch off, and the small credit link comes back.
