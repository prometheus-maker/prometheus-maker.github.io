1. What you get

Entry List: Event Guests is a Wix app with a site widget that shows who has registered for your events, read from your Wix Events guest lists. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one event as one list with the total, names in the style you choose, an optional club or team column and a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan groups entries by ticket type or a registration-form answer, shows places left per group, shows up to 6 events as tabs, can name only guests who ticked a consent question, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
