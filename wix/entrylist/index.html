What it does

Your event registrations, shown on your page as an entry list: "48 entered", then the names. Every new registration shows up by itself, so nobody has to retype the list.

Names show as first name and last initial ("Maya L.") by default. You can show full names, first names only, or no names at all and just the counts. E-mail addresses, phone numbers and other answers are never shown. An extra column can show one answer from your registration form, such as the club or team.

Add a question like "Division", "Belt" or "Age group" to your registration form and the list can group entries by it, or by ticket type: "Green belt, 14 entries". Type the places for each group and it shows how many are left, or "Full". A button takes visitors to the event page while registration is open.

Karate tournaments use it for entries by belt, tennis clubs for championship events and youth leagues for tryouts by age group.

The free plan shows one event as one list with the total and a small credit link. Premium groups entries by division or ticket type, shows places left per group, shows up to 6 events as tabs, can name only the guests who ticked a consent question, and removes the credit link.
