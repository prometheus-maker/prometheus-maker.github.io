This policy describes what the Gift Registry: Wedding & Baby app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you create gift registries, add the gifts you would like and share a link that guests open to see the list and what has already been bought.

Data the app handles

  • Registries: the title, occasion, date, accent colour, note and optional guest password you enter, and the gifts you add (name, price, quantity, note, link), are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Bought marks: the giver's name you type when a gift arrives, and the time you marked it, are stored on your site.
  • Guests: the guest page shows the registry by its link. The app stores nothing about visitors, keeps no logs of them and sets no tracking cookies.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The guest page is the app's only public page, and it shows only the registry and its gifts; it never shows the password or the giver's name.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a registry removes its gifts. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
