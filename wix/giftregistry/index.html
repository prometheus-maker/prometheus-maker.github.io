What it does

A wedding or a new baby brings a list of gifts, and guests need one place to see it. This app keeps the whole registry on your site: the gifts you would like, what has already been bought, and who gave what.

Create a registry with a title, an occasion, the date, an accent colour and a short note for guests. Then add the gifts one by one with a name, a price, how many you would like, a note and a link to where they can be found.

Share the guest link by copying it into a message, an invitation or a button on your site. Guests open a clean page with the title, date, note and the full list; they need no account and nothing about them is stored. When a present arrives, mark it bought and write the giver's name. The list then shows the gift as covered, so nobody buys it twice, and a thank-you list keeps every giver and gift together. On the free plan the guest page never shows what has been bought.

The free plan covers one registry and 15 gifts. Premium removes both limits and adds the bought marks, the thank-you list, the cash-fund note and a password on the guest page. Premium is $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly ($48 a year), with a 14-day free trial.
