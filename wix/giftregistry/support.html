Common questions

My guests cannot open the link

Press Copy guest link in the registry and send that exact link. A link copied from the address bar of the dashboard is not the guest link.

A guest does not see which gifts are bought

On the free plan the guest page never shows bought state. Marking gifts as bought, with the giver's name, and showing it to guests is part of Premium.

I need more than one registry or more than 15 gifts

The free plan covers one registry and 15 gifts. Premium removes both limits.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
