Support: Farmers Market: Vendor List

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, what you clicked and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I add the vendors?

Dashboard, then Apps, then Farmers Market. The Vendors tab has Add vendor: name, category, a short blurb, stall number, a logo from your media library and the links. The order you see there is the order on the page.

Can I bring a whole season in at once?

Yes, on the paid plan. The Import tab takes a CSV file or rows pasted from a spreadsheet. The first row names the columns; "name" is required, and category, description, website, instagram, stall and here are read when present.

Why do I see example vendors?

A new install starts with sample vendors so the page looks right before you add your own. Delete them in the dashboard; a site with no vendors shows nothing rather than made-up stalls.

The list is empty on my live page

Check that you published the site after adding the widget, and that at least one vendor is saved in the dashboard. The editor shows samples; visitors never do.

How does the "Here this Saturday" filter work?

Tick "Here this Saturday" on a vendor in the dashboard when they are coming to the next market. Visitors can then narrow the list to those traders.

How do the market days and the banner work?

The paid plan. Add a market day with its date, hours and the vendors attending. The Market Status widget shows the next one: "Market open today 9-1 · 34 vendors", or the cancellation note you type if you untick Open.

What happens to the applications?

Each one is saved in the dashboard's Applications tab and filed in your Contacts under the label "Vendor applicant". Approve copies the applicant into your vendor list. The app does not email applicants; you can attach your own automation to that label.

Can I change the colours, heading or layout?

Yes. In the editor, click the list, then Settings: heading, intro line, layout, columns, corners, accent, card and text colours, the site's own font, and the toggles for search, chips, blurbs and stall numbers.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

Your vendors, days and applications stay. The page keeps the free plan's part: up to 20 vendors, search and chips, and the credit link.
