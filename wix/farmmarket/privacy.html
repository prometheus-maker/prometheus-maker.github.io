This policy describes what the Farmers Market: Vendor List app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app shows a list of market vendors on your site, and gives you a dashboard page to keep that list, your market days, and the applications people send you.

Data the app handles

  • Vendor records: the name, category, description, logo link, website, Instagram, stall number and "here this Saturday" flag you enter are saved in a collection inside your own site and shown to anyone who opens the page. They are public, like the rest of your site.
  • Market days: the dates, hours, notes and the vendors you tick are saved in the same site, and the next day drives the banner on your site.
  • Applications: when a visitor uses the application form, the name, business name, category, email address, phone number, products and message they type are saved in a collection inside your site. The app also creates or updates a contact in your site's Contacts with the name, email and phone from the form, and adds the label "Vendor applicant". The app never sends email to the applicant; your own automations may.
  • Rate limiting: to stop form spam the backend stores a hash of the visitor's IP address (with a secret salt, not the address itself) and the time, for one hour.
  • Plan state: the backend saves whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, with a signature, in the same collection, so pages load without asking the platform each time. It is refreshed every few hours.
  • Widget settings: heading, text, layout, toggles and colours. They are stored with your site, like the settings of any other element.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors and stores nothing outside your site.

Site visitors

The vendor list sets no cookies and does not track anyone. Links on a vendor card open the vendor's own website or Instagram in a new tab only when a visitor clicks one.

Where the data goes

Nowhere else. The developer never receives your vendors, your applicants or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone.

Your choices

You can delete a vendor, a market day or an application in the dashboard at any time. Uninstalling the app removes the widget from your site; the collections stay in your site's CMS until you delete them.

Changes
