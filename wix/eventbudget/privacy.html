Privacy Policy: Budget: Wedding & Event

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Budget: Wedding & Event app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you keep the budget of a wedding, party or other event: one line per cost, with the amount you planned, the amount you really paid, the vendor and the due date.

Data the app handles

  • Budget lines: the event name, category, item, vendor, planned and actual amounts, currency, paid status, due date and notes you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail, reads no other data on your site and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a budget line removes it; deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
