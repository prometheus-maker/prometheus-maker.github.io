What it does

Every wedding and event has a long list of costs: the venue, catering, the photographer, flowers, the band. Most of them live in a spreadsheet, and by the week before the day nobody is sure which invoices are already paid and which are still due.

This app keeps that list in your dashboard. Add a line for each cost, choose a category, write who you pay and what you planned to spend. When the invoice arrives, enter the real amount and mark the line paid. The page then shows the total planned, the total spent so far, what is still to pay and how far over or under budget the event is, with a bar for each category and a red mark on anything paid after its due date.

Lines can be searched and filtered by status or category, and the whole budget exports as a CSV file for a spreadsheet or a partner. The free plan holds one event with ten lines and a single planned total. Premium adds unlimited events and lines, the actual cost and paid or unpaid status on every line, the category breakdown and the CSV export. Premium is $4 a month, or $3 a month billed yearly, with a 14-day free trial.
