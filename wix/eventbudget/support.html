Common questions

How do I start?

Open the app in your site's dashboard and press Add 8 sample lines to see a filled budget, or New budget line to enter a cost of your own.

Why does a line show a dash instead of the actual amount?

Actual costs, paid or unpaid status on the totals, the category breakdown and CSV export are Premium features. The free plan keeps the planned amounts and the paid or unpaid badge.

The totals do not change after I edit a line

Press Save line in the form and wait for the row to update. Totals are recalculated from the lines each time the page loads.

I have a second event

The free plan holds one event. Premium allows any number of events and lines; switch between events with the event menu above the totals.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
