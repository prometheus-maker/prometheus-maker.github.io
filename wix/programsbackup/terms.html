These terms cover the Course Backup: Course Export app ("the app") on your site.

What the app does

The app reads your Online Programs courses, their sections, steps and participants, and keeps dated copies of that content in the app's own storage on your site. Each copy downloads as a set of CSV files.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to your course content and your students' data. The copies are stored on your site. You are responsible for the content you publish and for handling your students' data lawfully, including any consent your own privacy policy requires.

Plans and limits

The free plan backs up one program with up to 50 steps and the participant list, and keeps the 2 most recent copies. Premium backs up every program, every step and quiz, each student's per-step progress, keeps 12 months of history and adds a weekly automatic copy. Premium is billed by the platform at $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.

What the copy is not

The app does not restore a copy back into Online Programs, because re-created programs would duplicate student enrollment and certificates. The CSV files are made for you to keep and to reuse by hand. Uploaded files and certificates are recorded by name only, never as bytes.

Availability

The app reads your site when you open its page; automatic copies are taken on the first visit after seven days, not by a timer. The app is provided as it is. The developer is not liable for losses that follow from a copy that could not be taken.
