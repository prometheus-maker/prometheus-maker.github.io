Privacy Policy: Course Backup: Course Export

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Course Backup: Course Export app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where it reads your Online Programs courses and keeps dated copies of them in the app's own storage on your site.

Data the app handles

  • Courses: each backup copy holds the program and section titles, the text, type and order of every step, and the status of each program. The copy is stored in the app's data collections on your site, not in a link to the course.
  • Participants: the participant list holds the name, e-mail address, enrollment status and dates of each student, as your site already records them. On the paid plan a copy also holds each student's per-step progress and quiz scores.
  • Files and certificates: uploaded files and certificate records are stored by name only. The app never downloads or copies their bytes.
  • Settings and history: the time of each backup, its counts and the change list against the previous copy are stored on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app reads your courses through your site's own services, keeps the copy inside your site, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Copies stay on your site until the app's retention rules remove them (the 2 most recent on the free plan, 12 months on the paid plan), or until you remove the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
