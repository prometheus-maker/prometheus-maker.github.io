Common questions

The page shows fewer steps than my course has

The free plan backs up one program with up to 50 steps. The card at the top of the page counts your whole site and says how many programs and steps the free plan leaves out. Premium covers every program and every step.

Where does the copy go

The copy is kept in the app's own storage on your site, not on a server of ours and not in a link to the course. Download CSVs puts it on your computer.

A copy has no student progress

Per-student progress is a Premium feature; the free plan keeps the participant list only. Progress rows come from each student's resolved steps, so a course nobody has started shows no progress rows even on Premium.

Uploaded files are missing from the CSV

File and certificate records are listed by name only. The app does not copy files or certificates as bytes.

The weekly copy did not run

The app cannot run a timer. The weekly copy is taken the first time you open the page after seven days have passed since the last one. You can always press Back up now.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
