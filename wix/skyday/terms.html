1. What you get

Skyday: Weather Forecast is a Wix app that shows the weather forecast on your event pages, for each event's date, hours and address, plus a weather widget for your business address and a dashboard page. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in your Wix dashboard and editor.

2. Weather data

Forecasts come from MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data and CC BY 4.0. Forecasts are predictions and can be wrong. Don't use the app for safety decisions; check your national weather service for warnings.

3. Plans and payment

The free plan shows the event-day forecast, the widget with today and the next three days, and a small credit link. The paid plan adds an hour-by-hour strip for each event, 7 days in the widget, a choice of °F or °C, 7 languages, a rain plan note, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the app keeps working with the free plan's options.
