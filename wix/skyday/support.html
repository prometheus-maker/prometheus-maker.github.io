Support: Skyday: Weather Forecast

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the event or page you tested and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I add the forecast to my event pages?

Open your dashboard, then Apps, then Skyday, and click Add to event page. Then publish your site once. You can also add it in the editor: open an event page and add the Event Weather plugin.

Why does an event show no forecast?

Online events, events without an address and past events show nothing. Events more than about 9 days away show the date their forecast will appear. Check that the event's address in Wix Events is a real street address, not only a venue name.

How do I add the weather widget?

In the editor, click Add (+), then App Widgets, then Skyday Weather. It uses the address in Settings, Business Info. To use another place, click the widget, then Settings, and type the place's latitude and longitude.

Where does the weather come from?

From MET Norway, the Norwegian weather service, which forecasts for the whole world. The forecast refreshes about every 30 minutes.

Can I show °F or °C?

The free plan picks the unit from your site's language and country (°F for the United States). Premium lets you choose.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The event block shows the event-day forecast without the hourly strip, the widget shows today and the next three days, and the small credit link comes back.
