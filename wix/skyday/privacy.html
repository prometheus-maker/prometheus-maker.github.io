This policy describes what the Skyday: Weather Forecast app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app shows a weather forecast on your event pages, for each event's date, hours and address, and offers a weather widget for any page of your site. A dashboard page lists your upcoming events and lets you add the forecast to your event page.

Data the app handles

  • Events: to show an event's forecast, the app's backend asks Wix for that event's date, time zone, venue name and the map coordinates of its address. These details are already public on your event page. Guest lists, tickets and orders are never read.
  • Business address: the widget uses the map coordinates, city and time zone from your site's Business Info, unless you type another place.
  • Weather requests: the backend sends only the map coordinates of the place (rounded to about 10 metres) to MET Norway's free forecast service at api.met.no, which answers with the forecast. No names, event titles or visitor data are sent. Forecasts are cached by place for 30 minutes or more.
  • Widget settings: your place, colours and other choices. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the backend also tells the widget and its settings panel whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, together with the app instance ID. This answer is kept in the server's memory for up to ten minutes.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors and writes nothing to a database.

Site visitors

The forecast does not set cookies and does not track anyone. Visitors' own locations are never asked for or used.

Where the data goes

Only the place coordinates go to MET Norway, as described above. The developer never receives your events, customers or anything else from your site, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
