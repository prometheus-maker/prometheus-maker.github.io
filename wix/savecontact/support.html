Support: Save Contact: vCard & QR Code

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the contact card?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Save Contact.

Where do the details come from?

From your Business Info: in your dashboard, Settings, then Business Info. Change your phone, email, address or logo there and the card and new contact files change within 10 minutes.

What happens when a visitor taps Save contact?

On iPhone and Android the phone shows your business as a new contact, ready to add. On a computer the contact file (.vcf) downloads and opens in the address book.

Can I put a QR code on my flyers?

Yes, with Premium. Open the widget's Settings and download the QR code as PNG or SVG. Scanning it saves your current contact details, so you don't need to reprint when your number changes.

I have several branches. Can each have its own card?

Yes, with Premium. Add your branches in Business Info, Locations. In the widget's Settings, pick one location or All locations.

What happens if the paid plan ends?

The card keeps working with Save contact, tap to call, email and directions. The QR code, locations, social links and the extra layouts go away, and the small credit link comes back.
