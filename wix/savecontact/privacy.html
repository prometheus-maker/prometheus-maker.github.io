This policy describes what the Save Contact: vCard & QR Code app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site: a contact card with your business details and a Save contact button that gives visitors your details as a contact file (vCard) for their phone or computer.

Data the app handles

  • Business details: the app's backend on Wix reads your site's Business Info (business name, logo, description, phone, fax, email, address and map position) and, if you choose locations in the paid plan, your Locations (name, address, phone, email). This is information you publish for your customers.
  • The contact file: its address contains your app instance ID and a signature, so only the app can make it. Anyone who opens it gets your public business details, the same ones the card shows.
  • Social links: links you type into the widget's settings (paid plan). Wix stores them with your site, like the other widget settings (what the card shows, button text, colours, font and layout).
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last card it got so the card shows at once on the next visit.

The backend keeps each contact file in memory for up to 10 minutes, stores nothing else and keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors, and reads nothing about them. Calling, emailing, the Directions link (Google Maps) and saving the contact happen only when a visitor taps them.

Where the data goes

Your public business details go to the visitors who open the card or save the contact. Nothing else leaves Wix. The developer never receives anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
