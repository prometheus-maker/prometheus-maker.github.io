1. What you get

Save Contact: vCard & QR Code is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your Business Info as a contact card with a Save contact button (a vCard file). You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan gives the contact card with Save contact, tap to call, email and directions, and a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds a QR code (on the card and as a PNG or SVG file), cards for your locations, social links, a strip and a button-only layout, your own button text and structured data for search engines, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.

3. Printed QR codes

A QR code opens the app's contact file for your site. It keeps working while the app is installed on your site. If you remove the app, printed QR codes stop working.
