1. What you get

Fixtures is a Wix app with a site widget that shows your club's upcoming games, results and league table, taken from the events on your Wix site and the scores you type in the app's dashboard. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up in the Wix editor.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan shows one team's upcoming games and its last 10 results, with your heading, colours and font, and a small credit link under the widget. The paid plan adds the league table with your own points rules, several teams as tabs, up to 50 games per tab, the form guide, a next-game banner, a note line per game (scorers, player of the match), the import of old results from a CSV file, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the widget keeps working with the free plan's options.
