This policy describes what the Fixtures app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a widget to pages of your Wix site that lists your club's upcoming games, results and league table. Games come from the events on your site; scores are the ones you type in the app's dashboard.

Data the app handles

  • Events: the app's backend on Wix reads the published events of your site in the categories you pick (title, date, time zone, venue name, category and event page link). It does not read guest lists, tickets or orders.
  • Scores: the scores, game status (played, postponed, cancelled) and notes you type in the dashboard, and old results you import from a CSV file on the paid plan, are saved in a data collection inside your own site. The developer has no copy.
  • Widget settings: heading, teams, tabs, points rules, colours, font and display options. Wix stores them with your site, like the settings of any other element.
  • Plan status: the widget and its panel ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan. The backend asks Wix and answers with that one fact and the app instance ID.
  • Browser storage: the visitor's browser keeps the last list of games it saw so the widget shows at once on the next visit.

The backend keeps no logs of visitors.

Site visitors

The widget does not set cookies, does not track visitors and does not collect anything they do. It shows games, scores and the table. If a visitor clicks a game, they go to that event's page on your site.

Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Wix. The developer never receives your events, scores, notes or anything about your visitors, and nothing is shared with anyone else.
