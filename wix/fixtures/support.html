Support: Fixtures

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the page with the widget and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do the games come from?

From the events on your site. Add each game as an event with a title like "Suns vs Crows" (also "Suns v Crows", "Suns - Crows" or "Crows @ Suns"), put it in a category such as "Games" or "U10 Boys", and it appears under Upcoming by itself. The event's location name is shown as the venue.

How do I add a score?

Open the app's dashboard page. Past games are listed there; type the score once and save. You can also mark a game as postponed or cancelled. The widget shows the new score the next time the page is opened.

Where is the widget?

Installing the app adds the widget to the page you were on, in its own section. To add another, open the editor, click Add Elements, then App Widgets (or My Widgets), then Fixtures.

The widget is empty on my site

It lists events whose title names two teams ("Suns vs Crows"). In the editor it shows sample games until your site has such events, so you can style it first; on the live site visitors see "No games scheduled yet" instead. Add your first game as an event and it appears.

How is the league table worked out?

From the scores you typed: played, won, drawn, lost, goals for and against, difference and points. You set the points for a win, a draw and a loss (3, 1 and 0 by default). The table is on the paid plan.

I run several teams

Give each team its own event category. On the paid plan, pick several categories and each one gets a tab.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

The widget keeps showing one team's upcoming games and last 10 results. The table, team tabs, form guide, next-game banner and notes switch off, and the credit link comes back. Your scores stay saved.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app reads your published events and keeps the scores you type inside your own site.
