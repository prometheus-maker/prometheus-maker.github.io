What it does

Returns arrive as e-mails, get lost in threads, and nobody remembers whether the coat came back. This app puts them in one dashboard page, and gives every customer a link to check on their own.

Add your shop name and share one returns link in your order e-mails or help page. A customer opens it, enters the order number and the e-mail they used at checkout, picks a reason, adds a note, and says whether they want a refund. The request appears in your dashboard with what you need to decide.

Approve it, reject it, mark the item received, or close it. You can write a note for the customer, such as where to send the parcel, and it shows on their status page. Each request also has its own link, so a customer can bookmark it and see the latest status.

Premium adds exchanges: the customer can ask for another size or colour instead of a refund.

The free plan includes 10 return requests each month. Premium makes them unlimited and adds exchanges, for $6 per month or $5 per month billed yearly.

Nothing is sent to your customers by the app; the status page and your own e-mails carry the updates. The shop checks the order number, so returns work for orders placed outside the shop too.
