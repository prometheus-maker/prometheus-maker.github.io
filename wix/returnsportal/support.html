Support: Returns Portal: Self-Serve

The Returns Portal: Self-Serve app keeps the returns of your Wix shop in one dashboard page and gives each customer a page to file one and follow its status.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Returns Portal: Self-Serve" in your Wix dashboard. Set your shop name: it is what customers see at the top of their page. Copy the returns link shown and put it in your order e-mails or on your help page.

A customer opens the link, enters the order number and the e-mail address used at checkout, picks a reason, adds a note and says whether they want a refund. The request appears in your dashboard. Approve it, not approve it, mark the item received, or close it, and write a note such as the return address. The customer sees the new status on their link.

Questions people ask

  • How do customers find my page? Share the returns link. It works for any order number you accept, and customers need no account.
  • How many requests do I get? Ten in each calendar month on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? Unlimited requests, and customers can ask for an exchange instead of a refund.
  • Can a customer withdraw a request? Yes, while you have not decided it yet.
  • Does the app send e-mails? No. The status link and your own e-mails carry the updates.
  • Where is the data? In the app's collections on your Wix site; removing the app removes it.
