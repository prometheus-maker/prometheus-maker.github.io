This policy describes what the Returns Portal: Self-Serve app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard where the returns your customers file appear, and a public web page where a customer can file a return for an order and follow its status.

Data the app handles

  • Return requests: what a customer enters on the public page (order number, e-mail address, item, reason, a note, their name if they give one, and whether they want a refund or an exchange) and what you enter in the dashboard (the status, your note for the customer) is stored in the app's data collections on your Wix site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Settings: the shop name and return instructions you save are stored in the app's settings collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the customer page asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. A customer sees only their own request, through the status link the app gives them.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your Wix site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
