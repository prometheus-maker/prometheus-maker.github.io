Support: Membership Extender

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, what you tried and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Why is a plan in the "Can't change" list?

The preview gives the reason next to each plan. The usual ones: the plan renews every month until cancelled, so it has no end date to move (pause it instead); the plan is paused, so resume it first; the plan has ended or was cancelled, so the member needs a new plan; the plan already ends on or after the date you picked.

Can I shorten a plan or move it to an earlier date?

No. Pricing Plans only lets an end date move later.

Why can't I pause a free plan?

Pricing Plans doesn't let apps pause free plans. You can extend them.

What happens to payments when I pause a plan?

Payments of a renewing plan pause too. When you resume, the days the plan was paused are added to its end date.

I extended by 14 days but the plan shows 14 days and one hour more (or less)

Pricing Plans adds whole 24-hour days. If a daylight saving change falls in between, the end time moves by an hour, but the plan still ends on the right day.

How many plans can I change at once?

Up to 10 per action on the free plan; run the action again for the next 10. Premium has no limit.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then Membership Extender.
