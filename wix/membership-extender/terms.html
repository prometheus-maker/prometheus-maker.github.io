1. What you get

Membership Extender is a Wix app that extends, sets a common end date for, pauses and resumes many Pricing Plans orders at once, and lists members without a current plan. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan changes up to 10 plans per action and shows the first 25 rows of each report. The paid plan removes these limits and adds CSV download of reports. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. Your responsibilities

You decide which plans to change and check the preview before you press Apply. Changing an end date or pausing a plan changes what your members can access. A plan can't be shortened again through this app, so read the preview before extending.
