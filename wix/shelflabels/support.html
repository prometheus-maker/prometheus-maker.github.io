Support: Shelf Labels: Barcode & Price

The Shelf Labels: Barcode & Price app turns the products in your Wix store into a printable A4 sheet of shelf-edge labels, with prices and barcodes.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Shelf Labels: Barcode & Price" in your Wix dashboard. The page lists the visible products in your store. Search or filter by category, tick the products you want, set how many copies of each, pick a sheet size, then press Open print sheet. The A4 PDF opens in a new tab; print it from there, or take the download if your browser blocks the tab.

Questions people ask

  • Which barcode goes on the label? The barcode saved on the product when it is a valid EAN-13 or UPC-A. Otherwise the app draws a Code 128 from the SKU.
  • Why is a product missing from the list? The page shows visible products only, up to 500. Hidden products and products outside that range do not appear.
  • Can I change a price on the label only? No. Labels use the product's price and sale price as they are in your store.
  • How many labels do I get? Twenty-five in each calendar month on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? Unlimited labels, and the Small (38 × 21 mm) and Large (99 × 38 mm) sheets as well as Medium.
  • Where do the sheets go? Nowhere. The PDF is built in your browser and saved to your computer; nothing is uploaded.
  • Does it work with variants? Yes. Each variant with its own price or SKU becomes its own row to tick.
