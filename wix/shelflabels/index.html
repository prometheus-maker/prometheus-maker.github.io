What it does

A shelf edge needs a label for every product: a price a customer can read from the aisle, and a barcode the till can scan. Doing that in a spreadsheet takes an afternoon each time prices change.

This app lists the products from your store inside your dashboard. Tick the ones you want, or tick all shown, and choose how many copies of each. Press Open print sheet and an A4 PDF opens with the labels laid out: name, variant, price, and the sale price with the old one struck through. Each label has a barcode.

The barcode is the one already saved on the product when it is a valid EAN-13 or UPC-A. When a product has no barcode, the app draws a Code 128 from the SKU, so the label still scans.

The Medium sheet is 63.5 × 38 mm and fits 21 labels on a page. Premium adds Small (38 × 21 mm, 65 a page) and Large (99 × 38 mm, 14 a page).

The free plan prints 25 labels a month. Premium is unlimited and adds the two other sheet sizes, for $6 a month or $5 a month billed yearly.

Nothing is uploaded or stored. The PDF is built in your browser and saved to your computer, and the app reads only your products, their prices and their barcodes.
