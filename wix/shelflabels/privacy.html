Privacy Policy: Shelf Labels: Barcode & Price

Effective date: 2026-11-20

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Shelf Labels: Barcode & Price app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard. The page reads your store's products and draws a print-ready A4 sheet of shelf labels: name, variant, price, sale price, SKU and a barcode.

Data the app handles

  • Products: names, variants, prices, sale prices, SKUs, category names and barcodes are read from your store when you open the page, to show the list and build the PDF. The app stores none of them. The PDF is built in your browser, in memory, and is not uploaded.
  • Label counter: the number of labels printed in the current month is stored in the app's own data collection on your Wix site, so the free plan's monthly limit can be counted. It holds a date and a count, no product or customer data.
  • Plan status: the page asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, reads no orders or customers, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties.

Keeping and removing data

The label counter stays on your Wix site until you delete it or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
