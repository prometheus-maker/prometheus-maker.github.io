These terms cover the Shelf Labels: Barcode & Price app ("the app") for Wix sites.

The service

The app gives a shop a dashboard page that reads the shop's products and builds a print-ready A4 PDF of shelf labels, each with a price and a barcode. The PDF is made in the seller's browser and saved to the seller's computer.

Plans

The free plan prints 25 labels in a calendar month. Premium makes the number unlimited and adds the Small and Large sheets. Plans are billed by Wix through the App Market; the price shown on the app's page at the time of purchase applies. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the free plan's limit applies again from then.
