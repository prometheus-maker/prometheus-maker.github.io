Common questions

No bookings appear in the list

The app lists the bookings for the next thirty days that your booking calendar returns. Add a booking in your bookings settings first, then press Refresh on the app's page.

I cannot add another person to a booking

The free plan keeps two named people per booking. The paid plan removes the limit for every booking.

The allergy and emergency fields are greyed out

Those fields are part of the paid plan. The free plan keeps the name, the age and the instructor note.

The CSV download is disabled

Export CSV is part of the paid plan and needs at least one participant. Add a person first, or upgrade to download the roster.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.

Someone asked me to remove their details

Open their booking, find the person's row and press Remove. The record is deleted from your site at once.
