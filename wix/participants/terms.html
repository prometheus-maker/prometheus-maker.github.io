The service

The app lets you record the people attending each of your bookings, with their ages, allergies and emergency contacts, and shows them grouped by booking in your dashboard. The records are yours: the app stores and displays them and does not change, reschedule or cancel anything in your booking calendar.

Plans and payment

The free plan keeps two named people per booking with a name, an age and an instructor note. The paid plan costs $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly (about $48 a year), after a 14-day free trial, and removes the limit, opens the allergy and emergency-contact fields and adds a CSV roster download. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by the App Market and your site's account; write to the address below for any billing question.
