What it does

A swim class takes one booking from one parent for three children. The booking shows a single customer name and a number of seats, so the teacher has no idea who is coming, what they are allergic to, or who to call in an emergency. This app adds a participant record for every person on a booking, kept with your own site's data.

Open the dashboard and the bookings for the next thirty days appear with the people already attached to each one. Choose a booking, type a name and an age, and add an allergy, an emergency contact and a short note for the instructor when needed. Each booking then shows its people as separate rows, so a class list is readable at a glance.

The app reads your bookings and does not change them: the booking itself, its time and its seats stay exactly as they were made. Participant records live in a collection on your site.

The free plan keeps two named people per booking. Premium removes that limit for every booking, opens the allergy and emergency-contact fields for each person, and lets you download the whole roster as a CSV file that opens in any spreadsheet. Premium costs $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly ($48 a year).
