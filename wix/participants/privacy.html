Privacy Policy: Participant Details: Bookings

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Participant Details: Bookings app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you record the people attending each booking: a name and an age for each person, and optionally an allergy, an emergency contact and a note for the instructor.

Data the app handles

  • Participant records: the name, age, allergy, emergency contact name and phone, and the instructor note you type are stored in the app's data collection on your site, linked to the booking id. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Bookings: the app reads the ids, dates and service names of your bookings for the coming thirty days through the platform's booking API, so it can list them beside their participants. The app never changes a booking.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Participant records stay on your site until you remove them in the dashboard or remove the app. Removing the app removes its collection and everything in it.
