This policy describes what the Shift Roster: Staff Schedule app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you record your staff, their weekly shifts and notes, and see the boards and hour totals.

Data the app handles

  • Staff: the name, role and colour you type for each person are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Shifts: the staff member, date, start time, end time and optional note of each shift are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail, contacts nobody about a shift and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page and shows nothing to your site's visitors.
