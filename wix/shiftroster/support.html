Common questions

The board is empty after I add staff

Shifts live on the board for the week shown at the top. Add a shift with a person, a day, a start time and an end time, or press Load a sample week in the team card to see how it works.

A shift crosses midnight

Type the start and end times as they are, for example 22:00 to 02:00. The app reads the end as the next morning.

Two shifts are flagged as overlapping

The warning names the person and both times. Move one of the shifts, or remove it, and the warning goes away. Overlaps are checked per person, not across the team.

Another week is locked

The free plan shows this week's board. Other weeks need Premium.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
