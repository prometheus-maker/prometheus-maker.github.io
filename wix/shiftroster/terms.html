What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you keep a team list and a weekly shift board (Monday to Sunday). You add staff and shifts yourself; the app counts hours and flags overlapping shifts for the same person.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the names, roles, shift times and notes you enter. The app stores them on your site in its own data collections. You are responsible for the information you enter about your staff.

Plans

The free plan covers up to five staff and the current week. Premium removes the staff limit, opens every past and future week (12 weeks either way), adds the reassign control on the board, printing and CSV export. Premium is billed by the platform at $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly ($48 a year), after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.
