This policy describes what the Job Board & Careers Page app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a dashboard page where you write job openings, and a public careers page where anyone can read the open roles and apply for one.

Data the app handles

  • Job posts: the title, location, type, closing date and description you write are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The open ones are shown on the public careers page.
  • Applications: what an applicant enters on the public page (name, e-mail address, a message, and a link to a CV or portfolio if they paste one) is stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Settings and statuses: the page look you save (business name, intro line, accent colour) and the status you set for an application (New, Reviewing, Rejected, Hired) are stored on your site.
  • Plan status: the dashboard and the careers page ask the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no visitor logs, sets no cookies, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The careers page is public: the job posts you leave open are visible to anyone who has the link, so do not put private details in a role description.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your Wix site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a job post deletes it. Removing the app removes its collections and everything in them; applicants should be told how long you keep their details.
