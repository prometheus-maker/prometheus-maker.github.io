Support: Job Board & Careers Page

Support for the Job Board & Careers Page app.

Getting started

Install the app, then open it in your Wix dashboard. Press New job, fill in a title, a location, the type and a description, and press Save job. Open the Careers page tab, copy the link and open it: the role is there.

Common questions

The New job button is greyed out on the free plan

The free plan keeps one job open at a time. Close the open job first, or upgrade to Premium to keep every role open at once.

An applicant says the form did not work

Ask them to press Send application again from the same page. The form needs a name, an e-mail address and a message, and a CV link must start with http:// or https://.

Can applicants upload a CV file?

Not yet. They paste a link to a CV or portfolio instead, for example a Google Drive, Dropbox or personal site link.

How do I close a role?

Press Close next to the job. It leaves the public careers page at once; Reopen puts it back.

Does the app send e-mail?

No. Applications appear in the dashboard, and you reply from your own mail.
