What the app does

The app reads the bookings in your site's booking calendar and stores dated copies of your bookings and clients in your site's dashboard, which you download as a ZIP file.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the data the app backs up. The app stores the copies on your site. You are responsible for keeping the downloaded files safe and for having the right to read the bookings on your site.

Plans

The free plan backs up the last 30 days and the next 10 upcoming sessions and keeps two copies. Premium backs up every booking all time, adds the full client list with contact details and booking counts, keeps 12 months of history and takes an automatic copy on the first page open after a week. Premium is billed by the platform at $6 a month, or $5 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial. You can cancel from your account at any time; the plan stays active until the paid period ends.

The app is provided as it is. A backup is a copy of what the booking calendar returned when it ran, so check a recent copy before you rely on it. The developer is not liable for bookings that changed or were deleted after a copy was taken, or for losses that follow from an incomplete backup.
