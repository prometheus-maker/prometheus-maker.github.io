What it does

Every appointment is revenue, and a booking you cannot find again is a client you may lose. This app keeps a dated copy of your bookings and the people who made them, in one ZIP you can keep.

Press Back up now and the app reads your calendar, page by page, and writes every session to a dated snapshot with the service, time, staff member, location, status and the client's name, e-mail and phone. Nothing leaves your site: the snapshot stays inside the app, and each download is a ZIP with a bookings file and a client file.

The page shows your own numbers first: how many bookings your site has, across how many services and clients, so you see what a backup covers. After every copy the app names what is new, missing or changed against the one before, so a cancelled booking is visible instead of silent.

Free backs up the last 30 days and the next 10 upcoming sessions, and keeps two copies. Premium backs up every booking all the way back, adds the full client list with contact details and booking counts, keeps 12 months of history and takes an automatic copy the first time you open the page after a week. Premium is $6 a month, or $5 a month billed yearly.
