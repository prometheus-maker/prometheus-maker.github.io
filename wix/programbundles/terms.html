1. What you get

Program Bundles: Course Pack is a Wix app for Online Programs. It lets you sell two or more programs together at one lower price. You install it from the Wix App Market, pick the programs in your Wix dashboard, set the price, and the app makes a pricing plan for the bundle and a bundle card for your site.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan makes one bundle. The paid plan makes any number of bundles and adds an early-bird end date, after which the bundle's plan stops selling. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. Your responsibilities

You choose which programs go into a bundle and what it costs. Check your prices before you publish a card. Your site's own Online Programs plan limits how many active participants it can have; the app adds buyers within that limit and cannot go beyond it. Buyers get access to a bundle's programs when Wix reports the plan order as paid; for offline payments that is when you mark the order paid.
