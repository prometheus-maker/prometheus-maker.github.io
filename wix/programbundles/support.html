Support: Program Bundles: Course Pack

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the bundle you made, and what you saw. A screenshot of the dashboard's bundle orders list helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

How do I start?

Open the app from your dashboard (Manage Apps, then Program Bundles), tick two or more programs, type a bundle price or take a quick discount, give the bundle a name and press Create bundle. The app makes the pricing plan and the bundle record.

Where is the bundle card on my site?

Add the Program Bundles card to a page from the Wix editor's Add panel and choose the bundle in the card's settings. Publish the site.

What does the buyer pay?

The bundle price you set, in the plan the app created. The card shows the single prices added up, the bundle price and the saving.

After payment, is the buyer in every program?

Yes. When Wix reports the bundle's plan order as paid, the app adds the buyer to every program in the bundle. A bundle orders list in the dashboard shows each order and what happened.

An order shows "Premium subscription required"

Your site's Online Programs plan allows a set number of active participants. Upgrade the site's plan or remove inactive participants, then the buyer can be added.

A buyer is on two programs but not the third

That program's own participant limit was reached, or Wix refused the add. The orders list shows the reason. Remove inactive participants and add the person from Online Programs, or let the next purchase try again.

Can I change a bundle's price?

Yes, on the paid plan: open the bundle in the dashboard and edit it, or change its plan in Wix's Pricing Plans page.

What does the early-bird end date do? (Premium)

After that date the bundle's plan stops selling. The card says until when. Clear the date to sell again.

How many bundles can I make?

The free plan makes one. Premium makes any number.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then Program Bundles: Course Pack.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app runs on Wix, and the developer receives nothing.
