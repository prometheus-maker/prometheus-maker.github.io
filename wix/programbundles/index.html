What it does

Program Bundles lets you sell two or more Online Programs together at one lower price. Pick the programs, type the bundle price, and the app makes a pricing plan for the package and a bundle card for your site.

The dashboard lists your programs with their single prices. Tick two or more and set the price, or take a quick discount: the saving appears straight away, as a whole percent and as money. Creating the bundle makes the pricing plan on your site, named after the bundle, with the programs listed in its description.

The bundle card shows the bundle name, each included program, the single prices added up and struck through, the bundle price, and a Save line. Its Buy button opens that plan's checkout. On phones the card stacks into one column, and its colours come from the panel.

After someone pays, the app adds the buyer to every program in the bundle, so one purchase opens the whole pack without you touching anything. A bundle orders screen lists every order it handled and what happened in each program.

The free plan makes one bundle. Premium makes unlimited bundles and adds an early-bird end date: the card says until when, and the plan stops selling after it.
