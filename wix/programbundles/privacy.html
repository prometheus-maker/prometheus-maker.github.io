This policy describes what the Program Bundles: Course Pack app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app sells two or more of your Online Programs together as one bundle. From your Wix dashboard you pick the programs, set a bundle price, and the app creates a pricing plan for the package and a bundle card for your site. When someone buys that plan, the app adds the buyer to every program in the bundle.

Data the app handles

  • Online programs: the program list with names and single prices, read to show what a bundle can contain.
  • Pricing plans: the plans the app creates for your bundles (name, description, price and its yearly or monthly variants), and the plan orders Wix tells the app about.
  • Bundle records: the bundle name, the program IDs and titles, the single total, the bundle price, the saving, the plan ID, the plan link name and an early-bird end date, stored in a collection on your own site.
  • Buyers: the member ID, contact ID and email from a bundle's paid order, and the participant records the app creates in each program, so a buyer can open the whole pack after paying.
  • An order log: for each bundle order, the order ID, plan ID, member ID, email, bundle name, how many programs were added and the result, stored in a collection on your own site. An error message may quote Wix's own reason (for example a participant limit).
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro box, kept in your browser's local storage.

Where the data goes

Everything is read and written through Wix's own APIs, by the app's code running on Wix. The developer has no other server and never receives your programs, plans, buyers or payments. Nothing is shared with anyone else. Wix handles the money and the payment details; the app never sees a card. Removing the app stops all processing; the two collections stay on your site until you delete them.
