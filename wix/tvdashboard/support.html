Support: TV Dashboard: Live Sales

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what the board showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

The numbers are not what I expect

Revenue counts paid orders created today in your site's time zone, after discounts and before refunds; shipping and tax are included in the order total. Open Orders in your dashboard and filter by today to compare. A very new order can take a few seconds to appear in the search.

Why is my revenue lower than the orders total?

Donation lines are shown in the donations tile, not in shop revenue, so a gift is never counted twice. Event ticket money is shown as tickets, not shop revenue.

TV mode fills only the window, not the whole screen

Some dashboard frames do not allow full screen. The board fills the browser window instead; press F11 for a true full screen. Esc brings the dashboard back.

Nothing shows on the board

Check that you are signed in to the right site, that your orders are marked paid, and that the site's time zone is right under Settings > Language & region. The free plan shows revenue and orders only.

Can a visitor to my site see this?

No. The board is a page in your Wix dashboard. Nothing is published to your site and no sales figure is sent anywhere else.

How do I change goals or tiles?

On Premium, open Goals, tiles and theme under the board, set them and press Save settings. The TV picks them up at its next refresh (within a minute).

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Manage Apps, then TV Dashboard.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the app works in your browser and the developer receives nothing.
