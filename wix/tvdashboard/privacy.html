This policy describes what the TV Dashboard app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app is a page in your Wix dashboard. It reads today's paid orders, bookings and event orders from your own site and shows the totals on a board you can leave open on a shop TV.

Data the app handles

  • Orders: created date, payment status, order total, currency, the buyer's first name, and each line's item name, quantity and total. Full names, addresses, emails and phone numbers are never shown on the board.
  • Bookings: start date, status and participant count for today.
  • Events: today's events and the quantities on their orders created today.
  • Site settings: the site's time zone and currency.
  • Your board settings: daily and monthly goals, theme and chosen tiles, stored in a collection on your own site and, as a copy, in the browser's local storage.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.

Where the data goes

Everything is read through Wix's own APIs from the browser while the page is open. The app has no server of its own: the developer never receives, stores or sees your orders, bookings, events or settings. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

Who can see it

Only someone signed in to your Wix dashboard on your site can open the page. Visitors to your site cannot reach it, and no sales figures are added to any public page.
