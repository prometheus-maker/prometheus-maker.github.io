1. What you get

Loyalty Rules is a Wix app that gives loyalty points for plan renewals, event ticket orders and paid invoices, and takes points back when an order is refunded, by rules you set. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard. It needs Wix Loyalty Program on the same site.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan runs one rule and shows the last 20 actions. The paid plan runs any number of rules and adds refunds, a bonus every set number of plan payments, the full history with a CSV file and catching up on past payments. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page.

3. Your responsibilities
