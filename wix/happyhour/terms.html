1. What you get

Happy Hour: Times & Specials is a Wix app that shows your happy hour or daily deal on your site: whether it is on now and until when, how long until it starts, the deals in the window with their prices, the week ahead, and a small card you can place anywhere on a page. You install it from the Wix App Market and set it up on the page in the Wix editor.

2. Your responsibility

You set the venue name, the days and hours, the deals and any dated exceptions, and you are responsible for keeping them correct. The app shows what you typed; it does not check your prices, and it is not a reservation, ordering or payment system. Times follow the time zone you chose, so a wrong time zone gives wrong times.

3. Plans and payment

The free plan covers one deal window today, one deal with its price and note, with a small credit link under the card. The paid plan adds the full week list, several deals per window, a countdown and progress bar, dated exceptions, a second venue, and removes the credit link. Wix sells the paid plan, charges you, and handles renewals, cancellations and refunds under the Wix App Market terms. Prices are shown on the app's Wix App Market page. If the paid plan ends, the app keeps working with the free plan.
