Support: Happy Hour: Times & Specials

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address and what the widget showed. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget on my site?

It was added to your page when you installed the app. In the editor, drag it where people look first (your home page or your menu page) and open Settings to change the venue, the times and the deals.

How do I set my happy hour?

Open the widget's Settings in the editor. Type your venue name, choose the time zone, then set a window for each day (for example Monday to Friday, 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM). Inside a window, add the deals: a label such as "Margaritas", a price such as "$5" and a short note like "rocks or frozen". The panel saves as you change things; close it and publish the site.

Why does the card say "Starts in 2h 10m"?

That is the time until your next window opens, counted on your venue's clock. Inside the window the card switches to "On now" with the time it ends and a progress bar.

Can I close the deal for a holiday?

Yes, with a dated exception: pick the date, then Closed, or another set of hours for that day. Exceptions are part of the paid plan; on the free plan the sample window runs every day.

Why is the time different from what I typed?

Check the time zone in Settings. The card follows that zone, not the visitor's, so someone reading it from another city still sees the real time at your venue. A window that runs past midnight, such as 10:00 PM to 1:00 AM, is shown as 10 PM–1 AM.

Can I run two venues?

The paid plan has a second venue with its own schedule; switch between them in Settings. The free plan covers one.

Where does the credit line come from?

On the free plan a small "Made with Happy Hour" link sits under the card. The paid plan removes it.

What does the widget write on my site?

Nothing beyond its own settings. It does not add collections, and it does not collect anything about your visitors.
