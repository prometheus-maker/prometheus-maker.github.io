What it does

Happy Hour puts the deal that is on right now at the top of your site. While the deal runs the card reads "On now" and "$5 margaritas until 7 PM"; before it starts, "Starts in 2h 10m". Someone standing outside with a phone gets the answer without scrolling.

You set it up in the widget's settings panel. Pick the days and hours, then add the deals in that window: a label, a price, a short note like "Rocks or frozen". Copy Friday's window to the weekend, or run a second window later in the evening. A dated exception closes or moves the deal on Christmas Eve.

The card follows your venue's clock, not the visitor's, so a guest reading it from another city sees the real time at the bar. Windows that run past midnight read "10 PM–1 AM". When today's deal is over, the card shows the day and time of the next one.

It fits bars, tapas places, restaurants, cocktail rooms, breweries, cafés and barber shops.

Free covers one window, today, with a small credit link. Premium adds a full week list, several deals per window, a countdown and progress bar, dated exceptions and a second venue, and removes the credit.
