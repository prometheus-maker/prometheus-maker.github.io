This policy describes what the Happy Hour: Times & Specials app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

You add the app's widget to a page of your Wix site and open its settings: your venue name, the time zone, a window of days and hours, and the deals that run in that window with their label, price and a short note. The widget shows a card on your site that says whether the deal is on now, when it starts, and the rest of the week.

Where your schedule is kept

Your venue name, times, deals and exceptions are stored in the widget's own settings on your site, like the text and images of any other element on the page. They are saved with your site and published with it. They are not sent to the developer's servers and not copied to any data collection.

Data the app's backend handles

The widget asks the app's backend two things when the page opens:

  • the site's time zone and, if you have Wix Business Info opening hours, those hours, used only as an optional starting point when you set the widget up;
  • whether the site is on the free or the paid plan, so the widget knows which features to show.

The answer is used by the page and not stored about visitors. The app does not ask for or keep names, phone numbers, email addresses or other personal details of anyone.

Site visitors

Visitors see the card the schedule makes for the current time at your venue. The app sets no cookies for visitors, does not track them, and builds no profile of them.

Where the data goes

Your schedule stays in your own Wix site. The developer never receives it, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The app sends no emails or text messages to visitors.
