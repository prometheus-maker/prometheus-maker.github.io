This policy describes what the Pledge-a-thon: Read-a-thon app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you record a pledge campaign, your readers and the units each reader completes, and a widget on your site where sponsors pledge a per-unit or fixed amount.

Data the app handles

  • Campaign: the campaign title, unit label, default amount per unit, money goal, end date and payment link you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site.
  • Readers: the reader name, class or team and the units you log are stored on your site.
  • Pledges: a sponsor's name, email address, the reader sponsored, the amount per unit or fixed amount, the optional cap and any message are stored on your site so you can record the pledge and collect the money. The sponsor's email is never shown on the public board.
  • Settings: the organisation name, accent colour, heading and thank-you line you set are stored on your site.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail from the public widget, takes no card payment and shares no data with third parties. Pledges are commitments: the money is collected through the payment link you set, on your own site.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting a reader removes that reader from the board; deleting a campaign keeps its readers until you delete them. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
