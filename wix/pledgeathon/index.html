What it does

A read-a-thon is simple on paper: sponsors promise an amount for every book read, then the school works out what each family owes. This app keeps the drive in one place and does the multiplication for you.

Set up a campaign with the unit you count, a default amount per unit, a money goal and an end date. Add your readers, grouped by class or team, then log the units each one finishes. The owed figure for every family updates as you type.

Sponsors use a short form on your page: they pick a reader, choose a per-unit pledge or a fixed amount, and can set a cap so the total never passes what they promised. The public board shows total pledged, units logged, days left and the class leaderboard.

A pledge is a commitment, not a card payment: the app records who promised what, and the money is collected through the donation page you already run on your site. Owners download a spreadsheet of what each family owes.

The free plan covers one campaign, 30 readers and the top five on the board. Premium adds five campaigns, unlimited readers, every reader, class totals and the amounts-owed spreadsheet, for $9 a month or $7.20 a month billed yearly, with a 14-day free trial.
