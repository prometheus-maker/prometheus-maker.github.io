Common questions

No services appear in the window form

The service list is read from your booking services each time the page loads. Add a service in your bookings settings first, then press Refresh on the app's page.

A booking shows no window

A booking shows a window only when its service has one saved. Next arrivals lists the bookings in the coming seven days that already have a window; set a window for the service and the bookings follow.

The width field is greyed out

A fixed two-hour window is the free plan. The paid plan sets any width from 30 minutes to 8 hours for every service.

The plan looks free after I paid

Open the app again; the plan is read each time the dashboard page loads. If it still shows free, write to the address above with your site name.
