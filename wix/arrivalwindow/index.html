What it does

Your customer hears a range, not an exact minute. Cleaning, plumbing, mobile grooming and home care all run on arrival windows: the job is booked for ten, but the customer is told to expect you between ten and twelve. This app keeps that promise in one place.

Open the dashboard, pick one of your services and set how wide its arrival window is. A booking that starts at ten o'clock with a two-hour window reads 10:00–12:00 as the note for the customer, while the calendar keeps the exact appointment time it already has. The app only reads your bookings, so nothing in the calendar changes.

The window follows the service, so a boiler repair can run three hours while a grooming visit takes one. Add a note such as "we will call 30 minutes before we arrive" and it travels with every window for that service. Next arrivals lists the bookings in the coming week with the range each customer will see.

The free plan covers one service with a fixed two-hour window and a note. Premium sets a width from 30 minutes to 8 hours for every service you offer. Premium costs $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly ($48 a year), with a 14-day free trial.
