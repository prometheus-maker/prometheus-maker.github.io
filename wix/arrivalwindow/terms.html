The service

The app lets you record an arrival window for each service you offer and shows the window beside your coming bookings. The window is your own promise to the customer: the app stores and displays it, and it does not change, reschedule or cancel anything in your booking calendar.

Plans and payment

The free plan covers one service with a fixed two-hour window. The paid plan costs $5 a month, or $4 a month billed yearly, after a 14-day free trial, and sets a window width for every service. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by the App Market and your site's account; write to the address below for any billing question.

You keep ownership of the window rules you enter. The app stores them on your site and uses them only to show the window in your dashboard, as the Privacy Policy describes.

The developer is not liable for losses that follow from a missed or late arrival.
