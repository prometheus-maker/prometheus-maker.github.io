Privacy Policy: Arrival Window: Appointments

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Arrival Window: Appointments app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you set an arrival window for each service you offer and see the coming bookings with the range each customer will be given.

Data the app handles

  • Window rules: the service name, the window width in minutes and the note you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Bookings and services: the app reads your services and the bookings booked for the coming days through the platform's booking API, so it can show the window beside them. The app never changes a booking.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Window rules stay on your site until you delete them in the dashboard or remove the app. Removing the app removes its collections and everything in them.
