What it does

Customers lose receipts and forget when the guarantee ends, and the shop hears about it only when something breaks. This app records each product, works out its cover end date, and gives the buyer a page to check it.

Register a sale in the dashboard, or put one registration link on your site. The customer enters name, email, product, serial number and purchase date. The app adds the cover months from your own rules and makes a warranty card with a reference code. The same page takes a problem report, so nobody has to hunt for an email address.

Every registration shows its state: covered, ending soon, or expired. Open one to add a claim, move it through in review, approved or resolved, and write a note the customer sees. The claim history sits under the card, so the whole story stays with the product.

Cover comes from your default months, with rules that lengthen or shorten it for products whose name contains a word. Dishwashers get 36 months, everything else 24, for example.

The free plan covers 50 registrations. Premium makes them unlimited, for $6 a month or $5 a month billed yearly.
