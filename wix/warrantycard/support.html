Support: Warranty Card: Registration

The Warranty Card: Registration app keeps product registrations and warranty claims for your Wix shop, and gives every customer a page to check their cover.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Warranty Card: Registration" in your Wix dashboard. Set your default cover in months, and add a rule for any product that needs a longer or shorter period. Copy the registration link shown and put it on your site or in your order e-mails.

A customer opens the link, enters name, e-mail, product, serial number and purchase date, and gets a warranty card with the cover end date and a reference code. If something breaks, they report it on the same page.

Questions people ask

  • How do customers find their card? Keep the link and the reference. Both are on the card the app shows after registering.
  • How many registrations do I get? Fifty on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? Unlimited registrations.
  • How is the end date worked out? Purchase date plus the cover months: your default, or the rule that matches the product's name.
  • Does the app send e-mails? No. The customer's page and your own e-mails carry the updates.
