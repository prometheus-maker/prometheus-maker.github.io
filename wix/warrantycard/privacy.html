This policy describes what the Warranty Card: Registration app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your Wix dashboard where product registrations and warranty claims appear, and a public web page where a customer can register a product they bought from you and follow a claim.

Data the app handles

  • Registrations: what a customer enters on the public page (name, e-mail address, product, serial number, purchase date and an optional order number) and what you enter in the dashboard is stored in the app's data collections on your Wix site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Claims: the problem a customer reports, the status you set and the note you write are stored in the same collections.
  • Settings: your cover months, warranty rules and shop settings are stored in the app's settings collection on your site.
  • Plan status: the customer page asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your Wix account, and Wix identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. A customer sees only their own registration, through the link and reference the app gives them.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your Wix site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
