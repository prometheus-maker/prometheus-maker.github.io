  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where is the widget?

It is added to your site when you install the app. In the editor, open Add Apps or the App Widgets list to place it on another page.

How do I change the language or colours?

Click the widget in the editor, then Settings. You can set the heading, the language of the readings, the sign shown first, the colours and the corners.

When does a new reading appear?

Every day for each sign, on the visitor's own date. The first visitor of the day may wait a few seconds while it is written.

Can I write in my own voice?

On Premium, open the app's dashboard and fill in your name, a few lines about your style and things to avoid. New readings for your site follow them.

How does the booking button work?

On Premium, pick one of your Wix Bookings services in the dashboard or paste any link. The button shows under every reading.

Why does it say "For entertainment"?

The readings are light, everyday texts and not advice. That line stays on every plan.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: the widget sends only the sign, language and date; nothing about your visitors is stored.
