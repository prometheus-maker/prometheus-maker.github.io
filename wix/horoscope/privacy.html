This policy describes what the Horoscope: Daily Astrology app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a horoscope widget to your Wix site. Visitors pick a zodiac sign and read a short reading for that sign. The readings are written once per day, sign and language by a text-generation service and then shown to every site that uses the same settings.

Data the app handles

  • Visitors: the widget sends only the chosen sign, the language, the period (day, week or month) and the visitor's calendar date to the app's backend. It sends no names, e-mails or IP addresses to the text-generation service, and the app keeps no data about visitors.
  • The visitor's sign is remembered in that visitor's own browser (local storage, key "horoscope-sign") so the next visit opens on it. It never leaves the browser and is not a cookie.
  • Premium voice settings: if you use Premium, the reader name, style notes, things to avoid and booking link you type in the dashboard are stored by the app, keyed to your site's app instance id, and the name and notes are sent with each request to write your site's readings.
  • Plan status: the backend asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan and keeps that answer for a few hours.
  • Counters: the app counts how many readings it writes per day and, on Premium, per site and month, to keep within its limits.

Text-generation service

Readings are written by DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com). It receives the sign, the date, the language, a fixed set of writing rules and, on Premium, your voice settings, only to write the reading. DeepSeek is based in China; see its privacy policy at https://platform.deepseek.com.

Where data is stored

Readings, counters, plan status and Premium voice settings are stored in the app's own storage hosted by Wix. The app's data collection on your site stays empty.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app stops all processing. Ask prometheus.maker@proton.me to delete your Premium voice settings at any time.
