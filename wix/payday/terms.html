1. What you get

Payday: Staff Pay is a Wix app that works out staff pay from your bookings using pay rules you set. You install it from the Wix App Market and use it inside your Wix dashboard.

2. Plans and payment

The free plan has every pay rule and shows pay for up to 2 staff members at a time. The paid plan shows pay for all staff in one report and downloads it as a CSV file.
