This policy describes what the Donor Receipts app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app is a page in your Wix dashboard. It reads the donation orders on your site, shows them with totals per donor, and turns them into receipt and statement PDF files. On the paid plan it can also save a receipt in the Files of the donor's contact when a donation is paid.

Data the app handles

  • Donation orders: date, amount, campaign, payment and refund status, the donor's name, email, billing address, note and checkout answers. They are read to show the tables and to write the PDFs. Orders without a donation are not used.
  • Contacts: when you file a receipt on a contact card, or when automatic filing is on, the PDF is uploaded to that contact's attachments. The app does not read other contact details.
  • Your receipt settings: charity name, registration number, address, email, website, logo, signature, wording and numbering, stored in a collection on your own site.
  • A receipt log: receipt numbers given to donations and, for each receipt filed on a contact card, the order ID, contact ID, donor email or name, amount, file name and result, stored in a collection on your own site.
  • Plan status: the app asks Wix whether your site is on the free or the paid plan.
  • Browser settings: whether you closed the intro and review boxes, kept in your browser's local storage.

Where the data goes

PDFs and CSV files you download are made in your browser and saved on your computer. Automatic filing runs as app code hosted by Wix and talks only to Wix's own APIs. The developer runs no server of its own, never receives, stores or sees your donations, donors or files, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app stops all reading and filing. The settings and the receipt log live in your site's collections, and filed receipts stay on the contact cards until you delete them.
