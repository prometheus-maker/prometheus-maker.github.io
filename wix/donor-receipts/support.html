Support: Donor Receipts

Support is open to everyone, on the free and the paid plan.

How to get help

  • Email: prometheus.maker@proton.me. Include your site address, the order number and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
  • Replies usually go out within two business days.

Quick answers

Where do I put my charity's details?

Open the app and go to Receipt settings. Add the name, registration or tax number, address, logo and signature, write the text that goes under the amount, then press Save settings. Preview receipt shows the result.

How do I make a receipt?

On the Donations tab, press Receipt next to the donation. The PDF downloads to your computer.

How do I make year-end statements?

On Donors & statements, pick the year. Statement next to a donor downloads theirs. Premium makes one for every donor at once, as one PDF or a ZIP.

Which donations are included?

Paid donations made through your donation form. Shop items bought in the same checkout are left out. Refunded money is taken off, and fees a donor chose to cover are shown on the receipt.

How do monthly donations show up?

Each paid payment is its own donation with its own receipt, and the year statement lists every payment.

How do receipt numbers work? (Premium)

Each donation gets the next number the first time a receipt is made for it, and keeps it. Set the prefix and first number under Receipt settings. On the free plan receipts show the order number.

How does contact card filing work? (Premium)

Turn it on in the Contact cards tab. When a donation is paid, its receipt is added to the donor's contact Files within about a minute. For older donations, tick them on the Donations tab and press File on contact cards.

How do I get a Gift Aid list? (Premium)

Add a Gift Aid checkbox to your checkout form in your checkout settings. Then open the Gift Aid tab, pick that field and download the CSV. The columns follow the HMRC donations schedule.

What should the receipt say?

That depends on your country and your organisation. The app offers starting texts that you can change. It does not give legal or tax advice.

Some letters look different

The PDF font covers Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters. Right-to-left scripts and Chinese, Japanese or Korean text are not supported yet.

Where the app lives

In your dashboard, open Apps, then Donor Receipts.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: PDFs are made in your browser, filing uses Wix's own APIs, and the developer receives nothing.
