Support: Lot Expiry: Batch Tracker

The Lot Expiry: Batch Tracker app keeps the batches of your products with their lot codes and expiry dates, and turns them into the lists a shop works from.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Lot Expiry: Batch Tracker" in your dashboard. The page lists your products; pick one in "Add a batch", type the lot code, the quantity, the made date and the expiry date, and press Add batch. The row appears with its status: expired, or how many days are left.

Printing

Press Print list to print the table you are looking at. On Premium, FEFO pick list prints the batches oldest expiry first with a box to tick off each one, and Lot labels prints sheets of labels with your shop name at the top. "Save as PDF" in the print dialog keeps a copy.

Questions people ask

  • Where do batches live? In the app's collections on your site. Removing the app removes them.
  • Does the app change my stock? No. It reads your product names for the picker and never writes to products or orders.
  • How many batches? Fifty on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? The 30, 60 and 90-day windows, the FEFO pick list, the lot labels and the CSV export.
  • Can I get the list into a spreadsheet? Yes, Export CSV on Premium.
  • Does the app send e-mails? No. You see the windows when you open the page.
