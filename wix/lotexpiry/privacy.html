Effective date: 2026-11-20

This policy describes what the Lot Expiry: Batch Tracker app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you record batches of your products, with a lot code, a quantity and dates, and print pick lists and lot labels from it.

Data the app handles

  • Batches: the product you pick, the lot code, the quantity, the made and expiry dates and any note you type are stored in the app's data collections on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Settings: the shop name you save for printouts is stored in the app's settings collection on your site.
  • Products: the app reads your product names and ids to fill the product picker. It never changes a product.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. The app has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collections and everything in them.
