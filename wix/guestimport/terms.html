What the app does

The app reads the guest list you paste or upload, checks each row, and adds the rows you confirm as RSVPs on the RSVP event you choose.

Your account and your data

You keep all rights to the list you upload. You are responsible for having the right to use the names and addresses in it, and for the guests you add to your event. The app keeps no copy of the list.

Limits and accuracy

The app checks names, e-mail addresses and extra-guest counts, and skips rows it recognises as already on the event. Those checks reduce mistakes but do not replace your own review of the preview, which you see before anything is written. Ticketed events that use reservations rather than RSVPs are outside the app's scope.
