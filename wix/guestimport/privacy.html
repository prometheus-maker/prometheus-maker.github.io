Privacy Policy: Guest List Import: CSV Upload

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Developer: Prometheus (https://prometheus-maker.github.io)

Contact: prometheus.maker@proton.me

This policy describes what the Guest List Import: CSV Upload app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app adds a page to your site's dashboard where you choose one of your RSVP events, paste or upload a guest list from a spreadsheet, review the rows and add the guests to the event.

Data the app handles

  • Guest list: the text or file you paste is read in your browser to show the preview. It is not sent anywhere except as the individual RSVPs the app creates when you press import.
  • Events and guests: the app reads the events on your site and the guests already registered for the chosen event, so it can list the events and skip duplicates. It creates RSVPs for the rows you import.
  • Plan status: the app asks its backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and the platform identifies the signed-in user to it in the usual way.

The app stores nothing of its own: there is no database, no copy of a list and no log of guests kept by the app. It sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. It has no public page.

Keeping and removing data

The guests you import are ordinary RSVPs on your event, kept by the platform in the same way as guests you add by hand. Remove them in your event's guest list, or remove the app; the app keeps no separate copy to delete.
