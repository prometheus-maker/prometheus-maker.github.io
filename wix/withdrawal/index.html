What it does

Since June 2026, shops selling to consumers in the EU must show a way to cancel an order on the site. A form buried in a help page is easy to miss, and a cancellation sent by e-mail leaves you without a reference number or a date.

This app gives you a button to place in your footer or on any page. A buyer clicks it, sees "Withdraw from contract", fills in the order number, name and e-mail, and confirms. The confirmation appears at once with a reference number and a UTC timestamp, and the buyer can print it or save it as a PDF. No account, no waiting for an e-mail.

Every request lands in your dashboard, newest first, with the order number, the reason and the 14-day deadline counted from the order date. Mark one handled or reopen it later; each has its own link you can share. The free plan keeps the latest 20 requests a month.

Premium keeps a full 12-month log, adds the model withdrawal form text you can show on the page, and covers every language of your site, for $6 per month or $5 per month billed yearly.

The app does not give legal advice; check your local regulations. Nothing leaves your site: requests are stored in the app's collections, and removing the app removes them.
