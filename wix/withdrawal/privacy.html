This policy describes what the Withdrawal Button: EU Cancel app ("the app") does with data.

What the app does

The app gives your site a public page where a buyer can withdraw from an order, and a page in your dashboard where the withdrawal requests appear.

Data the app handles

  • Withdrawal requests: what a buyer enters on the public page (order number, order date, name, e-mail address, items and reason) is stored in the app's data collection on your site. The app's backend keeps no separate copy.
  • Plan status: the public page asks the app's backend whether your site is on the free or the paid plan, and the backend answers with that one fact.
  • Account: the dashboard runs inside your account, and identifies the signed-in user in the usual way.

The app keeps no logs of visitors, sends no e-mail and shares no data with third parties. A buyer sees only their own request, through the reference number and page the app gives them.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on your site until you delete it, in the dashboard or by removing the app. Deleting the app removes its collection and everything in it.
