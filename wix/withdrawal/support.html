Support: Withdrawal Button: EU Cancel

The Withdrawal Button: EU Cancel app adds a public page where a buyer can withdraw from an order, and a dashboard page where every request appears with its deadline.

Getting started

Install the app, then open "Withdrawal Button: EU Cancel" in your dashboard. It shows the public page link for your site. Put that link in your footer or on your help page, or link to it from an order e-mail.

A buyer opens the link, presses "Withdraw from contract", fills in the order number, name and e-mail, and confirms. The receipt shows a reference number and the time, and they can print it or save it as a PDF. The request appears in your dashboard. Mark it handled when you have dealt with it, or reopen it later. Each request has its own link.

Questions people ask

  • Does a buyer need an account? No. The page is public and takes about a minute.
  • What is the 14-day deadline? It is counted from the order date and shown beside each request.
  • How many requests do I get? Twenty in each calendar month on the free plan, unlimited on Premium.
  • What is different on Premium? Unlimited requests, a full 12-month log and the model form text.
  • Does the app send e-mails? No. The receipt and the printable acknowledgement carry the record.
  • Where is the data? In the app's collection on your site; removing the app removes it.
